Cut beef into 1-inch pieces and brown in hot fat. Add onion and garlic; brown lightly. Mix together catsup. Worcestershire sauce, vinegar, brown sugar, paprika, salt, mustard and pepper. Stir in with meat. Add 3 cups water; cover and simmer 2 to 2 1/2 hours. Thicken with mixture of 2 tablespoons flour and 1/4 cup water. Pour goulash over cooked noodles and garnish with parsley. Serves 8.
