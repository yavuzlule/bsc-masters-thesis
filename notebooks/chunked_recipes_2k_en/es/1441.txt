Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. Combine cookie crumbs and margarine; press firmly on bottom of 13 x 9-inch pan. In medium saucepan over medium heat, melt 1 cup chocolate chips with sweetened condensed milk and vanilla. Pour evenly over prepared crust. Top with nuts and remaining chips. Bake 20 minutes or until set; cool. Chill if desired. Cut into bars. Store tightly covered at room temperature.
