Cut beef into 1-inch squares. Add 1 1/2 teaspoons salt. Chop the onion fine and brown in oil. Add beef and paprika. Let beef simmer in its own juice for 1 hour. Add sliced carrots and chopped parsley. Simmer for 1/2 hour. Add water, diced potatoes and remaining 1 1/2 teaspoons salt. Simmer until potatoes are done; add black pepper.
