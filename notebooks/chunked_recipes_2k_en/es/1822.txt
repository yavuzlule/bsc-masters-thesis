In saucepan, melt butter. Stir in flour, salt and pepper. Add chicken broth; stir constantly until mixture boils. Cook for 1 minute. Remove from heat; set aside. In skillet, brown pork chops; season with additional salt and pepper. Put sliced potatoes and onion into greased 13 x 9-inch baking dish. Cover with broth mixture. Put pork chops on top. Cover; bake for 1 hour. Uncover and bake for 10 minutes. Sprinkle with paprika and parsley before serving.
