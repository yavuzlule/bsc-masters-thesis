Sift together flour, baking powder and salt. Heat oven to 400\u00b0. Work shortening in a bowl until creamy. Add the 1 cup granulated sugar and beat until well blended. Add egg; beat thoroughly. Add sifted dry ingredients alternately with lemon juice to creamed mixture. Beat until smooth. Cover and chill 1 hour. Roll a small portion of dough at a time on a lightly floured board to a thickness of about 1/4-inch. , (Keep rest of dough refrigerated. ). Cut with a 2-inch cookie cutter. Place cookies 2 inches apart on a lightly greased cookie sheet and sprinkle with granulated sugar. Bake 10 to 12 minutes, until edges are lightly browned. Cool on wire racks. Makes about 4 to 5 dozen cookies.
