Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. Grease and flour two 9 x 5-inch loaf pans. In a medium bowl, stir berries, eggs and oil. In a large bowl, combine flour, sugar, cinnamon, soda, salt and nuts. Add berry mix to dry ingredients and stir until blended. Pour into greased pans. Bake 1 hour or until toothpick inserted in center comes out clean.
