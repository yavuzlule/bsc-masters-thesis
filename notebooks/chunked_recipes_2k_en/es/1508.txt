Mix flour, sugar and salt. Cut in margarine and stir in 1/2 cup of nuts. Press into 9 x 13-inch pan. Bake at 350\u00b0 for about 15 minutes. Mix 1 1/4 cups sugar and 2 tablespoons flour. Stir in beaten eggs, milk, salt and vanilla. Fold in cranberries, coconut and the rest of the nuts. Spread over partly baked crust. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes, until top is golden. Cool in pan 10 minutes on a rack; cut in bars while warm.
