Coat an 11 x 13 x 2-inch baking dish with vegetable cooking spray. Layer 2 cups sweet potatoes in half of dish. Layer 2 cups of red potatoes in other half. Sprinkle evenly with onion. Drizzle with 1 1/2 tablespoons of oleo. Sprinkle with 1/4 teaspoon of salt and 1/8 teaspoon of pepper. Repeat procedure with remaining potatoes, oleo, salt and pepper.
