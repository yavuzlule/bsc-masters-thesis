Slice onions and cucumbers. Sprinkle salt and alum over cucumbers and onions. Let stand 1 hour. Drain in colander; set aside. Make syrup of vinegar, sugar, celery seed, mustard seed, turmeric and ginger in large pot and bring to boil for 5 minutes. Pour drained cucumbers into syrup. Pour into hot, sterile jars immediately. Seal. I put the jars in water and bring to a boil before filling so they will be hot enough to seal. Yield: 6 pints.
