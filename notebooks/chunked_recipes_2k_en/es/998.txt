In 4-quart casserole, combine lamb, 1 1/4 cups water, soup mix and bay leaf. Heat, covered, 7 to 8 minutes on High. Continue cooking on power level 3 for 35 to 40 minutes. Stir occasionally. Add carrots, heat on level 3 for 15 minutes until ingredients are done. Stir once. Peel and cube potatoes and add to stew. Blend flour and remaining water. Heat 5 minutes on High, or until thickened, stirring twice. Serves 6.
