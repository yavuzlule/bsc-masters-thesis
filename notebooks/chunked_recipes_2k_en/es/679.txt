Line a 13 x 9 x 2-inch baking pan with, aluminum, foil. Grease well. Arrange crackers on bottom of, pan, breaking, if necessary, to completely cover pan. Combine, brown, sugar and margarine, in a medium pan. Cook and, stir, over, medium-high heat until mixture boils. Remove from heat;, pour over crackers. Bake, in a, 400\u00b0, oven 5 minutes. Remove, from, oven. Immediately sprinkle with chocolate pieces. When chocolate is soft, spread over top of sugar mixture. Sprinkle, with, nuts. Chill in the refrigerator 30 minutes. Break, into, bite, size pieces. Makes 24 to 36 pieces.
