Boil beans in beef and chicken stocks until tender. Meanwhile, saute meat, onion and garlic. Add diced tomatoes and juice after meat is cooked. Reduce this mixture to a paste. When beans are tender, remove 2/3 of beans and puree. Combine pureed meat and bean puree. Put back into pot with beans. Let simmer. Will thicken. Season with salt and pepper. Garnish with parsley.
