In large bowl, mix 1/2 cup flour, sugar, salt, lemon peel and yeast. Combine milk, water and margarine in saucepan. Heat liquid (120\u00b0 to 130\u00b0). Gradually add to dry mix. Beat 2 minutes on medium speed. Add egg and 1/2 cup flour. Beat high 2 minutes. Stir in more flour to make soft dough. Cover and rise in oven until double (30 minutes).
