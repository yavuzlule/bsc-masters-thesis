Grease and flour 10-inch tube pan. Sift flour, cocoa and soda; set aside. Cream Country Crock and sugar together in large mixing bowl. Add eggs, mixing, one at a time. Add sour cream, while continuing to mix. Add flour, 1/2 to 1 cup at a time. Add vanilla and lemon flavorings. Bake at 325\u00b0 for 1 to 1 1/2 hours. Use a toothpick to doneness of cake before removing.
