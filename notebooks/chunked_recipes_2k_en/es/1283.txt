Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. In bowl, combine flour, baking powder, salt and pepper. Set aside. Combine one cup sugar and oil; mix well. Beat in eggs and vanilla. Gradually add flour mixture. Stir in morsels. Drop by rounded teaspoonfuls onto ungreased cookie sheet. Shape into balls. Roll in remaining sugar. Bake 8 to 10 minutes. Makes 5 1/2 dozen.
