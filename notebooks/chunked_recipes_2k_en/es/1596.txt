Make a large patty for each person. Place each patty separately in foil. Sprinkle 1/2 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce over meat; dash with salt and pepper. Place slice of onion on top of meat. Place sliced potato on top of onion. Place sliced carrot around meat. Sprinkle with all-purpose flour, seasoning and a little more sauce over vegetables. Wrap tight in foil. Bake 1 1/4 hours at 325\u00b0. Serve hot.
