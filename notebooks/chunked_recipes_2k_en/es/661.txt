Break both kinds of bread in small pieces. If chicken stock is available, use enough to moisten breads (if not use water). Heat butter in skillet and add meat, onions, bell pepper, pepper and garlic. Cook until meat is done. Add seasonings. Mix lightly, add bread to mixture. Toss lightly with fork and add eggs; toss again. Put in a greased baking dish and bake 30 minutes at 350 degrees.
