Mix together nuts, cinnamon, cloves and sugar; set aside. Brush 9 x 13-inch pan with melted butter. Line bottom of dish with 10 sheets of filo, brushing each sheet with melted butter. Spread 1/3 of nut mixture evenly on top and cover with another 5 sheets of filo, brushing each sheet with melted butter. Place 1/2 of the remaining mixture on top of filo. Add 5 more sheets of filo. Repeat this step once more, using remainder of filo, brushing each sheet with butter.
