Heat oven to 375\u00b0. Mix flour, margarine, powdered sugar, baking powder and just enough milk until dough forms. If dough seems dry, mix in more milk one teaspoon at a time. Divide dough into 6 equal parts. Shape each part into a ball. Place on an ungreased cookie sheet. Flatten slightly. Make an indention 1 3/4 inches in diameter and about 3/4-inch deep in center of each. Fill each indention with about 2 tablespoons pie filling.
