Prepare Creamette shells according to package directions. Drain. Cook ground beef, celery and onion. Drain excess fat. Add tomatoes with liquid (breaking up with spoon), tomato paste, salt, oregano and pepper. Simmer 5 minutes. Combine meat mixture with shells and pour into a 3-quart casserole. Top with cheese. Bake in a 350\u00b0 oven for 25 to 30 minutes.
