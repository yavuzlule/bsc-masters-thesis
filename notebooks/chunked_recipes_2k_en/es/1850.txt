Heat oven to 350\u00b0. Mix meat, rice, 1/3 cup water, onion, salt, garlic and pepper. Shape into round balls. Place balls in an ungreased baking dish (8 x 8 x 2-inch). Stir together remaining ingredients and pour over meatballs. Cover with foil and bake 45 minutes. Uncover and bake 15 minutes longer.
