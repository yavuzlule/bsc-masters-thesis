Chop onion and saute in butter until tender, then slowly add crab meat. Stir on low heat so it wont stick. Add white pepper, then slowly add 2 cans of soup, then 2 cans of milk, stirring slowly. Do the same with the other 4 cans of soup. Let cook on low heat until ready to serve. Feeds 6 to 8 people. This can also be frozen for quick meal with no bother. Serve with crackers.
