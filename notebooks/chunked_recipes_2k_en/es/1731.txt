Rub chicken breasts with curry powder and put in baking dish. Pour on orange juice sweetened with a little honey. Bake until done in very slow oven (or adjust for microwave). Thicken juices with cornstarch. Serve with brown or white rice. , (Slow oven is 300\u00b0 and would take about 1 1/2 hours, depending on the number of chicken breasts. )
