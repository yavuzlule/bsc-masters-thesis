In casserole dish, crumble beef and sausage; add onion and garlic. Cook, covered, on High for 4 to 6 minutes in microwave. Drain fat. Stir in sauce, undrained beans, water and pepper. Cook, covered, on High 7 to 9 minutes or until thoroughly heated, stirring once. Arrange biscuits around outer edges, leaving center open. Sprinkle the center with cheese and parsley. Cook, uncovered, on High 2 to 3 minutes or until biscuits are done, turning at 1 1/2 minutes.
