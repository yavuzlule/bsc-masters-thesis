Grease and flour or \Pam\ the bottom and sides of a 9 x 13-inch cake pan. Spread the coconut and pecans in the bottom of cake pan; add cake mix on top. Cream together the cream cheese, oleo and confectioners sugar. Drop the creamed mixture by spoonfuls over the unbaked cake. It will pretty much cover the top of the cake. Bake in a 350\u00b0 oven for 45 minutes. It will look like a disaster, but taste great!
