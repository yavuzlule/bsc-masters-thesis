Saute onion and green pepper in small amount of margarine. Add ground beef and brown. Add tomato soup and pizza sauce. Stir in water. Simmer 15 minutes. Layer cooked noodles in a large greased casserole dish with lid. Pour meat mixture over noodles. Add mushrooms and pepperoni. Top with cheese. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 45 minutes.
