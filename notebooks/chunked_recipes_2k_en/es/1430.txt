Mix together garlic powder, parsley flakes. Parmesan cheese and oregano. Cut bread in half lengthwise. Spread oleo or butter mixture over both halves of bread. Cut halves into 1-inch slices. Dont cut all the way through the crust. Wrap each half in foil and bake in a preheated 350\u00b0 oven for about 15 minutes. Serve at once. Serves 10 to 12.
