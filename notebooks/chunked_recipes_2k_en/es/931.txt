In a bowl, whisk together the soy sauce, the garlic, the ginger, the honey and the pepper. Add the wings; toss them to coat and let them marinate, covered and chilled, for 2 hours. Line a baking pan with foil and arrange the wings, drained (reserving the marinade) in one layer on the foil. Brush the wings with the, oil; sprinkle them with the sesame seed and bake them in the middle of a preheated 400\u00b0 oven, basting them occasionally with the reserved marinade, for 30 to 35 minutes or until they are browned. Serves 4 as an hors doeuvre.
