Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. Cream butter; gradually add sugar, creaming until light and fluffy. Sift flour with baking powder and salt. Add eggs one at a time to creamed mixture, beating well after each addition. Add flour mixture alternately with milk and flavorings, beating after each addition until smooth. Pour batter into three greased and floured 9 x 2-inch cake pans. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 25 to 30 minutes. Cool in pans 10 minutes. Remove from pans; finish cooling on racks.
