Layer bottom of pan with vanilla wafers; put slice of banana on each wafer. Mix Eagle Brand milk and water; add pudding and beat with fork until lumps are gone. Add sour cream; mix until well blended. Add Cool Whip. Cover layer of wafers and bananas with 1/2 of pudding. Add another layer of wafers and bananas; cover with other half of pudding. Crumble remaining wafers on top.
