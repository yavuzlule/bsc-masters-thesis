Wash and boil potatoes (leave at stage you can grate). Cool and peel. Place in buttered pan. Mix together sour cream, chicken soup and water, 1/2 cup cheese, onion or chives, salt and pepper. Put on top of potatoes. Top with crushed corn flakes and melted butter. Sprinkle on the other 1/2 cup cheese. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 30 minutes.
