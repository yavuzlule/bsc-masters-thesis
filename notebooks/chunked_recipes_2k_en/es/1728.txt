With fork, stir together the baking mix and water to form a soft dough. Turn out dough onto floured surface; knead 10 times. Roll out dough to 3/4-inch thickness. Cut with 2-inch round cutter. Place 1 inch apart on ungreased baking sheet. Bake in a 400\u00b0 oven for 12 to 14 minutes or until golden brown. Makes 12 biscuits.
