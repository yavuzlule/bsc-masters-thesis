Combine beans, turkey, onion and chili powder in a skillet. Cook over medium heat until turkey is brown. Stir occasionally to mash beans and break up turkey. Stir in salsa and place in microwave-proof dish. Sprinkle with cheese and microwave on High 1 to 2 minutes until cheese is melted. Combine sour cream, cilantro, salt and pepper and spread on top. Sprinkle with avocado, tomato and green onions. Serve with tortilla chips. Makes 7 cups.
