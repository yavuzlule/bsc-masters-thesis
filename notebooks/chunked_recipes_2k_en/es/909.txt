In a mixing bowl, stir slightly softened ice cream until smooth. Spoon the ice cream into the pie crust and cover. Place pie in freezer for several hours, until hard. Remove pie from freezer and let stand for 10 minutes to soften. Spoon strawberry topping onto serving plate. Cut pie and place on top of strawberry topping. Garnish with chocolate sundae syrup and other toppings, as desired. Sprinkle with nuts and a cherry. Return to freezer.
