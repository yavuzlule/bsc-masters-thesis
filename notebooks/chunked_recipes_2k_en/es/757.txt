Cream margarine and sugar on medium speed for 5 minutes. Add eggs, one at a time, and vanilla. Mix on low speed until blended. Combine dry ingredients. Add to creamed mixture. Mix on low speed until blended. Add nuts; mix to blend. Spread mixture evenly in 2 greased 12 x 18 x 2-inch baking pans. Bake at 325\u00b0 for 25 minutes. Cut 5 x 6-inch. Yield:, 60 portions.
