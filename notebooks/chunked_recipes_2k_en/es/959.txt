Peel and cut eggplant into 1/4-inch slices. Peel tomatoes and slice. In a 2-quart casserole dish, arrange a layer of eggplant; sprinkle with salt and pepper. Put a layer of tomatoes on top; sprinkle with salt, pepper and oregano flakes you have crushed with your fingers and cheese. Repeat layers until casserole is full, ending with cheese on top. Cover; bake in a 400\u00b0 oven for 30 to 40 minutes, or until eggplant is tender when tested with fork.
