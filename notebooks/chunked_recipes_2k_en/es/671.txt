Combine first 5 ingredients, mixing well. Spoon mixture into a lightly greased 1 1/2-quart casserole. Cover and refrigerate for 8 hours or overnight. Remove from refrigerator; let stand 30 minutes. Bake, uncovered, at 350\u00b0 for 25 minutes. Remove from oven; top with marshmallows. Bake an additional 7 minutes or until marshmallows are golden. Yield: 6 to 8 servings.
