In large mixing bowl blend oil and sugars until mixed. Add vanilla and beat in eggs, one at a time. In another bowl blend dry ingredients. Add to egg mixture until well blended. By hand, stir in carrots and walnuts. , pour into greased and floured tube or Bundt pan. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 50 to 60 minutes. Cool in pan; invert on serving plate and sprinkle with powdered sugar. Could use glaze or cream cheese icing.
