Place rice, seasoning packet, butter, water chestnuts, mushrooms, onion and soy sauce in a 3-quart casserole. Microwave on High (100%) for 3 to 4 minutes or until onion is tender. Stir halfway through cooking time. Pour in chicken broth. Cover with a tight fitting lid or plastic wrap. Microwave on High for 5 to 7 minutes or until boiling. Stir, then microwave on Medium (50%) for 40 to 45 minutes or until rice is tender and liquid is absorbed. Yield:, 6 to 8 servings.
