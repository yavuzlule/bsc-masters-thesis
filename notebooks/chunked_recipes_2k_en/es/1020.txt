Cream butter, and, sugar. Add eggs, one at a time, and mix well. Add buttermilk. Add flour, 1 cup at a time, mixing well. Add vanilla. For, layer cake bake at 350\u00b0 for 35 to 40 minutes. For sheet, cake, pan, bake at 325\u00b0 for 1 hour and 20 minutes. Makes, three, layers, or, sheet cake. Real nice for birthdays.
