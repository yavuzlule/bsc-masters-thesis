Mix crushed Oreo cookies and melted butter; press into 9 x 13-inch cake pan. Freeze. Soften 1/2 gallon ice cream. Spread over frozen crust. Return to freezer. In saucepan, melt semi-sweet chocolate; add powdered sugar, butter and evaporated milk. Bring to rolling boil; cool. Spread over ice cream. Return to freezer. Keep frozen until just before serving.
