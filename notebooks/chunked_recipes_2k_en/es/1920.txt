In large bowl, mix butter, sugar, egg yolk, vanilla, flour and 1/2 cup nuts. Blend well. Spread in ungreased jelly roll pan 15 x 10-inch. Beat egg whites until frothy. Spread over mix. Sprinkle with colored sugar and 1/2 cup nuts. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 25 to 30 minutes until golden brown. Cool and cut into squares or bars.
