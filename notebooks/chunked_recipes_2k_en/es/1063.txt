Combine flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, salt, orange rind, cheese and blueberries in a large bowl. Add orange juice, shortening and egg. Beat until smooth. Pour into a well-greased 12 x 7 1/2 x 2-inch pan. Bake in a preheated moderate oven (350\u00b0) for about 45 minutes or until bread feels firm to the touch.
