Drain tamales; reserve sauce. Remove papers and slice tamales crosswise; set aside. Combine tamale sauce, flour, chili powder and garlic salt. Add eggs, corn, olives and tamales. Turn into 10 x 9 x 2-inch dish. Bake in a 350\u00b0 oven 40 minutes or until set. Sprinkle cheese on top and bake 3 minutes or until cheese melts.
