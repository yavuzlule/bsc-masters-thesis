Preheat oven to 350 degrees. In heavy saucepan, over low heat, melt caramels and margarine. Remove from heat, add milk. Mix well. Follow package directions for cake mix; pour 2 cups batter into a 9 x 13 inch pan that has been sprayed with Pam. Bake 15 minutes. Spread caramel mixture over cake; spread remaining batter over mixture. Top with pecans. Return to oven and bake 30 to 35 minutes.
