Roll the Wonder Bread with a rolling pin until thin, then cut crust off of bread. Spread Cheese Whiz on bread, then cut in 3 strips lengthwise. Roll one water chestnut (possibly 1/2 depending on size) in strip of bread. Cut bacon in half, then roll bacon strips around bread. Stick a toothpick in to hold together. Put cooling rack on top of cookie sheet, place bacon bombs on cooling rack. Preheat Oven to 375. Cook Bacon Bombs for approx 20 min. or until bacon looks crispy.
