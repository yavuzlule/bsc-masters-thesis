Take skin off chicken. Slice cream cheese and Swiss cheese into 1-inch slices. Slice butter into pats. Place 1 slice cream cheese. Swiss cheese and butter in middle of chicken breast. Roll up over cheeses and wrap a slice of bacon around outside. Place in baking dish. Any extra cheese and butter can lay on top. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 45 minutes to 1 hour until bacon is brown.
