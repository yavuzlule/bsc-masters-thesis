Dissolve yeast in warm water; sift flour, soda and sugar together. Add shortening, mixing well. Add buttermilk and dissolved yeast mixture; stir well. Refrigerate before using (overnight is best). Roll and cut into biscuits. Let rise 1 or 2 hours. Bake at 350\u00b0 until lightly browned. , (Will also keep in refrigerator at least a week. )
