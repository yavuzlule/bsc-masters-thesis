Peel and slice eggplant approximately 1/2 to 3/4 inch thick. Salt and pepper and let stand a few minutes. Brown in Wesson oil and drain. In casserole dish, place 1 layer of eggplant, then onion, then pepper, cheese and tomatoes. Repeat if using a deep dish. Top with bread crumbs. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 1 hour.
