Place popcorn in large paper bag. Mix the next 4 ingredients in a 2-quart bowl. Place in microwave for 1 1/2 minutes. Take out and stir. Pour in baking soda. Put back in microwave for 2 minutes on High. Take out and pour over popcorn in bag. Shake it then turn upside-down and put bag in microwave for 1 1/2 minutes. Take it out, shake and put back in microwave again for 1 1/2 minutes. Put in bowl or cookie sheet to cool.
