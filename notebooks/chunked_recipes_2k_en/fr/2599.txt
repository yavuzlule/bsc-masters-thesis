Boil the potatoes in skins about 1/2 hour. Cool in refrigerator, then peel and grate. Mix together potatoes. Cheddar cheese, dry onion, soup, 1/2 cup melted butter and sour cream. Press into greased 13 x 9-inch pan. Before baking, sprinkle cracker (or bread) crumbs and pour 1/4 cup melted butter over top. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 30 minutes.
