Preheat oven to 425\u00b0. Combine oats, flour, apple, brown sugar, baking powder, salt and 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon. Add milk, egg and oil, mixing just until dry ingredients are moistened. Fill greased muffin cups 2/3 full. Mix granulated sugar and 1/4 teaspoon cinnamon; sprinkle scant 1/4 teaspoon over each muffin. Bake 15 to 18 minutes or until golden brown.
