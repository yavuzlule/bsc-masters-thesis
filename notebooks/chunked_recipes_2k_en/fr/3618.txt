Put yeast, warm water and sugar in a small bowl. In a large bowl, add the flour, salt/yeast mixture and warm water. Mix together, then on a floured surface, knead for a few minutes. Put dough into a well-greased bowl. Cover with wax paper. Let rise for 1 hour, then spread dough onto a well-greased pizza pan or cookie sheet. Let rise another 1/2 hour. Bake at 350\u00b0 or until crust is brown on bottom.
