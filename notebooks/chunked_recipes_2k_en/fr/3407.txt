Steam carrots until barely tender, about 10 minutes. Remove from heat and set aside. Mix honey and lemon juice together. Heat vegetable oil and butter together in a large skillet over medium heat. Saute carrots 3 minutes, stirring constantly. Add brown sugar and cook 3 more minutes. Add honey and lemon juice and cook 3 minutes more, until carrots are glazed. Sprinkle with parsley just before serving. Makes 6 servings.
