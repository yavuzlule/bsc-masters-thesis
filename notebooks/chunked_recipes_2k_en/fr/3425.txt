Heat vegetable oil in a heavy skillet until hot. Stir in 1/4 cup flour. Stir constantly until mixture is about the color of a copper penny, about 5 minutes. Reduce heat. Add pepper and onion; cook until tender (about 10 minutes), stirring constantly. Add chicken, tomatoes. Worcestershire sauce, parsley, garlic, soy sauce, sugar, pepper, salt and hot sauce. Cover and simmer about 20 minutes. Serve over rice.
