Mix cream cheese, 1 tablespoon half and half and sugar with wire whisk until smooth. Gently stir in whipped topping; spread on bottom of crust. Pour 2 cups half and half into mixing bowl. Add pudding mix; beat with wire whisk until well blended, 1 to 2 minutes. Let stand 3 minutes. Stir in pumpkin and spices; mix well. Spread over cream cheese layer. Refrigerate at least 2 hours. Garnish with additional whipped topping and nuts, as desired. Makes 8 servings.
