Beat egg slightly and mix with milk, butter, bread cubes, salt and salmon, blending well. Pack firmly in greased 9-inch pie plate. Cover with wax paper. Microwave on High for 9 to 11 minutes, rotating dish 1/2 turn after 5 minutes. Let stand 10 minutes before slicing. Makes 6 to 8 servings. Serve with Dill Butter.
