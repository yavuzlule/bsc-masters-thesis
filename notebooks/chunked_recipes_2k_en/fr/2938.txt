Mix avocados with lemon juice to a spreading consistency. In a separate bowl, mix sour cream, mayonnaise and taco mix. Set aside. Spread all of the avocado mix on the bottom of a 13 x 9-inch pan or large plate. Spread 1 layer of refried beans on top of avocados. Spread 1 layer of sour cream mix. Spread the rest of the beans. Spread the rest of the sour cream mix. Now layer the rest of the ingredients. Serve with tortilla chips.
