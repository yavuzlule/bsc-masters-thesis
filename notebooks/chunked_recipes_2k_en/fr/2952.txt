Cut four 24 x 18-inch pieces of heavy-duty tinfoil. Place a chicken quarter in center of each; sprinkle with garlic salt and paprika. Top evenly with onion and next 4 ingredients. Spoon soup evenly over each portion. Seal bundles and place on a 15 x 10 x 1-inch jellyroll pan. Bake at 400\u00b0 for 1 hour and 15 minutes or until done.
