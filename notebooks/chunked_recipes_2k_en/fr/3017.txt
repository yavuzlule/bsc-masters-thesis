Beat butter for 30 seconds; add granulated sugar and beat until fluffy. Add vanilla and 2 teaspoons water, beat well. Stir in flour and pecans. Shape into 1-inch balls or 1 1/2 x 1/2-inch fingers. Place on ungreased cookie sheet. Bake in 325\u00b0 oven for 20 minutes. Cool completely. Gently shake a few cookies at a time in a bag with powdered sugar. Yields 36.
