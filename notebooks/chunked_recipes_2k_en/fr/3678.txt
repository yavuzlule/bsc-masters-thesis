Chitterlings can be purchased frozen and ready to go. The only problem is the cleaning. You must open each piece and scrape the fat. Cut into 6-inch lengths and rinse in fresh water several times. Soak overnight in the refrigerator in salt water. Rinse and place in a heavy kettle with a tight-fitting lid. Add other ingredients except green pepper. Simmer for 2 to 3 hours until tender. Add green pepper and cook another 15 minutes.
