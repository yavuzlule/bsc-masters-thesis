Mix warm water, 1 tablespoon sugar, ginger and yeast; let stand 15 minutes. Add evaporated milk, 2 tablespoons sugar, oil and salt. Next, beat flour in one cup at a time. When too stiff to stir, work all of the flour in with hands. Lay on countertop and divide the dough in 2 pieces. Shape and put into 2 (1 pound) coffee cans that have been greased well. Let rise until 1 1/2 to 2-inches above top of cans. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 45 minutes. Remove from cans and cool.
