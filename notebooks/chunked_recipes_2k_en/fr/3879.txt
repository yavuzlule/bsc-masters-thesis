Brown ground beef and onion in large skillet; drain grease. Add tomato sauce, water, spices and macaroni. Mix thoroughly. In greased 8 x 10-inch casserole dish, layer one-half of beef mixture and top with half of cheese. Layer remaining beef mixture on top of cheese; top with remaining cheese and pepperoni slices. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 25 to 30 minutes.
