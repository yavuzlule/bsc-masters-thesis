In large bowl, sift together the first 6 ingredients. Cut in margarine with fork. The mixture should resemble coarse crumbs. Stir in granola cereal. In small mixing bowl, beat eggs. Stir in applesauce. Fold egg mixture into dry ingredients; mix until just moist. Pour into greased 8 x 4-inch loaf pan. Bake in a 350\u00b0 oven for 50 to 60 minutes.
