Combine squash, onion and water in a medium saucepan. Bring to a boil, cover, reduce heat and simmer for 10 minutes or until tender. Drain well; mash. Combine squash mixture with remaining ingredients, stirring well. Spoon into a greased 1 1/2-quart casserole dish. Bake at 350\u00b0 for about 25 minutes or until browned.
