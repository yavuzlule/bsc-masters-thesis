Soak cucumbers in ice water 3 or 4 hours. Drain. Slice or cut into strips. Place in sterilized jars, along with onion and a generous amount of dill. Combine sugar, water, vinegar and salt and bring to a boil. Pour over pickles and seal. Store in 70\u00b0 to 75\u00b0 temperature until cucumbers look transparent, about 3 weeks. Makes 3 pints.
