Trim ends of cucumbers and scrape with fork to make a decorative pattern. Quarter the cucumbers lengthwise and remove seeds. Cut them crosswise into thin slices. Put in bowl and add chili. In small saucepan, combine sugar and vinegar; bring to boil. Cook, stirring, until the sugar has dissolved. Cool to room temperature and pour sauce over the cucumber mixture. Chill and sprinkle with cilantro. Serves 4.
