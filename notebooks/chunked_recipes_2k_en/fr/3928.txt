In a large saucepan, heat oil and brown the beef. Add onion soup mix and paprika, blended with tomatoes. Simmer, covered, stirring occasionally, for 1 1/2 hours or until beef is tender. Stir in flour and salt, blended with water. Bring to a boil, then simmer, stirring constantly, until sauce is thickened, about 5 minutes. Cook noodles according to package directions. Garnish with parsley. Serves 8.
