In large saucepan, melt the butter over moderate heat. Add onions and saute 5 minutes. Stir in wild rice, coating with butter. Pour in stock; bring to boil and stir. Lower heat; cover tightly and simmer, stirring occasionally for 1 hour. Add the water; stir in white rice. Cover and steam 20 minutes longer or until both rices are tender. Stir in the pecans and basil. Serves 4, easy to double.
