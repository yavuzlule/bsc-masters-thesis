In mixing bowl, beat egg. Gradually add sugar and beat until very light. Stir in sour cream. Combine flour, baking soda, baking powder, nutmeg and salt. Stir in the raisins. Add to egg mixture, stirring until smooth. Chill dough 1 hour or more. Roll out on lightly floured board to 1/2-inch thickness. Cut with a round cutter 1 to 1 1/2-inches in diameter. Make an \x\ slash on top of each with a sharp knife. Fry in hot oil at 375\u00b0 for 2 to 3 minutes or until golden and cooked through the middle. Mix sugar and cinnamon. Dust the hot cooked puffs with mixture or dust with plain powdered sugar.
