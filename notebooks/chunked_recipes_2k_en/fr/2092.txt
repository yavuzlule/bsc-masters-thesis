Brown ground beef and drain. Stir in Sloppy Joe mix, tomato paste and water; bring to a boil. Simmer 10 minutes; stir occasionally. Cook macaroni until done; drain. Mix all together and add corn and 1 cup cheese. Sprinkle the other cup of cheese on top. Bake in 9 x 13-inch baking dish at 325\u00b0, uncovered, for 30 to 45 minutes.
