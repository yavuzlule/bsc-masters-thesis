Cover cabbage leaves with boiling water. Let stand until limp, about 10 minutes. Remove and drain. Mix ground beef, rice, onion, salt, pepper, garlic salt and 1/2 of the tomato sauce. Place about 1/3 cup of meat mixture at stem end of each leaf. Roll leaf around mixture, tucking in sides. Place rolls, seam side down, in 8 x 8 x 2-inch baking dish. Mix remaining ingredients, pour over cabbage. Cover and bake at 350\u00b0 for 45 minutes or until ground beef is done.
