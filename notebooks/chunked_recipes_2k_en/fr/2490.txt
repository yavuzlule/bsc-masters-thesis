Beat egg whites until fluffy. Blend in sugar and salt. Place Mascarpone in bowl; stir until smooth. Blend into egg mixture. Add Marsala; beat until fluffy. Place enough ladyfingers to cover bottom of dish. Brush with sweetened Espresso (do not soak cookies). Place 1/2 of cheese mixture over ladyfingers. Dust with chocolate powder. Repeat process, placing another layer of ladyfingers on top of first layer. Brush with Espresso. Put in remaining cheese mixture; dust with chocolate powder. Chill before serving.
