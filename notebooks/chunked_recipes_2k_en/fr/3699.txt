In a saucepan, brown hamburger and drain. Stir in gravy. Stir in undrained mushrooms and catsup. Add 1 cup water. Cover; bring to boiling. Simmer, covered, 15 minutes. Stir in wine. Gradually blend 1 cup beef mixture into sour cream dip. Return to saucepan. Heat through. Do not boil. Serve over noodles. Sprinkle with paprika.
