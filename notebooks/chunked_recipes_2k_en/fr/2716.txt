Crush Pecan Sandies and walnuts. Melt butter and chocolate; add sugar. Beat until smooth; add 3 egg yolks and vanilla. Fold in 3 egg whites (beaten stiff). Place half crumb mixture into a 9 x 15-inch pan. Spread chocolate mixture over this. Chill 2 hours. Spread ice cream over top and sprinkle remaining crumbs on top of this. Crush Heath bars. Spread on top. Freeze overnight.
