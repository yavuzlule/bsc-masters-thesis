Cut beef steaks crosswise into 1-inch wide strips. Cut each strip crosswise in half. Cook and stir garlic in oil in large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat for 1 minute. Add beef strips (1/2 at a time). Stir-fry 1 to 1 1/2 minutes. Season with salt and pepper. Remove with slotted spoon. Keep warm. Add zucchini and stir-fry 2 to 3 minutes or until crisp-tender. Return beef to skillet with tomato halves and dressing. Heat through. Serve over hot pasta. Sprinkle with cheese. Yields 4 servings.
