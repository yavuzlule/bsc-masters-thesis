Trim fat and bone from pork chops. Layer first 7 ingredients in large baking dish in order given in recipe. Arrange pork chops on top. Sprinkle with salt and pepper and pour 1 small can tomato sauce over chops. Bake in 450\u00b0 oven 1 hour (covered), then in 375\u00b0 oven 1/2 hour (uncovered). Serves 6 to 8.
