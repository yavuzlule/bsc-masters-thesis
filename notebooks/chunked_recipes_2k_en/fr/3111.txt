Gradually stir milk and egg into dry pudding mix in saucepan, add rice and raisins. Stir over medium heat until mixture comes to boil. Remove from heat, cover for 5 minutes, then cool for 5 minutes, stirring twice. Pour into dessert dishes or serving bowl. Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate about 1 hour. Serve with sprinkled cinnamon and nutmeg. Makes 10 servings. Can be doubled.
