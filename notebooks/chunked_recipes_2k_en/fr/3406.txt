Beat butter, sugar and vanilla until light and fluffy. Add sifted flour and nuts; blend well. Chill dough. Shape dough around Hershey Kisses, being sure all is covered. Put on ungreased cookie sheet. Bake at 375\u00b0 for 10 to 15 minutes until lightly brown. Cool slightly. While warm, roll in powdered sugar twice. Makes 4 to 5 dozen.
