Mix the eggs and milk together in a shallow dish. When blended, add the steaks to the mixture. Cover; place in the fridge for 2 to 3 hours, turning over steaks occasionally. Add salt and pepper to the flour to taste. Remove steaks from the milk and egg mixture. Roll in the flour; cook in oil over medium-high heat. Turn only once when the first side is brown. When done, dry meat on paper towels, while being careful not to knock off any of the breading.
