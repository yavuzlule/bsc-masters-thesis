In a large salad bowl place lettuce (torn into bite-size pieces), cucumber, 1/2 box of croutons, 1/2 of cheese, salt and pepper. Do not add dressing until you are ready to serve salad. Shake dressing well and pour approximately 1/2 to 3/4 of dressing on salad. Add salt and pepper to taste. Toss salad well. Garnish individual servings with tomatoes and onion. Sprinkle cheese on top, if desired.
