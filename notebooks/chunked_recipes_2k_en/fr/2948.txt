Melt butter in a frying pan. Put salt and pepper on the meat. Brown chops and onions together. Grease casserole. Lay potato slices on bottom of casserole. Remove the browned chops from the pan; place on top of the potatoes. Add the soup and milk to the onions; stir until mixture is smooth. Pour over chops. Bake, covered, at 350\u00b0 for 45 minutes. Uncover; bake another 30 minutes.
