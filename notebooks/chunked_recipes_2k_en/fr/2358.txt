Dissolve each jello package in 1 1/4 cups boiling water. Add teaspoon vinegar to each. Pour 1/2 of first package into bowl; let set. Add 3 tablespoons of mayonnaise to second half. When jello in bowl has set, add 1/2 with mayonnaise. Continue on, adding 1/2 after each portion sets. A pretty jello mold for showers and parties.
