Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. Combine in large bowl Ricotta. Parmesan. Romano and 1/2 Mozzarella cheese. Add eggs and parsley. Soak noodles in cold water for 10 minutes. In pan, spread 1/2 cup sauce, then place 3 noodles on top. Spread 1/3 Ricotta filling over noodles. Pour 1 cup sauce over cheese filling. Repeat layers, ending with sauce.
