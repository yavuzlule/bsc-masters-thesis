Mix together flour, sugar, cinnamon, salt, baking powder and soda. Add eggs and oil; mix well. Add zucchini and nuts; stir just enough to mix together. Pour into a greased and floured loaf pan (8 1/2 x 4 1/2 x 3-inch). Bake about 1 to 1 1/2 hours. The top will split open and a tester comes out clean when done. If you prefer cupcakes, make about 2 dozen.
