Peel tomatoes and cut into chunks. Melt butter in frypan; drain mushrooms and add to butter along with sliced celery. Cook until browned. Stir in salt. Worcestershire and tomato chunks. Spoon into a, 1 1/2-quart casserole. Sprinkle with bread crumbs and shredded cheese. Bake in moderate oven (350\u00b0) for about 45 minutes or until bubbling hot and cheese is melted. Makes 4 servings.
