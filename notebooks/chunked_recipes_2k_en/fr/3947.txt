Preheat oven to 425\u00b0. Bake pie shell 8 minutes. Remove from oven. Reduce oven to 350\u00b0. Cut 6 slices cheese into pieces. In large skillet, brown meat; pour off fat. Add tomato sauce, pepper, onion and bouillon. Cook and stir until bouillon dissolves. Remove from heat; stir in eggs, flour and cheese pieces. Turn into prepared shell. Bake 20 to 25 minutes. Arrange remaining cheese slices on top. Return to oven for 3 minutes. Garnish with tomato and lettuce, if desired.
