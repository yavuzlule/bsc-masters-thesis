Grease cake pan. Combine melted oleo with brown sugar, nuts and dry butterscotch pudding. Scatter half of mixture over bottom of pan. Place frozen rolls 1-inch apart in pan. Top with remaining mixture. Place in unheated oven overnight. Next morning, turn on oven to 350\u00b0 for 20 minutes. , (Dont preheat oven. ). When done, turn upside down on cookie sheet while still hot.
