Heat oven to 375\u00b0. Line 12 muffin cups with paper liners. Stir together flour, baking powder and salt; set aside. Beat butter and 1/2 cup of sugar with an electric mixer until light. Add eggs, one at a time, mixing well after each addition. Stop mixer and add preserves, milk and vanilla. Mix in on low speed. Mix in dry ingredients with a spoon; fold in strawberries. Divide batter among muffin cups. Mix remaining 2 tablespoons of sugar with cinnamon. Sprinkle over muffins. Bake until set in center, 25 to 30 minutes. Cool in pan for 20 minutes before removing muffins from the cups.
