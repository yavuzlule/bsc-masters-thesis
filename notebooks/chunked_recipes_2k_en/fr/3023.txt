Cake:. Beat butter or margarine until creamy in a large bowl with electric mixer at medium-high speed. Add eggs, one at a time, beating well after each addition. Add coconut, sugar, flour, cocoa, vanilla and walnuts. Dont beat (heavy batter). Spread batter in greased (9 x 13 x 2-inch) pan. Bake in preheated 350\u00b0 oven for 45 minutes. As soon as cake is taken from oven, spread Marshmallow Creme over hot cake. Cool for 20 minutes.
