Melt butter and stir in bread crumbs. Set aside. Combine sugar and cinnamon. Heat oven to 350\u00b0. Grease baking dish, cover bottom with part of apples. Sprinkle sugar mixture, then walnuts and then bread crumbs. Repeat layers, ending with crumbs on top. Cover and bake 1 hour. Remove cover last 15 minutes of baking.
