Cream butter, shortening and sugar until very light and fluffy. Add flavorings. Add eggs one at a time and beat well. Add alternately the measured flour and baking powder with milk, beginning with flour mixture and ending with flour mixture. Bake in a greased tube pan in a preheated 325\u00b0 oven for 1 1/2 hours or until cake tests done. For a sheet cake, bake in a greased 11 x 15-inch pan at 350\u00b0 for 25 minutes or until cake tests done.
