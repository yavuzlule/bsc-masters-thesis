Pour boiling water into blender. Add gelatin and blend at low speed until completely dissolved, about 3 seconds. Add ice cubes and stir until ice is partially melted. Add strawberries, reserving 6 halves for garnish. If desired, add the whipped topping. Blend at high speed 30 seconds or until smooth. Pour into individual glasses. Chill until set, at least 30 minutes. Garnish with strawberries.
