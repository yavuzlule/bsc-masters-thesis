Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. Sift together flour, baking powder and salt. Set aside. Cream butter and sugar. Add eggs to creamed mixture. Combine milk, vanilla and oil in a measuring cup. Alternate flour mixture and milk mixture until batter is blended. Process the fruit/nut mixture until small pieces. Fold into batter. Bake in two 9-inch cake pans 35 to 40 minutes or Bundt pan 50 minutes.
