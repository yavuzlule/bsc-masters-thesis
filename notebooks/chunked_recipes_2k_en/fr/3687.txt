Stew and bone chicken (save broth from chicken). Mix melted margarine with cornbread mix. Put a little less than half of the mix in bottom of casserole. Put half of chicken on top. Dilute celery soup with a can of broth and pour over chicken. Repeat layers, using chicken soup diluted with 1 can chicken broth. Sprinkle top with crumb dressing mix. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 30 to 40 minutes.
