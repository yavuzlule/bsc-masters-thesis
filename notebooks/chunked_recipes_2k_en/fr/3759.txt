Beat cream cheese with sugar and 2 tablespoons milk in bowl until smooth. Spread evenly in bottom of pie crust. Arrange strawberries on cream cheese mixture. Prepare whipped topping as directed on package. Prepare pie filling mix with 1 cup of milk as directed on package for pie. Fold in 1 cup whipped topping. Spoon into pie crust and chill until set, about 2 hours. Garnish with remaining whipped topping and additional strawberries.
