Cut white meat chicken up into 1-inch pieces. Pit the kalamata olives and cut each olive into 3 or 4 pieces. Chop or slice garlic. Cut the basil leaves into strips. Begin heating a large pot of salted water to boiling. When water begins to boil, add pasta. Dont overcook!. When done, drain pasta and put in a large pasta bowl. Drizzle liberally with olive oil, then salt and pepper to taste (I like plenty of pepper here) and stir.
