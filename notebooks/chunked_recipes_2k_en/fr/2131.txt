Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. Spray baking dish with nonstick cooking spray. Lay the slices of bread in baking dish in two rows, overlapping them like shingles. In a mixing bowl, beat together the egg, egg whites, milk, sugar, brown sugar and vanilla. Pour the egg mixture over the bread. In a small bowl, stir together the remaining ingredients. Sprinkle mixture over the bread. Bake 30 to 35 minutes until it has browned on top and is firm to touch. Makes 9 servings.
