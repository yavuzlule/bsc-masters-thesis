Heat leeks and onion in butter in large skillet until transparent; do not brown. Add potatoes, stock and salt; simmer until potatoes are tender, about 30 minutes. Pour into blender; puree. Stir in cream and Tabasco. Strain through sieve (this is optional; I do not strain); chill. Just before serving, stir in yogurt; re-adjust seasoning. Serve very cold; garnish with chives. Serves 6.
