Cook the potatoes in their jackets; cool in refrigerator a day ahead of time. Peel and grate the potatoes. Melt the butter or margarine. Add the salt and half and half. Grate the Cheddar cheese; add a small amount of Parmesan cheese to the Cheddar cheese. Layer potatoes, melted mixture and cheese in a baking dish; top with crumbs. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 1 hour.
