Blend the yeast, 1 tablespoon sugar and lukewarm water together in a small bowl; set aside. Combine the sifted flour, salt, butter and shortening in a large mixing bowl. Cut butter and shortening into the flour and salt mixture with a pastry blender. Beat the egg yolks slightly with a fork; add to flour mixture, along with yeast mixture. Mix well. Beat the egg whites until foamy. Begin adding 1/2 cup sugar very gradually; beat until mixture holds a stiff peak.
