Soften and mix together first 3 ingredients. Spread mixture on one side of bread slices; lay in 9 x 13-inch baking dish. Beat eggs with milk. Pour over. Can add cubed ham or sausage. Bake at 325\u00b0 for 1 hour and 15 minutes or until set. Bake, covered, 45 minutes and uncovered for remaining time. Can be made night before and refrigerated. Just bake a little longer. Can serve with mushroom soup 1/2 diluted.
