Beat the eggs until well mixed. Add butter, salt, sugar and vanilla. Mix in flour, starting with 1 cup, until you form a soft dough. Roll dough out real thin. Cut in strips of 1 x 4-inches. Make a slit in center. Pull one end through slit. Fry in hot fat in frying pan until slightly tan in color. Drain on absorbent paper and sprinkle with confectioners sugar.
