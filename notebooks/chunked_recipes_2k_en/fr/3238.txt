Mix flour, cornmeal, baking powder, salt and 3/4 cup cheese. Make a well in the center. Mix beaten egg, margarine, honey and milk. Pour into well in center of mix. Stir just to moisten. Grease twelve 2 1/2-inch muffin tins. Place 2 or 3 strips of corn husks in each tin. Fill with batter and sprinkle with remaining cheese. Bake at 375\u00b0, for 20 minutes. These will keep at room temperature for 12 hours.
