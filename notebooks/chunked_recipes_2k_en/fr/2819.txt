Place pork roast in a Dutch oven. Salt and pepper to taste and cover with water. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat, then turn heat down to medium. Cook thoroughly, 1 to 1 1/2 hours. Remove from heat and slice. Place slices in an ovenproof casserole dish and sprinkle with a little more pepper. Place in a 325\u00b0 oven for 15 to 20 minutes. While roast is in oven, bring 1 1/2 cups of broth to a boil. Dissolve 2 tablespoons of flour in 1/4 cup water. Stir into boiling broth. Keep stirring to keep lumps from forming. Add mustard and cook until smooth. Gravy may be poured over roast, or served separately.
