In a large skillet, fry bacon until crisp; remove and set aside. Drain all but 2 to 3 tablespoons of drippings. Cook onion until tender. Stir in flour; blend well. Add vinegar and water. Cook and stir until bubbly and slightly thick. Add sugar and stir until it dissolves. Crumble bacon; gently stir in bacon and potatoes. Heat through, stirring lightly to coat potato slices. Serve warm. Yield: 6 to 8 servings.
