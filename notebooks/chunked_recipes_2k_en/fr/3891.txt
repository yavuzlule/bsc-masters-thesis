Set oven at moderate 350\u00b0. Blend butter, 1/2 cup sugar and egg yolks. Work in flour. Lightly flour hands and pat into well-greased 7 1/2 x 11 1/2-inch baking dish. Bake 15 minutes. Remove from oven and spread evenly with preserves. Beat egg whites until foamy; gradually beat in 1/3 cup sugar. Continue beating until peaks form, then fold in nuts. Spread carefully over preserves. Return to oven and bake 25 minutes. Cool and cut into bars. Makes about 16.
