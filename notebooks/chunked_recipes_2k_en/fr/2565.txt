Line a 9x13 dish or pan with the crescent dough, sealing the edges of the crescents together. Push dough about 1/2 inch up the sides of the pan. In a mixing bowl, combine the onion, soup, 1 cup Cheddar, eggs, salt and lemon pepper seasoning. Beat well. Pour into baking pan and sprinkle remaining cheese over the top. Bake in a 375 degree oven for about 30 minutes. Remove from oven and allow to cool slightly before cutting into small squares.
