Prepare tortellini according to pkg directions. Combine vegetable stock, garlic, artichoke hearts, mushrooms, pepper and parsley in a medium heavy saucepan. Bring to a boil, reduce to simmer and cook about 5 minutes. Remove from heat and stir in remaining ingredients. Toss with the cooked pasta. Stir in a few copped sun-dried tomatoes if desired. Serve hot as a main dish or side dish. Serve cold as a main dish or light salad.
