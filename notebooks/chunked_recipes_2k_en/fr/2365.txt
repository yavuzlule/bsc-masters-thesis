Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. Melt butter and chocolate in saucepan over low heat. Remove from heat. Add sugar. Add 1 unbeaten egg; mix with a fork. Add other egg; mix again. Add vanilla and chopped nuts. Sift together the flour, baking powder and salt; add to mixture. Mix thoroughly. Pour into an 8-inch square pan. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes.
