Brown ground beef; drain well. Use 2 to 3 tablespoons beef fat to saute chopped onion, celery and green pepper. Add tomatoes; mash with potato masher. Add hamburger, macaroni and as much tomato sauce as needed. Also, add desired amount of salt, pepper and chili powder (and a few dashes of Tabasco sauce, if desired). Simmer at least 30 minutes. , (You may need to add additional tomato sauce and/or water. ). No beans!. Enjoy!
