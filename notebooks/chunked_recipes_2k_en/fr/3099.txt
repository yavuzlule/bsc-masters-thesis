Preheat oven to 375\u00b0. Prepare Rice-A-Roni according to package. Pound chicken breast halves into 1/4-inch thickness. Put equal amounts of Rice-A-Roni on each breast half. Roll chicken up to enclose filling. Secure with a wooden pick. Dip chicken into milk; roll in bread crumbs. Put in a 13 x 9-inch baking dish.
