Saute onion, green pepper, garlic and olives in 1 tablespoon oil in skillet over medium heat. Combine eggs, turmeric, cumin, salt and pepper in bowl; beat until smooth. Add to skillet. Cook until eggs are set, stirring constantly. Heat tortillas in 2 cups hot oil in skillet for 5 seconds on each side or until softened. Spoon egg mixture onto tortillas; fold to enclose filling. Place on ovenproof plate. Top with taco sauce and cheese. Broil until cheese melts; serve immediately. Yield:, 2 servings.
