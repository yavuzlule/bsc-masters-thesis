Sift 1 1/2 cups flour, baking powder and salt into bowl. Cream 1/2 cup margarine and 1 cup sugar in mixer bowl until smooth. Add eggs 1 at a time, mixing well after each addition. Add vanilla and sour cream; mix well. Add flour mixture alternately with milk, mixing well after each addition and beginning and ending with flour mixture. Spoon half the batter into greased 9-inch baking pan. Top with blueberries, then remaining batter. Combine remaining 1/3 cup sugar, 1/4 cup flour and cinnamon in small bowl; mix well. Cut in butter until crumbly. Sprinkle over batter. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 30 to 35 minutes or until golden brown. Cool on wire rack for 5 minutes. Serve warm. Yield:, 9 servings.
