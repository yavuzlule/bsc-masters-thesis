At medium speed beat sugar and butter. Add 1 c. flour and then the rest of the ingredients. Add the other 1 1/2 c. flour. Pat dough into 2 (12x3) loaves. Bake on cookie sheet for 25-30 minutes at 350\u00b0. Remove and cool for 20 minutes. Remove loaves to a cutting board and cut slices about 1/2-inch wide into cookies. Place flat on a cookie sheet and bake again at 350\u00b0 for 20 minutes. Cool completely. Melt chocolate in a double boiler and brush each cookie with the chocolate and sprinkle with the chopped almonds.
