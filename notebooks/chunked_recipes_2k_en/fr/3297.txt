In large bowl cream butter and sugar until light and fluffy. Add eggs one at a time and mix well after each egg. Add flour, 7-Up, flavor and juice. Mix until flour is blended in, then blend until smooth about 3 minutes at high speed. Pour batter into Bundt cake pan. Bake in preheated oven at 350\u00b0 for 1 hour 10 minutes. Remove from oven. Cool 10 minutes in pan and remove to cake plate.
