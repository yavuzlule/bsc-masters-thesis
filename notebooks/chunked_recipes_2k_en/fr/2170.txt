Mix first 3 ingredients together and roll out to fit a cookie sheet. Peel and slice apples; crush cornflakes and sprinkle on pastry. Place apples in row until sheet is filled, then make a mixture of 3/4 cup sugar and 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon and sprinkle on apples. Dot with butter. Bake at 400\u00b0 for approximately 15 minutes. Beat egg, milk, vanilla and 2 tablespoons sugar; pour over apples. Bake again until custard sets, approximately 5 minutes.
