Combine eggs, oil, water and pumpkin puree (use straight pumpkin, not pumpkin pie mix). Sift dry ingredients together and gradually beat into first mixture. Bake in greased and floured 9 x 4 x 3-inch loaf pan for 1 hour at 350\u00b0. Or bake in muffin pans 25 minutes. Makes about 20 muffins. Chopped walnuts may be added to the mixture.
