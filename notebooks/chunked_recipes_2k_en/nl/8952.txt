Microwave marshmallows and 2 tablespoons milk. Stir. Refrigerate 15 minutes to cool. Stir in 1 cup of whipped topping. Pour 2 cups milk in bowl. Add pudding mixes. Beat with whisk 2 minutes. Let stand 1 minute or until thickened. Stir in remaining 1 1/2 cups of whipped topping. Spoon into crust. Arrange cookies over pudding mixture. Spread marshmallow mixture over cookies. Refrigerate 4 hours.
