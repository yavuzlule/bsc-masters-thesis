Wash zucchini and cut into thin slices. Peel and quarter onions and cut in thin slices. Cover vegetables with water and add salt. Let stand 2 hours, then drain thoroughly. Bring remaining ingredients to a boil and pour over vegetables. Let stand 2 hours, then bring to a boil and boil 5 minutes. Pack in hot sterilized jars and seal. Yields 3 pints.
