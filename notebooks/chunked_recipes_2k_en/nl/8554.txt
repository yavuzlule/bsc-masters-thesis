Place rice, salt and pepper in small heavy pan with tight fitting lid. Add 2 cups water; bring to a boil. Cover, then simmer for 20 minutes without lifting lid. Put cooked rice in mixing bowl; add oil, vinegar and mustard. Toss with a fork to mix well. Gently fold in cooked vegetables. You will need 2 cups total so use your choice of green peas, carrots, corn and green beans. Add the diced green pepper and cucumber. Lightly oil a pretty mold; do not use olive oil for this. Pack rice mixture into mold and refrigerate until thoroughly chilled. Unmold just before serving. Yield:, 8 servings. Carbohydrate 20 g, protein 1 g, fat 7 g, calories 149.
