Cream shortening, butter and sugar. Add eggs, one at a time beating after each addition. Sift flour and baking powder. Add it alternately with milk, a teaspoon of milk at a time. Stir in vanilla, pineapple and juice and blend well. Pour into a well-greased and floured tube pan (10-inch) and place in cold oven and bake at 325\u00b0 for 1 1/2 hours or until top springs back when lightly touched.
