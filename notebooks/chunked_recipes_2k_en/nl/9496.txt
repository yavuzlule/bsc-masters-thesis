Slice apples thin and place in a buttered 8-inch square dish. Mix together cinnamon and nutmeg. Sprinkle on top of apples. Cut each slice of bread into 4 strips. Arrange the 16 bread strips on top of the apples and spices. Combine the other ingredients, mixing well. Pour over bread. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 45 to 50 minutes. Quick and easy dessert.
