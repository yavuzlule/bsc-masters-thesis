Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. In large bowl, beat banana, both sugars, butter, cinnamon and vanilla. Beat in eggs. Sift flour and baking powder; add to banana mixture. Add coconut and stir until well blended. Spread into greased 13 x 9 x 2-inch baking pan. Sprinkle with nuts. Bake for 25 minutes. Cool and cut into bars.
