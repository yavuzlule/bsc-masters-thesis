Cook unpeeled potatoes. In frypan, fry bacon until crisp. Drain on paper towels, reserving 3 tablespoons drippings. Crumble bacon. To reserve drippings, add onion. Cook until tender. Add sugar, flour, bouillon and spices. Stir until smooth. Stir in vinegar and water; bring to boil. Peel warm potatoes. Slice and add to sauce mixture with bacon. Toss lightly to coat. Serve warm. Makes 4 to 5 servings.
