Dissolve gelatin in boiling water. Add the pear juice, salt and vinegar. Pour 1/2 inch of gelatin into a mold; chill until firm. Chill remaining gelatin until syrupy. Place gelatin bowl into a bowl of crushed ice and whip until thick. Beat cream cheese with ginger; fold in the whipped gelatin and fold in the pears. Pour over the firm first layer of gelatin. Chill until firmly set. Serves 8 to 10.
