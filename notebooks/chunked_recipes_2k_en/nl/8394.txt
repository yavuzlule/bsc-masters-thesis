Mix together meat, eggs, dressing, onion and seasonings. Add just enough bread crumbs to bind mixture. Cut a sheet of waxed paper into a rectangle the size of a cookie sheet (10 x 15). Spread and pat meat mixture over waxed paper. Arrange ham slices in a layer over meat mixture. Arrange cheese slices over ham. Roll up like a jelly roll. Place in baking dish and bake uncovered at 350 for about 1 1/2 hours. Pour tomato sauce over top and bake 20 minutes more.
