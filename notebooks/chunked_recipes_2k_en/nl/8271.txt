Combine 2 cups grated cheese and onion and roll inside tortillas. Place in 9 x 13 pan. In saucepan, blend butter and flour; add chicken broth and stir until thickened. Stir in sour cream, green chilies and 1 cup grated cheese. Pour over enchiladas and sprinkle with remaining cup of cheese. Bake at 425 degrees for 20 minutes.
