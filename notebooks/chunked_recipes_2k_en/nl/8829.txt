Combine all ingredients in a large bowl. Using a large spoon (or fingers), mix well. Roll dough into 3/4 x 1-inch diameter. Place on an ungreased cookie sheet. Bake in a preheated 350\u00b0 oven for 11 to 13 minutes. Cool briefly on a cookie sheet. Remove to wire rack to cool completely. Store in airtight container. Makes about 6 dozen.
