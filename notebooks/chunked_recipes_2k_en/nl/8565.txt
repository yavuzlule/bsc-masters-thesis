Cook the macaroni; drain. Mix seasoning mix with meat, tomato paste, tomato sauce and 1 1/4 cups water. Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. In a 2 1/2-quart casserole, layer 1/2 macaroni, 1/2 cottage cheese and 1/2 the meat sauce; repeat. Top with Cheddar cheese. Bake, uncovered, 40 to 50 minutes or until bubbling. Makes 6 servings.
