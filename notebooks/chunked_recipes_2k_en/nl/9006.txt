Brown ground meat. Cook macaroni according to package directions. Drain macaroni; add ground meat and Ragu sauce. Put 1/2 of mixture in 9 x 13-inch pan. Cover with sour cream. Place Provolone cheese slices next; put remaining macaroni mixture next and top with Mozzarella cheese. Bake in 300\u00b0 oven for 1/2 hour.
