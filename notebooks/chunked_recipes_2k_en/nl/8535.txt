In saucepan, melt butter; blend in flour. Add milk, broth, and 1/2 cup water. Cook and stir until thickened and bubbly. Add rice, chicken, mushrooms, green pepper, pimiento, and salt. Pour into greased 2-quart casserole. Bake in 350\u00b0 oven for 40 minutes or until hot and bubbly. If desired, top with 1/4 cup toasted slivered almonds. Serves 8 to 10.
