Use electric mixer on medium speed; blend in eggs with 1/4 cup brown sugar and 1/4 cup melted butter. Add cinnamon, salt and 2 tablespoons orange juice. Beat until fluffy and moist. If needed, add more orange juice. Pour into 2-quart casserole; cover with pecans. Sprinkle remaining sugar and butter; bake, uncovered, 20 minutes in oven at 375\u00b0.
