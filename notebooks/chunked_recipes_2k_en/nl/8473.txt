Meringue shell:. Beat egg whites with cream of tartar in medium bowl until foamy. Beat in sugar, one tablespoon at a time, until meringue forms, still glossy peaks. Lightly butter a 9 inch pie plate. Spoon meringue over bottom and side, to form a shell. Bake in a slow oven at 275 degrees for 1 hour. Turn oven off and allow meringue to cool in the oven.
