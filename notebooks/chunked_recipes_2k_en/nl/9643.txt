In a skillet, saute mushrooms in butter until tender. Stir in flour. Gradually add the chicken broth. Cook, stirring constantly, until sauce comes to a boil. Remove from heat. Stir in cream, parsley, salt, nutmeg, pepper and wine. Fold in the chicken and spaghetti. Turn into a greased 12 x 9 x 2-inch baking dish; sprinkle with Parmesan cheese. Bake, uncovered, at 350\u00b0 for 30 minutes, or until heated through. Garnish with fresh parsley, if desired. Serves 6 to 8.
