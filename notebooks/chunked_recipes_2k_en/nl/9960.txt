Melt butter, margarine or bacon drippings in large saucepan. Add onion slices and cook until light yellow. Add water, diced potatoes, salt and pepper; bring to a boil. Reduce heat and simmer until potatoes are slightly soft when tested with a fork. Add fish and simmer for 25 minutes. Add milk and heat, stirring occasionally. Serve in hot soup bowls accompanied with large, round crackers.
