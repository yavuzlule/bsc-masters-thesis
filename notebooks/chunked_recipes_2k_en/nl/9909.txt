Mix flour, cornstarch and sugar in a large mixing bowl. Blend in butter until a soft dough forms. If dough is too soft, cover it and chill for 20 to 30 minutes. Shape into 3/4-inch balls. Roll balls in coconut. Place balls 1 1/2-inches apart on ungreased baking sheets. Bake at 300\u00b0 for 20 to 25 minutes until golden brown. Makes 3 dozen.
