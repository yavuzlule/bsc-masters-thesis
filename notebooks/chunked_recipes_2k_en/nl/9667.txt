Combine all ingredients except flour. Mix thoroughly (not with mixer). Add flour gradually. Cover and let rise about 1 hour in a warm place. Punch down; put in greased small loaf pans or casserole dish. Let rise, covered, 30 to 40 minutes. Bake for 40 minutes at 325\u00b0. Recipe can be doubled, but use 9 x 13-inch pan.
