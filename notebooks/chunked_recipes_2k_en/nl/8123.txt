In 2-quart casserole, arrange bacon in a single layer. Cover with paper towel and cook 2 1/2 minutes. Remove towel and bacon, leaving drippings. Add sugar, cornstarch, salt and pepper; blend well. Stir in vinegar and remaining ingredients. Cook, covered, 10 minutes, or until sauce has boiled or thickened slightly, stirring occasionally. Crumble bacon over top. Serves 8 to 10.
