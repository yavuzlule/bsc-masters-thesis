Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. Brown chops in large frying pan; drain well. Place in 9 x 13-inch Pyrex dish. Mix soups and pour over chops. Sprinkle with pepper. Cover with heavy-duty foil and bake for 45 to 50 minutes. Remove foil during last 10 minutes. Serve chops with cooked noodles or rice. Serves 6 to 8. Chops may be browned and topped with sauce prior to baking. Refrigerate until ready to bake. Do not add salt!
