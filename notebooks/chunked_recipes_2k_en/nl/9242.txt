Mix together first 6 ingredients and set aside. Cut broccoli in florets about 2 inches long. Peel stalk and slice in 2 inch lengths less than 1/2 inch thick. Put 2 tablespoons oil in hot skillet or wok over high heat. Add broccoli and stir-fry until broccoli is dark green, not over 2 minutes. Remove and add 2 tablespoons oil to skillet. Add onions and beef; stir-fry 2 minutes. Add broccoli to skillet and stir-fry to warm broccoli. Serve with rice. Makes 2 to 3 servings.
