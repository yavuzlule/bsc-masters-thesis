Sprinkle crushed cookies onto bottom of a 9 x 13-inch pan. Pour 1/2 cup melted margarine over crumbs. Spread softened ice cream over crumbs and freeze. Combine remaining ingredients, except frozen whipped topping; boil for 4 minutes. Cool and pour over top of ice cream. Top with frozen whipped topping. Sprinkle with nuts, if desired, and freeze. Makes 10 to 12 servings.
