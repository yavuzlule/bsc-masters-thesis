Preheat oven to 375\u00b0. Brown meat and onions in a skillet, drain grease, if necessary. Stir in the taco seasoning mix, beans and tomato sauce. Simmer 5 minutes. Unroll dough into 4 long rectangles. Place in ungreased 15 x 10-inch jellyroll pan. Press over bottom and 1 inch up sides to form crust. Bake at 375\u00b0 for 25 to 30 minutes or until golden brown. Sprinkle with cheese. Return to oven until cheese is melted, about 2 to 3 minutes. Cut into squares and top with chopped lettuce, tomatoes, taco sauce, sour cream, etc.
