Lightly spoon flour into measuring cup, level off. Combine flour, brown sugar and butter in large bowl. Blend at low speed until fine crumbs form. Stir in nuts. Press 2 3/4 of mixture firmly in ungreased 14 x 9-inch pan. Add to remaining crumb mixture cinnamon, soda, salt and sour cream. Blend, then add egg and vanilla. Stir in apples and spoon over crumb base.
