Sift into bowl and set aside the flour, baking soda, cinnamon, nutmeg, salt and cloves. Cream shortening, brown sugar and white sugar. Add to creamed ingredients the egg and applesauce. Add sifted flour mixture that was set aside, raisins and nuts. Bake at 400\u00b0 for 8 to 10 minutes on greased cookie sheets. Sprinkle with powdered sugar.
