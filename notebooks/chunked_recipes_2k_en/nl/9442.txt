Preheat oven to 250\u00b0. Combine crackers and nuts in a greased large shallow baking pan. In a saucepan, bring butter, sugar and corn syrup to a boil and cook for 5 minutes. Remove from heat; add vanilla and soda. Pour caramel mixture over crackers and nuts. Stir well. Bake 1 hour, stirring every 15 minutes. Pour onto waxed paper and break apart; allow to cool. Store in airtight container. Yields approximately 9 cups of snack mix.
