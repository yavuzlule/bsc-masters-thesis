Remove casings from sausage. Combine ground beef and sausage in a bowl. Add red onion, garlic, egg and tomato paste. Combine to blend well. Add wine or broth and bread crumbs and mix thoroughly. Shape meat mixture into a loaf. Place in a loaf pan. Sprinkle with rosemary. Bake at 350\u00b0 for about 1 hour or until meat loaf tests done. Garnish with pepper rings.
