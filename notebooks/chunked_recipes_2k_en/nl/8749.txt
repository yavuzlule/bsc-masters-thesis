Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. Combine cream of chicken soup, ham, onions, sour cream, and 8 oz. of cheddar cheese and mix well. (Reserve 8 oz. of cheddar cheese for topping. ). Add Potatoes obrien and stir well. Place in 9x13 inch baking dish. Place reserved cheddar cheese on top. Cover with aluminum foil and bake 45 minutes. Remove from oven and remove aluminum foil. Bake additional 15 minutes.
