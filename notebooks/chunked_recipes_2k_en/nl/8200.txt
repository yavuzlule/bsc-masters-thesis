Heat oven to 425\u00b0. Grease and heat in oven a small No. 5 skillet. Break 1 egg in bowl; beat with fork until frothy. Add corn meal and sweet milk. Bake for 25 minutes, crumble when cool. Brown lightly chopped onions and celery in butter. Add crumbled cornbread, 2 eggs (beaten) and pepper. Moisten with 1 1/4 cups chicken broth. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 30 minutes.
