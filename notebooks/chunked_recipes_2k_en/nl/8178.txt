In a large bowl combine soup, milk and cheese. Add chicken, macaroni and eggs. Stir in 1/4 cup melted butter. Transfer to 13 x 9 x 2-inch baking dish. Cover and refrigerate for 8 hours or overnight. Toss the bread crumbs with remaining butter; sprinkle over casserole. Bake, uncovered, at 350\u00b0 for 60 to 65 minutes or until bubbly and golden brown. Yields 12 servings.
