Foil line cookie sheets. Preheat oven to 325\u00b0. Mix together the granola, condensed milk, ginger and almond extract in a large bowl. Stir the mixture until its well blended. Next, drop the dough by teaspoonfuls onto foil-lined cookie sheet. Place a candied cherry piece on each cookie. Bake at 325\u00b0 for 15 minutes. Yields 6 dozen.
