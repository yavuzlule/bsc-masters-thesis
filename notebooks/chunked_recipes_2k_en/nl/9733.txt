Prepare salad dressing mix with wesson oil, vinegar, and water as directed on envelope. Set aside. Cook pasta as directed on package; drain. Rinse with cold water and drain. Run cold tap water over vegetables in a strainer to thaw completely; drain. Combine pasta, vegetables, chicken and cheese in a large bowl. Toss with salad dressing. Chill. Serve on a bed of lettuce, if desired. Makes 8 cups or 8 servings.
