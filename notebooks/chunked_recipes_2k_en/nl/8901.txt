Prepare cake mix as directed on package. Bake in a 9 x 13-inch pan. Cool for 15 minutes. When cool, make holes through cake. Combine milk, sugar and 1/2 cup coconut in saucepan. Bring to a boil. Reduce heat and simmer for three minutes. Carefully spoon over warm cake allowing liquid to soak down through holes. Let cool. Fold 1/2 cup coconut into Cool Whip and spread over the cake. Sprinkle the rest of the coconut over the cake. Chill overnight. Store leftover cake in the refrigerator.
