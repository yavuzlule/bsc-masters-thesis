Preheat oven to 375\u00b0. Add sugar, oil, eggs, baking powder, soda, cinnamon, salt and mace to shredded carrots; mix. Add flour; combine with mixture. Add pineapple. Turn into a 9-inch greased and floured pan. Bake until cake springs back when lightly touched in center, 30 to 35 minutes. Refrigerate unused portion.
