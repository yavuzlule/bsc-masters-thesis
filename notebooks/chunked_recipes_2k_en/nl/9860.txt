In large nonstick skillet, heat oil over medium heat. Add onions, thyme and 1 tablespoon water. Cover; cook 5 minutes. Uncover; reduce heat to medium-low. Cook until onions are golden, about 15 minutes. Add garlic; saute for 1 minute. Add flour; reduce heat to medium. Cook, stirring, for 2 minutes. Stir in broth, remaining water and nutmeg. Bring to a boil; skim off foam. Cover and simmer for 30 minutes.
