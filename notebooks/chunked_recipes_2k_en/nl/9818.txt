In large bowl, sprinkle gelatin over cold water; let stand 1 minute. Add boiling water and stir until gelatin is dissolved. Add soft drink mix; mix well. Stir in sweetened condensed milk, lime juice and yogurt; mix well. Fold in fruit cocktail. Pour into 8-cup mold. Chill 4 hours or until set. Unmold into lettuce leaves. Garnish as desired. Refrigerate leftovers. Yields 12 servings.
