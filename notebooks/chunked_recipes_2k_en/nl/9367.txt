In a skillet, cook ground beef and onion until meat is browned and onion is tender. Drain off fat. Stir in the undrained tomatoes, beans, rice, water, green pepper, chili powder, salt and garlic salt. Reduce heat. Simmer covered for 20 minutes, stirring occasionally. Top with cheese. Cover and heat about 3 minutes or until cheese melts. Sprinkle corn chips around edge. Serves 6.
