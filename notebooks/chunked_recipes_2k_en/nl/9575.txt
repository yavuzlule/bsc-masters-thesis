In 4-cup glass measure, cook cream cheese and wine in microwave oven on High power for 2 minutes; stir every 30 seconds. Toss Swiss cheese with cornstarch. Drain crab; flake, removing cartilage. Stir crab. Swiss cheese, milk. Worcestershire sauce and parsley into wine mixture; microwave 3 minutes, stirring once. Transfer to fondue pot or chafing dish; keep warm. Dip bread cubes swirling to coat or spoon onto crackers. Makes about 3 cups.
