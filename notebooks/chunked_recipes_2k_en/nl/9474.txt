Preheat oven to 375\u00b0. Measure flour, baking powder, soda and salt into a bowl; stir with a fork to mix. Cream butter until fluffy; beat in egg, milk and lemon juice. Stir in flour mixture; stir in cranberries and walnuts. Drop dough by teaspoons about 1 inch apart onto greased baking sheet. Bake 15 minutes or until firm and golden. Remove from cookie sheets to wire racks; cool. Yields about 8 dozen cookies.
