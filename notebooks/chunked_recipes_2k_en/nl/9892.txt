Bake cake mix as directed in a cookie sheet. Let cool. Mix pudding with milk. Let cool. Next, mix the pineapple, 1 cup sugar, and cornstarch; cook until thick. Let cool. Cream together the cooled pudding, oleo and 1 cup sugar. Beat 20 minutes. Spread on cake. Top with the cooled pineapple and sprinkle with ground nuts. Delicious and freezes well.
