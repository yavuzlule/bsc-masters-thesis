Pound boneless chicken breast until 1/4-inch thick. Sprinkle halves with salt and pepper. Top each piece of chicken with 1 slice ham and 1 slice of cheese. Fold chicken over ham and cheese. Wrap each bundle in 2 strips of bacon and place in shallow baking dish. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 45 minutes or until chicken is cooked and bacon is browned.
