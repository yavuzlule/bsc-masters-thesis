In a shallow bowl, combine the Parmesan cheese, oregano, parsley, garlic salt and pepper. Dip the chicken breasts in melted butter, then coat with Parmesan mixture. Place in a greased 9-inch square baking dish. Drizzle with any remaining butter. Bake, uncovered, at 425\u00b0 for 15 to 20 minutes or until the chicken juices run clear. Makes 4 servings.
