Cream butter, sugar and egg until light and fluffy. Beat in flour, baking powder, orange juice and vanilla until smooth. Chill 2 to 3 hours or until firm enough to roll. Roll out half at a time on floured surface. Cut into your favorite shapes and place on ungreased cookie sheet. Bake at 400\u00b0 for 6 to 10 minutes. Decorate as desired.
