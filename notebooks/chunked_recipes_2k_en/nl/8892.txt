Dissolve Jell-O in water, vinegar and salt. Add mayonnaise and beat until smooth. Pour into refrigerator tray and place in freezing compartment 15 to 20 minutes until mixture is set about 1-inch from edges, but soft in center. Turn into bowl and beat again until fluffy. Fold in spinach, cottage cheese, celery and onion. Turn into mold and chill until firm.
