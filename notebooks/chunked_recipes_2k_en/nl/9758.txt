Use a blender or food processor and finely chop 1/3 cup chocolate chips. Place in a small mixing bowl. Add vanilla wafer crumbs, ground hazelnuts, 2 tablespoons white sugar and melted butter. Mix until well combined. Press into bottom and up the sides of a 9-inch spring-form pan. Bake in a preheated 300\u00b0 oven for 15 minutes. Cool.
