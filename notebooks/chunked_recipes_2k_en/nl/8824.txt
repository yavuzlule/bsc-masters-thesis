Heat oven to 450\u00b0. Mix baking mix, milk and cheese until soft dough forms. Beat vigorously for 30 seconds. Drop dough by spoonfuls onto ungreased cookie sheet. Bake for 8 to 10 minutes, or until golden brown. Mix margarine and garlic powder; brush over warm biscuits before removing from cookie sheet. Makes 10 to 12 biscuits.
