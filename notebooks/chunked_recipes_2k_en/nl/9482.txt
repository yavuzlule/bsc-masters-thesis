Blend cream cheese, sour cream, mayonnaise, lemon, 1/2 cup milk. Worcestershire sauce, mustard, garlic salt and 1/2 cup Cheddar cheese. Add more milk if necessary to make creamy. Add crab and blend. Place in 2-quart oblong baking dish. Sprinkle remaining 1/2 cup Cheddar cheese on top and sprinkle with paprika. Bake in preheated 350\u00b0 oven for 30 minutes, until crusty brown and bubbly on top.
