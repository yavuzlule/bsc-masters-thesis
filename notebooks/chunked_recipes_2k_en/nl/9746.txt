Beat butter, sugar, egg and vanilla until creamy. Stir soda into buttermilk; add to sugar mixture alternating with flour. Stir in rhubarb. Spread in greased and floured 9 x 13-inch pan. In small bowl, combine 1/2 cup sugar, 1 teaspoon cinnamon and 1 tablespoon melted butter. Sprinkle over batter. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 40 minutes.
