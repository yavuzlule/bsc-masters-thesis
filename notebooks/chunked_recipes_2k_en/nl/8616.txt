Cook potatoes in their skins. Cool. Peel and shred coarsely. Over low heat, combine cheese and 1/4 cup butter, stirring occasionally until almost melted. Remove from heat and blend in sour cream, onions, salt and pepper. Fold in potatoes and turn into a greased 2-quart casserole. Dot with 2 tablespoons butter and bake at 350` for 25 minutes or until heated through. Serves 6 to 8.
