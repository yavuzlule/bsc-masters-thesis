Combine shrimp, green onion, cornstarch, 1 teaspoon salt and pepper in a large bowl. Toss to mix. Stir-fry shrimp mixture in hot oil in wok for 3 to 5 minutes or until shrimp turn pink. Remove from wok. Stir rice, eggs and 1 1/2 teaspoons salt together in a bowl. Pour into hot wok. Stir-fry over medium-high heat for 2 minutes or until eggs are cooked. Add shrimp mixture. Stir-fry until mixed.
