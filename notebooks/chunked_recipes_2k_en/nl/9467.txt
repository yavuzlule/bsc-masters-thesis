Combine first 5 ingredients. Stir in pecans. Shred enough zucchini to measure 2 cups. Combine zucchini and remaining ingredients. Add to flour mixture, stirring just until dry ingredients are moistened. Spoon batter into 2 greased and floured 9 x 5 x 3-inch loaf pans. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 1 hour and 5 minutes or until toothpick inserted in center comes out clean. Cool in pans 10 minutes, remove from pans and cool completely on wire racks.
