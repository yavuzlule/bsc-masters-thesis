Preheat oven to 325\u00b0. Cream butter with 1/2 cup sugar and vanilla. Add egg yolks, beating well. Stir in milk. Sift flour, baking powder and salt together. Gradually add to batter. Add extract. Pour into greased and floured 9-inch square pan. Beat egg whites until frothy. Gradually add remaining 3/4 cup sugar until meringue holds its shape. Fold in nuts. Spread over batter; sprinkle with a little granulated sugar and bake 30 minutes. Cool before cutting.
