Mix butter, sugar and vanilla thoroughly. Measure flour by dip-level-pour method or by sifting with salt, then measure. Blend flour and salt with butter mixture. Mix in nuts. Chill at least 30 minutes. Make into 1-inch balls. Place on ungreased baking sheet. Bake at 350\u00b0 on middle shelf of oven, 8 to 12 minutes (check bottoms for browning). Roll in powdered sugar while still warm. Cool. Roll again (roll a third time to make whiter). Yields about 4 dozen.
