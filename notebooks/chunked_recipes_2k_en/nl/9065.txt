Coat roast with the first 2 tablespoons flour. Sprinkle with salt. Brown in hot oil. Add onions and saute 3 minutes. Combine water, salad dressing mix and salt. Pour over meat. Cover; simmer for 2 1/2 hours until meat is almost tender. Add pared carrots and potatoes. Cover and simmer for 1/2 hour until vegetables are tender. Remove meat and vegetables to platter. Combine remaining flour and water; add to broth. Cook and stir until thickened and bubbly. Very good.
