Grease a 9 x 12-inch casserole with butter. Place cubed bread in bottom of casserole. Place layer of bite-sized sausage (cooked and well drained). Place layer of cheese. Mix eggs, 1 1/2 cups milk, salt and mustard. Pour over cheese layer. Mix soup and 1/2 cup milk. Pour over egg layer. Let stand overnight in refrigerator. Bake 30 to 45 minutes, uncovered, in a 350\u00b0 oven.
