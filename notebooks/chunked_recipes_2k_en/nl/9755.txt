Cream butter and sugar together in mixing bowl. Add beaten eggs and pumpkin; mix well. Combine flour with baking soda, cinnamon, ginger, cloves and salt. Add flour mixture to pumpkin mixture. Mix enough to blend ingredients. Add milk and walnuts and mix well until batter is smooth. Bake in buttered loaf pan (9 1/2 x 5-inch) at 350\u00b0 for 45 minutes.
