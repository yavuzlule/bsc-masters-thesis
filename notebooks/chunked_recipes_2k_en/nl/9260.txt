Sift flour, soda, salt and sugar together. Add buttermilk and shortening (scalded). Dissolve yeast in 1/2 cup warm water. Stir into flour mixture. Let stand for 10 minutes. Knead on floured surface; shape into desired rolls. Place on lightly greased baking sheet. Let rise until doubled in bulk. Bake at 400\u00b0 until lightly browned.
