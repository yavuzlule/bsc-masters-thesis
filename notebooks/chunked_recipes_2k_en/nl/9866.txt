Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. In heavy saucepan, combine 1 1/3 cups sugar and cornstarch; add lemon juice. In small bowl, beat egg yolks. Add to lemon mixture. Gradually add water, stirring constantly. Over medium heat, cook and stir until mixture boils and thickens, about 8 to 10 minutes. Remove from heat. Add butter; stir until melted. Pour into prepared pastry shell.
