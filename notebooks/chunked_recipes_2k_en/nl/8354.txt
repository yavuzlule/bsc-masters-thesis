Heat oven to 350\u00b0. Grease 9 x 5 x 3-inch loaf pan. Mix all ingredients, except spinach, until well blended. Beat for 1 minute. Stir in spinach until well blended. Pour into pan. Bake for 55 to 65 minutes or until toothpick inserted in center comes out clean. Cool for 20 minutes; remove from pan. Makes one loaf (24 slices).
