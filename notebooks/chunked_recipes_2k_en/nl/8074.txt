Separate eggs, placing whites in large mixer bowl, yolks in small mixer bowl. Add cream of tartar to whites; beat at high speed until foamy. Gradually add 3/4 cup of the sugar; beat until stiff. To yolks, add flour, 3/4 cup sugar plus 2 tablespoons sugar, salt, water, orange concentrate and extracts. Blend at low speed until moistened; beat 1 minute at medium. Pour over whites. By hand, fold batter gently and thoroughly into whites, just until well blended. Pour into ungreased 10-inch tube pan. Bake at 350\u00b0, for 50 to 55 minutes, until top springs back when touched lightly, invert and cool.
