Steam broccoli until tender. Spread butter on chicken and bake for 35 minutes at 325\u00b0. Mix soup, mayonnaise and cheese. Place broccoli on the bottom of a 2-quart casserole dish or 8 x 8-inch baking pan. Place chicken on top of broccoli and pour soup mixture over both. Sprinkle with bread crumbs and bake for 30 minutes at 350\u00b0.
