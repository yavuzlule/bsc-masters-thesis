Heat oven at 375\u00b0. In large mixing bowl, combine all ingredients except candy kisses. Blend well at low speed. Shape into balls, using a rounded teaspoonful for each. Roll balls in additional sugar. Place on ungreased cookie sheet. Bake at 375\u00b0 for 10 to 12 minutes. Remove from oven. Top each cookie immediately with a candy kiss, pressing down firmly so cookie cracks around edges.
