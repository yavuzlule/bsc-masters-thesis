Cut chicken breasts into strips and saute chicken, onion and green pepper in oil until chicken is fully cooked. Pour in soup and 3/4 cup of water. Simmer for about 5 minutes. Fill soft taco shells with mixture and roll them up. Place enchiladas in a baking dish and cover with shredded cheese. Bake at 350\u00b0 until cheese is fully melted. Serve with garnish (sour cream and salsa).
