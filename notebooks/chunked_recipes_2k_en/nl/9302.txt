Sift together first 5 ingredients. Mix Crisco into dry ingredients. Add milk last. Roll out dough into a large rectangle; spread with melted butter. Sprinkle with sugar and cinnamon. Roll up in the shape of a jelly roll and cut in 1-inch strips. Place cut side down on a greased pan or any type cookie sheet. Bake for 20 minutes at 375\u00b0. When cool, if you prefer, you can make powdered sugar icing. Makes, approximately 18.
