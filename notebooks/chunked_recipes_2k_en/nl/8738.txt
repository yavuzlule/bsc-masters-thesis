Finely chop onions. Melt butter in a thick saucepan. Add onions and brown slowly and well. Dust in flour and pour in stock. Simmer for 1/2 hour; add seasonings and bay leaf. Put French bread at the bottom of a casserole. Pour on the boiling soup. Dust thickly with the grated cheese, then set in 350\u00b0 oven for 10 to 15 minutes to brown. Serve very hot.
