Beat eggs until creamy. Add butter, sugar, yeast mixture and flour. Cover and let raise for about 1 1/2 hours until doubled. Place dough on floured board and make the knots. If sticking, add a little more flour. Put on greased cookie sheet and let them raise again. Bake at 375\u00b0 for 10 minutes. Frost with powdered sugar and lemon flavoring while warm.
