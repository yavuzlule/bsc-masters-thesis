Add 1/2 cup sugar to peaches and set aside. Combine flour, salt and 2 cups sugar and add to milk. In a large saucepan, heat milk on medium. Beat eggs and gradually stir into milk. Cook on medium until mixture coats a spoon. Stir constantly. Remove from heat. Add whipping cream, peaches and flavorings. Chill in refrigerator, then churn in ice cream freezer. Makes 5 quarts.
