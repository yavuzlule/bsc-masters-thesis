Mix 1/3 cup Pet milk with cake mix and butter. Put 1/2 in a pan and bake 5 to 7 minutes at 350\u00b0. Pour the Pet milk that is left over 1/2 pound caramels. Melt and pour on top of cake mixture. Mix chocolate chips and nuts and pour on top of the caramel. Cook 18 to 20 minutes at 350\u00b0 (no longer).
