In a bowl, dissolve gelatin in boiling water. Break up and stir in cranberry sauce. Add pineapple, orange juice and lemon juice. Chill until partially set. Stir in nuts. Pour into an 11 x 7 x 2-inch dish. Chill until firm. Cut into squares. Serve on lettuce leaf and top with a dollop of mayo. Yield: 12 servings.
