Mix crushed pretzels, sugar and soft margarine and press in 9 x 13-inch pan. Bake 8 minutes at 350\u00b0; let cool. Mix softened cream cheese. Cool Whip and sugar. Mix together and pour over crust (be sure to seal edges). Mix strawberry jello with boiling water and frozen strawberries (do not thaw; add frozen). Pour over cheese mixture.
