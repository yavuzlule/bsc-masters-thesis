Melt butter in large skillet; add coconut and chopped pecans. Cook until golden brown, stirring frequently. Set the mixture aside. Combine cream cheese and sweetened condensed milk; beat until smooth. Fold in whipped topping. Layer 1/2 of cream cheese mixture in each shell. Drizzle 1/4 of chocolate fudge topping on each pie. Sprinkle 1/4 of coconut mixture evenly over each pie, repeat layers with remaining ingredients. Cover and freeze until firm.
