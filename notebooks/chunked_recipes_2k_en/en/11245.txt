Slice baguette in 1/4-inch slices. Combine all ingredients, mixing well. Using a flat knife, spread mixture thickly on slice baguette. Arrange on waxed paper covered tray (no spacing necessary; close together is fine). Place in a 400\u00b0 preheated oven for 8 minutes or until lightly browned. Allow to cool on the tray, then transfer to a plate. These are great the day before needed or the day you intent to serve them. If made the day before a double batch may be necessary. Family and friends may want to sample them before you make it to the table! Check after 5 minutes. Cooking times may vary depending upon your oven.
