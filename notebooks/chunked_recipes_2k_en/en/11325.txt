Mix apples and sugar. Let stand 10 minutes. Sift flour, salt, soda and cinnamon together; set aside. Beat the egg; add oil and blend into apple mixture, then add flour all at once. Beat with wooden spoon until blended. Bake in a 13 x 9 x 2-inch well-greased pan at 350\u00b0 for 35 to 40 minutes. Mix confectioners sugar and milk and drizzle over squares.
