In bowl, combine flour, cinnamon, soda, powder and salt. Set aside. In large bowl, place oil, sugar, eggs, vanilla and apples. Stir flour mixture; add walnuts and mix. Divide mixture between two greased 8 x 4-inch bread pans. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 40 to 45 minutes or until bread tests done. Cool for 10 minutes on rack before removing from pans.
