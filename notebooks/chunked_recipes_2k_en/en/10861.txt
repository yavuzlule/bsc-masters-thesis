Cook noodles according to package; drain. In baking dish, stir together (gently) the noodles, broccoli, chicken, onion and mushrooms. In a bowl, stir together the soup, milk, cheese, salt and pepper (reserve 1/2 cup, cheese). Stir all into noodle mixture. Bake, covered, at 350\u00b0 for 40 minutes. Sprinkle reserved cheese and paprika over all and return to oven for 10 minutes (or until cheese melts). Contains 290 calories per serving and 11 grams fat.
