In saucepan, melt butter in boiling water. Add flour and salt all at once; stir vigorously. Cook and stir until mixture forms a ball that doesnt separate. Remove from heat; cool slightly and add eggs, one at a time, beating with a spoon after each addition until smooth. Drop, by heaping tablespoonfuls 3 inches apart on lightly greased baking sheet. Bake at 450\u00b0 for 15 minutes. Reduce heat to 325\u00b0 and bake 10 minutes. Remove from oven; cut off tops. Turn oven off; return cream puffs to oven to dry 20 minutes. Cool on rack.
