Sift flour, salt and baking powder into bowl. Beat eggs with milk; add melted Crisco and whole kernel corn and combine the two mixtures lightly. Drop from a teaspoon into deep Crisco heated to 365\u00b0 (or when an inch cube of bread browns in 60 seconds). Fry until brown and cooked in center (about 4 to 5 minutes). Drain on absorbent paper.
