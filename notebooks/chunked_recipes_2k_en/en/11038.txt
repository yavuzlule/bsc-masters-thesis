Heat oven to 350\u00b0. Combine sugar, cinnamon, allspice and ginger. Add scalded milk slowly to eggs; mix well. Add sugar-spice mixture and butter or margarine; stir until sugar dissolves. Combine bread cubes and raisins in buttered 1 1/2-quart casserole. Pour custard mixture over bread cubes. Place casserole in pan of water. Bake 1 hour or until a knife inserted in center comes out clean.
