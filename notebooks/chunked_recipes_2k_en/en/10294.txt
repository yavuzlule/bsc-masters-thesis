Bring 1/2 cup of water to a boil, then add 2 cups of sugar and 1 cup of white syrup. Stir until dissolved and boil until it will spin a thread. Now add 2 cups of raw peanuts and cook slowly over a low fire until it turns a golden brown. Take from fire and add 2 tablespoons of baking soda and 1 teaspoon of vanilla. Stir quickly. Spread on two large, well buttered cookie sheets. Place in cold area.
