Chop cabbage and steam until limp, not soft. Drain well. Use a shallow baking dish and place in layers 1/2 of cabbage and 1/2 can of soup (spread over cabbage). Crumble 1/2 can onion, rings over this. Then slice Velveeta cheese over all. Repeat layers again. Bake at 350\u00b0 until bubbly, about 30 to 40 minutes.
