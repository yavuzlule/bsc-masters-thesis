Cut bacon across strip into 1/4-inch pieces. Chop onion and pepper about the same size. Fry bacon; drain. Discard half of the bacon fat. Add liquid from corn and chopped vegetables. Cook over medium-high heat until thick syrup forms on bottom of pan, stirring frequently. Add corn, bacon, pimento, salt, pepper and chiles. Stir to mix and continue cooking until corn is heated through. , (\Great for company. \)
