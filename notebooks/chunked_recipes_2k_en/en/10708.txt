In a large saucepan, melt the margarine and cook the garlic, celery and carrots on medium heat for 4 minutes. Carefully stir in broth, potatoes and dill. Heat to boiling. Reduce heat and simmer, covered, for 20 to 25 minutes or until potatoes are tender. Mash about 1/2 of the potatoes in the broth. Add drained beans to mixture.
