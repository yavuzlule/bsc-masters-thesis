Combine hamburger, crumbs, onion, pepper, egg. Italian seasoning and 1/4 cup tomato sauce. On a sheet of tinfoil, spread mixture into a 10 x 12-inch rectangle. Sprinkle with cheeses and roll into a log. Seal ends and bake at 375\u00b0 for 1 hour. At the end of the hour, pour remaining tomato sauce over meat loaf and bake for another 15 minutes.
