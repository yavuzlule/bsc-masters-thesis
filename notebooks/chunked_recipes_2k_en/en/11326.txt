Cut chicken wings in half. Discard the tip. In a bowl, mix orange juice, soy sauce and minced garlic. Add the chicken wings to the mixture and marinate at least one hour. It is better to do this the day before and marinate overnight. Place chicken wings on a cookie sheet and bake at 350\u00b0 for about 30 to 45 minutes or until very brown. Let cool for about 15 minutes before serving.
