Cut the corned beef and cheese into pieces to fit on the bread. Lightly butter 1 slice of bread; top with 2 slices of corned beef, 1 slice cheese, 1 teaspoon sauerkraut and 1/4 teaspoon mustard. Top with another slice of bread and bake in a preheated 400\u00b0 oven for 10 minutes. Makes about 18 sandwiches.
