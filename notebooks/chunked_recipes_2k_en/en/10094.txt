Bring milk, rice, sugar, salt and butter to a boil, stirring constantly to avoid burning. When mixture comes to a boil, turn heat down and cook for 1 hour, stirring often. Beat eggs well; add a few drops of vanilla. Pour eggs in a fine stream into rice mixture; then pour mixture from one pan to another 6 or 7 times (pour eggs slowly not all at once). Pour into dish. If desired, add raisins that have been plumped, before pouring from one dish to another. Sprinkle cinnamon on top.
