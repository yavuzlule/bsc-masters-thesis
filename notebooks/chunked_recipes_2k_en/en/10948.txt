Heat oven to 400\u00b0. Grease bottoms and sides of 12 medium muffin tins. Beat egg; stir in milk and oil. Mix in remaining ingredients just until flour is moistened. Batter should be lumpy. Fill muffin cups, 2/3 full. Bake 20 to 25 minutes or until golden brown. Immediately remove from pan. If using self-rising flour, omit baking powder and salt.
