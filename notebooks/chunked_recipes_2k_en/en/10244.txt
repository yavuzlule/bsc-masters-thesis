Mix together graham cracker crumbs, melted butter and 1/4 cup sugar. Press into 9 x 13-inch pan. Bake 8 minutes at 325\u00b0, then chill. Beat soft butter, eggs, powdered sugar and vanilla for 15 minutes. Pour over cooled crust. Sprinkle pineapple on top. Next, layer bananas. Spread with Cool Whip. Sprinkle nuts, dot with cherries and drizzle chocolate syrup. Chill overnight!
