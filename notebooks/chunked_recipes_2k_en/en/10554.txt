Mix sugar, margarine, eggs and flavorings thoroughly. Measure flour and sift. Stir flour, baking powder and salt together. Blend well and chill at least 1 hour. Heat oven to 400\u00b0. Roll dough 1/8-inch thick on a lightly floured board. Cut with a 3-inch cookie cutter. Place on ungreased cookie sheet. Bake for 6 to 10 minutes or until golden brown. Makes 4 dozen cookies.
