In a small heavy saucepan, melt chocolate with butter or margarine over low heat; cool. Combine next 3 ingredients; set aside. Beat eggs and sugar at medium speed until light. Beat in chocolate mixture and vanilla extract. Stir in flour mixture, cracker crumbs and nuts. Pour into a greased 9 x 9 x 2-inch pan. Bake in a preheated 350\u00b0 oven 40 to 45 minutes. Glaze while hot. When cold, cut into squares using a sharp knife. Makes 25 brownies.
