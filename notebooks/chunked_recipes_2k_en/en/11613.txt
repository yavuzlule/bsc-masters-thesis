Preheat oven to 325\u00b0. Combine cranberry sauce, dressing and soup mix in small mixing bowl and mix well. Spread half the cranberry mixture over bottom of 9 x 13-inch baking dish. Arrange chicken in single layer over prepared layer. Top with remaining cranberry mixture. Bake 30 to 45 minutes until chicken is cooked through. Serve over rice. Yield: 8 servings.
