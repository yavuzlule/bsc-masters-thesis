Mix dry ingredients; add milk, oil, eggs and corn. Mix well. Add peppers and onions. Pour 1/2 mixture into greased 9 x 14-inch baking pan or 10-inch iron skillet. Sprinkle 1/2 of Cheddar cheese; add balance of mixture. Top with remainder of cheese. Bake in a 375\u00b0 oven for 35 minutes or until done.
