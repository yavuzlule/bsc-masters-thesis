Sprinkle chicken quarters on both sides with salt and pepper. Melt butter in small saucepan; add lemon juice, tarragon, chervil and vermouth; brush part over chicken. Place chicken, skin side down, on rack of broiler. Broil 6 inches from heat for 20 minutes, basting frequently. Turn; broil 15 to 20 minutes longer or until nicely browned, brushing with remaining lemon-butter. Makes 4 servings.
