Cook sausage links in Dutch oven until brown. Drain in paper toweling and remove fat from pan. Brown ground beef and onion well; stir in tomato puree, paste, sugar, pepper, 1 teaspoon basil, 1 teaspoon salt and 1 cup water. Simmer, covered, for 45 minutes. Cut sausage into bite-size pieces and add to mixture. Cook 15 minutes, stirring occasionally. In the meantime, cook manicotti as label directs; drain in colander. Preheat oven to 375\u00b0.
