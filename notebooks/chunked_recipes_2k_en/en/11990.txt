Cream together sugar, butter or margarine and eggs; mix well. Sift flour and then measure out three cups; add soda and salt and sift again. Add flour mixture to butter mixture a little at a time letting your batter blend well between each addition. Add in bananas and nuts and mix well. Pour into well greased tube or Bundt pan and bake at 300\u00b0 for 1 hour or until toothpick inserted in middle comes out clean.
