Drain peas (if desired, instead of soaking, cover peas with water and bring to boil then let stand 1 hour). Combine peas, water, bouillon, onion, pepper, garlic, thyme, vegetable oil and salt in Dutch oven or large saucepan. Bring to boil, then reduce heat and cover. Simmer gently for 1 hour or until tender. Add additional water if pea mixture becomes too thick. Serves 8.
