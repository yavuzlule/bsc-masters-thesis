Stir together soup, sour cream, hash browns, 1 bag of cheese, melted margarine and hot sauce (if desired). Put in a 9 x 13-inch (or equal quantity) pan. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 45 minutes to 1 hour, or until lightly browned on bottom and sides. Add more cheese on top for the last few minutes of baking time and bake till bubbly.
