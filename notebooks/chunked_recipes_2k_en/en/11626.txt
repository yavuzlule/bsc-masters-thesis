Fry onions, bacon and mushrooms together, turning frequently until bacon is sufficiently cooked (not crispy). In a separate frypan, heat vegetable oil on medium-high temperature for 2 to 3 minutes. Season sliced potatoes with paprika, pepper and salt as listed in large bowl. Reduce temperature to low-medium; add sliced potatoes slowly to hot oil; turn potatoes frequently. Fry until light brown color is achieved. Combine all ingredients in one frypan and cook an extra 10 to 15 minutes and serve.
