Saute onion and garlic in oil in 4 or 5-quart pot or skillet. Add spareribs and brown. Sprinkle with paprika, add peas, water and wine. Cover and simmer 1 hour, until ribs are tender. Add tomatoes, green pepper, brown sugar, chili powder, salt and pepper. Cover and cook slowly for 30 to 45 minutes. Serves 4. Good over rice.
