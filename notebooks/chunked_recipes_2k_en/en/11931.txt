Heat 1 cup water and limeade concentrate to boiling. Pour over gelatin in large bowl. Stir until dissolved. Stir in 1/2 cup cold water, ginger ale and peppermint extract. Chill until partially set. Stir in pears and melon balls. Pour into 7 cup jello mold. Chill until firm. Unmold onto lettuce and garnish with extra cantaloupe balls and mint sprigs.
