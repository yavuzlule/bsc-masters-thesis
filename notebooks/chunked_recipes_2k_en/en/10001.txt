Pour oil in 3-quart microproof casserole or baking dish. Add beef strips and stir to coat with oil. Add onion, garlic, salt, pepper and tomatoes. Cover and cook on High (maximum power) 4 minutes, stirring once during cooking. Add green peppers and soy sauce, stir. Cook, covered, on High (max power) 4 to 5 minutes or until green pepper is just tender. Serve with hot cooked rice or chow mein noodles. Yields 4 servings. Total cooking time is 8 to 9 minutes.
