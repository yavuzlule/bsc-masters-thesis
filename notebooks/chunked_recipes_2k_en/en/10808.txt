Soften gelatin in 1/2 cup milk. Beat egg yolks and sugar until thick and lemon colored; stir in gelatin and scalded milk. Cook in top of a double boiler, stirring constantly, until thickened; cool. Stir in sherry and fold in whipped cream. Beat egg whites until stiff but not dry and fold into custard. Line a 3-quart bowl with ladyfingers; pour in filling and chill until set. Yields about 12 servings.
