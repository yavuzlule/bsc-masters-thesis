Slice potatoes in half and then slice each half into 8 thick crescent-shaped pieces. Chill sliced potatoes 10 minutes (or longer) in ice cold water to crisp them. Preheat oven to 475\u00b0. Drain potatoes well. Toss lightly with oil. Spray a baking pan with Pam. Arrange the slices in a single layer. Bake 25 minutes turning occasionally. Season and serve. Makes 4 (75 calorie) servings.
