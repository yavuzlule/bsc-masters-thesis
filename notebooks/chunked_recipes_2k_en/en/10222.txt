Preheat oven to 375\u00b0. Peel apples and slice into a deep baking dish. Sprinkle with spices (if desired) and granulated sugar. Blend together the oats, flour, brown sugar, cornflake crumbs and oil. Press this mixture on top of the apple slices. Bake for 30 minutes or until apples are tender when tested with a fork. Yields 6 servings. Fat:. None.
