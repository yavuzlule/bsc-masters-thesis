Mix chicken, 1 tablespoon sugar, 1 tablespoon soy sauce and egg in mixing bowl. Sprinkle on cornstarch and stir in well. In a saucepan, combine the broth, remaining soy sauce, sugar and sherry. Bring to a boil. Form meatballs with hands or drop from spoon into boiling broth. Poach 5 to 6 minutes. Remove with slotted spoon. You may then poach green beans or other vegetables in broth. Thicken broth with a cornstarch and water paste, if desired. Serve over rice.
