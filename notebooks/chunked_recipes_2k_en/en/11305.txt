Sprinkle both sides of meat with garlic salt. Place in 3-quart (13 x 9-inch) glass baking dish. Cover with plastic wrap. Microwave on Simmer for 45 minutes. Turn meat over; re-cover and continue cooking on Simmer for 40 to 45 minutes or until fork-tender. Remove meat and slice meat approximately 1/4-inch thick. Dissolve onion soup mix in hot meat juices; place sliced meat in seasoned meat juice (add sliced onions on top of beef, if desired). Cover with plastic wrap or glass cover. Microwave on Reheat for 8 to 10 minutes or until heated through. Let stand, covered, 5 minutes before serving. Serves about 8.
