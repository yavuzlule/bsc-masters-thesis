In bowl, combine flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt. Cut in Crisco until mixture is coarse crumbs. Stir in buttermilk. Knead dough gently. Roll out to 1/2 inch thick. Cut with 2 1/2 inch biscuit cutter and place on lightly greased baking sheet. Bake 450 degrees for 10 - 15 minutes or until golden brown. Makes 10 biscuits.
