Cream sugar, margarine and shortening in mixer bowl until light and fluffy. Add flour and eggs; beat well. Add flavorings; mix well. Stir in Sun-Drop. Pour into greased and floured tube pan. Bake at 325\u00b0 for 1 1/4 hours. Cool in pan for several minutes. Invert onto serving plate. Spread frosting over cake. Yield:, 16 servings.
