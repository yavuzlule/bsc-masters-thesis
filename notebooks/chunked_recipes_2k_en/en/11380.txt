Make batter as directed, except stir 1/8 teaspoon soda into mix before adding liquid. Use only 3/4 cup water and add 1 cup mashed bananas (2 to 3 medium). If desired, fold 1/3 cup finely chopped nuts into batter. Pour into prepared oblong or layer pans. Bake. If desired, fill and frost with sweetened whipped cream and sliced bananas.
