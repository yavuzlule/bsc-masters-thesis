Mix sugar, shortening and egg. Stir in milk. Sift together and stir 1 1/2 cups flour, baking powder and salt. The mixture for the struedel is the brown sugar, 2 tablespoons flour, cinnamon and butter. Spread half batter mixture in greased pan. Sprinkle 1/2 streusel mixture. Alternate rest. Bake in greased and floured pan 25 to 35 minutes at 375\u00b0. Double batter for 13 x 11 x 2-inch pan.
