Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. Pour melted butter into a shallow, 1 1/2-quart baking dish. In a large bowl, combine flour, 1/4 cup sugar and milk. Pour mixture evenly over butter. Combine strawberries, remaining 1/2 cup sugar and water in a separate bowl, then spoon evenly over the flour/milk layer. Do not stir. Bake for 40 to 45 minutes or until lightly browned.
