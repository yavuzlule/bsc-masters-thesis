Boil chicken until tender. Remove from bone. Line bottom and sides of greased 3-quart casserole dish with layer of tortillas. Sprinkle with 2 or 3 tablespoons chicken stock. Layer with 1/2 can undiluted mushroom soup and 1/2 of other ingredients in the order listed. Cover with another layer of tortillas; sprinkle with 2 or 3 tablespoons chicken stock and remaining ingredients. Top the last layer of cheese with can of tomatoes with chilies. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 1 hour.
