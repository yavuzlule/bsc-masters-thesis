Cut gumdrops into small pieces. Cream butter and sugar together. Add well beaten eggs. Add flour and applesauce alternately. Stir in baking soda and 1 teaspoon vanilla. Fold in nuts, raisins and gumdrops (chopped candied cherries and candied pineapple may be added for variety). Bake slowly in about 250\u00b0 oven approximately 3 hours. Will make 4 small loaves or one large tube pan. I use all orange slices, 2 pounds.
