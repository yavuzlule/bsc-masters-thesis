In a double boiler, beat the egg yolks and add 2 tablespoons sugar gradually while beating. Add salt and milk. Cook over hot water until mixture coats the spoon, stirring constantly. Soften gelatin in cold water; add to hot custard and stir until dissolved. Cool slightly. Beat egg whites stiff; fold in. Whip the cream; add remaining 3 tablespoons sugar and vanilla extract. Fold in. Line sherbet glasses or spring-form mold with the ladyfingers and fill with the custard. Chill until firm.
