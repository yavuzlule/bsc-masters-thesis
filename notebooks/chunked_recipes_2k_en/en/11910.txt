Combine first 4 ingredients in large non-aluminum skillet, bring to a boil. Add chicken and cover. Reduce heat and simmer 10 minutes or until chicken is done. Remove chicken from skillet and keep warm. Bring cooking liquid to a boil; cook 7 minutes or until reduced to 3/4 cup, stirring occasionally. Combine strawberry spread and cornstarch; stir into cooking liquid and cook 1 minute or until syrupy. Spoon 3 tablespoons sauce over each chicken breast, garnish with green onion strips. Yields 4 servings, 215 calories and 1. 5 fat.
