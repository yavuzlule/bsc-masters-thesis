Mix salt, pepper and flour. Roll individual servings of veal cutlets in seasoned flour. Brown in hot oil in skillet rubbed with garlic. Add 1 cup hot water; cover and simmer 1 1/2 hours. Arrange meat on hot platter. Into gravy left in pan, stir 1 cup cream. Heat and pour over noodles. Sprinkle with paprika. Makes 6 servings.
