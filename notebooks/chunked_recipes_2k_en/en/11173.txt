To reserved pineapple liquid add enough water to make 1 1/2 cups. In large bowl combine Eagle Brand milk. Add pudding mix and beat well. Add pineapple. Chill 10 minutes. Fold in whipped cream. Spoon 1 1/2 cups pudding into 3 to 4-quart glass serving bowl. Top with 1/3 each of chocolate wafers, banana and pudding. Repeat layering twice, ending with pudding. Chill thoroughly.
