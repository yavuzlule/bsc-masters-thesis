Combine shrimp, eggs, celery salt, salt and pepper. Combine mayonnaise with cream. Fold into shrimp mixture. Spread shrimp mixture between bread slices. Cut into halves, diagonally. Fasten corners with wooden picks. Place on cookie sheet; broil 4-inches from source of heat for 10 to 15 minutes or until bread is toasted. Yield: 8 servings.
