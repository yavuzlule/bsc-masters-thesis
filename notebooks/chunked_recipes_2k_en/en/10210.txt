Mix hamburger, ketchup, onion, eggs, milk and salt in mixing bowl. Cook macaroni and drain. Add mustard and mayonnaise to macaroni. Alternate layers of beef mixture and macaroni mixture in 13 x 9-inch pan. Sprinkle with cheese, then sprinkle with cornflake crumbs. Drizzle oleo over all. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 40 minutes.
