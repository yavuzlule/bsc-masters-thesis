Beat butter until softened and light. Add sugar and beat until fluffy. Add eggs, one at a time, and beat after each addition. Stir in almond flavor. Mix flour and salt together. Add to butter mixture alternately with milk (about 1/3 each time). Fold in nuts and cherries. Bake in greased tube or Bundt pan at 325\u00b0 for 1 1/4 hours.
