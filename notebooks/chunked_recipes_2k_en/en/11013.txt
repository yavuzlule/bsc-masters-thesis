Cook broccoli until barely tender. Drain and put in casserole dish. Melt the 1/2 cup butter and add the flour. Remove from heat and add bouillon. Gradually add milk. Return to heat and cook until smooth and boiling. Pour over broccoli. Heat the 3/4 cup water and 6 tablespoons butter. Add the stuffing and walnuts. Sprinkle over broccoli and bake at 400\u00b0 for 20 to 30 minutes.
