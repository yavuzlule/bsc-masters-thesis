Remove excess fat from chicken. Rub with salt, garlic powder and red pepper. Let stand 30 minutes. Combine onions, bell peppers and celery in a medium bowl; set aside. Combine flour, 1/2 teaspoon salt, 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder and 1/2 teaspoon red pepper in paper or plastic bag. Add chicken pieces and shake until chicken is coated. Reserve 1/2 cup of the flour.
