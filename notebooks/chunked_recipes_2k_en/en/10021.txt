In a medium saucepan of boiling water, cook carrots for 8 to 10 minutes, until just tender; drain. In a large nonstick skillet, melt margarine. Add onion; cook, stirring frequently, for 8 to 10 minutes, until golden. Add carrots, sugar and syrup; stir to coat. Cover and simmer for 5 to 10 minutes, until glazed. Stir apples, cinnamon and nutmeg into skillet. Cover and cook 5 minutes longer, until apples and carrots are tender. Makes 8 servings.
