Melt butter; mix crumbs in and press into 9 x 13-inch serving dish. Dissolve sugar in mixture of lemon juice and rind. Dissolve Jell-O in water and chill in refrigerator. Whip milk until thick; gradually add Jell-O, beating. Stir in lemon mixture. Put in crust and chill. Sprinkle graham cracker crumbs on top for garnish.
