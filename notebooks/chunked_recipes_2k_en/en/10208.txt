Rinse chicken breasts and pat dry. Mix salt, pepper, celery salt and paprika together and sprinkle over chicken pieces. Next, sprinkle lemon juice over chicken pieces and place in a crock pot or slow cooker. Mix the two soups together in a bowl. Pour over chicken and sprinkle with cheese. Cover and cook on low setting for 6 to 8 hours. Serve chicken and sauce over hot rice or noodles. Serves 8-12.
