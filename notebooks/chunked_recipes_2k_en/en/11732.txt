In a Dutch oven over medium heat, cook sausage, beef, onion and green pepper until meat is browned and vegetables are tender; drain. Add tomato sauce and paste, water and seasonings; bring to a boil. Reduce heat; cover and simmer for 15 minutes. Add corn and olives; cover and simmer for 5 minutes. Stir in noodles. Pour into two greased 13 x 9 x 2-inch baking dishes; top with cheese. Cover and bake at 350\u00b0 for 25 to 30 minutes or until heated through. Yield: 16 to 20 servings.
