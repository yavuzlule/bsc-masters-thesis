Cook potatoes in boiling water 30 minutes or until tender. Drain; let cool to touch. Peel and cut into 1/4 inch strips; set aside. Combine 1/4 cup butter and next 5 ingredients in a large bowl; gently stir in potatoes and cheese. Spoon into a lightly greased 13 x 9 x 2-inch baking dish. Combine 3 tablespoons butter and stuffing mix; sprinkle over potato mixture. Bake, uncovered, at 350\u00b0 for 25 minutes or until thoroughly heated. Yields 8 servings.
