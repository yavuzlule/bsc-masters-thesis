Stir together first 3 ingredients. Boil 7 1/2 minutes or until it makes a soft ball when dropped in cold water. Remove from heat and add chocolate chips and marshmallows. Add vanilla. Do not use a mixer!. Stir until marshmallows are melted. Add nuts and pour into well buttered pan. Let cool at least 4 to 6 hours. Do not cut until cooled.
