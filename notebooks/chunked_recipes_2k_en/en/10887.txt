Cream oleo until soft; add powdered sugar gradually and beat until creamy. Add egg, maple syrup and soda. Mix in flour. Add nuts. Pour into an 8 x 8-inch greased pan. , (I had to use a pan much smaller than this size. ). Bake at 375\u00b0 for 15 to 20 minutes. Cut into squares while warm and roll in powdered sugar. I put maple icing and walnuts on mine on top.
