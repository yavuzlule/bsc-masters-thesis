Cream sugar and shortening. Add vanilla and then salt. Add egg whites one at a time, beating after each one. Beat in 2 tablespoons flour. Add 3/4 cup buttermilk with baking soda added. Add remaining flour and buttermilk alternately. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 25 to 30 minutes in 10 x 15-inch sheet cake pan that has been greased and floured. Cool and frost with buttercream frosting.
