Preheat oven to 250\u00b0. Place apples in a single layer in a large, flat baking pan. Bake 20 minutes. Remove from oven, and stir in popcorn and pecans. Mix remaining ingredients in a cup and drizzle over apples, popcorn, and pecans. Return to oven, and bake for 30 minutes, stirring every 10 minutes.
