Wash and cut cucumbers in half lengthwise; pack in hot jars with the last 5 ingredients put in each pint jar. Combine sugar, salt, vinegar and water. Tie pickling spices in a cheesecloth bag; add to vinegar mixture. Simmer 15 minutes. Heat to boiling and pour over cucumbers; leave 1/4-inch headspace. Adjust caps. Process pints and quarts 15 minutes in boiling water bath. Yield: about 7 pints.
