Sift dry ingredients together. Blend in vegetable oil. Add enough milk to make a stiff dough. Thinly roll out dough (1/4-inch thick) on a lightly floured board into two 10 x 18-inch rectangles. Spread on sausage; roll up lengthwise. Chill well and slice 1/4-inch thick. Bake in a preheated oven at 350\u00b0 for 15 to 20 minutes.
