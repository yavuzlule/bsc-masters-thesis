Mix until crumbly the first 5 ingredients. Press 1/2 of crumbs in greased 7 x 9-inch baking pan. Top with rhubarb. Then combine in saucepan the next 4 ingredients. Cook until thick and clear. Pour over rhubarb. Top with remaining crumbs. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 1 hour. Cut in squares and serve with whipped cream.
