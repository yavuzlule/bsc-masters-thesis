Soften yeast in 1/4 cup warm water; set aside until remaining hot water, shortening, salt and sugar cool to lukewarm. Stir in 2 cups flour; beat well. Add remaining flour to make a moderately stiff dough. Turn on floured surface and knead until satiny, 8 to 10 minutes. Let sit 10 minutes and knead again a few minutes. Place in greased bowl, turning once to grease surface. Let raise in warm place until double in bulk. Punch down; raise again 1 hour until double. Cut dough in two portions. Place in small ball; set 10 minutes. Place in loaves; let raise until double about 1 hour. Bake at 300\u00b0 for 45 minutes.
