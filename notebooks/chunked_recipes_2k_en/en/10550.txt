Saute ground beef and onion in skillet until ground beef is brown. Stir frequently. Add chilies, olives, catsup, salt and chili powder and mix well. Cook for 5 minutes. Stir occasionally. Place one slice Colby cheese on bottom half of each loaf. Spoon ground beef mixture over cheese. Arrange bacon slice and Muenster cheese slice over ground beef. Cover with remaining loaf halves. Wrap loaves with aluminum foil. Bake at 375\u00b0 for 10 minutes.
