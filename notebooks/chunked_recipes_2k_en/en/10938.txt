Bring broccoli to a boil for approximately 5 minutes. Drain and set aside. Melt margarine; add chopped onion and saute in skillet. Add garlic cheese and cream of mushroom soup. Place broccoli in greased baking dish. When cheese and soup are melted, pour over broccoli. Top with crackers or bread crumbs and dot with butter. Bake at 350\u00b0 for about 25 to 30 minutes. Do not cover while baking.
