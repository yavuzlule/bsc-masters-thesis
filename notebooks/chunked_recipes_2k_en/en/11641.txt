Combine flour, salt, paprika, and mustard. Roll meat in flour mixture. Brown meat slowly in hot oil. Sprinkle remaining flour mixture over meat. Add remaining ingredients. Cover and simmer for 1 1/2 to 2 hours. Then remove vegetables and bay leaf. Pack meat into jars, leaving 1/2 inch headspace. Cover with sauce, 1 pint 1 hour, 1 quart 1 hour and 15 minutes at 10 pounds pressure.
