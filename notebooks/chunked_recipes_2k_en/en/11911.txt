Sift sugar and flour together in bowl; set aside. In saucepan melt butter; add cocoa and Coca-Cola. Bring to a boil; boil one minute. Add to dry ingredients. Mix buttermilk and soda and add. Add eggs and vanilla; stir until smooth. Last, stir in marshmallows. Pour into greased pan (9 x 13-inch); bake at 350\u00b0 for 45 minutes.
