Drain pineapple and save juice. Put pineapple in a butter dish. Mix sugar and flour and sprinkle over pineapple and add 1 cup pineapple juice. Pour over. Put a cup of grated cheese. Melt 1 stick of butter and 2 tablespoons of brown sugar and stir up crushed crackers (Ritz). Sprinkle over the ingredients. Bake 40 minutes at 325\u00b0.
