About 1 1/2 hours before serving or up to 1 week ahead: Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. In medium bowl with mixer at low speed, beat all ingredients until blended, occasionally scraping bowl with rubber spatula. Drop dough by 2 teaspoons about 2 inches apart, onto ungreased cookie sheets. Bake cookies 10 to 12 minutes until edges of cookies are browned. With pancake turner, remove cookies.
