In a skillet cook sausage until well done, drain. Separate crescent rolls into 8-inch triangles. Place dough in 12-inch pizza pan, with points toward center. Press over bottom and sides to form crust. Spoon sausage over crust. Sprinkle with potatoes. Top with Cheddar cheese. In a bowl, stir eggs, milk and 1/8 teaspoon pepper. Carefully pour over mixture in crust. Sprinkle Parmesan cheese on top. Bake at 375\u00b0 for about 25 minutes or until set. Makes 6 to 8 servings.
