Dissolve Jell-O in 2 cups boiling water. Add 2 cups reserved peach syrup (if not enough, add water until there is). Put mixture in fridge to set a bit (about 1 1/2 hours). Beat peaches with mixer until very mushy. Beat the whipping cream, adding the powdered sugar. Fold in the Jell-O mixture. Stir very well. Pour into 13 x 9-inch pan and keep in fridge until set.
