Sift together flour, soda, salt and spices. Blend shortening and sugar. Add eggs; beat well. Add dry ingredients alternately with coffee. Fold in chopped apples and raisins. Nuts may be added if desired. Bake in a greased and floured tube pan at 350\u00b0 for 1 to 1 1/4 hours. Cake may be iced when cool.
