In a small bowl, mix beef broth with cornstarch and teriyaki sauce; set aside. In a large skillet, heat 1 tablespoon oil over high heat; add mushrooms, green pepper and onions. Stir-fry until tender. Remove to bowl. Add remaining oil and beef; stir-fry until cooked. Add beef broth mixture and cook, stirring constantly until thickened. Add vegetables. Serve in warm pastry shells.
