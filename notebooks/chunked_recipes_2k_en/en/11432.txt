Take Irish potatoes. Peel and wash and chop small. Put a small amount of salt in the water you boil them in. Cook until real tender. Drain water off and break eggs in the hot potatoes. Mash real good, the hot potatoes will cook the eggs. Add onion (chopped fine), mayonnaise, sugar, vinegar, salt and black pepper. Stir all this up real good and taste to see if its flavored to suit your taste.
