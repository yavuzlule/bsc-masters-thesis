Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. Cook and drain broccoli. Place in bottom of greased, glass baking dish (13 x 9-inch). Arrange cooked chicken on top. Mix together soup, mayonnaise, curry powder and lemon juice. Pour mixture over chicken and broccoli. Top with shredded cheese, bread crumbs and paprika. Bake 30 minutes or until bubbly.
