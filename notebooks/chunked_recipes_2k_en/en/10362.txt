Pour the white glue into a clean, empty peanut butter jar. Add 1/2 cup of water and 10 drops of food coloring and stir. Combine the Borax and a cup of water in a medium size plastic bowl. Slowly pour the glue mixture into the Borax solution and stir until well mixed. The Borax causes the mixture to congeal. Scoop the gloop out of the water and knead it in your hands until it becomes dry enough to play with. If stored in a sealed container or plastic bag, it should keep for several weeks. Gloop dries out after a lot of handling.
