Beat the eggs and sugar together. Gradually add oil; add vanilla. Mix flour, soda, salt and cinnamon together. Gradually add flour mixture to first mixture (will be thick). Fold in apples. Add 1 cup nuts or raisins. Bake in a 375\u00b0 oven for 45 minutes. Use greased 9 x 13-inch sheet pan. Sprinkle with confectioners sugar.
