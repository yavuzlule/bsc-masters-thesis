Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. Chop bacon, tomato, onion and grated cheese. Mix all ingredients except biscuits. Separate each biscuit into 3 thinner biscuits. Lay each biscuit over a mini muffin pan cup. Press biscuit into cup. Fill cups with bacon mixture and bake 10 - 12 minutes until golden brown. Yields 30.
