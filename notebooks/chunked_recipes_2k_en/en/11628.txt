Cook the rice following the instructions on the package. This should take about 20 minutes. Pour the rice into a large bowl to let it cool. Scramble the eggs in a small pan over medium heat. , chop scrambled chunks of egg into small pea-sized bits with your spatula while cooking. When the rice is cooled, add the peas, carrots, eggs and onion to the bowl. Carefully toss all of the ingredients together. Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium/high heat. When the butter has completely melted, dump the rice mixture into the pan and add the soy sauce plus a dash of salt and pepper. Cook the rice mixture for 6 to 8 minutes, stirring often.
