Sift flour, soda and salt. Set aside. Measure margarine and sugar into a large bowl. Beat until well blended and smooth. Add egg and vanilla; beat well. Add sifted dry ingredients and mix until combined. Stir in Rice Krispies, chocolate morsels and raisins. Drop by tablespoonful onto lightly greased baking sheets. Bake in 350\u00b0 oven for about 12 minutes or until lightly browned. Remove immediately from baking sheet to cool. Makes 3 1/2 dozen cookies.
