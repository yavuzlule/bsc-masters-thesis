Place chicken on a rack in roasting pan. Season with salt and pepper and 1 1/2 teaspoons oregano. Arrange potatoes around chicken; sprinkle with salt, pepper and oregano. Pour butter and lemon juice over chicken and potatoes. Add chicken broth to pan bottom. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 2 to 2 1/2 hours. Baste with juices while baking.
