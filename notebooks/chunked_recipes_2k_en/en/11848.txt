Cream margarine and shortening; add sugar and beat until smooth. Add egg yolks and beat well. Combine flour and soda. Add to cream mixture alternating with buttermilk. Stir in vanilla; add coconut and chopped nuts. Fold in stiffly beaten egg whites. Bake in a 3 (8-inch) pans, greased and floured. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 25 minutes.
