Reserve stock. Debone chicken and cut in bite size pieces. Cook spaghetti in reserved stock; drain to just below top of spaghetti. Saute onion, celery and bell pepper in margarine until tender. Add cheese to hot spaghetti; stir until melted. Add all other ingredients together and season with garlic salt. Stir together well. Bake in oblong dish or pan at 350\u00b0 for 30 minutes. Sprinkle parsley flakes on top. Serves approximately 12 to 15 people.
