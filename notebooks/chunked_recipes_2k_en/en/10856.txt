In a small mixing bowl, beat together the cream cheese, sour cream, oregano, garlic powder and red pepper. Spread evenly in 9 or 10-inch quiche pan or pie plate. Spread pizza sauce over the top. Sprinkle with pepperoni, green onions and green pepper. Bake in preheated 350\u00b0 oven for 10 minutes. Top with cheese. Bake 5 minutes more, until cheese is melted and mixture is heated through.
