Fry ground beef with sliced onions; pour off excess oil (do not overcook). Stir in chili mix. Stir in tomatoes, chop or cut tomatoes before adding. Add tomato paste (about 2/3 can). Add jalapeno peppers (split into halves or quarters). Add kidney beans juice and all. Add water to thin out (your choice as to density). Let simmer about 1 hour.
