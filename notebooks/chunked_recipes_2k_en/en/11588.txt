Sort and wash beans. Combine beans and water in large Dutch oven; bring to a boil. Cover; reduce heat to medium and cook 40 minutes. Add next 8 ingredients; cover. Reduce heat and simmer 2 hours, stirring occasionally. Mash most of beans against side of Dutch oven with back of a spoon; stir well. Cover and bring to a boil; reduce heat to medium-high and cook, uncovered an additional hour or until desired degree of doneness, stirring occasionally. Discard bay leaves. Serve over hot cooked rice.
