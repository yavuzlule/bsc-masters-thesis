Cook noodles according to directions on package. Drain. Stir in tuna, celery, mayonnaise, onion, green pepper and salt. In saucepan, combine soup and milk; heat through. Add cheese. Heat and stir until cheese melts. Add to noodle mixture. Turn into 2-quart casserole. Bake, uncovered, in a 425\u00b0 oven for 20 minutes. Makes 6 servings.
