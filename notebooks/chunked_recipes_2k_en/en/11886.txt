Mix meat, peas, corn, cheese, 1 cup milk, onion and Worcestershire sauce. Pour into greased 12 x 8-inch baking dish or shallow 2-quart casserole. Bake at 375\u00b0 for 15 minutes or until bubbly at edges. Mix well baking mix, cornmeal, sugar, salt, egg and 2/3 cup evaporated milk. Pour on hot mixture, leaving center uncovered. Spread to edges. Bake 25 minutes or until golden brown.
