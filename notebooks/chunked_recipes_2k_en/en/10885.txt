Brown ground beef with onion, garlic salt, salt and pepper. Place 3 lasagna noodles into boiling water. Dont overcook noodles or they will split. In bottom of 9 x 12-inch pan, put thin coating of spaghetti sauce. Put the noodles in bottom of pan and put 3 more in water. Put on top of noodles in layers: drained ground beef (cooked). Ricotta cheese. Mozzarella cheese, sprinkle of grated cheese, drizzle with spaghetti sauce, sprinkle of parsley flakes and sprinkle a few anise seed (if you like). Do 2 more layers; use plenty of Mozzarella cheese and plenty of sauce. Put too much sauce will make the layers slide apart. Cover with foil; bake at 350\u00b0 for 45 minutes.
