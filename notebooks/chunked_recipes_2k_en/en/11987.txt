Cream together until fluffy the shortening and sugar. Add eggs; beat, until smooth. Stir together the flour, soda and salt. Add to first mixture. Stir crumbled mincemeat into dough. Drop onto greased cookie sheet 2 inches apart by rounded teaspoonfuls. Bake 8 to 10 minutes at 375\u00b0 until lightly browned. Makes about 6 1/2 dozen. A tasty, mild, fruity cookie.
