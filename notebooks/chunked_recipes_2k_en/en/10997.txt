Mix cake mix and Jell-O thoroughly; add oil and water. Blend well and add eggs, one at a time. Beat according to package (cake mix) directions. Fold in coconut, nuts and thawed, drained strawberries. Pour batter into tube (greased and floured) or 13 x 9-inch pan. Bake according to cake mix directions at 350\u00b0 (maybe less).
