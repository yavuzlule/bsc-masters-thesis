Mix first 4 ingredients and beat to soft peaks. Gradually add sugar and cocoa. Beat to stiff peaks. Make 10 big round cookie \shells\ on a cookie sheet using the egg white mixture. Bake at 250\u00b0 for 40 minutes. Cool. Spread 1 tablespoon Dream Whip on each cookie. Mix rest of Dream Whip with fudge and cream cheese. Spread 2 1/2 tablespoons of chocolate mixture on each cookie. Refrigerate.
