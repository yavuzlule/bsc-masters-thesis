Peel potatoes. Cut into thin slices (but not all the way through; use handle of a spoon to prevent knife from cutting all the way). Put potatoes in a baking dish, fanning them slightly. Sprinkle salt and spread butter. Sprinkle herbs. Bake at 450\u00b0 for 50 minutes. Remove from oven. Sprinkle on cheese. Bake for 7 to 10 minutes more. Add parsley ears and tail from parsley stem.
