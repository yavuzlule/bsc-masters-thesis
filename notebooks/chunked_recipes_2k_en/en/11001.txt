Combine first 4 ingredients in large Dutch oven. Bring to boil; cover and reduce heat. Simmer 2 hours or until meat is tender. Remove meat from broth; cool. Skin, bone and chop chicken. Skim off and discard fat from broth. Measure broth and return 3 quarts to Dutch oven. Add chopped meats and remaining ingredients. Bring to boil; reduce heat. Simmer, uncovered 4 hours, stirring frequently. Add additional broth or water if necessary. Remove red pepper. Double for after the game.
