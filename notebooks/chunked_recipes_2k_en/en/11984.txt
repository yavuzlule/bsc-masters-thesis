Add salt to eggs and beat until thick; add sugar gradually. Continue beating until sugar is dissolved. Add anise and beat until thoroughly blended. Beat in flour to make a moderately stiff dough. Chill dough for several hours or overnight; roll into a rectangular sheet 1/4 to 1/2-inch thick on floured board. Cut cookies in 1 1/2-inch squares; place on greased baking sheet and chill, uncovered, for about 1 hour. Bake at 325\u00b0 for 15 minutes. Cool and store in covered container. These can keep for weeks. Makes 60 cookies.
