In saucepan, dissolve jello in 1/2 cup boiling water and juice from the cherries. Cool slightly. In mixing bowl, beat eggs well. Add sugar; beat well. Blend in the half and half. Cut cherries into fourths and add to mixture; stir in vanilla and jello mixture last. Pour into freezer canister. Add milk until about 3/4 full. An electric freezer takes about 15 minutes to freeze.
