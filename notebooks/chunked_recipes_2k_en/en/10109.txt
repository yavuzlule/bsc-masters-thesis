Preheat oven to 425\u00b0. Brush surface of pie shell with egg white and bake 4 to 5 minutes. Place Swiss cheese and bacon evenly on bottom of shell. Combine eggs, cream, salt, nutmeg, sugar, pepper, cayenne and mustard. Mix thoroughly. Pour egg mixture over ingredients in shell. Bake 15 minutes at 425\u00b0, reduce oven to 300\u00b0 and bake 40 minutes or until knife comes out clean.
