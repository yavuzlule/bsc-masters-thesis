Press evenly into ungreased pan first 3 ingredients. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 10 minutes. Beat cream cheese in 3-quart bowl. Add sugar and cocoa gradually. Beat in eggs, one at a time. Pour in vanilla. Pour into spring-form pan. Bake at 300\u00b0 until center is firm, about 1 hour. Cover and refrigerate for 3 hours. Prepare topping. Spread over cake; loosen sides and remove.
