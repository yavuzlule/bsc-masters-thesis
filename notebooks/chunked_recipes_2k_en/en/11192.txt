Dissolve yeast in lukewarm water. Heat buttermilk to lukewarm. Add the salt, shortening and sugar to the buttermilk. Add the dissolved yeast, a cup of the flour with the soda and the egg. Beat vigorously. Beat and fold in the remaining flour until a soft dough. Fold lightly (about 5 minutes). Place in a clean lightly greased bowl. Cover and let rise 45 minutes to 1 hour until double in bulk. Shape into desired rolls. Bake in a 350\u00b0 to 375\u00b0 oven for 20 to 25 minutes.
