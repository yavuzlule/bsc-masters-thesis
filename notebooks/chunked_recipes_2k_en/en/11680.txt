Cream butter, shortening and both sugars. Add eggs and beat well. Blend in flour and soda; mix in vanilla. Roll in small balls. Sprinkle with sugar and place on greased cookie sheet. Flatten with glass or refrigerate until firm (overnight) and roll out and cut with cutters. Color may be added. Best when soft (before refrigerating). Bake at 350\u00b0 for 8 to 10 minutes.
