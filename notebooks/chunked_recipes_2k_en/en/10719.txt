Melt butter in deep pan; add farina and cook until golden color, stirring constantly. Stir in chopped nuts. Reduce heat. Meanwhile, bring water and sugar to a slow boil. Cook 3 minutes. Pour syrup slowly over farina, stirring with wooden spoon until halva is slightly thickened. Remove from heat. Cover; let stand 10 minutes. Shape with individual molds or with spoon. Place on large platters and sprinkle with cinnamon.
