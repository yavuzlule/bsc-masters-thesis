Dissolve yeast in 1 cup lukewarm water. Combine 1 cup lukewarm water, liquid shortening, sugar, salt and eggs. Add yeast mixture (after it has dissolved). Gradually add flour; stir until blended and knead until desired texture. Grease bowl; put dough into it and set in warm place to rise. Cover with towel. Form into desired shapes and let rise again. Bake 15 minutes at 400\u00b0, on greased pan.
