Make graham cracker crust in 9 x 9-inch dish by combining melted margarine and 2 tablespoons sugar. Refrigerate for 30 minutes, until set. Combine microwaved/soft cream cheese and powdered sugar; mix. Add 1/2 tub Cool Whip; mix. Place next to crust. Cover with chocolate pudding. Finish top off with whipped cream. Chill.
