Cook broccoli in boiling, salted water until tender; drain. Arrange broccoli in greased 11 1/2 x 7 1/2-inch baking dish. Place cooked chicken on top. Combine soup, mayonnaise and lemon juice; pour over chicken. Sprinkle with cheese. Combine bread crumbs and butter. Sprinkle over top. Bake 30 to 40 minutes at 350\u00b0 or until thoroughly heated. This dish can be prepared a day ahead and put in the refrigerator. It also freezes very well. Dish can also be made substituting asparagus for broccoli.
