Beat egg white until foamy. Brush over the bottom and sides of pastry. In a small bowl combine flour and sugars. Cut in margarine until mixture resembles fine crumbs. Sprinkle 2/3 into bottom of pastry. Top with peaches. Sprinkle with remaining crumb mixture. Bake at 375\u00b0 for 40 to 45 minutes or until filling is bubbly and peaches are tender.
