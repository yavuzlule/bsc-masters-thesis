Cook the lasagna noodles according to directions. Drain rinse and separate. , brown the beef and sprinkle the mix over the meat. Add tomatoes and blend well. Whip the cream cheese. In a buttered 1 1/2 quart casserole. Make 2 layers each of noodles, meat & dollops of cream cheese. Bake at 350 degree for 20 minutes or until bubbly. Makes 6 servings.
