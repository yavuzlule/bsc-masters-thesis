In a heavy pot, bring milk almost to a boil. Remove from heat and sprinkle in nutmeg to taste. Beat eggs and to them add sugar, vanilla and a pinch of salt. Add hot milk to egg mixture. Pour into unbaked 9-inch pie crust. Bake in slow oven at 325\u00b0 to 350\u00b0 for 30 minutes or until knife inserted in center comes out clean.
