In a large bowl, combine flour, sugar, undissolved yeast and salt. Gradually add water and 2 tablespoons peanut oil to flour mixture. Beat 2 minutes at medium speed of mixer, scraping bowl occasionally. Stir in enough remaining flour to make a soft dough. Knead on lightly floured surface until smooth and elastic, about 8 to 10 minutes. Cover and let rest 10 minutes. Roll dough to a 12-inch circle; place on a greased pizza pan. Form a standing rim on crust. Prick dough with fork, randomly. Let rest 10 minutes. Prebake at 450\u00b0 for 5 minutes.
