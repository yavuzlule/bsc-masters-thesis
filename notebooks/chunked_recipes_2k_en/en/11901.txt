Melt one stick butter and stir in all other ingredients except the marshmallows. Pour into a greased and floured 8 x 8 inch pan. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 25 minutes. After baking, cover the brownies with miniature marshmallows. Put back into oven long enough to melt the marshmallows, but do not let them brown. Cover with icing.
