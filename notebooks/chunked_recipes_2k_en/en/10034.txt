Drain pineapple, reserving 3 tablespoons syrup. Combine pineapple, chicken, onion, parsley, curry, lemon juice, salt and pepper. Combine yogurt with pineapple syrup. Mix 1/3 cup yogurt mixture into chicken mixture. Toss lightly. Cut avocados into halves and remove seed. , (They may be peeled or not, as desired. ) Spoon chicken into avocado halves.
