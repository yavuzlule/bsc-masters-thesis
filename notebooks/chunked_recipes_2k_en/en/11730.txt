Brown meat and onions. Add your soups, milk, enchilada sauce and chopped green peppers to meat and simmer for about 10 minutes. In a greased (Pam spray) pan, add ingredients as below: Layer of meat mixture, layer of crumbled Doritos and layer of grated cheese, repeating layers until all material is used. Bake at 350\u00b0 in oven for about 30 minutes or until cheese is melted. All or parts of the casserole can be frozen.
