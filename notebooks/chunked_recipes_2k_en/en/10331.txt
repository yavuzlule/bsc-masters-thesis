Mix and sift dry ingredients. Cut in shortening with 2 knives. Add beaten eggs. Stir in raisins and seed. Add milk; stir. Spread into a 9 x 13-inch greased and floured pan (or use cooking oil spray). Bake for 45 to 50 minutes at 375\u00b0. Can also use 3 small 6-inch round pans and bake for 35 minutes.
