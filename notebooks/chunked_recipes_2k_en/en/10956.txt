Cream butter with sugar; beat in eggs, then water. In separate bowl sift flour, cocoa, baking soda, cinnamon and nutmeg. Beat into creamed ingredients along with vanilla, chocolate morsels and apples. Turn into a greased and floured tube pan. Bake at 325\u00b0 for 1 1/4 hours. Cool thoroughly before slicing. May be topped with whipped cream.
