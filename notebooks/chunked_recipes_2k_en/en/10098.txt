Heat oven to 350\u00b0. Grease loaf pan, 9 x 5 x 3-inch. Cut shortening into flour, sugar, baking powder, salt, soda and orange peel. Add water to juice to make 3/4 cup. Blend in with cheese and egg. Fold in cranberries and nuts. Bake in prepared pan 60 to 70 minutes. Let loaf stand 8 hours before slicing.
