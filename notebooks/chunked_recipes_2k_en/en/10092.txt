Pour 1 gallon boiling water over the cucumbers. Add the salt. Let stand for 1 week, stirring each day. Drain water off. Add 1 gallon boiling water and the alum. Let stand 24 hours. Drain. Make a syrup of the sugar, vinegar and pickling spices. Bring to boil; pour over pickles. Drain and heat syrup for 3 mornings. Next morning:. Heat syrup and can or keep in a refrigerator in jar.
