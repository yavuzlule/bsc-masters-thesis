Dredge steak in flour; pound to 1/2-inch thickness. Cut steak into serving size pieces. Brown steaks in hot oil in a Dutch oven. Remove from skillet and set aside. Add onion to pan drippings; saute until tender. Return steak to Dutch oven; add remaining ingredients except noodles. Cover. Reduce heat and simmer 1 1/2 to 2 hours or until steak is tender. Serve over noodles.
