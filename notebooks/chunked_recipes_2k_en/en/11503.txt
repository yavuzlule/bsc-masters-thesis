Pound cutlets until they reach 3 x 3-inch size. Dip meat into egg and milk mixture, then dip into 3/4 cup dry seasoned bread crumbs. Sprinkle freely with Parmesan cheese; allow to stand 15 minutes. Saute in butter or oil until crisp (2 to 4 minutes, each side). Place in baking sheet, top with your favorite spaghetti sauce and Mozzarella cheese. Place in preheated oven until cheese melts. Serves 4 to 5.
