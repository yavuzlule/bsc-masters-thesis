Pierce hole in broccoli package and cook 3 minutes in microwave oven. In a mixing bowl, mix soup, sour cream, lemon juice, garlic powder and soy sauce. Set aside. Put rice and broccoli in greased 2-quart casserole dish and add half of the soup mixture. Add chicken and remaining soup mixture. Top with Parmesan cheese and bread crumbs. Cover with plastic wrap. Cook 8 minutes. Turn dish 1/2 turn and cook 6 to 8 minutes. Serves 6.
