In a glass pan with handles, stir together the peanuts, sugar, syrup and salt. Cook 8 minutes on High, stirring after 4 minutes. Add butter and vanilla. Cook one minute longer on High. Add baking soda and quickly stir until light and fluffy (foamy). Immediately pour on lightly buttered baking sheet. Spread out thin. When cool, break into pieces. Store in airtight container.
