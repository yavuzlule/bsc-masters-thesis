Bone chicken and discard skin. Mince the chicken and combine with 1 teaspoon cornstarch and the egg whites, beaten until frothy. Bring chicken broth to a boil. Dissolve remaining cornstarch in water and add gradually to broth. Add chicken mixture, corn, sherry and soy sauce and simmer a few minutes. Serve garnished with ham (boiled, baked or most flavorful and closest to the Chinese. Smithfield ham). Serves 4.
