Mix flour and sugar. Pour in milk and stir. Add butter. Cook on low heat. Beat egg yolks. Pour in mixture. Stir while cooking and cook until almost done. Add vanilla and coconut to mixture. Remove from heat. Pour slightly thickened mixture into baked pie crust. Top with beaten egg whites and sugar. Sprinkle coconut on top. Bake for 10 minutes at 450\u00b0.
