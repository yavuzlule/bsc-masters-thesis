Cook zucchini in boiling salt water for 5 minutes. Cook beef and garlic, stir in spices and sauce. Mix Ricotta, egg and parsley. Place 1/2 zucchini in greased 8-inch square baking pan. Sprinkle with bread crumbs. Spread 1/2 of Ricotta, 1/2 of beef mixture and 1/2 of Mozzarella. Repeat. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 25 to 30 minutes. Yields 5 to 6 servings.
