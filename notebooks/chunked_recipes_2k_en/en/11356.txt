Dust chicken with flour, salt, pepper and paprika. Brown in butter. Grease pan with butter, large enough to lay chicken in single layer. Put celery on bottom of pan, then browned chicken. Mix soup, cream, onions, pimentos and cheese. Pour on top of chicken. Sprinkle bread crumbs, almonds and a little grated cheese on top. Bake 1 hour at 350\u00b0 or until chicken is done. Serve with rice.
