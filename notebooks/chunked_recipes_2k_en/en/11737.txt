Dissolve yeast in lukewarm water; let stand 5 to 10 minutes. Combine sugar, flour and salt; cut in shortening. Pour 1 cup buttermilk on top of flour mixture; add yeast to buttermilk and mix slightly. Add other cup of buttermilk and mix well. Knead on floured board. Store in covered container in refrigerator. Will keep 1 week. Take amount needed; roll or cut biscuits. Dip in melted butter. Biscuits are lighter if dough is allowed to double in bulk before baking. Bake at 400\u00b0 for 15 to 20 minutes.
