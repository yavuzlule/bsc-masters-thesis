In a large bowl, combine pancake mix, sugar, cinnamon, ginger, cloves, pumpkin, oil, milk and eggs. Beat 3 minutes at medium speed. Stir in walnuts and turn batter into greased 9 x 5-inch loaf pan. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 45 to 55 minutes or until toothpick inserted in center comes out clean. Remove from pan and cool. Makes 1 loaf.
