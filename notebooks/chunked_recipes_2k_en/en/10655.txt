Sift together in large mixing bowl sugar and flour. Place in saucepan and bring to a boil butter or oleo, cocoa and water. Pour butter mixture over flour and sugar; mix well and add buttermilk, eggs, vanilla, baking soda and cinnamon. Mix and bake on a slightly greased 10 x 15-inch pan at 400\u00b0 for 20 to 25 minutes. Before cake is done, make icing.
