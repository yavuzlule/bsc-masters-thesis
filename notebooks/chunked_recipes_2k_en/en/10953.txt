Mix all ingredients until dough forms, about 20 strokes. Turn into a floured board. Shape into a ball and knead 10 times. Roll to 1/2-inch thickness. Cut with 2 1/2-inch cutter. Bake on a greased cookie sheet at 450\u00b0 for 8 to 10 minutes. Spread with Vanilla Glaze while warm (see Miscellaneous for Vanilla Glaze).
