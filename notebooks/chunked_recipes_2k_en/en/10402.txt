Heat oil in large pot over medium heat. Add onion, green pepper and garlic; saute 4 minutes or until onion is softened. Add carrot; cover and cook 2 minutes. Add zucchini, summer squash, chili powder, oregano and cumin. Saute 1 minute. Add tomatoes breaking up with wooden spoon the beer and salt. Bring to boiling. Lower heat; simmer, partially covered 15 minutes. Stir in all the beans. Simmer, stirring, until heated through.
