Bake cake according to package directions in 3 layers. In the bottom of the punch bowl, put the strawberry pie filling. Add a layer of cake. Mix pudding according to package directions and add drained pineapple. Spread on top of cake layer, then add second cake layer. Top this layer with cherry pie filling and add third layer of cake. Mix second box of pudding as directed and add 3 bananas, sliced. Spread on top of third cake layer. Top with Cool Whip. Garnish with pecans and cherries.
