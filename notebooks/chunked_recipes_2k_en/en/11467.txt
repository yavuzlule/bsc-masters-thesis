Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. Spray Pam on a 10-inch tube pan. Have all ingredients at room temperature. Cream together the butter, shortening and sugar. Add extracts. Beat in eggs, one at a time, beating well after each. Sift flour with baking powder and salt; add to egg mixture alternately with milk. Blend well after each addition. Pour batter into prepared pan. Bake for 1 hour at 350\u00b0. Cool 15 minutes before turning gently onto plate.
