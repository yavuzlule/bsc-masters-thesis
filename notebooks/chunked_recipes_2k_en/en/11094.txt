Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. Bring water to boil. Mix brownie mix, water, oil and egg in large bowl. Pour into greased and floured 12-inch pizza pan. Bake for 25 minutes. Beat cream cheese, sugar, remaining egg and vanilla in small bowl at medium speed until well blended. Pour over brownies. Bake for 15 minutes. Cool. Top with fruit; drizzle with chocolate.
