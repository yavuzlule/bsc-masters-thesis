Heat oven to 350\u00b0. In medium saucepan, melt margarine; blend in flour. Stir in milk, salt and chicken broth. Cook until thickened, stirring constantly. Grease 13 x 9-inch pan. Mix chicken and noodles with sauce. Pour in pan. Sprinkle with bread crumbs and cheese. Bake, uncovered, for 45 minutes to 1 hour, until top is lightly browned.
