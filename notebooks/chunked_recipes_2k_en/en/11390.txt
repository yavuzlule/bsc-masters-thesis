In 10-inch skillet, over medium heat in hot oil, cook cauliflowerets, beans, mushrooms and carrots with garlic, curry and cumin until vegetables are tender-crisp, stirring often. In small bowl, stir together vegetable juice, cornstarch and pepper; gradually stir into skillet. Cook until mixture boils and thickens, stirring constantly. Sprinkle with almonds. Makes 4 cups or 8 servings.
