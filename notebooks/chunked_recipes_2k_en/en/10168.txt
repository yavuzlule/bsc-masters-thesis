Combine butterscotch chips and peanut butter in top of double boiler. Place over hot, not boiling water, until butterscotch melts; remove from water. Stir until blended; add milk and stir until blended. Spread in foil-lined 8 inch square pan. Press chopped nuts into surface, if desired, and chill until firm. Cut into 1-inch squares. Yield:, 64 servings.
