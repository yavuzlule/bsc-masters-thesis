Mix flour, salt and sugar in a large bowl. Add shortening and mix until crumbly. In small bowl, beat together water, vinegar and egg. Combine the 2 mixtures until all ingredients are moistened. Divide dough into 5 portions and shape into flat, round patties ready for rolling. Wrap each patty in plastic wrap and chill at least 1/2 hour. When ready to roll, lightly flour both sides of patty and roll on lightly floured surface. For shells, bake at 450\u00b0. Will keep 3 days in refrigerator. Makes 2 double and 1 single crust or 20 tart shells.
