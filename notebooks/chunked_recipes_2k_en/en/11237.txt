Separate rolls and spread each with butter. Sprinkle mixture of sugar and cinnamon on rolls. Roll up as directed on can. Arrange in a circle on a lightly greased cookie sheet. Arrange remaining rolls on top. Bake 15 minutes at 375\u00b0. , (Cover tips with foil so as to brown evenly. ). Spoon jelly, preserves or relish in center of rolls, letting some run between rolls. Combine confectioners sugar and milk; drizzle mixture over the rolls. Serve warm.
