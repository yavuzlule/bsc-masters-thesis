Heat a wok or large pot until hot. Add a small amount of oil to coat bottom. When oil is hot, add cabbage, onion and celery and stir-fry until it is about half done. Add sausage rings and continue stirring over high heat until sausage is heated through. Add V-8 juice, beef broth, soy sauce and pepper. Cook until heated. Thicken with cornstarch and a little of the reserved beef broth. Heat through. Add sesame oil just before serving. Serve over hot, fluffy rice.
