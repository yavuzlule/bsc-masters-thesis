Heat strawberries, sugar and Jell-O just enough to dissolve Jell-O; stirring constantly. Let cool. Mix pudding with milk as stated on package. Add sour cream to pudding. Fold in 3/4 of Cool Whip. Layer cake, pudding mix and strawberries in a large dish, repeating until all is used. Top with remaining Cool Whip. Refrigerate for several hours.
