Peel onions; cut into even slices. Heat butter in large skillet. Add onions and toss for a few minutes until coated with butter. Do not brown. Add vinegar, brown sugar and curry powder. Stir to blend. Add parsley. Cook onions for about 5 minutes, covered, until almost tender-crisp. Remove cover and cook onions for 2 to 3 minutes, until glazed. Season with salt, black pepper and paprika. Add chili.
