Microwave chocolate and milk in 1 1/2-quart microwavable bowl on High 2 minutes, stirring after 1 minute. Stir until chocolate is completely melted and smooth. Stir in vanilla, salt and nuts. Spread in greased 8 x 4-inch or 9 x 5-inch loaf pan. Refrigerate until firm, about 2 hours. Makes about 2 dozen pieces.
