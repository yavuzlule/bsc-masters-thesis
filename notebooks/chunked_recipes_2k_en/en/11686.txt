In a bowl, beat eggs and sugar until light and fluffy. In a double boiler, melt chocolate and butter over hot water. Add egg mixture to the chocolate mixture. Combine flour, baking powder and nuts in a bowl. Add to chocolate batter and stir until smooth. Put in a greased 8-inch square pan. Bake at 350\u00b0 for about 30 minutes. Let cool and then cut into squares.
