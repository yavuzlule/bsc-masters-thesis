Drain liquid from one jar of artichokes into pan. Chop artichokes and set aside. Add onion to liquid in pan and saute. (Add a little garlic salt. ). Beat eggs; add bread crumbs and seasonings. Stir in cheese, parsley, artichokes and onion. Put in greased 9 x 9-inch pan. Bake at 325\u00b0 for 30 minutes. Cut into 1-inch squares and serve hot or cold.
