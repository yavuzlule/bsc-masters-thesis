Blend syrup ingredients (except soda) and boil slowly for 5 minutes. Take off heat. Add soda. Stir until blended. Pour over popped corn and mix. Bake in same pan. Stir until kernels are coated. Place in 200\u00b0 to 225\u00b0 oven for 1 hour. After baked, pour on counter to cool. Kernels will be separate.
