Cook the noodles according to package directions and drain. Brown meat with salt, sugar and pepper in a skillet. Add tomatoes, tomato sauce and garlic and cook for 10 minutes over low heat. Add the noodles, sour cream, cream cheese and green onions. Place in a casserole dish and cover with grated cheese. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 35 minutes. Serves 8 people.
