Cook chicken in seasoned water until tender; reserve broth. Debone. Cook potatoes, celery and carrots in broth until tender. Reserve 3 cups broth. Melt butter and flour; stir a few minutes. Add milk, 3 cups chicken broth, salt and pepper. Mix everything together, including peas. Pour in 13 x 9 x 2-inch baking dish. Shape crusts to fit and put them on top; make a few slits. Bake at 400\u00b0 for 45 minutes or until golden brown.
