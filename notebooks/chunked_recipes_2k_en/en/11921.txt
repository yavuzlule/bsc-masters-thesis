Bring 6 cups water to boil. Add salt and macaroni; cook until tender. Grate cheese coarsely, then put in large baking dish, layering alternately with cooked macaroni. Beat eggs in milk; pour over this mixture. Take fork; mix well, down through pie. Bake at 400\u00b0 or 450\u00b0 until brown, about 20 minutes. Black pepper is optional.
