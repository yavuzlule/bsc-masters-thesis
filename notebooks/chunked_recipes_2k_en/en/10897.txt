Have eggs, butter and milk at room temperature. Combine sugar, a cup at a time, to the softened butter until light and fluffy. Add eggs, one at a time, beating well after each. Sift flour four times and add to creamed mixture, 1/2 cup at a time, beating well after each. Stir in milk and flavorings. Pour in well-greased and floured tube pan. Bake at 300\u00b0 for 1 hour and 40 minutes.
