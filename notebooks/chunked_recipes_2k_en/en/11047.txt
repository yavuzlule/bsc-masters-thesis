Combine all ingredients in large mixer bowl. Blend well, then beat at medium speed of electric mixer for 4 minutes. Pour into greased and floured 8 x 4 inch loaf pans. Bake at 350 degrees for 50 to 55 minutes or until cake tester inserted in center comes out clean and cake begins to pull away from sides of pan. Do not underbake. Cool in pan 15 minutes. Remove from pan and finish cooling on racks.
