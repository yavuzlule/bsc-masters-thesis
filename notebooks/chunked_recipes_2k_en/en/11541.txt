Wash and scrub potatoes. Cut slits in tops. Place each potato in a piece of aluminum foil, wrapping securely. Cook 6-inches from heat, tops down, for 20 minutes. Turn and pierce foil tops with a fork. Cook for 20 to 25 minutes or longer, until done. Cut foil open. Combine cloves and butter. Frizzle over tops. Let stand for 5 minutes before serving. Serves 6.
