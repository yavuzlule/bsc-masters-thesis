Saute onion and garlic in small amount of oil until golden brown. Stir in tomatoes and paste; simmer 30 minutes, stirring often. Stir in oregano and simmer 30 minutes more. Mix eggs and salt. Dip eggplant in eggs; then in bread crumbs. Fry in oil; drain on paper towels. Mix cheeses together. Pour 1 cup mixture in 13 x 9-inch baking dish. Top with 1/3 eggplant and 1/3 cheeses. Repeat until used up. Bake in a 350\u00b0 oven for 45 minutes.
