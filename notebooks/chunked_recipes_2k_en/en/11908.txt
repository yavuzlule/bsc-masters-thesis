Heat oven to 350\u00b0. Mix brown sugar, margarine, vanilla and eggs in a large bowl until well blended. Stir in remaining ingredients. Drop dough by rounded tablespoonfuls about 3-inches apart onto lightly greased cookie sheet. Press 3 or 4, additional candies in each cookie, if desired. Bake until light brown, 10 to 12 minutes. Cool slightly. Remove to wire rack. Makes about 2 1/2 dozen cookies.
