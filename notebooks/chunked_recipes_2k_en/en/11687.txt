Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. In a medium bowl combine 1 1/2 cups flour and 1/2 cup confectioners sugar. Cut in margarine until crumbly. Press into bottom of lightly greased 13 x 9-inch baking pan. Bake 15 minutes. Meanwhile in a large bowl combine eggs, granulated sugar, baking powder. ReaLemon and remaining 3 tablespoons flour. Mix well. Pour over baked crust for 20 to 25 minutes, until lightly browned. Cool. Cut into bars. Sprinkle with additional confectioners sugar. Store, covered, in refrigerator.
