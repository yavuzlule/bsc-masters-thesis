Brown meat slowly. Spoon off excess fat. Add next 4 ingredients. Cover and simmer 40 minutes, stirring occasionally. Salt to taste. Cook noodles in salted water until tender; drain. Rinse in cold water. Place half of the noodles in large baking dish. Cover with 1/3 of the sauce. Add half of the Mozzarella cheese, then half of the cottage cheese. Repeat layers, ending with sauce. Top with Parmesan cheese. Bake in a 350\u00b0 oven for 25 to 30 minutes. Let stand 15 minutes. Cut into squares.
