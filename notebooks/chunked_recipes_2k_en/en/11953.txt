Prepare Duncan Hines angel food cake mix according to directions on box. Let cool. Break into bite size pieces. Line 13 x 9-inch pan with pieces. Prepare 1 package chocolate pudding mix according to directions. As it begins to boil, stir in 1 package chocolate bits. Cool slightly. Fold in 1 carton Cool Whip. Pour over angel food cake bits. Ice with 1 carton Cool Whip. Put pecan bits on top.
