Wash eggplant and cut into 1-inch slices. Sprinkle with salt and allow to sit for 1/2 to 1 hour in colander. Squeeze water out of slices and pat dry with toweling. Dip slices in seasoned flour and brown in frying pan. Place in greased 9-inch square baking dish. Top each piece with a slice of Mozzarella cheese. Place a second slice of eggplant on cheese. Pour Ragu spaghetti sauce over the top. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 45 to 60 minutes, covered for first 30 minutes.
