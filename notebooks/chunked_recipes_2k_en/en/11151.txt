Cook bacon until crisp. Drain bacon, reserving 1/4 cup drippings. Crumble bacon; reserve 2 tablespoons. Microwave beans as directed on package; drain well. Add mushrooms and onion to reserved 1/4 cup drippings in skillet. Cook and stir on medium-high heat 10 minutes; drain. Add bacon, hot cooked green beans and pepper; mix lightly. Spoon into 2-quart serving dish. Microwave process cheese spread as directed on label. Pour over green bean mixture. Sprinkle with reserved 2 tablespoons crumbled bacon. Makes 10 servings.
