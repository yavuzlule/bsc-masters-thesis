Preheat, oven, to, 325\u00b0. Grease, and, flour, tube pan. Blend together, oleo, and cream cheese; add sugar, blend until fluffy. Add, eggs, one at a time, blending well. Add vanilla. Blend in flour, and, salt. Pour batter into prepared pan and bake 1 hour and 15 minutes. Cool and dust with powdered sugar if desired.
