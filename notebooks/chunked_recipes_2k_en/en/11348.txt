Pick over beans; wash. Cover with 3 cups water and 1 tablespoon baking soda. Soak overnight or 8 hours; then add 2 cups water, mustard and next 6 ingredients. Cover and boil 20 minutes. Heat oven to 250\u00b0. Cut salt pork into small pieces and place in a bean pot or casserole dish. Cover with hot beans and their liquid. Sprinkle pepper on top. Cover and bake in a slow 250\u00b0 oven or until tender. Add 3/4 water when 2/3 done, if necessary.
