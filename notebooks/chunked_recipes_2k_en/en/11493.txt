Grease muffin tin very thoroughly. Sift flour and salt together. Separate, eggs, and, beat, yolks, until, thick, and creamy. Whip the, cream, until, it, holds its shape. Now add flour and whipped cream to the egg yolks alternately beginning and ending with the flour. Whip egg whites until stiff. Dump egg batter on, top of whites and mix with your hands until all the white patches have disappeared. Work quickly. Spoon into muffin tin, filling half full. Bake in preheated oven at 375\u00b0 for 20 to 25 minutes.
