Cook macaroni until tender. Put aside. In a saucepan, melt the butter. Saute the onion and pepper in butter until tender. Set aside. In a bowl, place the tuna fish, onion, pepper, butter and macaroni. Add the mushroom soup; mix well. Add grated cheese. Add finally the sugar and pepper. Mix well. Pour milk over top and mix slightly. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 30 minutes.
