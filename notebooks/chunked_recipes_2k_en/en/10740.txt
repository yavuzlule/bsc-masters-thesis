Cook noodles as directed; rinse and drain. Brown meat; add salt, pepper and garlic salt, then tomato sauce. Simmer 5 minutes; combine cheese, sour cream, onions and noodles. In a 2-quart casserole, alternate layers of noodle mixture and meat, beginning with noodles and ending with meat. Top with Cheddar cheese. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 25 minutes.
