Cream shortening. Gradually add sugar, beating at low speed of electric mixer until light and fluffy. Add egg, chocolate and vanilla; beat well. Combine flour, soda and salt; add to creamed mixture alternately with milk, stirring well. Stir in pecans or walnuts. Drop dough by teaspoonfuls, about 1 1/2 inches apart, onto greased cookie sheets. Bake in a 350\u00b0 oven for 12 minutes, or until done. Remove to wire racks. Frost with Chocolate-Coffee Frosting while still warm. Yields about 4 dozen cookies.
