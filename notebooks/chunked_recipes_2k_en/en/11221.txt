Combine all ingredients except cream and allow to marinate for 1 to 2 hours. Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. Bake, basting occasionally, about 30 minutes or until chicken is done. Remove baking dish from the oven and place chicken on a warm platter. Skim off fat from the cooking juices and set the baking dish over medium heat. Add cream. Simmer 5 to 10 minutes. Spoon sauce over chicken and serve. Serves 4 to 6.
