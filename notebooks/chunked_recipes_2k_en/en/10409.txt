Heat oil in frying pan. Dredge chicken pieces in flour and lightly brown in oil. Place in casserole with mushrooms and artichoke hearts. Stir in garlic and spices (with tomatoes). Pour over chicken. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 1 to 1 1/2 hours or until tender, adding sherry during last few minutes of cooking time. Makes 4 servings.
