Lightly oil a 9 x 13-inch baking pan. Stir together sugar, oil, margarine and eggs. Mix in nuts and vanilla. Stir together flour, baking soda and salt; add to sugar mixture. Pour into prepared pan. Bake in 350\u00b0 oven about 25 to 35 minutes (until browned and knife comes out clean). Cut into 32 pieces.
