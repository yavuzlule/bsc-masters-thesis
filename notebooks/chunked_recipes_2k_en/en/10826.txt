Cut fish in 1-inch cubes. Melt butter in large saucepan. Add onion, carrot and celery. Cook until onion is transparent. Blend in flour, salt and paprika. Cook 1 minute, stirring constantly. Gradually add chicken broth and milk. Cook, stirring constantly, until thickened. Add fish; simmer until fish flakes easily (5 minutes for fresh, 10 for frozen). Add cheese; stir until melted. Serve hot. Yields 2 1/2 quarts.
