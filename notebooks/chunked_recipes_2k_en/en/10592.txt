Place dry ingredients (except raisins) in large, deep bowl. Gradually add milk and margarine. Mix until firm enough to form ball; if still crumbly, add more butter or milk, one tablespoon at a time. Pat down to an 8-inch circle. Place on oiled cookie sheet. Cut the dough in the form of a cross to the depth of 1-inch. Bake at 375\u00b0 for 30 to 40 minutes or until brown.
