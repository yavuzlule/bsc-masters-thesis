Mix butter, sugar and eggs. Make a paste of red coloring and cocoa. Add to creamed mixture. Add buttermilk, alternating with sifted flour and salt. Add vanilla. In small cup, mix soda and vinegar, then add to rest of mixture, blending instead of beating. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes at 350\u00b0 in 2 (8-inch) greased and floured pans.
