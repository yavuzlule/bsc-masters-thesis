Wash and clean peppers. Parboil peppers for 5 minutes. Let cool and drain. Combine rice, meat, salt, pepper and butter; mix well. Stuff peppers; place peppers in a pan, uncovered, with a little water in the bottom. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 1 hour (check after 45 minutes). Heat can of tomato sauce and serve with peppers.
