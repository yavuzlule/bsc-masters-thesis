Melt butter in a 13 x 9 x 2-inch pan in oven. Remove and add seasoned salt and the Worcestershire sauce. Mix the cereal, pretzels and nuts in a large pan (oven broiler pan) and pour the butter and seasoning over it. Stir until all the cereal and pretzels are covered. Bake 1 hour at 250\u00b0, stirring every 15 minutes.
