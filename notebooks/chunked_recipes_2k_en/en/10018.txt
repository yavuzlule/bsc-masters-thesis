Mix brown sugar and corn syrup with melted butter and spread evenly in bottom of ungreased 8 or 9-inch round pan. Sprinkle with chopped nuts. Remove dough from can; do not unroll, separate. In shallow dish, mix sugar and cinnamon. Arrange coils, sugared-side down, over nuts in pan; sprinkle with remaining mix. Bake at 375\u00b0 for 19 to 24 minutes. Cool and invert. Serve warm.
