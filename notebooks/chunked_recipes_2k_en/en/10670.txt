In large bowl, combine all ingredients, except 1/2 cup Colby cheese. Mix well and turn into buttered 13 x 9-inch baking dish. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 55 to 60 minutes or until potatoes are tender. Top with remaining cheese and bake 3 to 5 minutes or until cheese melts. Let stand 5 minutes. Makes 6 to 8 servings.
