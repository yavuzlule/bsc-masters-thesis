Drain pineapple. Reserve juice and make up to one cup with water. Heat to boiling; dissolve jello in hot liquid. Gently stir in ginger ale; chill until partially set. Mix pineapple and the cranberry sauce; fold into jello. Turn into 9 x 9 x 2-inch dish and chill until firm. Mix cream cheese and whipped topping; spread over jello and sprinkle nuts on top.
