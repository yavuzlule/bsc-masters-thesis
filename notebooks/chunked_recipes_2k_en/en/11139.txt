Cream shortening in bowl. Add sugar, molasses and egg. Mix thoroughly. Sift remaining ingredients together. Stir into creamed mixture. Roll into small balls; dip in additional sugar. Place 2-inches apart on greased cookie sheet. Bake at 375\u00b0 (preheated) for 10 minutes. They will still be soft when you take them out. They firm as they cool. Makes about 5 dozen.
