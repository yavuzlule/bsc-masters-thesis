Cream shortening and margarine in bowl. Add mashed potatoes, potato water, sweetener and egg; beat well. Sprinkle with yeast. Stir in gently. Let stand, for 1 1/2 hours in warm place. Stir in flour gradually. Knead on floured surface until smooth. Let rise, covered, in warm place 5 to 6 hours or doubled in bulk. Roll out on floured surface 1/2-inch thick. Cut to desired size. Place on floured towel. Let rise in warm place 1 1/2 to 2 inches or until doubled in bulk. Deep fry in 375\u00b0, oil until light brown. Drain on paper towels. Yields 50 doughnuts.
