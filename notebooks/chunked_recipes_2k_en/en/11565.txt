In a 2-quart microwave-safe casserole, crumble meat. Add the green pepper. Cook in microwave, covered, on High for 2 to 3 minutes until no pink remains in meat, stirring once or twice. Drain off the fat. Stir in nacho cheese soup, milk, corn, chopped tomato and minced onion. Cook, covered, on High for 7 to 10 minutes or until chowder is heated through, stirring once. Makes 3 main dish servings. Serve with corn chips.
