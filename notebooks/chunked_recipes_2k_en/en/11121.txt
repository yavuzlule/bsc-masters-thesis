In a 10-inch skillet, brown meat with onion; drain off fat. Stir in gravy and 1/2 cup water. Add 1/2 teaspoon salt and frozen vegetables, breaking apart with fork. Bring to boiling. Cover; reduce heat. Simmer 10 minutes. Top with biscuits; simmer, covered, 15 minutes. Sprinkle biscuits with cheese; heat until cheese melts. Sprinkle with paprika, if desired. Serves 4.
