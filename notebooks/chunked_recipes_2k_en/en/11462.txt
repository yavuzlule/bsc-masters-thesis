Cut beef into desired sizes. Marinate for an hour or two with: garlic, vinegar, soy sauce, peppercorn or powder, salt to taste. Pirims sauce (squeeze of liquid from meat and fry with just enough oil until brown). Then add a little water (enough to cook), sliced onion, pickles, olives, tomato sauce or paste, bay leaf, red pepper (sliced), the liquid off the meat and a little sugar. Cook until tender (pressure cooker only 15 minutes). This is a Philippine food.
