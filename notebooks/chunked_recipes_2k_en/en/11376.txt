In medium bowl, combine oats, sugar and margarine; mix well. Spread in greased 9-inch square pan. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 13 minutes or until light golden brown; cool. In small, heavy saucepan over low heat, melt chips. Spread over oat mixture; sprinkle with nuts. Refrigerate until chocolate is set. Cut into bars and store in tightly covered container in refrigerator. Makes 2 dozen bars.
