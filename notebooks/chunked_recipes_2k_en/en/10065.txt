Melt butter in large, heavy skillet. Stir in sugar, dates and instant coffee. Remove from heat; blend in egg. Cook and stir over medium heat until mixture comes to a full boil. Boil for 1 minute, stirring constantly. Remove from heat; stir in cereal, 1 1/3 cups of the coconut and the nuts. Let stand 10 minutes. Shape into 1-inch balls. Roll in remaining coconut. Makes 5 dozen.
