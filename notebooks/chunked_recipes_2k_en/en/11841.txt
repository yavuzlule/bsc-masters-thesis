Heat oven to 350\u00b0. Cut up sausage into small pieces and cook. Melt cheese, add soup to cheese, stir until well blended. Cook broccoli. In a seperate bowl mix stuffing, sausage, celery and onions. Place cut up chicken in bottom of pan, then broccoli, then pour soup and cheese mixture on top. Top with stuffing mixture. Bake for 40 min. till cheese bubbles and stuffing browns.
