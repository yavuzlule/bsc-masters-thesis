Line cookie sheet with sides with foil. Arrange separated crackers on foil, side by side. Sprinkle nuts over top of graham crackers. Bring butter and brown sugar to a boil in small saucepan and pour over crackers evenly. Bake 10 to 12 minutes in a 350\u00b0 oven until bubbly. Remove from oven and let set a couple of minutes. Cut apart with knife while still warm and cool on wax paper. Makes about 3 dozen 1 1/2 x 3-inch cookies.
