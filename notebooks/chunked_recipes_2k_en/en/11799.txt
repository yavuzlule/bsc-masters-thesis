Saute onion slightly in Pam-sprayed pan. Add flour, mustard, salt and pepper. Gradually add milk and broth, stirring constantly. Cook until thickened. Remove from heat and stir in sour cream. Combine with potatoes and top with cheese and corn flakes, if desired. Bake in 13 x 9-inch Pam-sprayed pan at 350\u00b0 for 35 minutes.
