Boil giblets in salted water with bay leaf and beef bouillon cubes. Brown ground beef and drain. Saute green pepper, onion and celery in saucepan with a little butter. Chop cooked giblets after they cook and cool a little. Add giblets, ground beef and sauteed vegetables to rice. Add a teaspoon of poultry seasoning and 1/2 teaspoon sage to rice mixture. Cover and cook until rice is done. Serve with baked chicken.
