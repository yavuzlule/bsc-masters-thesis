Crush Oreo cookies and mix with melted margarine. Press into 9-inch pie plate and freeze (2 to 3 hours). Put ice cream in a bowl and stir until smooth. Place in prepared crust and sprinkle with pecans. Freeze for at least 1 hour (more time in freezer may be needed depending on how much the ice cream has melted). When ready to serve, slice pie. Top each slice with hot fudge followed by whipped cream.
