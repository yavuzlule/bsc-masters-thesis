Cut elephant into bite size pieces. This should take about 2 months. Add brown gravy and simmer. Add salt and pepper to taste. Heat whale oil to brown mushrooms, celery and onions. Mix all ingredients and cook over kerosene fire for 4 weeks at 465\u00b0. Yield:, 3,800 or more servings. If more guests are expected, 2 rabbits may be added, but do this only if necessary, as most people do not like to find hare in their stew.
