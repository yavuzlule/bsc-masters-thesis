Heat beans and water to boiling; reduce heat. Boil uncovered for 2 minutes; remove from heat. Cover and let stand 1 hour, or cover beans with water and let soak overnight. Drain and add 10 cups fresh water. Add salt meat to beans and water. Heat to boiling; reduce heat. Cover and simmer about 2 hours or until beans are tender. Stir in remaining ingredients. Cover and simmer 1 hour. Makes 12 servings; contains 1 gram fat per serving.
