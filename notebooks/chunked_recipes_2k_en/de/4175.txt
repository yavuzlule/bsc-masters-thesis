Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. Cream the butter, sugar, egg and vanilla. Mix in salt, flour and coconut. Stir well. Reserve 3/4 cup for topping. Spread the rest in a greased 9 x 11-inch pan. Spread apricot preserves over mixture and sprinkle nuts over preserves. Crumble reserved 3/4 cup dough over top. Bake 30 minutes. Cool and cut into 24 bars.
