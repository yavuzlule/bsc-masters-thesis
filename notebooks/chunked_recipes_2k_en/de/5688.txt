Cook noodles and drain. Cook onion in margarine in skillet until soft, then stir in soup, pimento and chili pepper. Grease 9 x 13-inch casserole dish. Layer 1/2 noodles, 1/2 the chicken and season with salt and pepper. Top with half the soup mixture and half the cheese. Repeat layers. Bake, uncovered, at 350\u00b0 for 45 minutes. Serves 8 to 12.
