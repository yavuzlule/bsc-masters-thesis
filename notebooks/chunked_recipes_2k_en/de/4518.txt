Combine first 6 ingredients in large shallow dish; stir with ladle. Add vegetables and chicken; toss to coat. Let marinate in refrigerator for several hours. Toss occasionally. Thread vegetables on 12 (6-inch) skewers alternating vegetables and chicken, weaving chicken strips in an accordion fashion. Broil about 6 inches from heat, about 3 minutes on each side. Baste chicken and vegetables thoroughly with marinade while broiling. Makes 6 servings (2 skewers each).
