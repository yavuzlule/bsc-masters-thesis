Mix 1 teaspoon milk, 1 teaspoon flavoring and 2 drops of food coloring in a small paper cup (bathroom size). Any flavoring can be used and any color food coloring can be used. Make 3 or 4 different colors for child to use. Give child 1 piece of bread and then let them paint silly faces on bread with a paint brush. Wipe paint brush on a paper towel before changing colors. Then toast bread as usual. This takes a little time; but our children are \worth it\. Butter toast and sprinkle on sugar or dip honey on top when toasted.
