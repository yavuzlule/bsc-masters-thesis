Place sugar in a bowl. Add oil and eggs; beat well. Sift flour, baking powder, cinnamon, soda and salt. Add flour mixture to egg mixture and blend. Add pecans and apples. Pour batter into greased and floured 9 x 13 x 2-inch pan. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 1 hour or until it tests done. Serve warm and top with ice cream or Cool Whip. Yields 15 servings.
