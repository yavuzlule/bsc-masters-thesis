Grease and flour 9 x 12-inch oblong pan. Sift together all dry ingredients into mixing bowl. Make well in center of dry ingredients; pour rest of ingredients into depression. Blend slowly (low speed on electric mixer), then beat at medium speed or with rotary egg beater for 4 minutes. Scrape down bowl. Fill pan with batter. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 45 minutes. Top with peanut butter frosting.
