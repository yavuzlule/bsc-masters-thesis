Rub the rib eye all over with pepper and place it in a shallow pan. Preheat the oven to 500\u00b0. When the oven is hot, put the roast in the oven and close the door. Do not open the door for 2 hours, then the roast is ready to serve. The roast should be cooked 5 minutes a pound at 500\u00b0, then the heat should be turned off. If the roast weighs 8 pounds, you cook the roast 40 minutes and turn the oven off. From the time you start the roast until it is ready to slice the time is 2 hours. Pour part of the fat out of the pan and add some water and salt for a delicious natural gravy. The roast will be pink in the middle every time. You may want to cook your roast for 6 minutes per pound if you do not like it quite as pink.
