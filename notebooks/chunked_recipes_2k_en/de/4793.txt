Heat oil in large pan. Add beef cubes, brown them on all sides, stirring every couple of minutes. Add 1 onion, chopped. Continue browning meat-onion mixture for a minute or so. Sprinkle flour on mix, stirring constantly, adding water slowly to prevent clumping of flour. Season with salt, pepper and paprika. Sauce should be spicy. Simmer slowly for 1 hour stirring occasionally. Serve over cooked noodles or rice with a salad. , (To ensure tender meat, cover pot during simmering. )
