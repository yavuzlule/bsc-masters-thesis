Mix dry pudding and pineapple with juice in bowl. Stir in rest of fruit and set aside. Combine Dream Whip mixed as box indicates or Cool Whip. Beat in cream cheese. Add sour cream. Mix well and add 2 packages sweetener. Mix together. Fold in fruit and pudding mixture. Sprinkle nuts on top. Chill in refrigerator, 4 to 5 hours, or overnight.
