Put all but blueberries in mixing bowl and beat with electric mixer until a smooth batter. Add and stir in berries. Then measure together 1 cup sugar, 1 teaspoon cinnamon and 1/2 cup flour and crumble in 1/2 cup soft butter to sprinkle on top. Pour into greased 13 x 9 x 2-inch pan or three smaller pans. Bake in 375\u00b0 oven for 45 to 50 minutes.
