Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. Combine flour, salt, eggs, sugar, strawberries, oil, soda, cinnamon and pecans in bowl; mix well. Pour into 2 greased 5 x 9-inch loaf pans. Bake for 1 hour. Remove to wire rack to cool. Cut into thin slices. Combine cream cheese, confectioners sugar and vanilla in bowl. Beat until smooth. Spread between slices of strawberry bread. Yield:, 30 servings.
