Combine flour, baking soda, salt and cinnamon in small bowl. Beat butter, sugars, eggs and vanilla in large mixer bowl until creamy. Gradually add in flour mixture. Stir in oats and morsels. Drop by teaspoonfuls onto ungreased baking sheet. Bake in preheated 375\u00b0 oven 7 to 8 minutes for chewy cookies or 9 to 10 minutes or crispy cookies. Let stand 2 minutes. Remove to wire rack to cool completely. Makes about 4 dozen cookies.
