Cook broccoli until tender and arrange it in a greased casserole. Cover with cubed chicken. Combine soup, mayonnaise, lemon juice and curry powder; pour over chicken and broccoli. Sprinkle with the shredded cheese. Combine butter and bread crumbs and sprinkle over all. Garnish with pimento. Bake 30 minutes at 350\u00b0. Serves 6 to 8.
