Combine dry ingredients; add to mashed sweet potatoes. Mix milk, eggs and add to potatoes. Stir until mixed well, but leave some lumps of potatoes. Pour filling into 2 pie shells; dot with margarine. Bake at 450\u00b0 for 10 minutes; reduce heat to 350\u00b0 and bake 35 minutes. Yields two 9-inch pies.
