Cut biscuits into fourths. Coat with mixture of sugar and cinnamon. Layer biscuits in well-greased Bundt pan. Combine melted margarine and brown sugar in bowl; mix well. Drizzle over biscuits. Sprinkle with pecans. Place in cold oven. Set temperature at 350\u00b0. Bake for 30 minutes. Let stand for 10 minutes. Invert into serving plate. Yield:, 30 servings.
