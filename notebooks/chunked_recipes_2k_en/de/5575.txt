Drain pineapple. Reserve juice. Combine juice, ketchup, vinegar, brown sugar, cornstarch and soy sauce. Blend well. Heat oil in wok or skillet. Add chicken and stir-fry 3 minutes. Remove chicken. Add pepper and onion. Stir-fry 3 minutes. Add chestnuts and juice mixture. Cook, stirring, until boils and thickens. Add chicken and pineapple. Heat through. Serve over chow mein noodles.
