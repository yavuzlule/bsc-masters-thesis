Grease a 9 x 13-inch pan. Preheat oven to 375\u00b0. Cook the noodles. While the water is coming to a boil and the noodles are cooking, blend 1 cup cottage cheese, egg yolks, sugar and vanilla. Beat the egg whites until they are stiff, but not too hard (you know, the soft peaks that sort of slump over when they get too high). By now, the noodles are about done.
