Cut or break up candy bars. Melt candy and oleo together in the microwave. Stir until smooth (small pieces may remain). Cool. Sift flour and soda together. In a mixing bowl, slowly add buttermilk to flour and beat well. Add eggs, one at a time, beating well after each. Add sugar, candy mixture and pecans. Beat well. Pour into well-greased Bundt pan. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 1 hour and 10 minutes or until toothpick comes out clean.
