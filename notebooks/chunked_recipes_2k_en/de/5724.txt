Put bread in bottom of 9 x 13 glass baking dish. Add sausage, then cheese. Mix remaining ingredients together and pour over bread and sausage. Cover and refrigerate overnight. Remove from refrigerator about 1/2 hour before baking. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 45 to 60 minutes. Serves 8 to 10. , (Can also add onion, red/green pepper and mushrooms. )
