Pour 2 cups cold milk into bowl. Add pudding mix. Beat with wire whisk until well blended, about 2 minutes. Let pudding stand 5 minutes. Stir whipped topping and 1/2 the crushed cookies into pudding with the rubber scraper until mixture is all the same color. Place about 1 tablespoon of the remaining crushed cookies into bottom of each paper or plastic cup. Add gummy worms to pudding mixture. Fill cups about 3/4 full with pudding mixture. Top each cup with the rest of the crushed cookies. Chill about an hour. Decorate the tops with plastic flowers!
