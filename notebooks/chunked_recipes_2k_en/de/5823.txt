Cook mushrooms, onion, green pepper and garlic in olive oil in a large, nonstick skillet over medium high heat, stirring constantly until tender. Remove from heat; drain. Combine cooked vegetables, drained kidney beans, olives and pepper. Spoon about 1/2 cup bean mixture evenly down center of each tortilla. Top with 1 tablespoon sour cream, 1 tablespoon salsa and 1 tablespoon cheese; fold opposite sides over filling. Coat a large nonstick skillet or griddle with cooking spray. Heat over medium-high heat until hot. Cook tortilla, seam side down, 1 minute on each side or until thoroughly heated. Top each tortilla with 1 tablespoon salsa.
