In a large bowl, combine the flour, sugar, baking soda, cinnamon and salt. In another bowl, beat eggs. Add carrots, oil, pineapple, nuts and vanilla. Stir into dry ingredients just until moistened. Spoon into 2 greased and floured loaf pans. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 65 to 70 minutes or until loaves test done. Cool 10 minutes in the pans before removing to wire racks. Cool completely.
