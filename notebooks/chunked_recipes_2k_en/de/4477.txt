Brown beef and onion and drain; add soups, milk, sauce and chilies to beef and onion. Bring to a boil; line baking dish (deep one), greased. I use a large baking dish, layer tortillas. Then meat sauce, cheese, repeat another layer. Top with grated cheese. Bake at 350\u00b0 for about 40 minutes. I spray Pam on foil and cover to keep the cheese from burning. Remove foil the last 15 minutes.
