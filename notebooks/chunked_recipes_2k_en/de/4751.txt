In a 10-inch skillet, cook mushrooms and shallots in butter, uncovered, over medium-high heat for 4 to 5 minutes, or until vegetables are tender, but not brown. Remove with slotted spoon; set aside. Stir wine, tomato paste, tarragon, salt and pepper into butter in skillet. Bring to boil; boil vigorously for 10 minutes, or until mixture is reduced to 1/2 cup.
