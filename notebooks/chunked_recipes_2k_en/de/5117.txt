Coat Dutch oven with cooking spray; place over medium heat until hot. Add onion, green pepper, jalapeno peppers and saute 5 minutes. Add chili powder, cumin and oregano; cook 2 minutes. Add chicken, 1 cup water, red pepper, black pepper. Worcestershire sauce, mustard, stewed tomatoes, chicken broth, and chili sauce and bring to a boil. Cover, reduce heat and simmer 20 minutes. Add beans and cook 5 minutes. Ladle chili into soup bowls; top each with avocado, onion and yogurt. Yield 9 cups.
