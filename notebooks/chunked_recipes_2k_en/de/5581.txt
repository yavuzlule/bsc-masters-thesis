Beat egg yolks until thick. Add 1/2 cup of the sugar, salt and juice. Cook until thick, beating constantly. Soften gelatin in cold water; add to egg yolk mixture and stir until dissolved. Add rind and cool. Beat egg whites until stiff, gradually beating in the remaining 1/2 cup sugar. Fold egg yolk mixture into stiffly beaten egg whites. Pour into baked pastry shell; chill until firm. Serve with whipped cream.
