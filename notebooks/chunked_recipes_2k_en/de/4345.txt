Cook sausage in a 12-inch skillet over medium heat for 2 minutes. Add onion; cook, stirring occasionally, until tender (about 3 minutes). Add rice; mix well. Stir in remaining ingredients except for green pepper. Bring to a boil, reduce heat, cover and simmer 20 minutes until liquid is absorbed. Stir in green pepper; let stand 5 minutes. Serve with additional picante sauce. Makes 6 to 8 servings.
