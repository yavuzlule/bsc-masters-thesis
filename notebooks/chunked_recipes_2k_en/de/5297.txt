Drain pineapple; reserve 1/4 cup juice. In a wok or skillet, heat oil over medium heat. Add chicken; cook 5 minutes, stirring occasionally. Season with salt and pepper, if desired. Stir in reserved juice and red pepper. Reduce heat uncovered 2 minutes. Add green beans, pineapple and sauce. Cover and cook until heated through. Serve over hot cooked rice. Makes 4 servings.
