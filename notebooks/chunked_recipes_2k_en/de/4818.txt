Mix cinnamon, 1/2 cup sugar and butter. Add crumbs; save 1/2 cup to sprinkle over top. Press remainder into a 9-inch spring-form pan, lining the bottom and side. Beat eggs with remaining 1 cup sugar. Add salt, lemon juice, cheese and flour. Beat thoroughly. Pour into pan. Add nuts and crumbs. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 1 hour.
