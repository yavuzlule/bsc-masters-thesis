Brown deer and onion in frying pan. Drain. While this is cooking, in a large pot, mix all ingredients except cheese and chips. Let this simmer for about 1/2 hour, then add meat and onion. Let this simmer for 1/2 hour more. Take 8 to 10 bowls, depending on how many are to be served. Put corn chips in bottom. Pour chili on top, then put a slice of cheese on. Serve. Leftover chili can be frozen, if desired.
