Take rhubarb and 1/2 cup sugar and let set until sugar is completely dissolved. Beat until creamy 1 1/2 cups sugar. Crisco and egg. Add remaining ingredients. Fold in rhubarb mixture and bake at 350\u00b0 for 45 to 60 minutes in a 9 x 13-inch pan. Use tester to make sure cake is done. A very moist cake.
