Sift flour once; measure and add baking powder and salt. Sift together 3 times. Cream butter thoroughly. Add sugar gradually and fluffy. Add egg yolks and beat well. Add flour alternately with milk, a small amount at a time, beating after each addition. Add vanilla and coconut. Fold in egg whites frequently and thoroughly. Bake in 3 greased and floured 9-inch cake pans at 375\u00b0 for 25 to 30 minutes. Cool and frost.
