Cream butter, sugar and vanilla in large mixer bowl until light and fluffy. Add eggs; beat well. Combine flour, cocoa, baking soda and salt; add alternately with buttermilk to creamed mixture. Add boiling water; beat until smooth. Pour into waxed paper-lined 13 x 9-inch pan. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 35 to 40 minutes or until cake tester comes out clean. Cool 10 minutes; remove cake from pan. Cool completely. Frost as desired.
