Combine peanuts, sugar, syrup, and salt in a 1 1/2 quart microwave safe container. Microwave 4 minutes on high in a 700 watt microwave oven. Stir, microwave 4 minutes longer. Stir in butter and vanilla. Microwave 2 more minutes on high. Add baking soda and quickly stir until light and foamy. Immediately pour onto lightly greased baking sheet. Spread to 1/4 inch thickness. When cool, break into pieces. Store in air tight container.
