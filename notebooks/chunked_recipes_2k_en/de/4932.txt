Mix chicken, cream cheese, milk and onion. Place 1/3 cup in each flour tortilla and roll. Place seam side down in greased baking dish. Mix together sour cream, soup and drained chilies and pour over enchiladas. Cover with foil. Bake 35 minutes at 350\u00b0. Top with Cheddar and Monterey cheese. Bake, uncovered, until melted.
