Line roaster with foil. Place brisket into roaster. Mix catsup and water together; pour over brisket. Sprinkle onion soup over the top. Seal with more foil. Cool slow (325\u00b0) for about 2 to 3 hours or until done. Remove from pan; reserve sauce. Allow to cool. Cut off fat and slice brisket; place in baking dish. Pour sauce over top. Heat through before serving.
