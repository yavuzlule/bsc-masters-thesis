Saute the peppers in the butter and oil until tender but not mushy. Remove from pan and set aside. Add the chicken to the skillet. If needed, add a little more butter and oil. Cook until the chicken is lightly browned and tender. Add peppers back to skillet along with the ice cream topping, sugar, ginger, and vinegar. Stir to mix well. Combine the soy sauce and cornstarch in a small bowl and stir until blended. Add to skillet, mixing well. Serve over rice and sprinkle the toasted cashews over the top. Makes 4 servings.
