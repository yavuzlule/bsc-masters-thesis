Combine syrup, sugar and salt in saucepan. Bring to a full boil. Stir in peanut butter. Remove from heat. Stir in vanilla, cereals and chocolate pieces. Press into buttered 9 x 9 x 2-inch pan. Chill about 1 hour. Cut in small bars or squares. , (For easy serving, store in refrigerator. ) Makes about 2 dozen bars.
