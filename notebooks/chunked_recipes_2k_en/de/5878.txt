Heat oven to 350\u00b0. Microwave butter in small microwavable bowl on High for 30 seconds or until melted. Stir in cereal and sugar; set aside. Beat cake mix, eggs, orange peel, orange juice and oil in a large bowl with electric mixer on low speed just to moisten. Beat on medium speed 4 minutes. Pour into greased and floured 10-inch tube pan. Bake 20 minutes or until beginning to rise. Sprinkle with cereal mixture. Bake 20 minutes or until done. Cool 15 minutes; remove from pan. Cool on wire rack. Drizzle with glaze.
