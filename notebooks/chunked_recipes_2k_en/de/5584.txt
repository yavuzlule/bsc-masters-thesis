In 2-quart saucepan, whisk 1/2 cup of the water into soup mix until smooth. Gradually add remaining 1 cup water, mixing well. Over medium-high heat, heat to boiling, stirring constantly. Reduce heat to low; simmer, uncovered, 2 minutes, stirring often. Stir in cheese until melted. Serve sauce over French fries or vegetables. Makes 1 1/2 cups.
