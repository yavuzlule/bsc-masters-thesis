Fill a 13 x 9-inch baking pan half full of hot tap water and place on oven rack in center of oven. Heat oven to 325\u00b0. Have ready 6 (6 ounce) custard cups or souffle dish. In a medium size bowl, whisk eggs to blend yolks and whites. Whisk in milk, sugar and vanilla until well blended. Place 1 slice bread in bottom of ungreased custard cups. Sprinkle with 1/2 tablespoon chocolate chips. Add 2 slices bread overlapping to fit and sprinkle with another 1/2 tablespoon of chips. Pour on about 1/2 cup milk mixture. Gently press down so slices begin to absorb milk mixture. Place cups in pan of hot water. Bake 25 to 40 minutes. Makes 6 servings.
