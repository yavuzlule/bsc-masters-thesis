Place roast in shallow Pyrex baking dish. Sprinkle with salt, pepper and garlic powder. Pour lemon juice over it and cover. Marinate for 2 to 3 hours or overnight (refrigerated). Roast pork in 325\u00b0 oven for 20 minutes per pound with marinade and uncovered. Heat cranberry sauce until melted, add brown sugar and orange juice; simmer until sugar dissolves and mixture thickens slightly. Brush glaze on roast 1/2 hour before it is ready.
