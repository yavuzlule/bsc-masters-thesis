Broil onions 20 minutes. Cut off tops. Slice down center side; cut off bottom. Separate the onion circles (leaves). Mix together beef and next 5 ingredients. Add tomatoes (helps cook the rice). Hold the onion circles (leaves) open and add small ball of meat mixture; roll onion around the meat. Put in frying pan with olive oil; brown bottom 15 minutes. Add beef broth. Simmer 45 minutes.
