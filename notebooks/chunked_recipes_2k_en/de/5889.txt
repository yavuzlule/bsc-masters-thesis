Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. Grease 9-inch spring-form pan. Combine 1 cup nuts, brown sugar and cinnamon. For topping, cut 1/4 cup butter and 1/3 cup flour for coarse crumbs. Stir in 1/4 cup of nut mixture. Set both mixtures aside. Toss pears with lemon juice and set aside. Mix the flour, baking powder, soda and salt; set aside. In large bowl beat the 1/2 cup butter, sugar and vanilla. Add eggs one at a time. Add flour mixture and sour cream alternately to batter. Beat on low speed after each addition. Spread 2/3 mixture in prepared pan. Sprinkle with nut mix and layer pears on top. Spread with rest of batter and sprinkle topping. Bake 10 minutes and sprinkle with 1/2 cup more walnuts. Bake 50 minutes more.
