Need 3 layer pans (I use foil ones). Mix sugar, shortening and eggs. Add flour, baking powder and spices alternately with milk. Pour 2/3 of the batter into 2 greased layer pans. To remaining mixture, add 1 tablespoon cocoa mixed with boiling water. This will be the middle layer. Pour into third greased pan. Bake at 375\u00b0 about 20 minutes. Frost with Mocha Icing between layers and on top.
