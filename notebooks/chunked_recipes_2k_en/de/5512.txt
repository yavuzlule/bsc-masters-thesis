Break cauliflower into pieces (about 4 cups); cook in salted water until tender and drain. Combine with ham and mushrooms. In medium saucepan, melt 1/4 cup butter. Stir in flour. Add milk all at once and stir constantly until mixture thickens and bubbles. Add cheese and sour cream to sauce; stir until cheese is melted. Combine with vegetables and ham. Turn into a 2-quart casserole. Combine crumbs and 1 tablespoon butter; sprinkle on top. Bake, uncovered, at 350\u00b0 for 40 minutes or until hot. Makes 6 servings.
