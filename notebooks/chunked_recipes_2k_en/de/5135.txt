Place sausages on a rack in a baking pan. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 15 minutes or until lightly browned. Cut into 1-inch pieces. Set aside. In a skillet, saute peppers in oil for 3 minutes. Place in 3-quart casserole. Add the sausages and the next 8 ingredients. Combine bread crumbs and butter. Sprinkle on top of casserole. Bake, uncovered, at 350\u00b0 for 30 to 40 minutes or until heated through. Yields 6 to 8 servings.
