Combine dressing, mayonnaise, salt and pepper, mixing well. Combine chicken, celery, orange sections or pineapple and walnuts into a bowl. Stir in mayonnaise mixture. Then add rice and mix lightly with fork. Chill at least 1 hour. Serve on crisp lettuce. Make about 6 cups or 4 to 6 servings luncheon on entree salad.
