Layer first 5 ingredients in order listed in a 1-quart wide-mouth glass canning jar. Press down firmly on each layer before adding the next ingredient. Combine flour, salt and baking powder together, mixing well. Add flour mixture on top of chocolate chip layer. Add a gift card with the following instructions:. Mix jar ingredients with 6 Tbsp. soft margarine, 1 beaten egg and 1 tsp. vanilla. Roll into 1-inch balls and place 2 inches apart on ungreased cookie sheet. Bake at 350 degrees for 10 minutes. Makes 3 dozen cookies.
