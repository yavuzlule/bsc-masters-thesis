Blanch and peel skin from tomatoes. Cut out stem and remove pulp. Cook mushrooms in milk for 3 minutes. , (Use a teflon pan. ) Add salt, rosemary, and savory to taste. Stir into cheese and bread crumbs and fill each tomato with 1/4 of mixture. Bake 8 minutes in 400 degree oven. Serve cut side down so filling is a surprise when cut open.
