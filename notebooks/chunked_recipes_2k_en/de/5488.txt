Beat egg whites until frothy. Beat in 1/3 cup sugar. Beat until very stiff and glossy. Sift remaining 1 cup sugar, cake flour, baking powder, baking soda and salt into another bowl. Add oil, bananas, half of the sour milk and the vanilla. Beat for 1 minute at medium speed. Add remaining sour milk and the egg yolks. Beat 1 minute longer. Fold in egg whites. Gently fold in nuts. Gently spoon into 2 greased and floured 8-inch cake pans. Bake in a preheated 350\u00b0 oven for 35 minutes. Let cool. Fill and frost as desired. Makes 10 servings.
