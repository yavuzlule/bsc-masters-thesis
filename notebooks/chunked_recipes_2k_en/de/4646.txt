Microwave potatoes on High 2 to 2 1/2 minutes on each side. Cool; peel and dice large. Combine all ingredients except half of onions. Pour into a Pam sprayed 2-quart casserole. Bake uncovered at 350\u00b0 for 25 to 30 minutes. Top with remaining onions. Bake 2 to 5 minutes more or until onions are light brown.
