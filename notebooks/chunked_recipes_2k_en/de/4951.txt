Mix cream cheese, sugar and 1 tablespoon milk with wire whisk until smooth. Gently stir in whipped topping. Spread on bottom of pie shell. Pour 1 cup milk in bowl; add pudding mix. Beat with wire whisk until well blended, 1 to 2 minutes, then let stand 3 minutes. Stir in pumpkin and spices and mix well. Spread over cream cheese layer. Refrigerate for at least 2 hours. Chill for 2 hours. Garnish with whipped topping.
