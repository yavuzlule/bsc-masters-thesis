Cream shortening and extract together. Add sugar and eggs one at a time, beating thoroughly after each addition. Add alternately dry ingredients and milk. Drop by teaspoon onto a greased cookie sheet. Flatten top with back of spoon and sprinkle with a mixture of cinnamon and sugar. Bake at 375\u00b0 for 10 minutes. For variety you may add chocolate, peanut butter or butterscotch chips.
