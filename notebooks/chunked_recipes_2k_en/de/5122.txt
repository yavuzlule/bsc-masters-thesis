Combine yellow cake mix, 1 egg, and oil. Press into 13 x 9-inch greased pan and bake at 300\u00b0 for 20 minutes. While cake is cooking mix 4 eggs, sugar. Karo syrup, butter, and chopped pecans. Pour mixture over cooked cake and bake 45 minutes on 300\u00b0. Since oven temperatures vary, during the last 5 to 10 minutes of cooking check closely. Do Not Over Cook. (Better than pecan pie if you can believe it. )
