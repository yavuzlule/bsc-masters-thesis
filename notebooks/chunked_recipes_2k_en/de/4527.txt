Add 2 Tbsp. sugar to 1/2 cup warm water. Let stand 10 minutes. Mix in 1 1/2 cups sugar, 4 cups warm water, oil, yeast, salt and gradually add flour. Mix up at 6 p. m. , work down at 9 p. m. Pan out at 10:30. Let stand overnight at room temperature. Bake in the morning at 350\u00b0 for 15-20 minutes.
