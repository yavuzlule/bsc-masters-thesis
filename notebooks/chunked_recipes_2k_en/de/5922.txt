Dice and peel apples. Add sugar, raisins and walnuts. Let set until rest is mixed. In bowl, mix eggs, oil and vanilla. Beat well, then add flour, soda, cinnamon and salt. Stir egg mixture into apple mixture. Stir well by hand. Its very thick. Bake at 325\u00b0 for 30 minutes. Can sprinkle cinnamon and sugar on top.
