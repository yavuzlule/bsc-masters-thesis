Soak deboned chicken in salt water a few minutes. Grease baking dish. Wrap 1/2 strip bacon around center part of each breast. Place a piece of dried beef in baking dish. Put a piece of chicken breast on, beef. Cover with sour cream, mushroom soup and sherry, mixed together. Sprinkle with paprika and pepper. Bake at 325\u00b0 for 3 hours.
