Melt butter in small saucepan. Saute garlic for 2 minutes. Add wine. Add cornstarch to thicken a little. Arrange the shrimp in a large flat pan and pour mixture over the top. Marinate in the refrigerator for about an hour. Broil 3 to 4-inches under broiler for 5 to 6 minutes per side. Serve the shrimp in an attractive serving dish.
