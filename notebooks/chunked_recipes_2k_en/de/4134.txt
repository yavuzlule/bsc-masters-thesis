In medium bowl, combine oatmeal, flour, sugar, baking powder and salt. Stir to mix. Make well in center; add pumpkin, egg, butter and vanilla. Stir until dry ingredients are moistened. Stir in raisins and nuts. Spread mixture in a 9 x 9 pan that has been greased. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 35 to 40 minutes or until firm and lightly browned. Let cool in pan. Cut into bars.
