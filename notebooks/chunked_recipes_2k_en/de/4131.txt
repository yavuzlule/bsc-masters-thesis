Place first eleven ingredients in microwave covered dish. Microwave at High for 10 minutes. Medium-high for 10 minutes and Low for 30 minutes. Stir. Add more water, if necessary. Microwave until tender. Add vegetables. Microwave at High until tender. Gradually sprinkle four tablespoons of flour over stew while stirring. Cook (microwave) until thickened, stirring once. May also be cooked conventionally.
