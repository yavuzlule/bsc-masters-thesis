Preheat oven to 325\u00b0. Grease and flour Bundt pan. In bottom of pan, sprinkle 1/2 cup of chopped pecans. Mix ingredients together and pour into Bundt pan. Bake 1 hour. Remove from oven and while still hot, pour rum glaze over it. Leave cake in pan for at least 30 minutes to allow glaze to soak in.
