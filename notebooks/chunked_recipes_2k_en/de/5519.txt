Preheat oven to 400\u00b0. In small bowl, combine the flours, sugar and baking powder. In large bowl, mix cereal, milk and bananas. Let stand 1 to 2 minutes. Add egg and oil to cereal mixture and beat well with a spoon. Stir in flour mixture until just combined. Spoon into greased muffin tins or use paper. Makes 12 to 20 muffins. Bake about 12 minutes.
