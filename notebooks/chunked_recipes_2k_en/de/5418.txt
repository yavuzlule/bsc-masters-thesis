Cook together slowly sugar, water, corn syrup and salt. Remove from heat just, before, it, reaches, soft, ball, when a little is dropped, in, cold, water, at 230\u00b0. Add marshmallows (cut in pieces) and, stir until they are melted. Add mint and nutmeats and stir, with, circular, motion, until, every nut is coated with the, mixture. Pour, out onto wax paper. Separate nuts. Can be kept in tightly covered jar for some time.
