Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. Grease 9 x 12 baking pan. Brown onions and garlic in oil. Add box of thawed spinach; cook until all soft. Transfer to bowl. Add eggs, breadcrumbs and Cheddar cheese; mix well. Pour into pan (sprayed with Pam) and sprinkle with Parmesan cheese. Cover and bake 20 minutes. Uncover; bake another 20 minutes until golden brown. Cool slightly and cut into squares.
