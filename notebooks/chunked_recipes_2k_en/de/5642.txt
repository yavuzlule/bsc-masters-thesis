Heat oven to 350\u00b0. Grease 8-inch square baking pan. Melt butter in large saucepan. Remove from heat and stir in brown sugar and honey until combined. Stir in oats and almonds. When well mixed, press into prepared pan using back of wooden spoon. Bake 20 to 25 minutes or until lightly browned. Remove from oven and place on wire rack. Press mixture down again and cut into bars. When cool, remove bars and store in airtight container at room temperature or wrap and freeze.
