Combine gelatin and boiling water, stirring until completely dissolved. Cool. Add fruits and nuts; stir to combine. Pour half of the gelatin mixture into an 8 x 12-inch pan. Refrigerate until set, about 1 hour. Keep remaining gelatin at room temperature. Spread sour cream evenly over set gelatin. Pour remaining gelatin over this. Cover and refrigerate until set, about 1 1/2 hours or overnight.
