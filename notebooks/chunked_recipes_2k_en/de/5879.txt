Grease well two 1 pound coffee cans. Place a rack or trivet on the bottom of large pot. Fill pot with water; bring to boil. In large bowl, combine dry ingredients. Combine molasses and milk; add to dry ingredients. Stir in raisins. Fill coffee cans 2/3 full; cover with foil and secure with rubber band. Place cans on trivet in kettle. , (Water should come 1/2 up sides of can. ). Put lid on pot; keep water at simmer and steam bread 3 hours. Remove from pot; remove foil covers.
