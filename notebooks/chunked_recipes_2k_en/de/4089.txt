Boil chicken. Cook spaghetti. Combine soups with melted butter and sour cream. Stir in chicken and mushrooms, then add spaghetti. Combine all together. Add salt and pepper and spread into a 13 x 9-inch casserole dish. Sprinkle grated Cheddar cheese on top, then sprinkle Parmesan cheese. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 25 to 30 minutes.
