Generously butter a 12-cup Bundt pan. Sprinkle with nuts. Cut partially thawed bread into 24 cubes and place in pan. Sprinkle pudding, sugar and cinnamon on bread. Cover and let rise to double, 3 to 4 hours. Bake at 375\u00b0 for 20 to 25 minutes. Let stand 30 minutes. Turn out on plate lined with waxed paper.
