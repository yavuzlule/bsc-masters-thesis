Prepare pie crust in pie pan. Brush with slightly beaten egg whites. Combine eggs, sugar, salt and cornstarch. Add milk and vanilla; mix well. Pour into 2 pie crusts (placed on cookie sheets for support). Sprinkle nutmeg on top. Bake in oven on 400\u00b0 for 30 to 40 minutes or until knife inserted comes out clean. Cool; serve.
