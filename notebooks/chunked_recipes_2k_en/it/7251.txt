Scrub ham with a brush to remove any mold on the skin. Soak ham in cold water for 8 hours. Place ham in roaster, fat side down, add 5 cups water. Cover top of pan with foil (making airtight as possible) before putting on the roaster lid. Place roaster in a cold oven and set temperature at 500\u00b0. Bake at 500\u00b0 for 10 minutes. Turn off. Leave ham in oven for 3 hours, undisturbed.
