Bring milk and margarine, to a boil. Dissolve yeast in warm water. Beat eggs,, sugar, and, salt. Pour milk mixture, yeast and egg mixture together, and, stir, in milk. Cover and refrigerate overnight. Two hours, before baking, set out in a warm spot. After 2, hours,, punch, dough, down,, divide, into thirds. Roll each third into, a circle and cut into 12 wedges with a sharp knife. Roll, each, wedge, beginning at wide end. Brush with egg and water mixture and sprinkle with sesame seed before baking. Bake 10 minutes at 425\u00b0. Yield: 3 dozen.
