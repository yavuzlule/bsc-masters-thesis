Heat oven to 350\u00b0. Grease and flour 2 round pans, 9 x 1 1/2-inches. Beat all ingredients except walnuts on low speed 30 seconds, scraping bowl constantly. Beat on high speed 3 minutes, scraping bowl frequently. Stir in walnuts. Pour into pans. Bake 35 to 40 minutes or until toothpick inserted in center comes out clean. Cool 10 minutes; remove from pans and cool completely. Fill and frost with caramel frosting.
