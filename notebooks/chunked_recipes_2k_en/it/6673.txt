Put raw hamburger in bottom of 9 x 13-inch pan. Cover with a layer of stuffing mix (not prepared). Cover stuffing with onion. Pour soup over stuffing and onion. Then pour milk over soups. Top with frozen Tater Tots; cover with foil. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 1 to 1 1/2 hours. Remove foil the last 15 minutes to brown the Tater Tots.
