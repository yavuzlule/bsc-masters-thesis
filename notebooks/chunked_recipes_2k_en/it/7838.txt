Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. In a skillet, saute onion and zucchini in margarine for approximately 10 minutes (zucchini should be firm). In a large bowl, combine contents from 1 box of Stove Top stuffing mix, soup, milk and egg and mix together. Add zucchini and onion to mixture. Put in 2-quart casserole dish and top with cheese. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 45 minutes to 1 hour. Cool for 10 minutes and serve.
