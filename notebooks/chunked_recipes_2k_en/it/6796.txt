Combine sugar, melted butter and cinnamon (and raisins if you wish). Spread 1/2 of mixture on each rectangle. Scatter nuts over top. Roll lengthwise as for jelly roll; seal edges. Cut into 1-inch slices. Place cut side down in two well-greased 9 x 9 x 2-inch pans. Cover and let rise 30 to 40 minutes. Bake at 375\u00b0 for 20 to 25 minutes. Remove to wire racks.
