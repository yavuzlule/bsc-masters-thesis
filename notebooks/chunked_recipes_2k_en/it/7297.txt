Mix 4 egg yolks, 1/2 cup sugar, 2 tablespoons flour, 3 tablespoons water and 1/4 teaspoon salt in top of double boiler. Stir over boiling water until mixture is thick and smooth. Add butter, lemon juice and lemon rind. Set aside to cool. Beat 2 egg whites until stiff and fold in the cooled custard. Pour into baked 9 inch pie shell. Cover with a meringue made from remaining egg whites, with a few grains of salt, beaten in until frothy. Add 4 tablespoons sugar slowly and a few drops of vanilla until stiff, but not dry. Pile on pie. Bake at 300\u00b0 for 20 minutes until meringue is golden brown.
