Combine flour, paprika, salt and the one teaspoon chili powder. Dredge beef cubes in the flour mixture and brown in hot lard in Dutch oven. Add onions and garlic; cook until soft. Add tomatoes, 3 tablespoons chili powder, cinnamon, cloves and red pepper. Cover and simmer 2 hours. Add potatoes and carrots; cook until vegetables are tender, about 45 minutes. Makes 8 servings.
