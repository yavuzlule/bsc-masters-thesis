Combine turkey, onion, garlic and pepper together in a nonstick pan and brown. Mix tomato soup, water and Worcestershire sauce together. In a 9 x 12-inch loaf pan, cut 1 head cabbage to bite size pieces. Cover with the turkey mixture. Sprinkle 2 tablespoons rice over mixture and add the tomato soup mixture. Cover with foil and bake at 350\u00b0 for approximately 1 hour.
