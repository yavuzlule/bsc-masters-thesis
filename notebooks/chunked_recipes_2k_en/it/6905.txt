Dissolve yeast in 1/2 cup warm water. Add 2 cups warm water, salt and pepper. Stir in large bowl. Add flour; turn out onto floured board. Knead dough until it becomes semi-firm and will not stick to hands. Put dough in bowl and rub 1 tablespoon oil over and around the dough so as not to form a crust. Let rise 1 hour.
