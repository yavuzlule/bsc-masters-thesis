In a large resealable bag combine flour, salt & pepper;, add beef cubes and shake to coat. In a skillet over medium heat, brown the beef in oil. Place beef and dripping in a greased shallow 2 1/2 qt. baking dish. To skillet add water, broth, garlic, onion, thyme and rosemary; bring to a boil. Simmer uncovered for 5 minutes. Stir in mushrooms. Pour over meat;, mix well. Cover and bake at 350\u00b0 for 1 1/2 to 1 3/4 hours or until beef is tender. Sprinkle corn or peas over meat. Spread potatoes evenly over top. Brush with butter;, sprinkle with paprika. Bake 15-20 minutes longer. Yield: 6-8 servings. Great with homemade dinner rolls and a salad.
