Boil potatoes until jackets are tender. Fry bacon until crisp; save 4 tablespoons of fat. Drain bacon well and crumble (set aside). Heat bacon fat and stir in flour and onion. Cook slowly. Continue to stir for a minute. Off heat, add water and vinegar; mix well. Return to low heat and cook, stirring, until smooth and thickened. Add sugar, salt, pepper and bacon. Cook for 10 minutes.
