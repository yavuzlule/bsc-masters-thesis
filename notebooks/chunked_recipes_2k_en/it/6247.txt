Cook onion and garlic in 2 tablespoons butter until onion is tender. Add pork and 1 cup of either broth. Cook over low heat for 20 minutes. Add balance of both broths and the sauerkraut. Cover and simmer for 45 minutes. Blend flour and the 2 tablespoons butter. Add to soup. Cook and stir until thickened. If you wish, add rice before the last 20 minutes of cooking. Makes 8 to 10 servings.
