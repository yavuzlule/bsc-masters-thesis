Sift together flour, salt, cinnamon and allspice. Stir in brown sugar. Mix shortening in with dough blender until coarse meal. Reserve 1/2 cup mixture for topping. Stir in buttermilk, vanilla and soda (mix soda with a little warm water in measuring cup that buttermilk was in). Spread in a greased 9 x 13-inch pan; sprinkle with reserved topping on top and also sprinkle on cinnamon. Bake at 375\u00b0 for 20 to 25 minutes.
