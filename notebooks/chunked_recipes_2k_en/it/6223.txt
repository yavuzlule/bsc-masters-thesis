In a large skillet, brown chicken pieces on all sides, about 15 minutes. Remove and drain all but 2 tablespoons fat from skillet. Add rice, onion, salt and poultry seasoning. Cook and stir until rice is lightly browned; add mushrooms, carrots and water. Put chicken on top. Sprinkle with paprika. Cover and simmer for about 45 minutes or until chicken and rice are done.
