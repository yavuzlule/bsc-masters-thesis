Brown meat; add onion, green pepper, mushrooms and seasonings. Cook until vegetables are tender. Add tomato soup and paste, water and Worcestershire sauce. Cook noodles until tender; drain and rinse. In large dish, well-greased, spread half of noodles; cover with half of meat mixture and sprinkle with half of cheese. Repeat. Bake at 375\u00b0 for 45 minutes.
