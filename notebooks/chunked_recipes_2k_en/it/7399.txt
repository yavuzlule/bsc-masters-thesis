Melt chocolate and butter or margarine in a large saucepan over low heat. Remove from heat and cool. Stir in marshmallows and 1/4 cup nuts. Divide mixture in half and shape each into 6-inch long log, 1 1/2-inches in diameter. Roll each in rest of nuts and chill. When chilled, slice into 1/2-inch pieces. Makes about 2 dozen.
