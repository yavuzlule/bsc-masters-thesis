Take one large field, half dozen children, 2 or 3 small dogs, a, pinch, of, brook, and some pebbles. Mix children and dogs well together, put them on the field, stirring constantly. Pour the brook over the pebbles. Sprinkle the field with flowers. Spread over, all, a, deep\tblue, sky and bake in sun. When brown, set away to cool in bathtub.
