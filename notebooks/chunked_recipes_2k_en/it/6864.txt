Brown meat on all sides; strain meat fat. In saucepan, stir together tomato sauce, water and salt. Add stew meat. Bring to a boil; place lid on pan. Simmer on low heat for 1 1/2 hours, stirring occasionally. Add vegetables; cover and cook 30 minutes more. Add peaches and liquid to stew. Cover and cook 10 minutes more. Makes 6 servings.
