Cook celery, green pepper and onion slowly in small amount of water for 10 minutes. Place 1/2 of diced bread into 9 x 12-inch cake pan or equivalent casserole dish. Mix together crab meat, cooked celery, onion and green pepper, mayonnaise. Accent and salt. Spread over diced bread and top with remaining 1/2 of bread. Mix together eggs and milk and pour over ingredients in dish. Cover and place in refrigerator for at least 4 hours. Remove from refrigerator, spoon mushroom soup over top and sprinkle with paprika and cheese. Bake for 10 to 15 minutes at 325\u00b0.
