Melt oleo in bottom of jelly roll pan (15 1/2 x 10 1/2-inch). Sprinkle evenly the crumbs. Sprinkle coconut over that evenly. Then spread fruit over that. On top of this, add dates plus nuts. Press mixture down and pour milk over it. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 25 to 30 minutes. Cool completely before cutting.
