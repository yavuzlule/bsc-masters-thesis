Dissolve raspberry Jell-O in 1 cup boiling water. Stir in pie filling. Pour into 9-inch square pan and chill until partially set. Dissolve lemon Jell-O in 1 cup boiling water. Combine cheese and mayonnaise and mix well. Stir in lemon Jell-O, pineapple and marshmallows. Fold in whipped cream. Spread lemon mix over cherry layer. Sprinkle with pecans. Chill until firm. Cut into squares and serve.
