Combine first 5 ingredients. Stir in nuts. Peel zucchini and shred enough for 2 cups. Combine zucchini and remaining ingredients. Add to flour mixture, stirring until dry ingredients are moistened. Spoon batter into 2 greased loaf pans. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 1 hour and 5 minutes or until done by testing with a toothpick. Cool in pans for 10 minutes. Turn out of pans and cool.
