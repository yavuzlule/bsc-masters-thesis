Mix oil, sugar, and egg yolks in large mixing bowl. Beat well for approximately 2 minutes. Sift allspice, cinnamon, and cloves together. Add salt, baking powder and 2 cups of flour. Add flour mixture alternately with the fruit juice to the first mixture. Mix 1 cup flour with the fruits and nuts. Add to the batter. Beat 4 egg whites until stiff. Fold beaten egg whites into the batter mixture. Pour batter into a 10 inch tube pan. Bake at 275\u00b0 for 4 hours.
