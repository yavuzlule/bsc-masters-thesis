Brown ground beef. Cook the spaghetti. Place cooked spaghetti in a 9 x 13-inch baking dish or pan. Add hamburger and diced Colby cheese. Sprinkle with minced onion, oregano leaves (crushed) and Italian seasoning. Pour can of tomato sauce over mixture with the bacon bits. Place bacon strips over the top and bake at 350\u00b0 for 1 hour.
