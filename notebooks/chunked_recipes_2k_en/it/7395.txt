Sprinkle gelatin over water to soften, using a double boiler; stir over hot water until dissolved. Beat cream cheese until smooth. Stir in sherry, salt, pimentos, crab, pepper, 2 tablespoons parsley and the dissolved gelatin. Pour into a 3-cup ring mold. Refrigerate 4 hours or until set. To serve, turn out on plate. Garnish with the remaining parsley. Place parsley sprigs in center.
