Butter a 9 x 13-inch baking dish. Place a layer of chilies on the bottom. Sprinkle with 1 cup Monterey cheese and 1/4 cup green pepper. Repeat this layer. Mix the remaining ingredients except the Cheddar cheese and pour over the casserole. Top with the Cheddar cheese. Bake in a 350\u00b0 oven for 45 to 50 minutes or until knife comes out clean. Cool 5 minutes before serving.
