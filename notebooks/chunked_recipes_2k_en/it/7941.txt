In saucepan, cook carrots just until crisp-tender. Drain, reserving 1/4 cup liquid. Place carrots in 1 1/2-quart baking dish. Combine mayonnaise, onion, horseradish and reserved cooking liquid. Spread evenly over carrots. Sprinkle with cheese. Top with bread crumbs. Bake uncovered at 350\u00b0 for 30 minutes. Yields 8 to 10 servings.
