Turn crock-pot to high heat. Rinse and dry chicken. Trim and discard any fringe fat. Put 1 teaspoon or cube of bouillon in chicken cavity. Put chicken in crock-pot, breast side up. Add remaining bouillon. Sprinkle chicken with parsley and paprika. Add remaining ingredients. Lower heat and cover. Slow-cook 8 hours. Chicken will be very tender. Serve, surrounded with vegetables.
