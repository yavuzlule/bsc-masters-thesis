Preheat oven to 400\u00b0. Grease 10-inch glass pie plate. Combine cheese, chicken, broccoli (drained) and onion in prepared pie plate. Combine remaining ingredients in blender or in a bowl. Mix or blend at high speed until smooth. Pour over ingredients in pie plate. Bake 25 to 30 minutes or until a knife inserted in the center comes out clean. Cool 5 minutes before serving.
