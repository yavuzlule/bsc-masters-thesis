Combine suet, molasses, and milk. Sift flour. Measure. Reserve 1/4 cup for dredging the fruits. Sift remainder of flour with salt, spices, cream of tartar and baking soda. Combine with first mixture. Add dredged fruits. Mix thoroughly. Fill well oiled 1 pound pan 2/3 full. Cover. Steam 3 hours. Serve hot with any desired pudding sauce. Makes 12 servings.
