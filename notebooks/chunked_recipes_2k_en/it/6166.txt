Clean and rinse halibut. Arrange pieces in single layer in bottom of 9 x 13-inch baking dish. Dot with butter. Bake at 375\u00b0 for 1/2 hour basting with butter frequently. While halibut is baking, mix together grated cheese and mayonnaise. , (Mixture will stick to spoon in clumps. ). When halibut is almost done, top with cheese and mayonnaise mixture. Continue to bake until cheese has melted and is lightly browned.
