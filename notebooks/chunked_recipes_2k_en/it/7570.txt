Cream together brown sugar, 1/2 cup sugar, shortening, vanilla and egg; mix well. Add cream of tartar, flour, baking soda and salt; mix well. Roll into 1-inch balls and roll into sugar. Place on ungreased cookie sheets, 2 inches apart. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 10 minutes, or until done. Makes 3 to 4 dozen.
