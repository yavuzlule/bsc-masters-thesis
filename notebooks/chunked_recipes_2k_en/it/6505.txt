In a large casserole dish (deep sides) add 1/2 tomato sauce, chopped onions and water. Add raw rice; let it settle to the bottom. Place chicken on top; nestle down in sauce and cover chicken with remaining tomato sauce. Top with sliced black olives. Bake in oven at 350\u00b0 for about 45 minutes to 1 hour or until chicken is tender and rice is done.
