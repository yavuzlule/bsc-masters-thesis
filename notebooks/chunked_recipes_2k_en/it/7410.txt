Line a 13 x 10-inch pan with 1 large sheet of foil to cover meat completely later. Put in brisket, season beef and arrange onion slices on top. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 1 hour. When onions brown slightly, then cover (seal) beef tightly with foil. Add 1 cup of water to pan (may have to add more water). Reduce heat to 300\u00b0. Cook 2 more hours. Keep sealed. After 3 hours, open and check. If fork-tender, its cooked long enough. Make gravy from the juices.
