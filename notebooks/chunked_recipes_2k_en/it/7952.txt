Pound chicken breast halves until 1/4-inch thick. Sprinkle with salt and pepper. Top each piece of chicken with 1 slice ham and 1 slice cheese. Fold chicken over ham and cheese. Wrap each bundle with two slices bacon. Place bundles side by side in a shallow baking pan. Bake in a preheated (350\u00b0) oven for 40 to 45 minutes or until chicken is cooked and bacon is brown. Serves 6.
