In bowl, combine 10x sugar, butter, peppermint extract and vanilla. Add milk and mix well. Roll into one-inch balls and place on a waxed paper lined cookie sheet. Chill for 20 minutes. Flatten with the palm of hand to 1/4-inch thickness. Chill for an hour. Dip into coating chocolate; place on waxed paper until chocolate is set. Yield: Approximately 5 dozen.
