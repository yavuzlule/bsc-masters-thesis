Mix cream cheese, sugar and vanilla at medium speed with mixer until well blended. Add eggs and blend again. Stir in 3/4 cup chips. Pour into pie crust and sprinkle top of mixture with remaining 1/4 cup chips. Bake at 350\u00b0 for approximately 40 minutes or until center is almost firm. Cool and refrigerate at least 4 hours, overnight is fine. Garnish with additional chips and/or chocolate Cool Whip.
