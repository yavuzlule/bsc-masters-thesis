In a 2 1/2-quart casserole, cook olive oil, garlic and onion, covered, on High 3 to 5 minutes, until tender, stirring halfway through. Add kidney beans with liquid, tomato sauce, pasta, bouillon and 3/4 cup water. Cook covered on High 14 to 18 minutes, until pasta is tender. Stir halfway through. Stir in peas; sprinkle with cheese. Let stand, covered, 5 minutes. Makes 4 servings.
