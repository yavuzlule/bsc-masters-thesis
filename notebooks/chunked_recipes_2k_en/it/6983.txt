Simmer raisins and water over low heat for 20 to 30 minutes. Drain raisin liquid into a measuring cup; add enough water to make 1/2 cup. Mix shortening, sugar, eggs and vanilla; stir in raisin liquid. Measure flour, baking powder, soda, salt and spices. Add rolled oats, nuts and raisins. Drop by spoonfuls onto a cookie sheet. Bake until golden brown at 350\u00b0. Makes 5 dozen.
