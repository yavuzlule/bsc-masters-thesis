Cook first 3 ingredients for 4 minutes in a 1 1/2-quart casserole dish, nothing plastic. Remove and stir. Replace and cook for 4 more minutes. Remove and stir well; add butter and vanilla. Stir well. Cook 2 more minutes. Remove from oven; add baking soda. Stir until foamy. Pour on a greased cookie sheet and let cool, then break into pieces.
