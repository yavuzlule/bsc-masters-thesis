Beat egg whites stiff. Add sugar gradually. Add vanilla. Crush crackers (not too fine). Fold crackers, nuts and baking powder into egg white mixture. Put into a well-greased 9-inch pie plate. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 30 minutes. Cool completely. Cover with 1/2 pint whipped cream (Cool Whip is fine). Sprinkle with grated chocolate on top. Put in refrigerator 8 hours or overnight. Serves 6.
