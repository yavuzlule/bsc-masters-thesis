Sift together flour, sugar, baking powder, soda and salt. Stir in shortening. Dissolve yeast in 2 tablespoons warm water and add to buttermilk. Add to flour mixture and mix well. Roll out on floured board 1/4-inch thick. Cut with a biscuit cutter. Dip biscuit in butter and fold over. Bake at 400\u00b0 for 15 minutes. Do not let rise before baking.
