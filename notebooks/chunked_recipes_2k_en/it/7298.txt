Pour boiling water over oats; cover and let stand 20 minutes. Beat butter until creamy; gradually add sugars and beat until fluffy. Blend in vanilla and eggs. Add oats mixture; mix well. Sift together flour, soda, salt, nutmeg and cinnamon; add to creamed mixture. Mix well. Pour batter into well-greased and floured 9-inch square pan. Bake in preheated 350\u00b0 oven for 50 to 55 minutes. Do not remove from pan.
