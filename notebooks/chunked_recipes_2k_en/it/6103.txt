Cook grits according to directions. Add garlic cheese and butter; stir to melt. Beat eggs and pour into 1 cup measure; finish filling measure with milk. Add to grits. Bake at 350\u00b0 in a 1/2-quart ungreased casserole dish for 45 minutes. Sprinkle with Parmesan cheese, then continue to bake for 20 minutes until top browns.
