Bake and cool pie shell. Wash and hull the strawberries. Cut up the berries. Mix the sugar and cornstarch in a 2-quart saucepan. Stir in the water gradually until the mixture is smooth. Add the cut up berries. Cook, stirring constantly, over medium heat, until thick and clear. Stir in the food coloring. Cool. Stir in the remaining berries. Pour into the pie crust. Chill until firm (3 hours). Top with Cool Whip.
