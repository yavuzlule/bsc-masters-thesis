Fry bacon. Spread frozen pie crust with 1 tablespoon melted butter. Crumble bacon into bottom of pie and add Swiss strips. Mix 2 cups heavy cream with 4 eggs and 1/4 teaspoon salt and pepper. Pour over bacon and Swiss. Bake at 425\u00b0 for first 15 minutes. Drop temperature to 350\u00b0 and bake another 30 minutes.
