Mix butter, 1 egg and cake mix together and put in a greased 9 x 13-inch pan. Mix 3 eggs, cream cheese and sugar and pour on top of cake mixture. Cook at 325\u00b0 to 350\u00b0 until done. If desired, you can sprinkle some extra confectioners sugar on top, once it has finished cooking. Place a piece of aluminum foil on rack below cake pan to keep mixture from browning too fast.
