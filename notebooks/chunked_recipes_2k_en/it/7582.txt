Place egg whites in bowl. Attach bowl and wire whip. Turn to speed 8 and whip until stiff, but not dry. Remove from bowl and set aside. Place egg yolks on speed 4 for 2 minutes, until thick and lemon colored. Add remaining ingredients and mix on speed 2 until combined, 30 seconds. Place mixture in 8 1/2 x 4 1/2 x 2 1/4-inch greased loaf pan. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 40 to 45 minutes. Cool in pan for 10 minutes, then remove and serve warm. Yields 10 to 12 servings.
