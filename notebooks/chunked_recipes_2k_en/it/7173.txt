In large bowl, combine beef, bread crumbs, onion, egg, ketchup, 1 teaspoon salt and 1/8 teaspoon pepper. Blend well and shape into a loaf. Place in a shallow baking pan. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 1 1/4 hours. Combine whipped potatoes, green onions, parsley, remaining 1/4 teaspoon salt and remaining 1/8 teaspoon pepper. Spread over top and sides of meat loaf. Bake 20 minutes longer. If desired, broil until golden brown.
