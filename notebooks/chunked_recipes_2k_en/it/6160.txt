Preheat oven to 375\u00b0. Shred cheese and mix all ingredients. Form into one inch balls. Bake 15 minutes on ungreased baking sheet until golden brown. Makes 6 dozen. Sausage cheese balls can be frozen uncooked. For a firmer texture add an extra 3 cups of baking biscuit mix and an extra 2 cups of shredded cheese to the ingredients listed above.
