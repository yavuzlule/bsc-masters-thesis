Slice unpeeled potatoes. Peel and chop onions. Put oil in cold skillet and heat to about 300\u00b0. Scatter potato slices over bottom of skillet. Cover and cook about 5 minutes. Turn potatoes over and brown other side in covered frypan about 2 or 3 minutes. Uncover, add chopped onions and water. Replace cover and cook 3 minutes longer. Uncover and add eggs and meat. Stir until eggs are cooked. Serve warm.
