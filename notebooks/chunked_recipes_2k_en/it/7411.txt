Saute onion in oil until tender. Stir in catsup, water, vinegar, mustard and pepper. Bring to a boil, stirring occasionally. Place frankfurters in a 1-quart baking dish. Pour sauce over. Bake, uncovered, at 375\u00b0 for 30 minutes or until bubbly. If desired, serve over toast or rice. Makes 5 servings.
