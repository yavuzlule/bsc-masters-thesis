Shake little 1/4 sections in a bag with a mixture of 3/4 cup sugar and 1 teaspoon cinnamon. After all the sections are covered, put in greased angel food pan. Boil 3/4 cup butter, 1 cup sugar and 1 1/2 teaspoons cinnamon until sugar dissolves. Pour over biscuit pieces and bake at 350\u00b0 for 40 minutes.
