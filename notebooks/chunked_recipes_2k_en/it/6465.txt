Beat egg whites in mixer bowl until soft peaks form. Add cream of tartar and vanilla and beat until stiff peaks form. Add sugar, 2 tablespoons at a time, beating after each addition. Fold in chocolate chips. Drop mixture by heaping teaspoonfuls onto ungreased foil-lined baking sheet and bake at 250\u00b0 for 20 to 25 minutes, or until pale cream in color. Outside will be slightly soft. Makes 36 cookies.
