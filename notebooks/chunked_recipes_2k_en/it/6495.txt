Sift the sugar, flour and salt in large bowl. Mix the margarine, shortening, cocoa and water in a saucepan over medium heat until it boils. In a measuring cup, add soda to buttermilk. Pour hot mixture over sugar mixture and stir well. Add buttermilk, soda and vanilla. Add eggs, one at a time, and stir well. Bake in oblong pan for 20 minutes at 400\u00b0.
