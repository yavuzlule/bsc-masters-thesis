Combine the sugar, oil and eggs. Add the apples; set aside. Sift together the flour, salt, cinnamon, nutmeg and baking soda. Add the sifted ingredients to the apple mixture. Pour into greased 9 x 13-inch cake pan and bake for 1 hour in preheated oven at 350\u00b0. Serve it hot, warm or cold. Try it plain, frosted with whipped cream, ice cream or confectioners sugar.
