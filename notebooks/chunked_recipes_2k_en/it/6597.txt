Put oleo, honey and sugar in quart pan. Bring to a boil. Cook 6 minutes, stirring constantly. Remove from heat and add 1/4 teaspoon cream of tartar, scant teaspoon salt and 1 teaspoon soda; stir. While still foaming, pour over corn. Stir well until corn is coated. Put on 1 or 2 cookie sheets. Bake 1 hour at 200\u00b0; cool.
