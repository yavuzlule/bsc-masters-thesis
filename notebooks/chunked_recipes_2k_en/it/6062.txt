Cream butter and sugar in a medium bowl. Add eggs, one at a time, mixing well after each addition. Combine flour, soda and salt; add alternately with sour cream to butter mixture and beat well. Stir in vanilla. Pour batter into greased 10-inch tube pan or 2 (4-inch) loaf pans. Bake at 325\u00b0 for 50 minutes to 1 1/2 hours or until cake springs back when lightly touched and has separated from sides of pan. This cake should sit at least 1 day before serving and keeps very well. It is good sliced and toasted, served with ice cream or fresh, sliced fruit.
