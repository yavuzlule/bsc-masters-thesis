Peel potatoes and put in a roaster or baking dish. Cut up onion in a separate dish with the mushroom soup with about 1/2 cup water. Mix onion, water and mushroom soup in dish together. Stir, then pour over potatoes. Put butter on potatoes and bake in oven at 350\u00b0 for 1 to 1 1/2 hours until potatoes are done.
