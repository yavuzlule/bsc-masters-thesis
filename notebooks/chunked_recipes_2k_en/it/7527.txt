Cover split peas with cold water. Simmer gently 2 minutes and remove from heat. Cover and let stand 1 hour. Add ham bone, onion, basil, pepper, marjoram, thyme, salt and bay leaf. Bring to a boil and simmer 1 1/2 hours, stirring occasionally. Add and cook slowly uncovered 30 to 40 minutes longer:, chopped celery, carrots and potatoes. Remove meat from ham bone and return to soup; discard bone.
