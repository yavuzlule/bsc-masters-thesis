Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. Cover bottom of cookie sheet with graham crackers. Melt butter and oleo over medium heat; add sugar. Bring to a hard boil, stirring continually. Boil 2 minutes. Pour over graham crackers covering completely. Sprinkle with pecans. Bake 10 minutes. Watch that it doesnt burn around to edges. Remove from oven and let cool 2 to 3 minutes. Remove from pan to cooling rack.
