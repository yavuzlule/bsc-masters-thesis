Cook bacon until crisp; drain and set aside 2 tablespoons drippings. Crumble bacon. In a large bowl, beat eggs. Add bacon and 2 tablespoons of drippings, cottage cheese, pineapple and vanilla. Pour into a greased 11 x 7 x 2-inch baking dish. Bake uncovered at 350\u00b0 for 40 to 45 minutes. Garnish with parsley. Yields 8 servings.
