Saute ground beef and onions together. Salt and pepper to taste. Add pinch of sage, 1 pinch of mustard. Cook until brown; drain and wash off. Peel diced boiled potatoes, then mash boil beans, corn and carrots separately. Cook squash until tender (drain). In square 9 x 9-inch pan. Layer ground beef mixture, green beans, squash, corn, carrots, mashed potatoes. Cover with cheese. Bake at 325\u00b0 until cheese is melted and browned.
