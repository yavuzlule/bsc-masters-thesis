Cook bacon until crisp. Remove and drain, leave grease in skillet and add brown sugar, dry mustard, salt and vinegar. Slice and ring onions. Cook all together in pan for 20 minutes. Drain all beans except pork and beans. Mix onion mixture with beans. Place in 3 quart casserole dish, crumble bacon on top. Bake 1 hour at 350\u00b0. Serves 12.
