Peel eggplant and cut into small pieces. With the chopped onion, boil eggplant in salted water until tender. Mash in pan, then add tomatoes, cheese and 1/3 cup bread crumbs. Bring mixture to boil; add eggs until they are done. Pour into 2-quart baking dish. Heat 1 cup dry crumbs in 1/4 cup butter in oven until golden brown to make buttered bread crumbs for topping the casserole. Bake until browned, about 1 hour, at 350\u00b0.
