Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. Unroll dough. Separate into 8 triangles. Cut each in half to make 16 triangles. Slice apples. At the wide end of each strip of dough, place 4 to 5 slices of apple and roll. Place on cookie sheet. Combine sugar and cinnamon. With pastry brush, brush each one with melted butter. Sprinkle with cinnamon sugar. Bake 11 to 13 minutes.
