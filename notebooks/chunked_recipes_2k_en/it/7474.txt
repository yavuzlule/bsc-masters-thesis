Saute onions in butter. Add chicken broth. Add 2 cans soup, chiles, olives and chicken. Simmer 7 minutes then add 1/4 cup cheddar cheese, and sour cream. Layer in 9 x 13 inch pan. Tortilla, chicken mix, cheddar cheese, repeat, ending with 1 cup Cheddar cheese sprinkled on top. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 30 minutes covered. Remove cover last 5 minutes.
