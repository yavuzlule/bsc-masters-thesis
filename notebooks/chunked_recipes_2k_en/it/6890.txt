Preheat oven to 400\u00b0. Mix bisquick, milk, brown sugar, oleo and nutmeg. Spread in an ungreased square pan (9 x 9 x 2-inch). Drain peaches, reserving 1 cup syrup. Arrange peaches on mixture in pan. Pour reserved syrup over peaches. Bake until golden brown, about 40 minutes. Serve with whipping cream or ice cream and gobble up!
