Put meat, bones and water in stock pot. Simmer, uncovered, for 3 hours (do not boil). Strain. Remove meat or marrow from bones. Add marrow to stock. Reserve meat or discard. Chill stock overnight; skim and discard fat which floats to the top. Bring stock to boiling. Add remaining ingredients. Simmer, uncovered, for 2 hours. Strain. Reserve vegetables for soup or later use. Use broth within 2 weeks or freeze for later use. Yields 6 to 8 pints.
