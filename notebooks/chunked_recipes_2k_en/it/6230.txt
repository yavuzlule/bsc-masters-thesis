Drain manicotti and place on foil in single layer. Mix all ingredients together. Stuff pasta. Melt butter; add sauce and garlic powder. Pour some sauce on bottom of baking dish. Add manicotti in single layer. Spoon remaining sauce over top. Sprinkle 1 cup Mozzarella cheese over sauce. Cover. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 45 minutes. Uncover last 15 minutes.
