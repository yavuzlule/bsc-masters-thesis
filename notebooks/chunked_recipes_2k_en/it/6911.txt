Grease and flour two 9 1/2-inch cake pans. Sift together flour, sugar, baking powder and salt into a large mixing bowl. Add shortening, 2/3 of the milk and vanilla. Mix at low speed until dry ingredients are dampened. Beat at medium speed for 2 minutes. Add the 1/3 milk and eggs. Beat at low speed until mixed well, then beat on medium speed for 2 minutes. Pour in pans. Bake at 375\u00b0 about 25 minutes.
