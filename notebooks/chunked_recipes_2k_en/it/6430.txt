Clean and dress, the, fish. Cut, into, 3, x, 5-inches. Place dressed fish over, 1/2, of, the, crisp bacon in a baking pan. Sprinkle with the salt and white pepper. Cover with the remaining crisp bacon and, bake in oven at 325\u00b0 for 45 minutes to an hour. Baste, occasionally with melted fat. Serves 6 to 8 people.
