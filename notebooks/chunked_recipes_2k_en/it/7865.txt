Poke holes all over brisket. Line a 9 x 12-inch pan with foil. Lay brisket in pan. Mix all ingredients and pour over brisket. Leave a little air space and set in refrigerator overnight. Cook at 275\u00b0 for 6 hours. Open foil and pour 1 1/2 cup Hunts natural barbecue sauce over meat. Re-wrap and cook 1 1/2 hours more. Shred meat before serving or freezing.
