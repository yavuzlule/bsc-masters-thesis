Put salted turkey in roaster. Pour over turkey 1 cup boiling water with 1 stick of melted margarine. Cover airtight with lid or tent made of heavy-duty Reynolds Wrap. Place in a 450\u00b0 preheated oven. Bake for 1 hour. Do not open oven door. Leave in oven overnight, or until oven is cold. , (I cook mine last thing before going to bed. ). This time is for a 12-pound turkey. Add about 5 minutes per pound for larger turkey.
