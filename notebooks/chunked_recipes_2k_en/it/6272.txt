In a 1 1/2-quart casserole, combine potatoes, cheese and onion. Dissolve bouillon cube in 1/2 cup of boiling water. Pour over potato mixture. Dot with 1 tablespoon margarine. Bake, covered, in a 350\u00b0 oven for 30 minutes or until potatoes are done, stirring gently once or twice. If desired, top with triangles of cheese and bake until cheese melts. Serves 6.
