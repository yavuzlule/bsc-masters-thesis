Follow instructions closely:. Mix in 4 cup size bowl the brown sugar, margarine. Karo syrup and salt. Cook on High for 2 minutes. Stir. Cook on High for 2 more minutes. Remove from oven. Add soda and vanilla. Stir; pour over corn in sack. Shake. Fold sack loosely. Put in microwave. Cook on High for 1 1/2 minutes. Shake. Cook on High for 1 1/2 minutes. Shake. Cook on High for 1 1/2 minutes. Pour in oblong pan. Stir and separate. Cool.
