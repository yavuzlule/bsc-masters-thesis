Sprinkle the steaks with cracked pepper. Heat the oil and butter in a heavy skillet; brown the steaks quickly, about 4 minutes per side. While steaks are cooking, drizzle about 1 teaspoon of oil on each bread slice. Broil lightly. Turn and top with onion slices. Drizzle with more oil. Broil until lightly browned. Sprinkle with cheese; broil for a few seconds. Place the steaks on 4 slices of bread. Top with remaining slices, this time onion side up. Serve.
