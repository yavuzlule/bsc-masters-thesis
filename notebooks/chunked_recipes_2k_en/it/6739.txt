Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. To get potatoes like the steakhouses, do the following:, pierce each potato several times with fork. Coat outside of each potato with vegetable oil. In Ziploc bag, roll potato in coarse kosher or rock salt. DO NOT WRAP POTATOES IN FOIL. Place potatoes directly on oven rack and bake at 350\u00b0 for 1 hour. You will never eat foil wrapped potatoes again!
