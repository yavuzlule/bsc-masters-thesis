Remove stem, seeds and membrane from jalapeno; chop. Cook and stir pepper, onion and garlic in margarine in 2-quart saucepan until onion is tender. Add tomatoes, celery, lemon juice, salt, sugar and pepper. Heat to boiling. Reduce heat; simmer, uncovered, for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally. Stir shrimp into tomato sauce. Heat to boiling. Reduce heat; simmer, uncovered, 5 minutes. Serve over rice. Garnish with parsley.
