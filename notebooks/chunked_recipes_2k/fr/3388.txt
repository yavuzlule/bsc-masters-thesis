Drain all fruits and turn out into 1 1/2-quart casserole and mix together lightly. Blend together the soft butter, brown sugar, curry, cornstarch and lemon peel. Sprinkle over fruit mixture. Let mixture stand for several hours to draw out juices. Bake uncovered about 1 hour at 325\u00b0. Spoon some of the juices over fruit several times during baking. Serve while hot. Serves 6.
