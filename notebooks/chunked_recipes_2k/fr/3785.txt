Flatten chicken slightly and dust with flour. Mix eggs with Parmesan cheese, 2 tablespoons parsley, salt and pepper. Dip chicken in egg mixture. Heat oil in pan and brown chicken on both sides over medium-high heat. Discard oil and wipe pan out. Melt butter in pan. Add chicken broth and 2 tablespoons parsley. Squeeze juice from lemon in sauce and put lemon in sauce. Return chicken to pan. Simmer 10 minutes or until tender. Add wine and pepper. Simmer 5 minutes. Remove to serving dish. Pour sauce over chicken and garnish with lemon and parsley.
