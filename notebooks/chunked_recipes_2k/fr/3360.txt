Mix graham cracker crumbs, margarine and 1/2 cup of the powdered sugar. Press mixture into a 9 x 12-inch pan. Combine and mix the cream cheese, the other half of the powdered sugar, vanilla, pineapple, marshmallows and Cool Whip. Pour mixture over crust. Top with the pie filling. Cool for, 3 hours in refrigerator. Delicious!
