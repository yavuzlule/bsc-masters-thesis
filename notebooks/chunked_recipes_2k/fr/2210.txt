In mixing bowl, combine eggs and milk. Sift dry ingredients, add and beat. Heat oil to 360\u00b0 in 8-inch skillet. Pour 1/2 cup batter through a funnel in a spiral shape. Fry until golden brown, about 3 minutes. Using tongs, turn cakes carefully. Cook 1 minute more. Drain on paper towel and sprinkle with powdered sugar. Makes 4 cakes.
