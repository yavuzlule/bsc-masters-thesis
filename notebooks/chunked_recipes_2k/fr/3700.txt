Butter a large casserole. Cook broccoli and green onion in a small amount of water with dash of salt for 5 to 6 minutes. Drain well. Mix soup and half and half in bowl. In casserole, layer rice, broccoli, green onions and ham. Pour soup mixture over; top with shredded cheese. Bake at 375\u00b0 for approximately 35 minutes or until cheese is melted and light brown. Serve hot from oven with salad and rolls. Makes 4 to 6 servings.
