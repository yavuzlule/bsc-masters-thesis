Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. Butter a 12 x 8-inch shallow baking dish. Discard outer leaves from cabbage. Wash head and cut into 12 wedges. Put 1 cup water in large skillet. Bring to a boil. Add 1 teaspoon salt and the cabbage. Cook over medium heat, covered, 10 minutes. Drain well. Arrange cabbage in prepared baking dish. Sprinkle with mixture of flour, sugar, 1 teaspoon salt and the pepper. Dot with butter. Pour hot milk over cabbage. Sprinkle with cheese. Bake, uncovered, 35 minutes. Makes 8 servings.
