Cook pasta according to package directions; drain and keep warm. In 3-quart saucepan, cook bacon until crisp. Drain, reserving 2 tablespoons bacon fat. Add onion, garlic, basil, oregano and peppers. Cook 5 minutes. Add tomatoes and vinegar; cook over medium heat for 10 minutes or until slightly thickened. Stir in salmon and cream; heat through. Serve over hot pasta and garnish with Parmesan.
