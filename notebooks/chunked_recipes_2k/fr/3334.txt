Line a deep dish or pie pan with pastry shell. Beat eggs, add sugar and continue beating until light. Add salt, lemon juice and rind, cream, cottage cheese and flour; beat thoroughly and strain through a fine sieve. Pour into pastry shell. Cut 1/2 pie crust into tiny pieces. Sprinkle it and nutmeats on top and bake at 350\u00b0 for about 1 hour or until center is firm. Let stand about 1 hour or until completely cooled.
