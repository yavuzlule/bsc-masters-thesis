Saute onions and celery in butter. Add soups, parsley and seasoning; mix well. Cook macaroni in chicken broth, add water if necessary. Do not drain. Butter a 9 x 13-inch dish. Mix chicken, macaroni, soup mixture and pimiento. Pour into prepared dish. Sprinkle with cheese. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 20 to 30 minutes.
