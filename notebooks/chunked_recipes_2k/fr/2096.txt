Cover a 9 x 13-inch buttered Pyrex dish with the Doritos chips. Cook chicken and cut into bite-size pieces; place over the Doritos. Mix together soups, milk and picante sauce. Pour over chicken and Doritos. Sprinkle grated Cheddar cheese on top. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 30 minutes. Best made at least 6 hours before serving. Serves 6.
