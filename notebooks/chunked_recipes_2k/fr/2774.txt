Mix margarine, sugar, eggs and molasses. Mix in coffee. In a separate bowl, mix together flour, pepper, cloves, cinnamon, salt, nutmeg, baking powder and 2 tablespoons anise seed. Mix dry ingredients into coffee mixture. It will be sticky so add a little more flour to make dough stiff. Roll out thin. Cut with cookie cutters. Bake at 350\u00b0 and watch carefully. Make a thin frosting of powdered sugar, water and a drop of vanilla. Frost first, then use colored sprinkles.
