Saute onion in 1/4 cup margarine along with carrot and zucchini. Put half of stuffing in a 9 x 13-inch pan. Combine sauteed vegetables, sour cream, soup and eggs. Pour over stuffing. Place remainder of stuffing on top. Drizzle margarine and add grated cheese. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 1 hour or until baked through.
