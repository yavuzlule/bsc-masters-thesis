Heat oven to 350\u00b0. Mix egg and water. Crush 4 cups corn chips. Add egg mixture to crushed chips. Spread in lightly greased 13 x 9-inch baking dish. Combine chili, 1 cup enchilada sauce, green pepper and onion. Spread over corn chips in baking dish. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 35 minutes. Top with cheese and remaining whole corn chips. Bake 5 minutes more. Top with lettuce and tomatoes. Serve with remaining sauce. Serves 6 to 8.
