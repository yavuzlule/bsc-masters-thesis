In bowl, combine graham cracker crumbs and sugar. Melt margarine and add to mixture. Press 2/3 crust mixture in a 9 x 13-inch baking dish. Set aside. In mixing bowl, beat Cool Whip and cream cheese together. Add confectioners sugar and continue to beat until smooth. Put cream cheese mixture on top of crust mixture. Top with blueberry pie filling. Sprinkle top with remaining crust mixture. Refrigerate before serving.
