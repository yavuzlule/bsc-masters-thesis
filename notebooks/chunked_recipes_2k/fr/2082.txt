In one bowl, combine all ingredients except frosting. Beat with mixer at high speed for 2 minutes. Grease and flour medium size (9 cup) Bundt pan and pour in batter. , (This will give a high cake; larger pans will give a flatter cake. ). Bake at 350\u00b0. Test at 45 minutes and then every 5 minutes until done. Cool in pan 10 minutes, then turn out on rack. If frosted while warm, frosting will melt to sort of a glaze. Equally good frosted when cool.
