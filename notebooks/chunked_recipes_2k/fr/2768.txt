Measure oatmeal, and blend in a blender to a fine powder. Cream the butter and both sugars. Add eggs and vanilla, mix together with flour, oatmeal, salt, baking powder, and soda. Add chocolate chips. Hershey bar, and nuts. Roll into balls and place two inches apart on a cookie sheet. Bake for 10 minutes in 375 degree oven. Makes 112 cookies.
