Cream the peanut butter, oil. Crisco and sugar. Add eggs one at a time. Add flour, baking powder, salt, milk and vanilla; mix well. Take out 1 1/2 cups of batter. Add cocoa to that and mix. Pour batter in greased tube pan. Then take cocoa batter and whirl into batter a few times. Try not to hit sides of pan. Put pan of water under cake to keep moist while baking. Bake 1 1/2 hours at 325\u00b0.
