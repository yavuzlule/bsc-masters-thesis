Wash radishes, spinach and lettuce; chill in refrigerator. Save 2 or 3 radishes for \roses;\ crisp with ice cube. Tear spinach leaves and lettuce into bite size pieces in salad bowl. Slice 5 or 6 radishes thinly. Add to greens. Peel oranges; cut into sections. Cut avocado into bite size chunks or slices. Reserve 8 to 10 large onion rings. Add remainder to salad. Just before serving, add dressing and toss lightly. Garnish with onion rings and radish \roses. \. Serves 6.
