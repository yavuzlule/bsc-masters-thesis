Combine all ingredients, except fruit pectin, in a Dutch oven; bring to a boil. Boil for 6 minutes, stirring frequently. Stir in fruit pectin; continue boiling for 3 additional minutes, stirring frequently. Remove from heat and skim off foam with a metal spoon. Quickly pour jelly into sterilized jars, leaving 1/2-inch headspace. Cover at once with a 1/8-inch layer of paraffin. Cover with lids. Yield: 3 1/2 cups.
