Mix sour cream and soups; add 1/2 the cheese and mushrooms, along with the chiles. Divide the soup mixture in half and add to the shredded chicken. Fill the flour tortilla shells and roll. Place in a 9 x 13-inch pan. Pour the remaining soup mixture over the top of tortillas and sprinkle with green onions and remaining cheese. Bake for 30 minutes at 350\u00b0.
