Sprinkle yeast and sugar in water; stir to dissolve. Set aside to proof. Measure flour and salt into a large bowl; make a well and stir in yeast mixture. Knead dough until it is smooth. Dough will be sticky at first. Use olive oil on hands to facilitate kneading. A few drops at a time will surface. Knead dough at least 10 minutes, until it is no longer sticky. Grease top of dough; turn dough to coat surface. Cover bowl; set aside 1 hour to rise.
