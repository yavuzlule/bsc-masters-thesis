Melt 1 tablespoon butter in 2-cup glass measuring cup (about 30 seconds). Whisk in 1 tablespoon flour and salt and pepper to taste. Gradually add 1 tablespoon white wine and 1 cup milk. Whisk until smooth. Cook on High for 2 to 3 minutes, stirring every minute, until sauce thickens. Stir in 1/4 cup shredded cheese until melts.
