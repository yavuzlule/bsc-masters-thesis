Dissolve yeast in 1/4 cup water. Combine butter, salt and sugar in separate bowl. Add 1 cup hot water; beat in egg. When this cools, add yeast mixture. Add flour. Place in greased glass bowl and cover with foil. Leave in refrigerator 2 to 12 hours. Take out in time to rise again. Press in pan. Use floured knife or spatula to separate dough, creating pull apart rolls. Bake 15 to 18 minutes at 425\u00b0.
