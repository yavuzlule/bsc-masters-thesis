Combine jello powder, crushed pineapple and water in large saucepan. Cook for 5 minutes, stirring constantly. Combine cream cheese with about 1 cup of mixture; slowly beat with electric beater. Add to rest of cooked jello mixture. Let stand in refrigerator until slightly jelled, 1 to 2 hours. Fold in Cool Whip. Sprinkle with nuts. Refrigerate in a 9 x 13-inch pan for 2 hours.
