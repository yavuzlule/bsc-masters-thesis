Combine meat, onion and garlic in a large casserole. Cover and cook at High setting for 10 minutes or until meat loses its reddish color, stirring once or twice. Add remaining ingredients, mixing thoroughly. Cover casserole and heat until mixture comes to a boil. Reduce microwave temperature to Slow or Defrost and cook for about 1 hour, stirring a few times. After cooking, allow chili to stand for a few minutes before serving.
