Preheat a 10-inch browning dish 5 minutes at 100% power. Pour in oil. Add onions, chicken strips and broccoli. Stir until sizzling stops; cover. Microwave at High power for 6 to 9 minutes or until chicken is no longer pink and broccoli is tender-crisp. Add mushrooms and soy sauce mixture, stirring to coat chicken. Microwave 2 to 3 minutes or until sauce thickens slightly, mushrooms are tender and mixture is heated throughout.
