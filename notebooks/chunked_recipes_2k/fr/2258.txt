Clean groundhog good. Put in pot and bring to a boil; cook for 10 minutes. Turn head down, cook for 20 minutes more. Remove from pot. Put in a baking pan. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 1 1/2 hours or real tender. Add 1 cup of water from the pot to bake in. Add all ingredients 1/2 hour before groundhog is done.
