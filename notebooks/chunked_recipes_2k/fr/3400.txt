Preheat oven to 325\u00b0. Beat eggs; add sugar and beat well. Add milk, cornstarch and flavorings. Mix well. Stir in rice, raisins and nutmeg to taste. Pour in 2-quart baking dish. Sprinkle with additional nutmeg. Set in pan of hot water. Bake for 2 hours, until knife inserted comes out clean. Stir several times while baking to prevent raisins and rice from settling to bottom.
