Sift flour, measure and sift again with salt and soda. Cream shortening and sugar, add eggs one at a time, bearing well after each addition. Add dates, nuts, orange peel and vanilla. Add sifted dry ingredients to creamed mixture alternately with buttermilk. Then into well-greased loaf pan. Bake for 1 1/2 hours at 300\u00b0. When baked, pour orange topping mixture over cake and allow to age for at least 24 hours.
