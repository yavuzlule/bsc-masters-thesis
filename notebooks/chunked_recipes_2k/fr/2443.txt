Combine ingredients. Add scallops and toss to coat. Cover and refrigerate 1 hour (stir occasionally). Remove scallops from marinade. Reserve. Thread scallops on 4 skewers. Place skewers in shallow microwave dish and cover with plastic wrap. Vent corner. Cook 3 to 4 minutes on High (100%). Rotate dish 1/4 turn halfway. Let stand 2 minutes.
