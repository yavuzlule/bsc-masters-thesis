Mix together the first 9 ingredients. Then in separate bowl, beat cream cheese, egg, sugar and salt until creamy. Add chocolate chips and mix thoroughly. Fill miniature muffin cups 1/2 full of cake mixture. Top with 1 teaspoon of cream cheese mixture. Sprinkle chopped nuts over top. Bake for 20 to 25 minutes at 350\u00b0. Yields about 50.
