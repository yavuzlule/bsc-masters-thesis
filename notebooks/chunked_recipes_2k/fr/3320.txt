Melt butter and shortening in large saucepan over low heat. Stir in cocoa. Combine flour, baking powder and salt; stir in chocolate mixture. Add nuts and mix well. Spread in well-greased 13 x 9 x 2-inch baking pan. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 30 to 35 minutes. Spread frosting while brownies are still warm.
