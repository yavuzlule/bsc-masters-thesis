Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. Mix soup and sour cream together. Add chicken and lemon juice; mix well. Pour into casserole dish (9 x 13-inch). Melt butter. Mix together with crackers and poppy seed. Sprinkle over chicken mixture. Bake until cracker crumbs are crisp and casserole is heated through, about 30 to 40 minutes.
