Melt butter in a large skillet over medium heat; add zucchini, carrots and onion and saut 8 to 10 minutes or until tender. Remove from heat; stir in sour cream and next 3 ingredients. Spoon into lightly greased 1-quart baking dish. Sprinkle with breadcrumbs. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 20 minutes or until thoroughly heated. Yield: 4 servings.
