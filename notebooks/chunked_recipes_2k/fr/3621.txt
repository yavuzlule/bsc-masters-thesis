Cut apples into 1/2 inch slices. Cut each squash in half lengthwise and remove seeds. Peel squash and cut into 1/2 inch slices. Arrange in a lightly greased 13 x 8 x 2-inch baking dish and top with apples. Combine remaining ingredients, mixing well. Spoon over apple slices. Cover tightly and bake at 350\u00b0 for 1 hour and 15 minutes or until squash is tender.
