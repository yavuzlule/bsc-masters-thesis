Drain beans and corn; layer in bottom of oven-safe baking dish. Add salt and pepper to taste. Mix onion, cheese, sour cream and soup in separate bowl. Pour over vegetables and spread to edges of dish. Mix crackers and butter. Crumble over top of soup mixture. Bake, uncovered, at 350\u00b0 for 35 to 45 minutes or until crackers brown and mixture is bubbly.
