Cook the first 5 ingredients until thick; cool until lukewarm. Add the eggs, 2 teaspoons sugar and the softened yeast. Mix well, then add the flour. Knead well; place in greased bowl. Let rise for 1 hour or more. Punch down; roll out. Cut with biscuit cutter. Let rise until doubled in a greased pan. Bake at 365\u00b0 for 15 minutes.
