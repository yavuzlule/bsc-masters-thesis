Cut chicken breasts diagonally into thin strips. Heat one tablespoon oil in large skillet or wok. Stir-fry chicken strips and garlic until chicken loses its pink color. Remove from pan and set aside. Heat the remaining tablespoon of oil. Stir-fry the onion, zucchini and green pepper for 3 minutes. Add mushrooms and tomatoes. Continue to stir-fry an additional 3 minutes. Return chicken to skillet. Add spaghetti. Stir-fry until heated through. Serves 3 to 4.
