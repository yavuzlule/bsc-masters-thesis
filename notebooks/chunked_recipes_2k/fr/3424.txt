In saucepan, stir in sugar and flour. Add water and mix well. Stir in raisins, salt and cinnamon; cook and stir over medium heat until bubbly. Cook and stir 1 minute more. Remove from heat; stir in butter. Pour into pastry lined pie plate. Top with lattice crust. Bake at 375\u00b0 for about 45 minutes or until crust is golden brown. Yields 6 to 8 servings.
