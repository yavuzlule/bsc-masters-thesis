Prepare brownies first: Mix eggs and sugar. Melt butter and add cocoa, then add the flour, salt, vanilla, and nuts. Mix. Bake at 350\u00b0F for 30 min. in a 9 x 13 in. pan. Top with marshmallows. Bake 3 min. , longer. , (Marshmallows till not be completely melted. ). Cool on a wire rack. In a saucepan combine the chocolate chips, peanut butter, and butter. Cook and stir over low heat until smooth. Remove from heat, stir in cereal. Spread over brownies. Refrigerate.
