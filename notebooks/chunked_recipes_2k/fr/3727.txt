Combine sugar, butter, extract and juice. Beat in eggs, one at a time, until smooth. In a separate bowl, mix flour, baking powder and salt. Stir in the wet ingredients alternately with the milk. Add the lemon rind and pecans. Pour batter into a greased 4 1/2 x 8 1/2-inch loaf pan. Bake for about 1 hour or until a toothpick comes out clean.
