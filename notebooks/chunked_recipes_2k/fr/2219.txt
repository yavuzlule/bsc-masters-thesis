Mix the dry ingredients. Stir in the liquids and mix well. Grease a mold*. Fill not more than 2/3 full. Put on the cover. Place on a rack in a deep kettle. Add boiling water to come halfway up around the mold. Cover. Steam 3 to 3 1/2 hours, keeping water boiling. Add more water as necessary. Run a knife around the inside of the mold to loosen the loaf to remove it.
