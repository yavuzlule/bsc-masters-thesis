In skillet crumble and brown sausage and onion; drain. In a mixing bowl blend together milk, mushroom soup and pepper. In 9 x 9-inch casserole dish layer one half of potatoes, one half of soup mixture, one half of grated cheese and one half of sausage. Repeat; cover with foil. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 1 1/2 hours. Uncover; sprinkle a little more cheese over top. Return to oven until cheese is melted.
