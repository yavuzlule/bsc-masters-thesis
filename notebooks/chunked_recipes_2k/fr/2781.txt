Preheat oven to 375\u00b0. In large bowl, beat milk and butter until smooth. Add biscuit mix and vanilla; mix well. Shape into 1-inch balls. Roll in sugar. Place 2-inches apart on an ungreased baking sheet. Do not flatten. Bake 6 to 8 minutes or until lightly browned (do not overbake). Press Hersheys Kisses in center of each ball immediately after baking. Cool. Store tightly covered at room temperature.
