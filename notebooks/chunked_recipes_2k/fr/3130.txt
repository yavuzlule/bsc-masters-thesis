Sift flour, sugar, baking powder and salt into mixing bowl. Add the beaten egg. Mix in milk and vegetable oil just until ingredients are mixed. Stir in chips and orange peel. Line muffin tins with paper liners or grease tins. Fill 2/3 full with batter. Sprinkle nuts on top. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 20 to 25 minutes until lightly browned.
