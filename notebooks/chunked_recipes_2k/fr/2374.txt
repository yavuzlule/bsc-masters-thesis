Beat cream cheese on medium speed with mixer until light and fluffy. Gradually add sugar, mixing well. Add eggs, one at a time, beating well after each addition. Stir in vanilla. Place a vanilla wafer in a paper-lined cup of a muffin pan. Spoon cream cheese mixture over wafers, filling cups. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 20 minutes. Leave in muffin pan overnight in refrigerator; top with blueberry pie filling to serve.
