In a bowl, stir together mushroom soup, milk, dry onion soup and undrained can of mushrooms. Reserve 1/2 cup of soup mixture and set aside. Stir uncooked rice and vegetables into remaining soup mixture. Place rice mixture into a 12 x 7 1/2 x 2-inch baking dish. Arrange chicken pieces atop. Pour reserved soup mixture over chicken. Sprinkle paprika on top. Cover tightly with foil. Bake at 375\u00b0 until rice is tender, 1 1/4 to 1 1/2 hours.
