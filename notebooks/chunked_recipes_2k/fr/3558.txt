In a 2-quart saucepan, melt butter over medium heat. Mix in flour; cook and stir 1 minute. Gradually whisk in milk, sherry, chicken granules and salt; cook and stir until smooth (about 8 minutes). Gradually stir in scallops, shrimp and mushrooms. Cook, stirring gently, just until scallops and shrimp are cooked through; only about 10 minutes.
