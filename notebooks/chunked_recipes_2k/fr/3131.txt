Sift flour, baking powder, salt and sugar. Add milk and melted butter to dry ingredients; stir until smooth. Spread in greased baking pan, 12 x 8 x 2-inches. Spread sliced peaches or your favorite fruit over batter in pan. Bring sugar, butter and juice to a boil; pour over fruit. Bake, uncovered. Sauce will be in bottom of pan. Serve warm. Bake at 375\u00b0 for 45 to 50 minutes. Serves 8.
