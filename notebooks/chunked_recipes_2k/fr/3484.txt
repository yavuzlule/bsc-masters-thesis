Cook squash and onion in water for 5 minutes. Drain. Mix together soup and sour cream. Fold in shredded carrot, squash and onion. Melt margarine in medium saucepan. Stir in stuffing mix. Spread half of stuffing in greased 8 x 8 x 2-inch baking dish. Pour vegetable mixture over stuffing and top with other half of stuffing. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 30 minutes. Let stand about 15 minutes before serving.
