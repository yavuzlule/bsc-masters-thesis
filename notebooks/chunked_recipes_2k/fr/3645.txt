In a small saucepan, soften gelatin in 1/2 cup of the orange juice. Stir in undrained, crushed pineapple and remaining orange juice. Chill until partially set. Fold in pears and Equal and turn mixture into a 6-cup mold. Chill until firm. Unmold onto a plate. Makes 8 servings. Calories per serving:, 111.
