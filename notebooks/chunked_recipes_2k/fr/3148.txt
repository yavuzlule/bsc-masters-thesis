Combine squash, onion and water in saucepan. Bring to a boil; reduce heat and simmer 5 to 10 minutes or until squash is tender. Drain and mash. Combine squash, cheese, pecans, mayonnaise, pepper, egg, sugar and salt, stirring well. Spoon into lightly greased 2-quart casserole. Combine cracker crumbs and butter, stirring well. Sprinkle over casserole. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 40 minutes or until thoroughly heated. Yield: 6 to 8 servings.
