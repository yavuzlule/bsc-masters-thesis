Cream the eggs. Crisco and sugar, then add dry ingredients alternately with milk; mix well. Chill dough overnight or a couple of hours. Roll out and cut into desired shapes. Bake in a 350\u00b0 oven for 8 to 10 minutes or until light brown. Frost when cool. Makes about 3 dozen. If the cookies are rolled out thin, they will be harder than they would be if rolled thicker.
