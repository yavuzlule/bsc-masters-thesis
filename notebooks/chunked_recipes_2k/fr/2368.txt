Line a 9 x 12-inch pan with whole graham crackers. Prepare pudding mix as directed on package. Let stand 5 minutes and blend in whipped topping. Spread half the pudding mixture over the crackers. Add sliced bananas over pudding. Add another layer of graham crackers. Spread blueberry pie filling on crackers. Spread rest of pudding mixture over blueberry pie filling. Chill 3 hours .
