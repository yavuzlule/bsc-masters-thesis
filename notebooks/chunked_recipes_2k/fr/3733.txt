Combine tapioca pudding and orange jello with 3 cups cold water. Cook until clear or until boiling stage over medium heat. Cool. Drain the crushed pineapple and mandarin oranges. Fold Cool Whip, miniature marshmallows, crushed pineapple and mandarin oranges into the cooled mixture. Place in 9 x 13-inch pan or jello mold and refrigerate until set.
