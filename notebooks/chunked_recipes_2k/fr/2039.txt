Liberally spray large nonstick skillet with cooking spray. Add oil and heat over medium heat. Add garlic and onions. Heat until soft (approximately 3-4 minutes). Fill large saucepan with 2 inches of water. Place chicken breast in steaming basket or stainless steal saucepan with lid. Bring water to boil and steam chicken for 7 minutes. Remove from heat and let set in steam until fully cooked. Remove chicken from basket and let cool. When cool, cut into 1 inch chunks. Meanwhile, cook penne according to packaging. Drain and keep warm. Add chicken chunks, olives, basil, and oregano to skillet with onions. Turn to medium heat for 5 minutes. In large bowl, toss chicken with hot penne pasta. Sprinkle top with cheese and serve.
