Cut beef into narrow strips; brown slowly in oil 15 to 20 minutes. Add onion, garlic, salt and pepper. Stir, cooking 1 to 2 minutes longer. Dissolve bouillon cube in the hot water. Add to meat; cover skillet and simmer 20 to 30 minutes. Add tomatoes and green pepper; cook 10 minutes longer. Uncover skillet and combine cornstarch, water and soy sauce. Mix and bring to a boil. Lower heat and simmer 5 minutes or until thick and clear, stirring constantly. Serve over hot rice. Add extra soy sauce, if desired.
