Heat oven to 400\u00b0. Combine flour, 1 cup of oats, sugar and baking powder. Cut 1/2 cup butter in until crumbly. Remove 1 cup of mixture and combine with 1/4 cup oats. Cut in 1/4 cup butter until crumbly; set aside. Mix water and remaining oat mixture with fork, just until moistened. Press in bottom of 9-inch deep-dish pie pan. Bake 15 minutes. Cool slightly.
