Cook broccoli; drain. In a bowl, mix soup, sour cream, flour, onion, salt and pepper. Heat. Remove from heat. Add broccoli; stir gently. In a saucepan, melt margarine and add dressing. Mix well. Pour broccoli mixture into a 2-quart casserole dish. Top with dressing mixture. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 30 to 35 minutes. Serves 6.
