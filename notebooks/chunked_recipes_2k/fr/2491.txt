Drain spinach thoroughly, squeezing out liquid. In skillet, cook sausage until brown; drain fat. Stir in tomato sauce; set aside. In large bowl, combine drained spinach, cottage cheese. Parmesan and egg; spread mixture in bottom of 12 x 7 1/2 x 2-inch baking dish. Spoon sausage mixture over spinach mixture. Top with Mozzarella; cover and chill several hours or overnight. Before serving, sprinkle croutons over cheese. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 40 minutes. Serves 8. Contains 344 calories, 20 gr. fat, 86 mg. cholesterol.
