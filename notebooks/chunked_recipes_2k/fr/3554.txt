Spray 9 X 13 inch pan. Mix pineapple, and cherry pie filling in the bottom of a 9 X 13 inch pan. Sprinkle dry cake mix over fruit. Then slice sticks of butter (place over cake mix). Bake 20 minutes at 350\u00b0 or until golden brown. Prep time - 3 minutes. Cool time - 20 minutes. Serves 16 to 20. Good and so easy. Top with whipped cream if desired when serving.
