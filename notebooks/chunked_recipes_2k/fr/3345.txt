In large heavy kettle, heat oil; add meat. When meat is browned, add onion, garlic, nutmeg, chili powder, sliced onion, tomato paste, water and red pepper. Simmer until meat is tender. A half hour before serving, heat peanut butter and oil in small saucepan. Stir over medium heat 5 minutes. Add beef stew and simmer over low heat 20 minutes. Serve over rice.
