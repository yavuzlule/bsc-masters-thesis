Combine crumbs, milk and heat to boiling. Cook, stirring constantly about 1 minute. Remove from heat; add small amount of eggs and mix well. Stir into balance of hot mixture. Add remaining ingredients. Mix well. Pour into greased 8 1/2 x 4 1/2 x 2 1/2-inch loaf pan. Place in shallow pan of hot water (approximately 1-inch deep). Bake at 375\u00b0 for 1 hour. Serve hot with sliced ripe olives. Makes 6 servings.
