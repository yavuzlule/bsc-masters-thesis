Remove skin from chicken; rinse. Sprinkle with paprika, salt and pepper. Place chicken in a crock-pot. Stir together soup and sour cream; spoon over chicken. Cook on low 8 to 9 hours. The \natural\ gravy is delicious spooned over rice. , (May be cooked on high for half the time, but dont add the sour cream until the last 30 minutes. )
