Cream the butter and sugar and add the egg yolks one at a time. Beat thoroughly after each addition. Blend in the grated lemon and orange rinds, salt, and lemon juice. Bake in a 9 inch pastry shell or 6 individual tart shells, in a 350\u00b0 oven for 25 to 30 minutes. Serves six. Do not use light butter/oleo, it doesnt work.
