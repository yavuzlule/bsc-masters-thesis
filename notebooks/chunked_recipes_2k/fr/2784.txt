Pare 1 cucumber. Cut both into slices (reserve 6 slices for garnish, refrigerate wrapped). Pour 1/4 cup buttermilk and 1/2 cucumber slices into blender, beat until smooth, add remaining slices, salt, pepper and onion. Beat until smooth. Stir in remaining buttermilk. Cover and refrigerate at least 2 hours or very cold. Serves 6 to 8. Add garnishes before serving. , (Cut up chives may also serves as garnishes. )
