Mix cream cheese, margarine and flour. Roll into ball. Chill for 1 hour or overnight. Pinch out 8 balls. Press balls into muffin tin with fingers, working up to the top. Set aside. In a separate bowl, beat egg. Add brown sugar and mix well. Add margarine, vanilla, almond extract, white syrup, salt and chopped pecans. Put 1 tablespoon of the mixture into each cup. Bake at 325 degrees for 25 minutes.
