Beat eggs in separate container. Mix other ingredients. In large skillet, lightly buttered, put sausage mix. Add eggs; stir until eggs are softly cooked. Remove from heat. Place eggs in spaghetti strainer (something to let the juice drain through). Soften flour tortillas. Take 1 tortilla; place 1 slice of cheese in center, then 2 tablespoons of mixture. Wrap in wax paper. Can be frozen. Yields 10 to 12 burritos.
