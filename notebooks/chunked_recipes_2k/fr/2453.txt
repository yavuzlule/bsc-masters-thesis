Beat eggs until foamy, stir in applesauce, sugar, oil and milk. Gradually add, dry ingredients, stir in nuts and candied fruit. Spread in greased 9 x 5 x 3 inch pan. Sprinkle on top cinnamon, brown sugar and chopped pecans, press lightly with hand. Bake 350\u00b0 for 1 hour. Can sprinkle confectionary sugar to cover top last 1/2 hour if too brown.
