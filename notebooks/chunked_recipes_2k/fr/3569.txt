Cook chicken (save broth) and rice. Soak bread in broth. Add chicken, rice, celery, salt, pimento and soup. Mix well. Stir in eggs. Place in greased 9 x 13-inch pan. Bake at 325\u00b0 for 1 hour or until firm. Cut in squares and serve with chicken or mushroom soup on top. , (The mixture will seem runny but it firms up when baked. ). Easy recipe and delicious!
