Combine turkey, bread crumbs and garlic powder. Form into balls. Spray large skillet with cooking spray. Brown turkey balls on all sides; drain. In same skillet, heat Butter Buds and saute onions. Add flour. Using wire whisk, add instant broth and water, stirring until gravy consistency. Bring to a boil. Add turkey balls and simmer 10 minutes. Stir in yogurt but do not boil, just heat slightly. Serve over hot noodles. Serves 6.
