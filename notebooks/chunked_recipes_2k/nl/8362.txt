Melt butter over low heat. Remove, and, stir, in brown sugar. Stir well. Cool and stir in, eggs. Sift dry ingredients together and stir in, then add, vanilla and nuts. Spread in well-greased 9 x 13-inch pan. Bake at, 350\u00b0, for, 20 to 25 minutes or when lightly touched with, finger in center, slight imprint remains. Cut while warm.
