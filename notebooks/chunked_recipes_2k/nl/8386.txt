Cut a thin slice off the top of each roll and reserve. Hollow out roll bottoms, leaving 1/2-inch shells; reserve bread pieces. Tear tops and bread pieces into pea size pieces (3/4 cup soft bread crumbs). Stir together bread crumbs and egg. Add sausage; mix well. Shape mixture into 16 (1 1/2-inch) meatballs. Arrange meatballs in an 8 x 8 x 2-inch baking dish. Cover with vented clear plastic wrap. Microcook meatballs on High 5 1/2 to 7 1/2 minutes, or until juices run clear, turning meatballs over and rotating dish once or twice. Drain.
