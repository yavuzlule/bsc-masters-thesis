Combine flour, salt and pepper in a small bowl; set aside. Combine eggs, 1 1/2 teaspoons oil and water; mix well. Clean mushrooms with damp paper towels. Dredge mushrooms in flour mixture; dip in egg mixture. Roll in breadcrumbs. Chill at least 1 1/2 hours. Deep fry mushrooms in hot oil (375\u00b0) until golden brown. Drain on paper towels. Serve mushrooms with Tartar Sauce. Yields 32 appetizers.
