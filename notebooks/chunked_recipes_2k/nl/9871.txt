Mix together 1 cup flour, margarine and sugar well. Put into 8-inch square pan and bake 15 minutes at 350\u00b0. Mix eggs, lemon juice, confectioners sugar, 1 tablespoon flour and soda well and spread over crust. Bake 25 minutes more. Cool. Cut into squares and sprinkle with confectioners sugar. Yields 16 (2-inch) squares.
