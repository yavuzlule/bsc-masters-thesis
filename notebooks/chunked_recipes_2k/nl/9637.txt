Mix all ingredients adding flour (cup at a time) last. May require a bit more or less flour; dough should be easy to roll and not too sticky. Roll out to about 1/8-inch thick (dont make it too thin); cookies are supposed to be soft and fluffy, not thin and crusty. Place on cookie sheet, not too close together and bake at 375\u00b0 for about 8 to 10 minutes, depending on thickness. When done, the backs of the cookies should be a very light shade of tan (not dark!). Remove from cookie sheet and place on a flat surface to cool. After cookies are completely cooled, you can frost and decorate. Makes about 10 to 12 dozen.
