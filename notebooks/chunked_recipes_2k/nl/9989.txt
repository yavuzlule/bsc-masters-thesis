Saute celery, green pepper and onion in butter until tender and golden brown; set aside. Heat chicken in broth and add mushroom soup, mushrooms and sauteed mixture. Grease a 9 x 14-inch Pyrex dish. Put half of spaghetti in the bottom of the dish, then half of the chicken mixture. Repeat layers. Top with grated cheese. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 30 minutes.
