Mix together the flour and sugar and set aside. Mix together the cocoa. Crisco, butter and water. Bring to a boil, then pour over flour and sugar. Mix together the buttermilk, soda, vanilla and eggs. Add to the mixture. Pour in 11 x 16-inch greased pan. Bake at 400\u00b0 for 15 to 20 minutes or until done. Start making frosting 5 minutes before cake is done.
