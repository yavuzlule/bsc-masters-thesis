Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. Place butter in a square pan and melt in oven. Sprinkle in crumbs and stir well. Press mixture to bottom of pan. In a bowl, mix milk, sour cream and lemon juice. Spread evenly over crumbs. Spoon pie filling evenly over creamy layer. In a small bowl, mix nuts and cinnamon. Sprinkle over pie gently. Bake 20 to 25 minutes or until set. Cool slightly. May be served warm or cold.
