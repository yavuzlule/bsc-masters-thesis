In a 10-inch ovenproof skillet, cook onion and green pepper in the oleo. Cut ten 1-inch pieces of frankfurters; set aside. Thickly slice remaining franks; add to skillet. Stir in the beans, soup, corn and reserved liquid. Heat to boiling, stirring occasionally. Roll each biscuit to a 3-inch circle; spread with mustard. Place a reserved frank in the center. Wrap dough around frank; seal and make a 1/2-inch slit in the top. Arrange biscuits, seam sides down; bake at 375\u00b0 for 15 to 18 minutes.
