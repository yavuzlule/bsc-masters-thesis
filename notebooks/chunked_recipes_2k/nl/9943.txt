Combine sugar, oil and eggs; beat well. Combine dry ingredients. Add sugar mixture alternately with buttermilk, beating well after each addition. Stir in nuts and coconut flavoring. Pour batter into a well-greased and floured 10-inch tube pan. Bake at 325\u00b0 for 1 hour and 5 minutes, or until cake tests done. Pour hot Coconut Syrup over hot cake. Leave cake in pan at least 4 hours.
