Melt shortening and margarine until it bubbles; add to sugar. Stir in the molasses. Dissolve soda in boiling water and add to mixture. Sift in approximately 4 pounds flour. Add spices along with the flour. The dough should be almost stiff enough to roll. Cover and let set overnight. Do not refrigerate. On a floured, cloth-covered board, roll dough as thin as possible. Cut with cookie cutters and place on a greased pan. Bake at 275\u00b0 for exactly 10 minutes. Let cookies cool before removing from pan. Makes 7 to 8 pounds of cookies.
