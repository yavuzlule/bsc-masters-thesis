Put 4 slices cubed bread on bottom of a greased 9 x 13-inch Pyrex dish. Add chicken mixture. Top with 4 slices of cubed bread. Mix eggs and half and half cream. Pour over casserole. Let stand, covered in refrigerator, overnight. Next day, spread soup over mixture. Top with grated Cheddar cheese. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 1 hour. Serve immediately. Serves 10.
