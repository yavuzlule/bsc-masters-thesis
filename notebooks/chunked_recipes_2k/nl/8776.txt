Brown hamburger and drain well. Add diced cheese, parsley, salt, pepper, bread, milk and egg. Mix well. Boil noodles and stuff with meat mixture. Put some spaghetti sauce on bottom of cake pan and put noodles in (tightly). Top with remaining sauce. Cover with foil and bake 1/2 hour at 350 - 375 degrees. Remove foil and top with parmesan cheese and a bit of parsley for color. Bake an additional 15 minutes.
