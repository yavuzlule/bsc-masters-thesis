In a skillet, saute onion and green pepper in margarine until tender. Remove from heat. Stir in soup, mushrooms, pimientos and basil; set aside. In large bowl combine noodles, chicken and cheese. Add mushroom sauce and mix well. Transfer to a greased 9 x 13-inch pan. Bake, uncovered, at 350\u00b0 for 40 to 45 minutes or until bubbly. Sprinkle with crumbs. Bake 15 minutes longer. Yield:, 12 to 15 servings.
