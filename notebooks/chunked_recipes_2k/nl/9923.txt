In bowl, combine zucchini, salt and water. Cover and let stand for 2 hours. Drain and rinse well. Combine all ingredients in large pan. Bring to boil, stirring. Boil 5 minutes, stirring. Fill hot jars, leaving 1/2-inch space. Pour hot vinegar mixture over zucchini. Adjust lids. Process in boiling water bath 15 minutes. Yields 4 to 5 pints.
