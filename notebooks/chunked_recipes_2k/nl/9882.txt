In large mixing bowl, beat egg until foamy. Add Rice Krispies, catsup, water, onion, dry milk, salt and pepper. Mix well; add ground meat. Mix only until combined. Portion using 1 level tablespoon. Shape into meatballs. Place in single layers in well-greased or foil-lined shallow baking pan. Bake at 400\u00b0 for about 12 minutes or until well browned.
