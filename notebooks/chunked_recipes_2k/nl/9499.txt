Melt chocolate chips with 14 ounces of almond bark in a large saucepan over low heat, stirring until smooth. Remove from heat. Stir marshmallows, cereal and peanuts together. Pour into greased 12-inch pizza pan. Top with cherries. Sprinkle with coconut. Melt remaining 2 ounces almond bark with oil over low heat, stirring until smooth. Drizzle over coconut. Chill until firm. Store at room temperature.
