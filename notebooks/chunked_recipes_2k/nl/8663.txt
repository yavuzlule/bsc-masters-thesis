Slice beef across the grain into thin strips. Heat oil in skillet; cook beef until browned, stirring often. Remove beef from skillet. In remaining 1 tablespoon hot oil, cook broccoli and onion with garlic powder until tender-crisp, stirring often. Add soup, water and soy. Heat to boiling. Return beef to skillet. Heat through, stirring occasionally. Serve over rice. Serves 4.
