Trim tips and extra flap of fat on wings. Mix ingredients in stainless, plastic or glass bowl. Marinate wings 3 or 4 hours. (Overnight is better. ). Pat dry on paper towels, both sides. In a single layer, saute in butter to brown both sides. Brush with more hot sauce. Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. Bake on a cookie sheet lined with foil (it gets messy) for 45 to 55 minutes. Dont overcook. Wings can be turned over, but its not necessary. Serve with a Blue cheese dressing or dip.
