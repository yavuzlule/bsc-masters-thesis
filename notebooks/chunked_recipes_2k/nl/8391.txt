Peel, wash, and, slice, the, potatoes crossways; drain. Place the 4 cups, of, cooking, oil, in a frypan and heat until hot. Place the potatoes, in the frypan a few at a time. Cook until a light, brown;, remove, potatoes and remove the cooking oil from the frypan. Pour, 1 cup of water into the frypan and add the sliced onions. Let, the onions cook for 5 minutes and add the potatoes. Sprinkle, the, flour, over the potatoes and cook over a slow fire for 10 to 15 minutes, stirring occasionally. Serves 6 to 8 people.
