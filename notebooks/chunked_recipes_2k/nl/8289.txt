Wash the rice and set aside to drain. Heat the butter in a nonstick pan. Add cinnamon stick, cardamon pods, bay leaves and cloves and stir for 1 1/2 minutes. Add salt and water (8 cups) to rice, then bring to boil, stirring it a couple of times. Reduce heat to low. Add raisins, almonds and pistachios. Cook, covered, for 10 minutes. Serve piping hot.
