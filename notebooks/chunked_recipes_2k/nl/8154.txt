Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. In a bowl, combine spinach, artichokes, cream, salt, pepper and 1/3 cup Parmesan cheese. In a small bowl, mix cream cheese until fluffy. Add milk and mix until well blended. Spoon mixture (spinach) into shallow casserole or an 8 x 8 glass baking dish. Pour cream cheese mixture over spinach. Sprinkle remaining Parmesan. Bake 25 to 30 minutes.
