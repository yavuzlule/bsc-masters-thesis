Heat oven to 375\u00b0. Beat margarine and sugar in a large bowl until light and fluffy. Add eggs, one at a time, beating well after each addition. Add chocolate, instant coffee and rum extract; mix well. Stir in flour and 1 cup chopped walnuts; pour into baked pie shell. Decorate top with 1/2 cup walnut halves or pieces. Bake in lower third of oven for 25 minutes. Cool on wire rack. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour before serving. Store in refrigerator.
