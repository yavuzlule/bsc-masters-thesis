Crumble ground chuck in large pot. Cover with water, salt and pepper to taste. Start cooking on high. Chop up onion and celery. Skim off excess grease. Take tomato sauce and dump in. Rinse can with water and add to meat mixture. Let boil and add homemade noodles. Cook just until noodles are done. Soup will be thick so add more broth, sauce or water if you like. This is delicious with garlic bread and cole slaw.
