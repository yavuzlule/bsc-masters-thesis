Heat oven to 375\u00b0. Mix flour and baking soda in a small bowl. In a large bowl, beat peanut butter, butter, sugars and vanilla with electric mixer until fluffy. Beat in eggs. Then add flour mixture gradually. Mix. Stir in chips. Drop by teaspoons onto ungreased cookie sheet. Bake 8 to 10 minutes. Cool. Then enjoy. Serves 100 cookies.
