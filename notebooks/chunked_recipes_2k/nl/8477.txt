Mix all ingredients together for marinade. Place chicken pieces in single layer in shallow baking pan (12 to 15 pieces). Pour marinade over chicken. Marinate 4 to 6 hours or overnight in refrigerator. Bake at 325\u00b0 for 1 1/2 to 2 hours. Turn once and baste with sauce. Serve sauce over rice. Serves 6 to 8.
