Melt 1/2 cup oleo in large shallow baking pan. Pare potatoes and slice crosswise into 1/4-inch slices. Coat both sides of potato with oleo in baking pan and place 1 layer deep. Sprinkle with onion salt, pepper and chili powder. Bake at 425\u00b0 until browned, about 25 minutes. , (The potatoes can be cut into wedges instead of crosswise. )
