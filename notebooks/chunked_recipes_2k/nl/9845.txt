Preheat oven to 350 degrees. In large bowl, combine vegetables, soup, 1/2 c. cheese, sour cream, pepper, pimento and 1/2 can French Fried Onions. Pour into shallow 1-qt. casserole. Bake, covered, for 30 minutes or until vegetables are done. Sprinkle remaining cheese and onions in diagonal rows across top; bake, uncovered, 5 minutes or until onions are golden brown.
