Sprinkle 1/4 cup flour (taken from amount called for in recipe), over, blueberries. Sift, together, remaining, flour, baking powder, salt, and, sugar. Beat egg one minute, add milk and melted, butter. Combine, flour, mixture, and, egg mixture together and beat, just long enough to hold together. Fold in blueberries with spoon. Bake in muffin pan (greased) 15 to 20 minutes at 425\u00b0. Makes 12 muffins.
