Cream butter or margarine; add sugar and beat well. Mix coffee, vanilla and egg until dissolved, then add to butter mixture. Beat until blended. Add flour and almonds. Beat 1 to 2 minutes. Drop from teaspoons onto greased cookie sheet; leave 2 inches between cookies. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 12 to 14 minutes, until lightly browned around edges. Cool 1 to 2 minutes; remove from pan with spatula. When cold, store in airtight container.
