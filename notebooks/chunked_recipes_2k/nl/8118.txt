Mix graham crumbs and 1 stick melted oleo together and pat into a 9 x 13-inch pan. Beat confectioners sugar, 2 sticks oleo, eggs and vanilla for a minimum of 14 minutes. Layer first crumbs, filling, pineapple, sliced bananas. Cool Whip, cherries and lastly top with nuts. Refrigerate 14 hours or overnight. Excellent for a party.
