Prepare cake mix using eggs, water and oil as directed in basic recipe. Reserve 2 cups of yellow batter. Pour remaining yellow batter into prepared 10-inch tube pan. Combine 1 cup reserved batter, cherries and red food coloring. Spoon over yellow batter. Blend remaining yellow batter, marble pouch, chocolate chips and 2 tablespoons water. Spoon over batter. Swirl batter in pan to marble. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 35 to 45 minutes.
