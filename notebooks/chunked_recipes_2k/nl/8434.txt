Shell can be substituted for other type crust (6 ounce size). Beat whipped topping, 1 cup milk and vanilla in large bowl with electric mixer on high speed 6 minutes or until topping thickens and forms peaks. Add remaining milk and pudding mixes; blend on low speed. Beat on high speed 2 minutes, scraping bowl occasionally. Spoon into pastry shell. Refrigerate 4 hours. Makes 8 servings.
