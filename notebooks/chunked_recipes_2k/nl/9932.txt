Combine mayonnaise, barbecue sauce, grated onion, lemon juice and pepper in a small bowl; stir well. Cover and chill dressing mixture thoroughly. Press chopped tomato between several layers of paper towels to remove excess moisture. Arrange lettuce on individual salad plates; top each serving with tomatoes and chopped cooked chicken. Just before serving, spoon dressing mixture over salads; sprinkle with crumbled bacon and garnish with egg slices. Yield:, 4 servings.
