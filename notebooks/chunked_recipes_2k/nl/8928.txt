Preheat oven to 325\u00b0. Butter Bundt type cake pan. Coat with pecans. Beat pudding, cake mix, rum, oil, water and eggs together just until smooth. Pour into pan and bake 1 hour. Cool cake. Poke holes in cake. Heat butter, sugar and rum until sugar is dissolved. Dribble over cake. Let set several hours or overnight. Remove from pan. Yumm!
