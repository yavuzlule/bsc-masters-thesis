In, a, mixing, bowl,, combine, ground beef, one package brown gravy mix, 1/2 cup water, onion, onion powder and garlic powder. Shape, in, 6, or, 8 patties and place in pan at least 1-inch deep;, broil, in oven, turning to brown on both sides. When browned,, combine, remaining package of gravy mix with 2 cups, of hot, water;, pour, over patties after they have been drained of grease. Place in oven again and turn heat to 450\u00b0 (425\u00b0, glass). When gravy begins to boil, leave in oven until it, thickens a little, about 10 minutes depending on pan size. Serves 6 to 8.
