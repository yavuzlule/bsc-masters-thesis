Brown onion and hamburger in skillet; drain. Place smashed Doritos in bottom of 9 x 13-inch dish. Combine tomato sauce, red beans, taco sauce and green chiles and tomatoes with hamburger. Pour over Doritos. Bake for 30 minutes in a 350\u00b0 oven. Place cheese on top and bake for another 10 minutes. Serves 8 to 10.
