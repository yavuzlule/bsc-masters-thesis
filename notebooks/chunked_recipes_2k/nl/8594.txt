Cream shortening with 1 cup sugar until light and fluffy. Add egg and molasses; mix well. In separate bowl combine flour, soda, cinnamon, salt, ginger and cloves. Add about 1/4 of dry mixture at a time to creamed mixture. Mix until smooth after each addition. Chill 1 hour. Roll dough into 1-inch balls. Roll in sugar. Place 2 inches apart on ungreased cookie sheet. Bake at 325\u00b0 for 10 minutes.
