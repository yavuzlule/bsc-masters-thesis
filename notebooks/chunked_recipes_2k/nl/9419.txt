Combine corn, potato, onion, green pepper, ham, tomato, salt and pepper. Melt, butter, in, large, skillet. Add, corn mixture. Pat down evenly. Cook about 10 minutes until bottom begins to brown. Turn, hash\tand, continue, cooking another 5 minutes, loosening from pan frequently with spatula. Sprinkle with shredded cheese. Makes 4 to 6 servings.
