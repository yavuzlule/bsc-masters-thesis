Grease an angel food cake pan with shortening. Put nuts and raisins in bottom of pan. Cut dough lengthwise in half. Cut each half into 6 pieces. Arrange in pan, then sprinkle with butterscotch pudding and cinnamon. In saucepan, mix the melted butter, sugar and brown sugar. Bring to a boil. Remove from heat and pour over dough. Cover pan with a cloth and let rise overnight (about 8 hours). Bake at 350\u00b0 for 30 minutes. Remove from oven and let set 2 to 3 minutes. Invert pan on plate and serve. Preparation time:, 20 minutes.
