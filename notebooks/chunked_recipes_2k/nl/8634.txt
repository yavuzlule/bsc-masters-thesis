In 2 ounces oil, fry chicken until slightly brown; set aside. Saute onion, garlic and pepper in remaining 2 ounces oil in large Dutch oven. Add chopped tomato and simmer until soft. Add water. Vigo flavoring, bay leaf and salt. Stir well; bring to a boil. Add chicken and simmer until the chicken is tender, 20 to 30 minutes. , (You may want to take the chicken off the bone and return to pot. ) Bring back to a boil; add rice and simmer (without lifting lid) for 25 to 30 minutes or until done. Garnish with pimento and green peas.
