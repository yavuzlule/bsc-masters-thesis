In a large skillet, saute onion and garlic in oil until tender. Add tomatoes, oregano, basil, salt and pepper. Cover; simmer for 20 minutes. Place ziti in an ungreased 11 x 7 x 2-inch baking dish. Cover with sauce. Bake, uncovered, at 350\u00b0 for 20 minutes. Sprinkle with both cheeses. Bake 10 minutes longer, or until cheese melts. Yields 6 to 8 servings.
