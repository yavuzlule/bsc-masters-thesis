Cook zucchini in boiling salt water until just tender. Drain. In saucepan, melt 4 tablespoons butter. Add carrots and onion. Saute until tender. Remove from heat. Stir in 1/2 cup stuffing cubes, soup and sour cream. Gently stir in zucchini. Spoon into 1 1/2-quart casserole. Melt 2 tablespoons butter. Add remaining stuffing cubes and toss gently. Sprinkle over casserole. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 30 to 40 minutes.
