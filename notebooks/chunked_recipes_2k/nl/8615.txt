Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. Combine apples and sugar in a mixing bowl. Blend oil, vanilla and egg whites with apples and sugar. Combine the dry ingredients and mix in well. Stir in raisins and lemon rind. Pour into 8-inch square cake pan. Bake 35 to 40 minutes in a lightly greased pan until toothpick comes out dry.
