Heat oven to 350\u00b0. Brown meat and drain. Add green pepper and onion. Cook until tender. Stir in tomatoes, tomato sauce, water and seasonings. In a 2-quart casserole dish, layer half the noodles, half the meat sauce and half the Velveeta cheese. Repeat layers. Sprinkle with Parmesan cheese. Bake 30 minutes.
