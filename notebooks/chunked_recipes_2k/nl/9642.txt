In a mixing bowl mix chicken, cream of chicken soup and cream of mushroom soup. Cut up onion in small pieces; add evaporated milk and 1/2 small can of mild green diced chilies. Mix all this together; set aside. Layer bottom of 8 x 8 or 9 x 9-inch pan with crushed Doritos. Put shredded cheese over top of mixture. Cook for 40 minutes at 300\u00b0.
