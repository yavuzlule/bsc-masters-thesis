In a covered saucepan, simmer cranberries and water for 20 to 25 minutes or until tender. Press through a sieve or food mill. Return to saucepan. Add apples, cover and simmer for 35 to 40 minutes or until apples are tender but retain their shape. Add sugar. Simmer for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally. Yields 8 to 10 servings.
