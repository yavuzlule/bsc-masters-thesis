Open quahaug and split in half. Loosen completely from shell, cutting, through, muscle. Saute onion in butter until yellow. Remove, from heat;, add the other ingredients and mix well. Cool slightly,, then, firmly pat about a tablespoon on top, of each quahaug half (in shell). Sprinkle red pepper and parsley, on top. Top, each, one with 1/4 slice of raw bacon. Place, in, large shallow, pan, and, bake, in 350\u00b0 oven for 30 minutes.
