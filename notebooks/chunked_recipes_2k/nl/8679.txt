Brown beef and garlic. Drain. Blend cottage cheese. Mozzarella and mayonnaise in bowl. Stir in beef mix. Fill manicotti noodles. Place in baking dish. Sprinkle with remaining filling. Cover completely with sauce. Sprinkle with oregano and Parmesan cheese. Cover and bake 15 minutes at 325\u00b0. Uncover and bake 10 minutes more.
