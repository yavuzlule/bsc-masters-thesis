Prepare gelatin according to package directions. Pour into 2 individual ring molds and chill until firm. Unmold onto lettuce and fill with 1 fruit exchange each: 1/2 diced banana, 1/2 chopped apple sprinkled with cinnamon or 1/2 cubed pineapple. Serves 2. Exchange Per Serving: 1 fruit. Calories Per Serving: 40.
