Let cream cheese soften out of the refrigerator for about 1/2 hour. Preheat oven to 375\u00b0. Beat cream cheese, egg and vanilla extract together for 1 minute. Add sugar gradually together with other ingredients. Do not overbeat. Batter will be slightly lumpy. Pour batter into graham cracker Ready-Crust. Bake at 375\u00b0 for 45 minutes. Let cool and top with cherry pie filling and Cool Whip.
