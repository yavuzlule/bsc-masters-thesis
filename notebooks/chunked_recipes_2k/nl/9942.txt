Preheat oven to 400\u00b0. In a medium bowl, combine Fiesta Herb with Red Pepper soup mix and tortilla chips. In a large plastic bag or bowl, combine chicken and egg, beaten with water, coating well. Dip chicken in tortilla mixture, coating well. In a 15 1/2 x 10 1/2 x 1-inch jelly roll pan sprayed with no-stick cooking spray, arrange chicken and drizzle with margarine. Bake, uncovered, for 12 minutes or until chicken is done. Makes about 24 chicken strips. Can be served with salsa.
