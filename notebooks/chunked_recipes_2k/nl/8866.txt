Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. In large bowl, mix all ingredients except pie filling. Spread half of batter in a 9 x 13 x 2-inch greased pan. Top with pie filling. Dollop remaining batter over fruit, covering as much of the filling as possible. Swirl in batter; uncovered fruit peeking through can be expected. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 35 to 40 minutes or until top is browned. For ultimate flavor, serve warm.
