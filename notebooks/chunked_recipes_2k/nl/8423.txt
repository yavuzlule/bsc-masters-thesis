Mix flour, salt and baking soda; set aside. Mix margarine and peanut butter; add both kinds of sugar. Mix well. Add eggs and beat well. Stir flour mixture into peanut butter mixture. Drop dough from a teaspoon onto baking sheet. Flatten with a fork. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 10 to 15 minutes until lightly browned. Yields 4 to 5 dozen.
