Heat oil in a Dutch oven. Brown meat. Add onions, garlic. Worcestershire sauce, bay leaf, oregano, salt, pepper and water. Simmer, covered, 2 hours or until meat is tender. Add potatoes and carrots. Continue to cook until vegetables are tender, about 30 to 45 minutes. Mix flour and cold water; stir into stew. Cook and stir until thickened and bubbly. Remove bay leaf.
