Sprinkle dry yeast over warm water. Heat cottage cheese to lukewarm. Combine in mixing bowl with sugar, onion, butter, dill, salt, soda, egg and yeast mixture. Add flour to form a stiff dough beating well after each addition. Cover; let rise in warm place 50 to 60 minutes. Stir down dough. Turn into greased 1 1/2-quart casserole. Let rise in warm place until light 30 to 40 minutes. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 40 to 50 minutes. Brush with butter and salt.
