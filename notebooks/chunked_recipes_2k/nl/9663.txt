In a small bowl, mix the cream cheese and sugar together until fluffy. In a large bowl, mix the milk, vanilla and pudding together until fluffy. Add cream cheese mix to pudding mix; stir until blended. Fold in Cool Whip. Chill for 7 hours. Remove middles from 10 cookies, then chop into fine powder. Use these for top layer. Chop centers and rest of cookies to fine powder. Layer cookies and Cool Whip mixture in a flowerpot, starting with cookies. Add flower. Keep cool until serving.
