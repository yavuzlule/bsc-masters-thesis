Slice apples. Place sliced apples in a lightly greased 13 x 9 x 2-inch baking dish. Combine 1/2 cup sugar, nutmeg and cinnamon. Sprinkle on the sliced apples. Position plastic mixing blade in food processor bowl; place flour, brown sugar and butter in bowl. Top with cover and process until mixture resembles coarse meal. Sprinkle mixture over apples. Bake, uncovered, at 300\u00b0 for 1 hour. Serve warm with ice cream, if desired. This is a really good dish. Makes 8 servings.
