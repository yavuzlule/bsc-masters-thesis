Melt butter over low heat in an 8-inch square pan. Sprinkle with brown sugar. Arrange fruit over sugar. Heat oven to 350\u00b0. Mix Bisquick and 3/4 cup sugar. Add soft shortening, egg and 1/4 cup of milk. Beat vigorously. Stir in 1/2 cup of milk gradually and vanilla. Beat 1/2 minute. Pour batter over fruit in prepared pan. Bake 35 to 40 minutes. Invert pan at once on a serving plate. Allow pan to remain over cake for a few minutes.
