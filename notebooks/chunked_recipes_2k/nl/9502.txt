Preheat oven to 400\u00b0. In a large bowl, pour boiling water over sugar. Crisco and salt; let cool. Meanwhile, dissolve yeast in lukewarm water and let set 5 minutes. Stir in 1 tablespoon sugar; add to cooled water mixture. Beat eggs thoroughly and put into water mixture. Mix 4 scant cups of flour into water and mix with mixer. Add 4 more scant cups of flour and stir by hand.
