Do not stir any of the ingredients in the first step. Sift cocoa and sugar in a large mixing bowl. Add milk and lay margarine sticks on top. Do not stir. Cook on High for 6 minutes. Remove from microwave; stir thoroughly. Add vanilla flavoring and nuts; stir until well mixed. Pour into buttered platter. Cool in refrigerator for 1 hour. Cut and serve. Do not double this recipe.
