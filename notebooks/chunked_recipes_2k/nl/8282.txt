Bake cake according to directions on box. Let, cake cool, then crumble. Fix vanilla pudding according to directions on box. In a separate bowl combine cherries, coconut and pineapple. In a large bowl layer 1/2 cake, then 1/2 pudding and 1/2 fruit mixture. Top with Cool Whip. Repeat process again, topping with Cool Whip. Refrigerate until serving time.
