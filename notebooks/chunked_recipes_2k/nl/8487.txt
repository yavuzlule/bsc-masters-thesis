Mix floured pecans. Melt butter and pour over flour and pecans mixture. Mix well and press into bottom of large dish. Bake at 350\u00b0 until brown. Cool. Blend cream cheese, confectioners sugar and 1 cup Cool Whip. Spread over cooled crust. Mix pudding and milk. Spread over cream cheese layer. Put Cool Whip on top of strawberry. Decorate with strawberries, if desired.
