Mix all ingredients but chicken. Pour liquid in a gallon size plastic bag with zipper seal. Place wings in bag. Seal the bag to coat the wings. Place bag in 9 x 13-inch pan and marinate overnight. Pour contents of bag in pan and bake 1 hour in 375\u00b0 oven. After 1 hour, drain the liquid from the pan and bake for another 15 minutes. Remove and serve hot.
