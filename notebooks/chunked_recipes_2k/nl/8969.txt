Layer saltines on cookie sheet lined with foil. In saucepan, melt butter, add brown sugar. Bring to a boil and cook 3 minutes. Spread over crackers and bake 5 minutes at 350\u00b0. Sprinkle morsels over crackers, let stand 5 minutes, then spread. Cool in refrigerator until hard. Remove foil and break into pieces. Tastes like Heath bars.
