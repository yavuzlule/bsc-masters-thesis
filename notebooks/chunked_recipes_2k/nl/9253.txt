Heat oven to 350\u00b0. Spread apples evenly in an 8 x 8-inch square or 9-inch round baking dish/pan. Sprinkle with water, cinnamon and salt mixture. Toss lightly. Mix sugar, flour and butter with a pastry blender until crumbly. Spread over apples. Bake at 350\u00b0, uncovered, about 40 minutes. Serve warm with milk, cream or ice cream.
