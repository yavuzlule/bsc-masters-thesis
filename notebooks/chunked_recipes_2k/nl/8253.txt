Wash seeds. Steam seeds (colander inside a pot). Put water into the bottom of the colander or strainer. Cook covered at a fairly high heat about 30 minutes; add more water if needed. Dump seeds on paper towel and pat dry. Spread on cookie sheet. Pour a little oil (just enough to make seeds shiny when you stir them around). Sprinkle with salt. Bake at 300\u00b0 for about 1/2 hour. Eat for snack.
