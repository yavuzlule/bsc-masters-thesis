Beat eggs until light and fluffy; add sugar, orange juice, salt and oil and blend thoroughly. Add enough flour to form medium dough, which leaves bowl clean. Knead until smooth. Cut into large pieces of dough; place on flat surface sprinkled with flour and roll as thin as possible. Cut in strips 2-inches wide. Deep fry in hot vegetable oil, twirling strips immediately with fork to shape Diples. , (May also be shaped into bows. ). Deep fry until lightly browned. Serve with syrup, cinnamon and crushed walnuts.
