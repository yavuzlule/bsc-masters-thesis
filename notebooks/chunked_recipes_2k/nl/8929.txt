Combine rice and broccoli. Mix egg, onion. Worcestershire sauce, salt and milk. Stir into the rice-broccoli mixture. Pour into a greased 2-quart casserole. Top with melted butter and shredded cheese. Sprinkle with paprika. Bake in slow oven (325\u00b0) for 30 to 40 minutes. Makes 6 generous servings.
