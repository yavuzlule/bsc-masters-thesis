In first bowl, beat cream cheese and beaten egg. In a second bowl, mix flour, oats, brown sugar, baking powder, salt and cinnamon. In a third bowl, mix whole egg with oil; add to bowl 2 after beaten. Add 1/2 tablespoon cream cheese to muffin cups and then fill 2/3 full with flour mixture. Bake at 400\u00b0 for 15 to 18 minutes (or until done). Makes 10 muffins.
