Slice flank steak lengthwise with the grain into 1/4-inch strips. Combine soy sauce, garlic, salt and pepper; mix with steak strips and let stand about 15 to 20 minutes. Drain. Arrange in single layer on a rack set in shallow baking pan. Bake in very slow oven (150\u00b0) overnight or 12 hours, until dried. Cut into 1-inch pieces. Store in airtight container.
