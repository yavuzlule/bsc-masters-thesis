Mix together first 3 ingredients. Stir in next 2 ingredients. Stir in last 2 ingredients. Roll into balls the size of a small walnut. Place balls, about 3 inches apart, on greased baking sheet. Flatten with a fork, dipped in flour, making a crisscross pattern. Bake 8 to 10 minutes in a moderate oven (350\u00b0 to 375\u00b0), no more. Makes about 50 cookies.
