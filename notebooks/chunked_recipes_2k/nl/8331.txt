Brown the hamburg. Layer in roaster or 5-quart casserole dish the hamburg, onion, green pepper, mushrooms, salami, pepperoni, potatoes, salt and pepper and 1/2 of diluted soup. Sprinkle each layer with cheese, ending top layer with soup mixture. Cook, covered, in oven at 350\u00b0 for 2 hours, or until potatoes are well cooked.
