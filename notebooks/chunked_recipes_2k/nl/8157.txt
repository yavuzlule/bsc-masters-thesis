Brown ground beef; drain. Add cumin, chili powder, garlic powder, salt, peppers and tomatoes. Heat through. Cover bottom and sides of 13 x 9 x 2-inch baking dish with tortillas. Pour beef mixture over tortillas. Place a layer of tortillas over meat mixture and set aside. Combine cottage cheese. Monterey Jack cheese and egg. Pour over tortillas. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 30 minutes. Remove from oven. Sprinkle with Cheddar cheese, tomatoes, green onions, olives and lettuce. Yields 6 to 8 servings.
