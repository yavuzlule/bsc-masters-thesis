Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. Combine soup, water or milk and rice. If using chipped beef, rinse off the salt and pat dry. Place some of the beef on the bottom of a 9 x 13 casserole dish and place chicken on top. Add additional beef on top of each chicken piece. Add cheese, then pour soup mixture over all. Cover with foil and bake for about 1 hour.
