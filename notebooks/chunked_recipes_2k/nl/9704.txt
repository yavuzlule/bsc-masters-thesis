Combine sugar and 2 cups water in small saucepan. Bring to a boil; boil 1 minute. Remove from heat. Add sliced lime or orange. Cool to room temperature. , (Can be made ahead. Cover and refrigerate up to 24 hours. ). Strain into pitcher; add lemon juice and 5 cups more water. Pour into ice filled glasses. For pink lemonade, add 2 drops grenadine or 1 tablespoon cranberry juice to each glass.
