Measure out sugar and set aside. Measure juice and put into dishpan. Add pectin and butter. Bring to boil, stirring frequently. Add sugar, stirring frequently, and bring to a rolling boil. Boil for 1 minute. Skim off foam, if necessary. Add to hot, sterilized jars. Wipe rims of jars after adding liquid; place flats on and screw on lids tightly. Invert jars for 30 minutes and then turn upright, making sure jars are not touching and are not in a draft.
