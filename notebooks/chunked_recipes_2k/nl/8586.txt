Preheat oven to 375\u00b0. Combine corn, soup, milk, celery, 2/3 cup French fried onions, cheese and bacon bits in large bowl. Spoon mixture into 2-quart square baking dish. Cover; bake 30 minutes or until hot and bubbly. Stir; sprinkle with remaining 2/3 cup French fried onions. Bake, uncovered, 3 minutes or until fried onions are golden. \tMakes 4 to 6 servings.
