Melt over hot (not boiling) water the chocolate morsels with shortening; set aside. Stir together and set aside flour, baking powder and salt. Beat until thick the eggs and sugar. Add flour mixture, chocolate mixture and vanilla. Blend. Pour into 8-inch square, greased, wax paper lined pan. Sprinkle chopped nuts over top. Bake at 375\u00b0 for 25 minutes. Yields 16 (2-inch) squares. Allow to cool before cutting.
