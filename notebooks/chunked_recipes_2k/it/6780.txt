In a very large mixing bowl, mix butter and sugars together. Blend in eggs and vanilla. Add peanut butter; mix well. Stir in oats, chocolate pieces and baking soda. Mix well. Drop 1/4 cup measure 4 inches apart on ungreased cookie sheet. Flatten with a fork to 2 1/2-inches in diameter. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 10 to 12 minutes. Cool on wire racks. Store tightly covered.
