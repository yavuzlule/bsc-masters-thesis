Line, jelly, roll pan with foil. Place soda crackers 7 down and, 5, across. Boil, butter, and brown sugar for 2 1/2 minutes after a rolling boil. Pour over soda crackers. Bake in, 350\u00b0 oven, for, 5 minutes. Take out of oven and sprinkle chocolate chips on top. Let set for a minute and then spread over crackers. Add walnuts. Cut into squares while warm.
