Boil chicken until tender, remove meat from bones. Cut chicken into bite size pieces. Place chicken in greased 13 x 9 x 2 inch casserole dish. Add soup and broth, stir together until mixed. Melt 1 stick margarine and add stuffing, mix stirring to coat. Pour over casserole and spread evenly. Bake at 375\u00b0 approx. 15 - 20 minutes or until top is browned and casserole is heated thoroughly.
