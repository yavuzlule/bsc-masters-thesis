Cook shells and set aside. In saucepan, saute garlic and mushrooms in margarine. Mix with cottage cheese, spinach and Mozzarella cheese. Spoon cottage cheese mixture into cooked shells and place in bottom of 9 x 13-inch pan sprayed with Pam. Pour spaghetti sauce over shells and sprinkle with Parmesan cheese. Cook at 350\u00b0 for 30 minutes or until done.
