Melt butter in pan and saute onion, celery and garlic. Add flour, cooking about 15 minutes until mixture is light tan in color. Add half and half; add cheese. Cook 10 minutes. Add pimentos, mushrooms and seafood. Cook about 20 minutes. Mix with cooked noodles. Place mixture in 9 x 13-inch pan. Bake, uncovered at 375\u00b0 for about 5 to 10 minutes until hot.
