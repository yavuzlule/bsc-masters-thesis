Cook and mash 3 cups sweet potatoes. Add egg. Add sugar, vanilla, nutmeg, milk, salt and 1 tablespoon butter. Melt 1/2 stick oleo or butter in casserole. Add layer of crushed cornflakes, layer of sweet potatoes mixture and a layer of miniature marshmallows (1 cup). Repeat above 3 layers. Sprinkle a few crushed cornflakes over top layer of marshmallows. Cover. Bake 15 minutes in 350\u00b0 oven. Uncover, brown and serve.
