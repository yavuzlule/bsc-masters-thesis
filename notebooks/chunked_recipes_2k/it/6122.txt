Peel, core and fine dice the apples or \coarse grate\ in processor. Place sugar and water in deep pan over low fire. While syrup is cooking, make up one batch of Best Biscuit mix. Roll dough 1/4-inch thick in a rectangle. Spread apples evenly over dough. Then roll up and cut as for cinnamon rolls. Place in a 9 x 13-inch pan. Dot with oleo and sprinkle cinnamon on top.
