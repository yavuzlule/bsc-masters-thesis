Preheat oven to 375\u00b0. Brown sausage; drain. Add bread cubes, broccoli, mushrooms and cheese. Mix lightly. Spoon into a 9 x 13-inch baking dish or 2-quart casserole. Pour combined eggs, milk, dressing, flour and seasonings over bread mixture. Cover and refrigerate one hour or overnight. Bake, uncovered, for 1 hour. Makes 8 servings.
