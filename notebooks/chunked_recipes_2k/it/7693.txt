Clean and boil potatoes whole with skins, leave firm, 15 minutes. Peel potatoes while still hot. Put in plastic bag. Seal and refrigerate overnight. Butter casserole; grate potatoes. Put grated potatoes in buttered casserole with salt and pepper to taste. Pour 1 3/4 cups heavy cream into casserole. Bake 1 hour at 350\u00b0 uncovered.
