Saute the zucchini, onion and carrots in margarine until tender. Add garlic to desired taste. In a bowl, mix together well the sour cream, chicken soup, and croutons. Add to sauteed zucchini and carrots. Place in a 13 x 9-inch baking dish sprayed with Pam. Sprinkle with extra croutons, if desired. Heat in a 350\u00b0 oven for 20 to 25 minutes.
