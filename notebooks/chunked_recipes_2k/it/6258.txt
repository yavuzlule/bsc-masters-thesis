Cut, potatoes into thin slices to measure about 4 cups. Melt 2 tablespoons margarine in large heavy skillet. Arrange potato slices in 3 layers, each 1 slice thick, sprinkling each layer with 1/2 teaspoon salt and dash of pepper. Dot top layer with, 2 tablespoons, margarine. Cover; cook over medium heat for, 20 minutes. Uncover, and cook until potatoes are brown, turning once. Yield:, 4 to 6 servings.
