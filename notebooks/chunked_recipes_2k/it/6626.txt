Tear aluminum, foil into squares, large enough to cover and seal, over, meat, and, vegetables. Season meat with salt, pepper and, garlic, salt. Place, on, foil. Place, a slice of onion along with potatoes, bell pepper and carrots, enough for an individual, serving, on, top, of, meat. Salt and pepper as desired. Put, 4 tablespoons of bouillon over meat and vegetables. Wrap and, seal, well. Bake, in, 400\u00b0 oven for about 45 minutes. We also, cook these on the barbecue pit but increase cooking time depending on heat from coals.
