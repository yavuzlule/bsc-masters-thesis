Place Starter in refrigerator for 3 to 5 days. Mix together well and add to Starter 3/4 cup sugar, potato flakes and 1 cup warm water. Let Starter stand out of refrigerator for 8 to 12 hours. Stir well. Take out 1 cup to make bread or to give away. You must take out at least 1 cup or Starter will deflate.
