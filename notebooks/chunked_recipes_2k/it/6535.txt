Spread one package of rolls on a greased 10 x 13-inch cookie sheet. Mix the cream cheese, 1 cup sugar, egg yolk and vanilla. Spread on top of rolls. Put another package of rolls over. Beat egg white slightly; brush on rolls. Mix 1/4 cup sugar, cinnamon and nuts; sprinkle on top. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 35 minutes.
