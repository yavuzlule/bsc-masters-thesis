Melt 2 tablespoons butter in a large saucepan. Saute onion in butter until brown. Add washed, drained spinach to onion and cook, covered, for 3 to 5 minutes. Drain and chop. Return spinach to a saucepan. While spinach is cooking, work remaining butter and flour together with a fork. When spinach is cooked, lower heat and add butter-flour mixture a little at a time until all is smoothly incorporated. Add milk and seasonings. Reheat. Use as a garnish with other vegetables. Serves 6.
