Cut corn from cob, about 4 to 5 cups of corn. Melt butter or drippings in skillet; add corn and saute quickly for about 5 minutes. Add bacon and salt; arrange in buttered casserole in alternate layers with the sliced tomato. Place in oven at 350\u00b0 for about 30 minutes or until corn is tender. Serve hot. Makes 5 to 6 servings.
