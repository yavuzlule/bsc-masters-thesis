Rub roast on all sides with flour. Sprinkle with salt and pepper. Heat oil in deep, heavy, nonstick Dutch oven. Add beef. Brown about 10 minutes at medium heat, turning roast often. Turn heat to lowest setting. Add herbs, mustard, ketchup, red wine, water and bay leaf. Cover and cook slowly for 1 1/2 hours, adding water if liquid boils down too dry. Add mushrooms and cover. Simmer 1/2 hour or until beef is tender. Remove meat and mushrooms to platter. Keep warm.
