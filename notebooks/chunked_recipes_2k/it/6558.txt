Break spaghetti into pieces and cook. Drain. Put chicken, pimento, green pepper and chopped onion in a casserole. Mix water and soup; add to chicken mixture. Add 1 1/4 cups cheese and spaghetti; season to taste. Toss lightly until well mixed and coated with sauce. Sprinkle with remaining cheese and bake at 350\u00b0 for 45 minutes.
