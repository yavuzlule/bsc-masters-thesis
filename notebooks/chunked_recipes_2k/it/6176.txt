Preheat oven to 400\u00b0. Shake chicken in bag of flour and seasonings. Line 9 x 13-inch glass baking dish with foil; add margarine and place dish in oven until margarine sizzles. Place chicken in dish, skin down. Bake for 30 minutes; turn and bake another 30 minutes. Do not cover. Yields 4 to 6 servings.
