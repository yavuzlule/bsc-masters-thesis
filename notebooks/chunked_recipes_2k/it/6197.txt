Brown 1 clove of garlic in a tablespoon of oil. Remove from flame and cool slightly. Add 1 large can of tomato sauce or puree,, one can tomato paste and 4 cans water. Add 1 teaspoon salt, 2 teaspoons granulated sugar, garlic powder (2 shakes), sprinkle of black pepper and 1 or 2 basil leaves. Bring to a boil; lower flame and simmer for 2 hours. Serves 6 to 8.
