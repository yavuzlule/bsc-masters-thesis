Mix together margarine, flour and pecans and spread into a lightly greased 13 x 9-inch pan. Bake 20 minutes at 350\u00b0. Let cool. Combine cream cheese, powdered sugar and 1 cup Cool Whip. Spread over baked crust. Combine pudding mix and milk. Mix well. Pour over cream cheese mixture. Chill 1 hour. Spread remaining Cool Whip over top. Sprinkle with nuts or chocolate curls, if desired.
