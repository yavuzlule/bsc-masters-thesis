Discard outer cabbage leaves. Wash rest and chop or shred fine. Saute in oil/fat until browned. Soak bread in water; squeeze thoroughly and mix with cabbage. Put in a large mixing bowl. Add rest of ingredients, blending well. Grease a 10-inch tube pan well; pour in mix and bake at 325\u00b0 for 1 hour and 15 minutes or until firm.
