In a large Pyrex or tin cake pan, put butter (melt in oven in pan) and graham cracker crumbs (with 2 tablespoons sugar). Sprinkle the crumbs over bottom of Pyrex dish, then add a layer of each:, coconut on top of that, chocolate chips on that. Eagle Brand milk over that, then nuts. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 25 minutes. Cut in squares.
