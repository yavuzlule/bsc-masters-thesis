Preheat oven to 250\u00b0. Place roast in a Dutch oven and arrange potatoes, carrots and onion around and over roast. Sprinkle herbs and seasonings over top. Drizzle wine over top. Roast for 4 1/2 hours, covered. Place meat and vegetables on platter. Place pan juices on high heat. Blend margarine and flour to a smooth paste and stir into juices. Cook until thickened. Spoon some over roast and sprinkle with parsley. Pour remaining gravy into gravy boat. Makes 8 servings.
