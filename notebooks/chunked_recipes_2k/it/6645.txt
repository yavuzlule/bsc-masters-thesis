Boil 2 quarts water to which has been added the next 6 ingredients. Add shrimp tails; cook on medium heat until shrimp turn pink. Remove shrimp; save stock. Make a dark roux of next 4 ingredients. Keep roux soft; add shrimp stock, salt, hot sauce and tomato sauce. Simmer until tomato color is dark. Add shrimp and crab meat or lobster with oysters. Serve over hot rice.
