Heat oven to 350\u00b0. Grease 2 (9 x 5 x 3-inch) loaf pans. Cut shortening into flour, sugar, baking powder, soda, salt and orange peel. Add water to juice to measure 1 1/2 cups. Blend juice with cheese and eggs. Mix juice and cheese mixture with dry ingredients. Fold in cranberries and nuts. Bake for 60 to 70 minutes. Let stand for 8 hours before slicing.
