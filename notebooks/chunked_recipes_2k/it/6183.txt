Stem, wash, drain and coarsely chop spinach leaves. Set aside. Cook pasta as package label directs until al dente; drain. In pan, combine oil and garlic. Saute over medium heat about 2 minutes, or until garlic is golden. Add spinach, pepper flakes and salt; saute until spinach wilts. Remove from heat. Add pasta; toss to coat well and mix. Makes 4 servings.
