Preheat oven to 350\u00b0 for metal pan or 325\u00b0 for glass dish. In a 9 x 13-inch pan, melt butter in oven. Sprinkle crumbs over butter; pour milk evenly over crumbs. Top with remaining ingredients in order given. Press down firmly. Bake 25 to 30 minutes or until lightly browned. Cool. Cut into bars. Condensed milk will spread more evenly if can is placed in hot water.
