Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. Grease and flour a 9 x 13-inch pan. Prepare cake mix according to box directions. Bake as directed. Cool for 5 minutes. Poke holes with wooden spoon handle over top of cake, 1-inch apart. Spoon Eagle Brand milk into holes. Warm hot fudge sauce in microwave until soft. Spoon into holes. Wait 30 minutes. Mix crushed Heath bars and Cool Whip and spread on top. Cover and refrigerate.
