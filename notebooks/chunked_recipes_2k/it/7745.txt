Heat oven to 425\u00b0. Wash chicken and pat dry. In oven, melt butter and shortening in baking pan. Mix Bisquick and seasonings in plastic bag. Shake 2 or 3 pieces at a time in bag to coat thoroughly. Place chicken skin side down in single layer in pan. Bake 40 minutes. Reduce oven temperature to 375\u00b0. Turn chicken; bake 20 minutes longer. Serves 6.
