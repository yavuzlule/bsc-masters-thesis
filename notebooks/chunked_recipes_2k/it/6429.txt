Rinse chicken pieces and pat dry. Set aside. Combine rice, chicken soup and water in crock-pot. Stir well to mix in soup. Place chicken pieces in a see-through browning bag. Add onion soup mix. Shake bag to coat chicken pieces thoroughly. Puncture 4 to 6 holes in bottom of bag. Fold top of bag over and place in crock-pot on top of rice. Cover and cook on low for 8 to 10 hours. Remove chicken pieces to warm platter. Serve with rice.
