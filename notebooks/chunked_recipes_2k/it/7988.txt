Combine flour and 2 tablespoons sugar in medium bowl with pastry blender or 2 knifes; cut in cold margarine until mixture resembles coarse crumbs. Sprinkle in water, 2 tablespoons at a time, mixing lightly with a fork after each addition, until pastry just holds together. Cover dough; refrigerate 15 minutes. Roll pastry on lightly floured surface into an 11-inch circle; cover loosely with plastic wrap and set aside.
