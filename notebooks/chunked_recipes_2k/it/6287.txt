Drain juices from cherries and pineapple. Heat juices to boiling and pour over Jell-O, stirring until dissolved. Let cool until starting to gel. Beat cheese with mixer until very soft. Stir in pineapple, cherries and nuts. Add to Jell-O mixture and blend well. Then stir in Coca-Cola and blend again. Pour into oiled molds to congeal. Makes 1 large mold or 2 medium size molds.
