Crush Oreos into crumbs (food processor). Put 1 cup in bottom of a 9 x 13-inch buttered pan. Combine Cool Whip, cream cheese, sugar and butter in a large bowl. Beat until creamed. In separate bowl, put pudding, milk and vanilla. Mix well. Combine pudding and cream cheese mixture; pour over crumbs, then cover with rest of crumbs. Press down to level. Refrigerate or freeze for at least 12 hours.
