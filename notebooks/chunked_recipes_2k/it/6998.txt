Mix meat, rice, water, onion, salt, garlic salt and celery salt in bowl. Shape into balls. Place into a 9 x 9-inch or larger baking dish. Mix tomato sauce, 1 cup water and Worcestershire sauce. Pour over meatballs. Cover with foil. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 45 minutes. Remove foil and cook 15 minutes longer. Makes 12 to 15 meatballs.
