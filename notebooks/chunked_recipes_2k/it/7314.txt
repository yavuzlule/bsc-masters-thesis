Cook unpeeled potatoes in water until soft, but firm. Drain and cut into 1-inch cubes. Place in an ungreased 2 1/2-quart baking dish. Add pimientos, onion, garlic and onion salt. Toss gently. Sprinkle with cheese. Place bread cubes on top and pour oleo over. Bake uncovered at 350\u00b0 for 30 minutes or until bread is toasted.
