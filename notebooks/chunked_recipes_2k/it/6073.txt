Mix in medium bowl the butterscotch mix, 2/3 cup dry milk and 1 1/4 cups of water. Mix well with wire whisk. Pour into pie crust. Mix in bowl the chocolate pudding mix, remaining 2/3 cup dry milk and 1 1/4 cups of water. Mix well with wire whisk. Pour over butterscotch layer. Chill until set. Spread whipped topping over chocolate layer. Sprinkle pecans and mini chocolate chips. Ready to serve.
