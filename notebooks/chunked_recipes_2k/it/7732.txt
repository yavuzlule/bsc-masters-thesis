Cook mushrooms and onions in 4 tablespoons butter. Remove vegetables with slotted spoon. Put aside. Blend flour, salt (1/8 teaspoon) and dash of pepper into pan drippings. Add 3/4 cup water, milk, bouillon, lemon juice and nutmeg. Cook and stir until bubbly. Add all vegetables. Turn into 1-quart casserole. Combine bread crumbs and melted butter. Sprinkle around edge. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 20 minutes. Makes 6 servings.
