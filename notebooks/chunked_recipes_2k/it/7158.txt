Brown meat and add garlic, parsley flakes and basil. Blend tomatoes and tomato paste in food processor or blender. Add to meat mixture and simmer 1 hour. Mix cottage cheese, eggs, salt, pepper. Parmesan cheese and parsley. Cook noodles until tender. Layer in a 9 x 13-inch pan. First layer noodles, then cottage cheese mixture, then Mozzarella cheese and then meat. Repeat. Bake at 375\u00b0 for 30 minutes.
