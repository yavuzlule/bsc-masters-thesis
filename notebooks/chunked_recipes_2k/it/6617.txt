Mix equal parts of olive oil and Cabernet; marinate steaks overnight. Prepare grill. Saute garlic and mushrooms in oil until soft. Strain stewed tomatoes, reserving juice; dice tomatoes and add to the sauteed garlic and mushrooms. As the tomato, garlic and mushroom mixture reduces, slowly add the reserved juice from the tomatoes and 1 cup of Cabernet as the sauce thickens. Grill the steaks to desired doneness. Once turned, add cheese as one would to a cheeseburger. Cover steaks with sauce and serve with J. Lohr Estates Seven Oaks Cabernet. Serves 4.
