Brown meat and drain. Add taco seasoning and canned tomatoes. Heat through. Cover the bottom of a 9 x 13-inch pan with tortillas. Pour beef mixture over tortillas. Add another layer of tortillas over beef. In a small bowl combine cottage cheese. Monterey Jack and egg. Mix and pour over tortillas. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 30 minutes. Sprinkle top with remaining ingredients.
