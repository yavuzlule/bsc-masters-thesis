Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. Generously grease and lightly flour a 10-inch tube or Bundt pan. Prepare filling; set aside. In large mixer bowl, combine sugar, oil and eggs. Beat 1 minute at high speed. Add remaining ingredients except filling; beat 3 minutes at medium speed, scraping bowl occasionally. By hand, stir in nuts. Pour half of batter into prepared pan. Carefully spoon prepared filling over batter; top with remaining batter. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 70 to 75 minutes until top springs back when touched lightly in center.
