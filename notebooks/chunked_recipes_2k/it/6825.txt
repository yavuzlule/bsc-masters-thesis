Preheat oven to 400\u00b0. Combine chips, cheese and parsley. In small bowl, combine mayonnaise and 2 tablespoons lemon juice. Dip fish in remaining 2 tablespoons of lemon juice, then mayonnaise mixture, then chip mixture. Arrange in greased baking dish. Bake 5 to 10 minutes or until fish flakes with fork. Refrigerate leftovers. Makes 4 servings.
