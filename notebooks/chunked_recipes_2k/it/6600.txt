Slice mushrooms. Dredge chicken with flour; shake off excess. Heat oil in large saucepan. Add chicken and brown well. Remove chicken. To oil remaining, add onion, green pepper and garlic. Saute 3 minutes. Add mushrooms and saute 4 minutes. Stir in tomatoes, water, salt. Italian dressing and red pepper. Bring to a boil. Stir in rice. Return chicken to saucepan. Cover, reduce heat and simmer for 45 minutes or until chicken is tender. Serves 4.
