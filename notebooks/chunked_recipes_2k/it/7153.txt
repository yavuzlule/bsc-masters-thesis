Preheat oven to 350\u00b0 (325\u00b0 glass). Grease a 13 x 9-inch pan. In a large saucepan, combine chocolate and butter. Heat on low until melted, stirring occasionally. Remove from heat. Beat in eggs, then sugar, vanilla and salt until blended, then blend in flour. Spread evenly into greased 13 x 9-inch pan. Bake for 20 to 25 minutes. Brownies should be moist. Dont overbake. Cool on pan.
