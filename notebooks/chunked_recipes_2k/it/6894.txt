Combine all ingredients in large mixer bowl. Blend well, then beat at medium speed of electric mixer (4 minutes). Pour in 2 greased and floured 8 x 4-inch loaf pans. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 50 to 55 minutes or until cake tester inserted in center comes out clean. Cool in pan 15 minutes. Remove. Finish cooling on a rack.
