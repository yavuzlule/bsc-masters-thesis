Grease and flour a 9-inch pie plate. Mix the first 7 ingredients together and put in pie plate. Drain sliced peaches; reserve syrup. Spread peaches on top of the batter. Cream together the cream cheese, 1/2 cup of sugar and 3 tablespoons peach syrup. Spread over peaches. Mix together 1 tablespoon of sugar and cinnamon; sprinkle on top. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 30 to 35 minutes.
