Dissolve bouillon cubes in the boiling water, pour over stuffing crumbs and stir well. Saute celery & onion in butter until tender. Add to crumb mixture. Brown meat/pork and drain well. Stir meat and the rest of the remaining ingredients into the crumb mixture. If it seems too dry, add a little more chicken broth to moisten it. Spoon mixture into a lightly greased 13x9x12 baking dish. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 1 hour or until brown. Serve with traditional Turkey dinner.
