Cream the shortening and sugar. Beat 2 eggs. Sift together flour, cinnamon, cloves and allspice. Add to creamed mixture. Bring to boil applesauce, water and baking, soda. Add to batter; mix well. Bake in 2 greased and floured loaf pans at 350\u00b0 for 45 to 50 minutes. Optional:. Add 1/2 cup chopped nuts.
