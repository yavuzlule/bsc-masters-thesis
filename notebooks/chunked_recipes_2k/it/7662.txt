While rice is cooking, pound steak to 1/4-inch thick and cut into 1/4-inch strips. Sprinkle meat with paprika and allow to stand while preparing other ingredients. Using a large skillet, brown meat in butter. Add garlic and broth. Cover and simmer for 30 minutes. Stir in onions and green peppers. Cover and cook for 5 minutes more. Blend cornstarch, water and soy sauce. Stir into meat mixture. Cook, stirring, until clean and thickened, about 2 minutes. Add tomatoes and stir gently. Serve over beds of fluffy rice. Makes 6 servings.
