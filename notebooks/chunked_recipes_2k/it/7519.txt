Combine grits, milk, onion and salt in a medium size bowl. Beat yolks until thick and lemon color. Stir yolk and cheese into mix. Pour into a greased casserole dish. Set casserole in a baking pan and pour boiling water in pan to a depth of 1 inch. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 1 hour. Serve immediately. Serves 6.
