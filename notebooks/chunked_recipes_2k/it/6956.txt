Preheat oven at 350\u00b0. While mixing dry ingredients, place butter in 9 x 13-inch pan. Let it get sizzling hot in oven. Mix all other ingredients in large bowl except chocolate chips. Pour hot butter over mixture. Mix well, then add chocolate chips and press into same 9 x 13-inch pan. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 15 to 20 minutes or until light brown on top.
