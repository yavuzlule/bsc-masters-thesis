Soften yeast in lukewarm water. Add salt, sugar and flour. Stir until mixed. Add enough of 1/2 cup lukewarm water (no more) to make sticky dough. Cover; let rise until doubled. Divide; put each 1/2 into a greased two-quart casserole. Let rise again until doubled. Bake at 375\u00b0 for one hour. Brush tops with margarine; salt if desired.
