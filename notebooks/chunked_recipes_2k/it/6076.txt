Cook first 7 ingredients in 2 cups of water until soft. DO NOT DRAIN. In separate frying pan, melt butter or margarine. Stir in the flour using a wire whisk, if possible. Stir in about one half to 1 cup of the water from the potatoes. Add milk and stir until bubbly. Add to the potatoes. If soup seems to thick, add a little more milk or tap water.
