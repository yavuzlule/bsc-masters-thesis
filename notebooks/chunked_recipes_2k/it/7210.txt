Preheat oven to 450\u00b0. In a bowl, combine the flour, baking powder, baking soda and salt; cut in butter until mixture resembles coarse crumbs. Stir in enough buttermilk just to moisten dough. Turn onto a lightly floured surface; knead 3 to 4 times. Pat or roll to 3/4 inch thickness. Cut with a floured 2 1/2 inch biscuit cutter. Place on a baking sheet coated with nonstick cooking spray. Brush with milk. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes or until golden brown. Yield:, 8 biscuits.
