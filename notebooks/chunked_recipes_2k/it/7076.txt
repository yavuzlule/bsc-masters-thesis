Blend cheese and margarine; stir in flour, paprika and salt, mixing well. Blot olives on paper towels until dry. Divide dough into 24 pieces. Mold a piece of dough around each olive. Arrange on cookie sheet and bake at 425\u00b0 for 8 to 10 minutes or may be frozen and packed in plastic bags up to 2 months before serving. Bake at 400\u00b0 for 15 to 20 minutes, if frozen. Recipe may be doubled or tripled.
