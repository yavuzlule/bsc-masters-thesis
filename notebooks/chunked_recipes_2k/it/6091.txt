Melt butter. Add thinly sliced leeks and saute until soft (8 minutes). Stir in chicken broth and potatoes, peeled and sliced au gratin style. Partially cover; simmer for about 10 minutes. Using a wooden spoon, mash some potatoes against the side of the pan. Stir in coarsely chopped watercress. Simmer for 1 minute. Serve in bowl. Season with salt, pepper and swirl 1 tablespoon heavy cream.
