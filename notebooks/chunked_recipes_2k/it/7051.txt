Toss cheese with flour, salt and pepper, combine with potatoes. Place in a 2-quart casserole; add cream. Bake uncovered 1 hour and 15 minutes until lightly browned (350\u00b0). Makes 8 servings. Casserole may be assembled up to 24 hours before baking. Cover and refrigerate. One 24 ounce package of defrosted hash brown potatoes may be substituted with excellent results.
