Boil the chicken and the stick of butter for 30 minutes, or until chicken is tender. Pull chicken into small pieces, then mix them with the stuffing and soups. Use 1 cup of the broth from the pot you boiled the chicken in and put it with the other ingredients. Put in a 13 x 9-inch cake pan and bake for 30 minutes at 350\u00b0. Add salt and pepper for taste.
