Cook hamburger; drain and then add burrito mix, refried beans and water, mixing well. Cook for 20 to 30 minutes. Heat shells in oven or microwave oven for 2 to 3 minutes. Add lettuce, meat mix, tomatoes, cheese and taco sauce to burrito. Before you roll your burrito up, you can also add the other ingredients:, black olives, green onion and sour cream to the filling. Roll up and serve.
