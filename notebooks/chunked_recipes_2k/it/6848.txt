Preheat oven to 375\u00b0. Peel and slice sweet potatoes. In a big ovenproof dish, place 1/4 margarine on bottom. Layer 3 pork chops, layer sweet potatoes and layer 1/4 margarine. Sprinkle half the sugar over top. Repeat the pork chops, sweet potatoes, margarine and sugar. Cover dish and place in oven. Cook for 1 hour.
