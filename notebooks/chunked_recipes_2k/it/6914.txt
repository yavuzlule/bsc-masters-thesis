Chop onion, bell pepper and garlic; saute in oil. Add tomato sauce, ketchup and hot sauce; let simmer 10 to 15 minutes. Drain stewed tomatoes and add to mixture. Drain and crush whole tomatoes and add to mixture; simmer 15 minutes. Add Ragu sauce and continue to simmer 20 to 25 minutes. Serve over spaghetti noodles.
