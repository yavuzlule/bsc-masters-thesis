Melt oleo; toss with herb-seasoned dressing mix. Stir half of dressing mixture with the squash, soup, sour cream, onion, carrots and black pepper. Can add a little salt to taste. Turn squash mixture into lightly greased 9 x 13-inch baking dish. Sprinkle remaining half of dressing mixture over top. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 45 minutes. You can also add browned hamburger meat, drained, to this casserole to make a main dish.
