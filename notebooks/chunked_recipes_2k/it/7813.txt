Combine cake mix, oranges, 1 package pudding mix, 1 cup pineapple and 4 eggs in a large mixing bowl. Beat for 4 minutes. Pour into greased 9 x 13-inch pan. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 30 to 40 minutes or until cake tests done. Combine remaining 1 cup pineapple, 1 package pudding mix and Cool Whip in bowl, mixing well. Spread over cooled cake. Chill in refrigerator until serving time.
