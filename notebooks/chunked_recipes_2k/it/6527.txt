Sift flour into mixing bowl. Cut shortening into flour with a pastry blender until shortening particles are the size of a pea. Add cold water gradually, tossing dough lightly with a fork to mix. Do not overmix as dough will become tough. Roll dough with 1 tablespoon of flour until dough is 2 inches larger than inverted pie pan. Ease dough into 9-inch pie pan and flute edges. Follow pie recipes for baking directions.
