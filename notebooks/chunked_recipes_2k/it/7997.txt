Brown ground beef; pour off excess fat. Add taco seasoning mix and water. Put 3 to 4 tablespoons taco meat in each flour tortilla. Roll and place seam down in a glass casserole. Top with thick n chunky salsa. Cheez Whiz and Colby cheese. Microwave 6 to 8 minutes, until cheese has completely melted. Serve with cucumbers and sliced tomatoes.
