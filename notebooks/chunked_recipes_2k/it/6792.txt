Place cutlets between unwaxed paper. Flatten to 1/8 inch thickness. Combine pepper, cheese and bread crumbs; dredge cutlets in mixture. Coat a nonstick skillet with cooking spray. Add olive oil. Place over medium-high heat until hot. Add cutlets; cook 2 minutes on each side or until browned. Add wine and lemon juice; cook 2 minutes or until heated. Transfer to serving platter; sprinkle with parsley.
