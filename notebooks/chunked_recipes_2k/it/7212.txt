Place lettuce, cauliflower and peas in layers in a large salad bowl. Combine dressing mix and mayonnaise. Make hole in center of salad and pour this mixture. Cover hole with lettuce. On top of salad, place the crumbled bacon and Parmesan cheese. Cover tightly and refrigerate 24 hours. Stir just before serving. Only stir enough to eat, it doesnt last long once mixed.
