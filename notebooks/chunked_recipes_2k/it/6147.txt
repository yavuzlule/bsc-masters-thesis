Sift together first 3 ingredients in large bowl. Stir together in medium bowl the buttermilk, brown sugar, egg, oil and vanilla and then add to dry ingredients, stirring until just combined. Fold in rhubarb and nuts. Pour batter into 13 x 9 x 2-inch baking pan. Sprinkle with mixture of 1/2 cup granulated sugar, cinnamon and butter which you combined by stirring with a fork. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 40 minutes or until toothpick inserted in center comes out clean. Serve warm or cool.
