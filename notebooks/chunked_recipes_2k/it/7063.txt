Place Twinkies in 9 x 13-inch glass pan, sides touching, and filling side up. Set aside. Melt in double boiler or nonstick pot, chocolate chips, mint chips, water and sugar. Let cool. Beat 3 egg yolks and add to chocolate mixture. Add whipped whipping cream. Add 3 beaten egg whites. Pour mixture over Twinkies. Refrigerate for 8 hours or overnight.
