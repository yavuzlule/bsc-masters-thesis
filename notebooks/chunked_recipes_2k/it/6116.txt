Cook the asparagus spears; drain and cool. Use a rolling pin to flatten out the bread. Cut off the crusts after you have rolled over the bread. This given an uptown finish to the roll-ups. Spread the cream cheese over the bread slices, sprinkle bacon bits over this, add the asparagus spear and roll as for a jellyroll. Cut each slice in half, place on cookie sheet, brush each roll with butter and sprinkle the Parmesan cheese on top. Bake in hot oven (400\u00b0) until the Parmesan has a golden glow (about 5 to 8 minutes), but watch closely so the rolls dont burn. You could also broil 6 inches from broiler until lightly toasted. Yields 24 appetizers.
