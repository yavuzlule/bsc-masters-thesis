Crush berries. Add sugar and white wine. Stir well. Chill. Soften gelatin in cold water; add boiling water. Stir to dissolve;, cool. Combine gelatin and chilled strawberry mixture; beat with rotary beater until slightly thickened. Fold in whipped cream. Turn into 2-quart ring mold. To serve, fill ring with additional strawberries and whipped cream. Garnish top with large berries. Yields 10 to 12 servings.
