Cook noodles according to box directions. Rinse and chill. Dice tomatoes, cucumbers and onion. Combine the spaghetti, which can be cut into smaller pieces, with the tomatoes, cucumbers and onion. Add the seasoning salt and mix well until all ingredients are coated. Pour the Italian dressing over combined ingredients. Mix together. Chill. Delicious for summer with hamburger, chicken, steak, etc.
