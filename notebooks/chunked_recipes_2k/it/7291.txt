Mix well with fingers the oats, flour, sugar, salt and shortening. Pat 1/2 mixture into greased 9 x 9 x 2-inch pan. Spread jelly evenly over the dough. Sprinkle remaining batter over the jelly layer. Lightly pat down the topping with your fingers. Bake in a preheated 350\u00b0 oven for 25 to 30 minutes, or until golden brown. Cool in pan on a rack. Cut into 24 bars.
