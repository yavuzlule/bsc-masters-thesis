Butter a 9 x 13-inch pan. Put 3 cups of fruit in pan. Cream butter and sugar. Add flour, baking powder, salt and milk. Mix together and pour over fruit. Then mix 1 1/2 cups of sugar and 1 tablespoon cornstarch and sprinkle over top of batter. Pour 1 cup of hot water over all. Bake 1 hour at 350\u00b0.
