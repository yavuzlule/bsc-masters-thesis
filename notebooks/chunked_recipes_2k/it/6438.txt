Thoroughly cream shortening and 3/4 cup sugar; add egg and beat until light and fluffy. Mix together 2 cups flour, baking powder and salt; add to creamed mixture alternately with milk. Spread in greased 11 x 7 x 1 1/2-inch pan. Top with berries. Mix 1/2 cup sugar, 1/2 cup flour and cinnamon; cut in butter until crumbly. Sprinkle over berries. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 45 minutes. Cut in squares. Serve warm.
