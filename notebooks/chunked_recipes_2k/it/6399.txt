Mix cake mix according to directions and put in a 9 x 13-inch greased pan. Bake 15 minutes at 350\u00b0. Cool thoroughly. Mix together milk, cream cheese and pudding and spread on cake. Top with drained crushed pineapple. Then spread Dream Whip on top of pineapple and sprinkle with chopped nuts. Chill 12 hours in refrigerator.
