Combine vegetables, soup, 1/2 cup cheese, sour cream, pepper, pimento and 1/2 can Durkee French fried onions. Pour into a 1-quart casserole. Bake, covered, at 350\u00b0 for 30 minutes. Top with remaining cheese and onions; bake, uncovered, 5 minutes longer. Preparation time:, 5 minutes. Makes 6 servings.
