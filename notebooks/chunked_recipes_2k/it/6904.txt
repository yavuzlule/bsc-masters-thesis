Mix ground meat. Season-All, eggs, bread crumbs. Romano cheese and 1 to 2 to sprigs of chopped parsley together. Take part of the mixture and put into cupped palm of your hand. Make an indentation in the meat mixture; put in 1/4 hard-boiled egg, 1 hunk Mozzarella cheese, a small piece of bacon and parsley. Cover with some meat mixture. Brown in a skillet with oil in it, then cook in your favorite tomato sauce.
