Line 9 x 13-inch pan with crust made of graham cracker crumbs and 1/2 cup melted oleo. Mix together confectioners sugar, eggs, 2 sticks oleo and vanilla. Beat until creamy and spread on crust. Spread crushed pineapple. Slice bananas lengthwise over pineapple. Frost with Cool Whip. Top with crushed nuts and cherries. Refrigerate 8 hours or overnight.
