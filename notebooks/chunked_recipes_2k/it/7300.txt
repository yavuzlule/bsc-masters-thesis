Preheat oven to 400\u00b0. Sift dry ingredients together, then, using a pastry blender or two knives, cut in butter. Combine milk and egg and slowly add to flour until mixture is wet enough to hold together. Turn onto a floured board. Knead, then roll to 1/2-inch thickness and cut with a biscuit cutter. Bake for 10 to 12 minutes or until lightly golden. Yields about 2 dozen biscuits.
