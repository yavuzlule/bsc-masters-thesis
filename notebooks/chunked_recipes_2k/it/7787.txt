Beat eggs at medium speed with an electric mixer until thick and pale. Add cake mix and next three ingredients mixing well. Stir in bananas and walnuts. Pour batter into 2 greased 8 1/2 x 4 1/2 x 3-inch pans. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 1 hour, or until a wooden pick inserted in center comes out clean. Cool in pans on wire rack 10 minutes. Remove from pans and cool completely on wire racks.
