Melt butter in a saucepan. Add flour and stir until bubbly. Do not brown. Immediately add whipping cream and stir well. Mixture should have a fairly thin consistency. Add Provolone; cut into small cubes, a few at a time and Parmesan, a little at a time. Season with spices. If sauce is too thick, thin with milk. When sauce has a smooth, creamy consistency, serve over hot noodles. Yields 4 to 6 servings.
