Combine milk, brown sugar, orange peel, ginger, cinnamon and salt. Stir thoroughly and add eggs and pumpkin; blend well. Beat until smooth. Pour into a greased shallow 1 1/2-quart baking dish. Place the dish in a large pan in the middle of the oven; pour enough boiling water in the pan to come halfway up the sides of the baking dish. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 1 1/4 hours or until a knife inserted in the center of the pudding comes out clean. Remove the baking dish; cool the pudding to room temperature before serving or refrigerate at least 3 hours. If cooked in six (4-ounce) individual glass molds, bake at 350\u00b0 for 40 minutes.
