Brown ground beef and drain. Add onion and taco seasoning according to package directions. Add 1/2 cup grated cheese to meat and onion mixture. Set aside. Combine soups and enchilada sauce. Top each tortilla with 1 heaping tablespoonful of meat mixture. Place, seam side down, in a 9 x 13-inch baking dish. Pour sauce over tortillas and top with remaining cheese. Bake in preheated 350\u00b0 oven for 25 to 30 minutes.
