In a 2-cup glass measure with handle, combine peanut butter chips and 2/3 cup sweetened condensed milk. Cook on 100% power (High) in microwave for 1 to 1 1/2 minutes, stirring after 1 minute, until chips are melted and mixture is smooth. Stir in 1/2 teaspoon vanilla; spread evenly into foil lined 8-inch square pan.
