Peel potatoes; place in a saucepan and cover with water. Cook until tender, but firm; drain and cube. In a bowl, mix potatoes, onion, cheese and mayonnaise. Transfer to an ungreased 13 x 9 x 2-inch baking dish. Sprinkle with bacon and olives. Cover and bake at 350\u00b0 for 30 minutes or until heated through. If desired, sprinkle with parsley and paprika.
