Combine eggs, banana, sugar, oil and milk. In a separate bowl mix flour, salt, baking soda and baking powder. Add to banana mixture. Fold in apple and nuts. Pour batter into 3 small greased and floured pans or two (9 x 5-inch) pans. Bake at 325\u00b0 for about 1 hour. Cool in pans 10 minutes; remove to wire racks to cool.
