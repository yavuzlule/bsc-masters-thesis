Combine celery, onion, bay leaf, garlic and water. Cook for 15 minutes, or until celery is tender. Remove bay leaf. Drain; reserve liquid. Set vegetables aside. Add water to reserved liquid to make 2 cups. Melt margarine in saucepan; stir in flour and dry milk slowly. Add reserved liquid to flour mixture, stirring constantly until smooth. Add salt and pepper and Worcestershire. Add vegetables; put in blender.
