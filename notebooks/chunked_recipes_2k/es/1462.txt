Combine brisket, bones and water in deep pot. Add onions and tomatoes and bring to boil. Skim off any foam and reduce heat to low. Simmer 1 hour uncovered. Add cabbage, salt, pepper and garlic and cook another hour over low heat. Stir in lemon juice and sugar; cook 20 minutes longer on low heat. Taste to correct seasoning. Remove bones. Slice meat and serve as a garnish with soup. Serves 6 to 8.
