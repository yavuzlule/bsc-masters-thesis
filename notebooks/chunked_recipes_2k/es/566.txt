Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. Beat egg whites at high speed until foamy. Gradually add sugar, one tablespoon at a time, beating until stiff peaks form. Fold in vanilla, cereal, coconut and nuts. Drop by tablespoon on a foil lined cookie sheet. Place in oven and immediately turn off oven. Do not open door for 8 hours. Carefully peel cookies from foil. Store tightly sealed. Yield:, 3 dozen.
