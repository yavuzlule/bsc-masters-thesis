Boil chicken breast off of bone; salt and pepper. Remove skin. Boil broccoli in 1/2 cup of water 2 minutes and drain. Cube chicken; mix broccoli, cheese soup and milk together. Pour in bottom of dish. Put drained broccoli on top of chicken mixture. Add finely grated cheese on top. Bake in a 350\u00b0 oven until cheese melts.
