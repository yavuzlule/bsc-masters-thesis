In a large skillet, melt butter or margarine and brown onion; add ground meat and cook until meat is brown and onion is tender. Stir in flour, salt, garlic, pepper and mushrooms. Cook over low heat for 5 minutes, stirring constantly. Stir in soup and heat until bubbly, stirring constantly. Reduce heat and simmer, uncovered, for 10 minutes. Stir in sour cream and heat through. Ladle over Poppy Noodles; garnish with fresh chopped chives.
