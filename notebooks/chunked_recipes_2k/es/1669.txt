Slice washed potatoes. Place in bowl. Mix in 2 tablespoons Cajun seasoning. Stir in unbeaten egg whites, coating the potatoes. Place mixture in sprayed pan(s) and bake at 350\u00b0. Turn potatoes with a spatula every 15 minutes until done. This gives them a crunchy, browned exterior. Cooking time is 45 to 60 minutes.
