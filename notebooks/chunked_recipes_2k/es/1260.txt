Line bottom of 9 x 12-inch Pyrex baking dish with buttered bread which has had the crust removed and cut into 9 pieces. Sprinkle with Old English cheese. Brown Owens hot sausage. Drain and thoroughly blot until no grease remains. Sprinkle over cheese. Blend eggs, half and half, salt. Spice Islands Beau Monde, dry mustard and Worcestershire sauce. Pour over sausage and cheese. Sprinkle with paprika. Chill. Bake in 350\u00b0 oven for 40 minutes. Great for brunch!
