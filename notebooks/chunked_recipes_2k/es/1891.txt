Cook noodles according to package. Saute onions and bell pepper in margarine. Stir in flour slowly until thick. Add seasonings and cheese; stir until melted. Add 2 cups milk, soups, chicken (chopped), and noodles. Turn into shallow baking dish and cover with bread crumbs. Bake at 400\u00b0 for 15 to 20 minutes or until browned on top.
