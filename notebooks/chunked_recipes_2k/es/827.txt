In medium bowl, combine tuna, anchovies, green pepper, pimento, egg and 2 tablespoons Russian dressing. Mix well and set aside. In small bowl, mix remaining Russian dressing with cognac and set aside. In hot butter in a chafing dish or larger pan, saute bread until well browned and butter is absorbed. In same pan, spread tuna mixture over bread. Cover with Russian dressing mixture. Sprinkle 1/2 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce over each. Heat over low heat for 5 to 10 minutes or until very hot. Serve immediately.
