In deep bowl, put pudding, cream cheese and 1/2 cup of milk. Mix until creamy with hand mixer. Add powdered sugar and mix thoroughly, then add remaining milk, 1/2 cup at a time, and mix until creamy. Crumble enough Oreo cookies to line bottom of dish as a crust in a lasagna pan. Add the pudding mix and top with whipped topping. Refrigerate until serving. Prior to serving, crumble a few more Oreos on the top to provide a crisp, crunchy taste.
