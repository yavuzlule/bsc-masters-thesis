Prepare pie crust as directed on box for 9-inch pie. Line plate bottom with crust. Mix sugar and flour. Mix with apples. Fill apples in bottom of crust; add the blueberry filling on top of apples. Cover with top crust. Slit vent in top; flute edges high to prevent spill-over in oven. Heat oven to 400\u00b0. Bake 10 minutes. Reduce to 350\u00b0 and bake 25 minutes longer. Makes one 9-inch pie. Serves 8.
