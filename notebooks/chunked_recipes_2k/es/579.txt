Grease and flour a 9 x 13-inch pan. Mix all of the ingredients and pour into prepared pan. Bake at 350\u00b0 until done. Remove cake from oven and top with 2 teaspoons sugar, mixed with the juice of an orange to make 1/3 cup. Put on top of cake while warm. Mix sugar and cinnamon to sprinkle on top, if desired. Cool and cut into squares. Serve with Cool Whip.
