Beat eggs until light. Add sugar and beat until mixture is very stiff and light, about 15 minutes at high speed on electric mixer. Add extract, flour and soda; mix thoroughly. Roll out on lightly floured surface with regular rolling pin to 1/2 to 1/4-inch thickness. Roll with springerle rolling pin to 1/4-inch thickness, pressing only once for clear imprint. Cut along dividing lines to separate cookies.
