Heat wok or large frying pan on high. Add oil, then chopped green onions, including most of green parts. Stir-fry for 1 minute. Add leftover meat that has been cubed; stir-fry another minute. Add cooked mixed vegetables; season to taste with garlic powder and ginger. Stir-fry 1 minute more, then add rice and mix well. Add scrambled eggs which have been mashed well with a fork. Sprinkle with soy sauce to taste; stir-fry for 2 more minutes. Enjoy.
