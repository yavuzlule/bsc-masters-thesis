Flatten chicken between sheets of wax paper. Spread butter over each piece and season with salt and pepper. Place 1 slice Mozzarella cheese over each piece. Roll up and fasten with toothpicks. Roll each in flour, dip in egg and then roll in bread crumbs. Place in a medium size casserole. Cover and bake at 350\u00b0 for 30 minutes. Remove cover and bake 15 minutes longer.
