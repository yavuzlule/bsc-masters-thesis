Cut, fillets, into serving pieces and place in greased, shallow baking dish. Sprinkle with lemon juice, paprika, salt and pepper. Melt butter in saucepan. Blend in flour, dash of salt and, pepper, and milk. Cook and stir until thick. Pour over fish, and sprinkle crumbs over all. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 35 minutes. Garnish with parsley. Makes 3 or 4 servings.
