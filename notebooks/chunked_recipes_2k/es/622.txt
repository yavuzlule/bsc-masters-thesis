Cream sugar and butter with electric mixer at low speed. Add milk, egg, 2/3 cup flour, baking powder, salt, spices and vanilla. Blend well. Add remaining 2/3 cup flour and stir just until moistened. Fold in blueberries. Fill paper lined muffin cups 3/4 full. Bake at 375\u00b0 for 20 to 30 minutes. Yields 1 dozen.
