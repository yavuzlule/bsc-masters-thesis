Cook potatoes in boiling water to cover 15 minutes or until tender. Drain and mash. Stir in butter and next 3 ingredients. Let cool 10 minutes. Stir in eggs. Spoon mixture into a large decorating bag fitted with a large star tip. Pipe 14 (2-inch) rosettes onto a lightly greased baking sheet. Drizzle with melted butter and sprinkle with paprika. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 15 minutes or until lightly browned around the edges.
