Remove stones from prunes and press pulp through food mill or coarse sieve. Add juice, sugar and salt. Beat egg yolks; add milk and cook over boiling water, stirring constantly, until mixture coats spoon. Remove from heat at once and stir in gelatin, which has been softened 5 minutes in the cold water. Add to prunes when cool. Fold in stiffly beaten egg whites. Pour into baked shell and chill in refrigerator.
