Melt butter in medium saucepan. Combine sugar and cocoa. Add to butter. Blend in eggs, one at a time. Add vanilla. Stir in flour and nuts. Spread in greased 8 x 8 x 2-inch pan. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 30 minutes. Be careful not to overbake. Cool and cut into 16 squares. Sprinkle with confectioners sugar for an extra touch.
