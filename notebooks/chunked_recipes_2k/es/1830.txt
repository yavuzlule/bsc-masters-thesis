Cream butter and brown sugar. Add egg and vanilla; beat until fluffy. Stir together flour, baking soda and salt. Add to creamed mixture alternately with yogurt. Blend well. Turn into a greased 9-inch square cake pan. The top can be sprinkled with chopped nuts if desired. Bake in a 350\u00b0 oven for about 35 to 40 minutes or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
