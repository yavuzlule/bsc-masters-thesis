Heat oven to 400\u00b0. Mix thoroughly the butter, shortening, 1 1/2 cups sugar and eggs. Blend in flour, cream of tartar, soda and salt. Shape dough by rounded teaspoonful into balls. Mix 2 tablespoons sugar and the cinnamon. Roll balls in mixture and place 2-inches apart on ungreased baking sheet. Bake 8 to 10 minutes or until set. Remove immediately from baking sheet.
