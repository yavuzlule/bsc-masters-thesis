Mix corn, tomatoes, corn meal, cheese, onion and green pepper. Let stand 30 minutes. Add eggs, milk and seasoning. Pour into buttered baking dish at 325\u00b0 for 1 1/4 hours or until firm in the center. This may be served from the dish or may be baked in a loaf and unmolded onto a platter. Buttered peas may be used as a garnish with sections of hard-cooked eggs on the platter.
