Blend spinach and 1/2 cup chicken broth in blender until pureed. In saucepan, melt margarine. Add onion and saute until tender. Stir in flour and cook about 1 minute. Add remaining chicken broth and cook until thickened. Stir in pureed spinach mixture, whipping cream, pepper and nutmeg. Heat thoroughly. Makes 2 or 3 servings.
