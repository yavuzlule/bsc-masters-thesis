Cook peppers and onions in oil until tender-crisp. Blend syrup drained from pineapple with vinegar, sugar, water, catsup and cornstarch. Add to vegetables and cook, stirring until sauce boils and thickens. Add drained pineapple. Serve over fried chicken or leftover chunked pork that has been fried or fried tofu chunks. Serves 6 to 8.
