In a 9 x 12-inch casserole dish, layer the sausage, croutons, cheese and mushrooms. Mix the milk, eggs, soup and mustard in a bowl and beat together until will blended. Pour milk mixture over layers in casserole and refrigerate overnight or up to 24 hours. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 1 hour. Let stand 15 minutes before serving. Serves 10 to 12.
