Mix in a bowl the cream cheese, margarine or butter and flour. Wrap in wax paper and refrigerate overnight. Divide in quarters and roll each into 8-inch circles. Melt 1 stick of butter and brush on dough, then sprinkle filling. Cut each circle into 8 triangles, rolling large end to pointed end and pinch ends. Beat egg yolk with 2 tablespoons water and brush on top.
