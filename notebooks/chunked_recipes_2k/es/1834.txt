Grease and flour a 12-inch tube pan. Mix together flour, baking powder, salt, baking soda, cinnamon and cloves. Cream together sugar and shortening; add eggs and mix well. Add chopped pecans to dry ingredients, then add creamed mixture, folding together with pumpkin and alternating with water. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 1 hour or until toothpick inserted in center comes out clean.
