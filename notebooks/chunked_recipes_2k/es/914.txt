Place steak in shallow pan. Combine oil, vinegar and garlic. Pour over steak. Cover and let stand in refrigerator for 3 hours or better yet overnight, turning several times. Preheat broiler. Broil steak 3-inches from heat, about 5 minutes. Season. Turn; broil 3 minutes. Add more seasoning. Remove to cutting board. Carve very thin slices diagonally across the grain. Serves 4 to 5.
