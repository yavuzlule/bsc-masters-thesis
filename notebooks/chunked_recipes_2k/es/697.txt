Heat oven to 350\u00b0. Cream shortening and confectioners sugar. Blend in flour. Press mixture in bottom of ungreased baking pan (13 x 9 x 2-inches). Bake 12 to 15 minutes. Mix remaining ingredients; spread over hot, baked layer. Bake 20 minutes longer. While warm, spread with Orange-Lemon Icing. Cool. Cut into bars about 3 x 1-inch. Makes 32 bars.
