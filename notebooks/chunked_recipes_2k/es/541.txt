Preheat oven to 375\u00b0. In a 1-quart casserole sprayed with nonstick cooking spray, place tomatoes in juice. Stir in green pepper, onion, garlic, basil and pepper. In a small bowl, combine bread cubes and margarine; sprinkle over tomatoes. Bake about 30 minutes until hot and bubbly. Makes 4 servings.
