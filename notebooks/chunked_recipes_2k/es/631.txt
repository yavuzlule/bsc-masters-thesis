Mix sugar and flour. Add shortening and blend until smooth. Beat about 3 minutes. Add eggs; beat 1 minute. Add flavoring last. Beat about 3 minutes with electric mixer. Line pan with wax paper and grease well. Bake at 400\u00b0 for about 15 minutes. Makes 3 layers. Stack with seven minute icing. Place strawberries on top of cake and let run down sides of cake. Very good.
