Melt 1 stick of oleo in a 9 X 13 pan. Add 2 cups crushed graham crackers on top of oleo. Continue to layer the following: 1-6 oz package chocolate chips, 1 -6 oz package butterscotch chips, 1 cup shredded coconut, 3/4 cup chopped walnuts, 1 can condensed milk--pour over the top. Top with rice crispies or crushed corn flakes (a hand full). Bake at 350\u00b0 for 20-25 minutes.
