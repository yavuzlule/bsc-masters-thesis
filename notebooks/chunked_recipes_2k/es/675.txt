In a 9 x 13-inch baking dish, layer buttered noodles, sauerkraut, corned beef (broken in pieces) and tomatoes. Dot with Thousand Island dressing; sprinkle with Swiss cheese. Bake, covered, at 350\u00b0 for 40 minutes. Uncover; sprinkle crushed rye crackers on top. Bake 10 minutes more until bubbly. Serves 6 to 8.
