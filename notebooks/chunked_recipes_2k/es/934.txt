Cook beef, onion and green pepper in large skillet over medium-high heat until meat is brown, stirring to separate meat; drain fat. Stir in spaghetti sauce, mix well. Combine Parmesan cheese, eggs and butter in large bowl; mix well. Place in bottom of 13 x 9-inch pan; spread cottage cheese over top. Pour sauce mixture over cottage cheese. Sprinkle Mozzarella over top. Bake in preheated 350\u00b0 oven until cheese melts, about 20 minutes.
