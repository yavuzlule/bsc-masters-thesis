Layer a 9 x 13-inch pan with one can of the crescent rolls. Brown the ground beef in a skillet. Drain the beef and place it on top of the crescent rolls. Spoon the taco sauce onto the beef. Sprinkle the Cheddar cheese over the beef and taco sauce. Cover all of this with the remaining can of crescent rolls. Bake at 350\u00b0 for approximately 15 minutes.
