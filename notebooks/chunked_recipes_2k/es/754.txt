Heat oven to 350\u00b0. Mix together the persimmons and baking soda; set aside. Mix together the butter, sugar, vanilla and lemon juice; set aside. Mix together the flour, salt, cinnamon, nutmeg and cloves; set aside. Blend butter mixture with flour mixture. Add persimmon mixture. Add raisins and nuts. Put in 1 large loaf pan or 2 small loaf pans. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 50 minutes for large loaf or 30 minutes for small loaves.
