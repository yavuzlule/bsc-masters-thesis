Place 1 apple slice in each triangle of the crescent dough. Roll from large end to smaller. Place all rolled up slices in a 9 X 13 inch baking dish. In a small saucepan, melt margarine, then stir in sugar and cinnamon. Pour this mixture over all the rolled up slices and then pour Mountain Dew over all and bake at 350 degrees for 45 minutes. Good served with ice cream.
