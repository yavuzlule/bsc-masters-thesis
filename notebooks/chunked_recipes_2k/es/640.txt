Prepare Dream Whip with 1/2 cup milk and the vanilla as directed on the package. Combine 1 cup of the prepared Dream Whip with the Jell-O instant pudding, 2/3 cup milk, pumpkin and spice. Beat slowly with mixer until well mixed. Pour into pie shell. Chill in refrigerator until set, about 2 hours. Top with remaining Dream Whip. Serves 6.
