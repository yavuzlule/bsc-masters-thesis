Combine dressing, green onions and mustard; set aside. Toss tuna with dill. In a 3-quart serving bowl, layer lettuce, shells, cucumber, tomatoes, tuna, peas and bacon. Spread dressing mixture over bacon, sealing to edge of bowl. Sprinkle with cheese; cover. Chill several hours or overnight. Toss to serve. Makes 8 servings. Prep. Time: 25 minutes plus chilling.
