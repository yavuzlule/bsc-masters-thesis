Slice sweet potatoes into an 8-inch pie plate; set aside. In a saucepan, combine brown sugar, butter, water and salt. Bring to a boil. Pour hot syrup over potatoes. Bake uncovered at 350\u00b0 for 30 minutes, basting occasionally or until syrup thickens and potatoes are glazed. Sprinkle with nutmeg or mace. Yields 2 servings.
