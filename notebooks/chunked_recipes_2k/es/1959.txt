Combine flour, soda, salt, sugar and cinnamon. Add eggs and oil, stirring until moistened. Do not beat. Stir in 1 1/2 teaspoons vanilla, pineapple, bananas and 1 cup pecans. Pour into 3 round, greased cake pans. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 25 minutes. Cool 10 minutes. Remove from pan and cool. Mix oleo, cream cheese, powdered sugar and 1 teaspoon vanilla. Frost cake with icing, then put 1/2 cup pecans on top.
