Cut tomatoes in half; scoop out pulp and reserve for use in other recipes. Sprinkle insides of tomatoes with 1 teaspoon salt. Place tomato shells upside down on paper towels to drain. Let stand for 30 minutes. Chop and set aside. Combine olive oil and butter in a large skillet over medium heat; add green peppers, red peppers, onions and garlic. Cook, stirring frequently, until onions are translucent. Add reserved tomatoes and basil. Reduce heat to low; cover and cook for 10 minutes. Add remaining salt and pepper. Increase heat to high and cook, uncovered, until most of the liquid has evaporated, stirring frequently.
