Put pecan pieces and maraschino cherries into bundt pan. Add the 1 c. granulated sugar in a plastic bag along with the 2 tsp. cinnamon. Toss a few of the biscuit quarters in the mixture and put into bundt pan. Continue coating all the, biscuit pieces. Heat margarine or butter, brown sugar, and 1 Tbsp. cinnamon. Pour over the biscuits. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 35 minutes. Cool 15 minutes, then, turn out onto cake dish. Serve warm.
