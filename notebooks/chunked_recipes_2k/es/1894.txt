Pop corn in air popper. Place popcorn and peanuts in double brown grocery bag; set aside. Combine sugar, butter, syrup and salt in glass bowl. Microwave on High 3 to 4 minutes, stirring after each minute, until mixture boils. Microwave 2 minutes more. Stir in soda. Pour syrup mixture over popcorn in bag. Close bag; shake well. Microwave 1 1/2 minutes on High; shake. Microwave 1 1/2 minutes on High; shake. Pour into roasting pan. Cool to separate.
