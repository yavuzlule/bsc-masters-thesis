Cream shortening and sugar. Beat in whole egg and 2 egg yolks. Sift flour, baking powder and salt. Combine 2 mixtures; blend. Grease and flour 13 x 9-inch pan. Sprinkle nuts, chocolate pieces and marshmallows over batter. Beat 2 egg whites very stiff. Fold in brown sugar. Spread over cake. Bake 30 to 40 minutes.
