Heat oil in frying pan, and, cook, chicken, in, hot oil until it is lightly browned, and, no longer pink inside, about 10 minutes. Remove chicken, from, pan, and, keep warm. Place vegetables in pan drippings, (adding, more, oil if needed) and saute for 3 to 4 minutes. Drain oil, and return chicken to pan along with the tomato, sauce, tomato paste and salad dressing. Heat through and serve over hot cooked rice.
