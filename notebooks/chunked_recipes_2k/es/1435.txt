Stir sugar and syrup well in casserole dish; microwave 4 minutes. Take out; stir in peanuts; microwave 4 minutes. Take out; add vanilla and butter and stir well. Microwave 1 1/2 minutes. Take out; add baking soda and stir well until light and foamy. Pour into greased cookie sheet; let harden in cool place, then break apart. Makes 1 pound.
