Mix together butter, sugar, eggs and lemon extract. Blend in flour, baking powder and salt. Cover and chill at least 1 hour. Roll dough 1/8-inch thick on a lightly floured board. Cut into desired shapes. Cut a small hole in center with a sharp knife. Place cookies on foil covered baking sheet. Place a Life Saver in the center of each cookie. Bake at 375\u00b0 for 7 to 9 minutes until lightly browned or until candy is melted. Cool on baking sheet. Store in layers between wax paper. These freeze nicely. Yields 6 dozen.
