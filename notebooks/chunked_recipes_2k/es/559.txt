Cook hamburger, onion and bell pepper until tender and meat is no longer pink. Add garlic, spaghetti sauce, salt and pepper; set aside. Cook egg noodles in pot until almost tender. Layer casserole dish with half of noodles. Spread half of sauce mixture over noodles. Layer with half of each of the cheeses. Repeat the layering:, noodles, sauce. Mozzarella cheese and Cheddar cheese with Parmesan cheese on top. Bake in oven until brown.
