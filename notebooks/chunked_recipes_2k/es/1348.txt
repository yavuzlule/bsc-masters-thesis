Mix cake mix, 1/2 cup, sugar and Crisco oil together, beating well. Add eggs, one at a time, beating well after each egg. Then add sour cream. Mix well, but do not beat hard after adding sour cream. Spray or grease and flour Bundt pan. Add 1/2 of the batter to the pan. Mix together cinnamon and 1 tablespoon sugar. Sprinkle over batter in pan, then add remaining batter. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 50 minutes. Let cool in pan for 10 minutes before turning out.
