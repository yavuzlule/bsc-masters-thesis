Preheat oven to 325\u00b0. Place pork roast in shallow roasting pan; sprinkle with salt and pepper. Arrange bacon on pork. In small saucepan combine apple juice, cinnamon and cloves; bring to boil. Remove from heat; brush pork with spiced apple juice. Bake for 1 1/2 to 2 hours or until tender, basting frequently with spiced apple juice.
