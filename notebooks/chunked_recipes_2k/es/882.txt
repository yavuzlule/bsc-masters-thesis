Place chicken in ungreased 2-quart casserole or 9 x 9 x 2-inch baking pan. Toss croutons, celery and onion with the 1/2 cup water and the margarine until croutons are moistened. Reserve about 1 1/2 cups of this stuffing. Spread remaining stuffing over chicken. Mix soup with 1 1/2 cups water; pour over stuffing. Top with reserved stuffing. Cook, uncovered, in a 350\u00b0 oven for about 30 minutes.
