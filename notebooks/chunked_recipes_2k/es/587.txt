Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. Blot sausage dry with paper towels. Combine with cheeses, salad dressing, olives and red peppers. Lightly grease a mini muffin tin and press 1 wrapper in each cup. Brush with oil. Bake 5 minutes until golden. Remove from tin. Place on a baking sheet and fill with sausage mixture. Bake 5 minutes until bubbly. Makes 4 to 5 dozen.
