Combine the 1 1/2 cups biscuit mix, sesame seed, water and the 3 tablespoons margarine. Pat into an 8-inch square or a 9-inch round pan; set aside. Melt the 1/3 cup margarine; stir in the 1/2 cup biscuit mix, onion and seasonings. Cook over low heat until bubbly; remove from heat. Stir in chicken broth and milk. Heat to boiling and boil 1 minute. Stir in chicken and mixed vegetables. Turn into ungreased 8-inch square baking dish or 9-inch pie plate. Place dough over top. Cut several slits in top and bake for 25 to 30 minutes at 425\u00b0 or until golden.
