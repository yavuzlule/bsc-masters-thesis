Sift flour, baking powder and salt together. Cut or rub in shortening. Add milk to form a soft dough. Turn out on lightly floured board and knead 1/2 minute. Pat or roll out onto an 8-inch square about 1/4-inch thick. Roll sugar into dough. Cut into strips about 3/8-inch wide. Double each strip over and twist. Place on baking sheet which has been buttered slightly. Bake in hot oven at 450\u00b0 for 10 to 12 minutes.
