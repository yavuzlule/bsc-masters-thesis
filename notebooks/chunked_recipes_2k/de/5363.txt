Brown ground beef and drain. Add jar of spaghetti sauce. Mix half the sauce mixture with spaghetti and place in 9 x 13 x 2-inch baking dish. Saute green peppers and onions in margarine until tender. Add cream cheese and enough milk to make smooth. Spread cheese over spaghetti. Sprinkle with Parmesan cheese. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 25 minutes. Top with Durkee onions. Bake 5 minutes more. Serves 8.
