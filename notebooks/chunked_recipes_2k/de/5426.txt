Preheat oven to 375\u00b0. Combine butter, vanilla and salt in bowl and blend well. Gradually beat in brown sugar. Add flour and chocolate chips; mix well. Press evenly into ungreased 15 x 10 x 1-inch pan. Sprinkle nuts over top. Bake at 375\u00b0 for 25 minutes. Cool, then break in irregular pieces and drain on absorbent paper. Yields 1 3/4 pounds.
