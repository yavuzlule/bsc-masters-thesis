Separate eggs. Beat egg yolks. In saucepan, melt butter over low heat; stir in flour. Add milk. Cook over moderate heat. Stir constantly until thickened and bubbly. Remove from heat. Stirring vigorously, gradually add beaten egg yolks. Mix in salt, pepper and broccoli. Beat egg whites until peaked. Fold in broccoli mixture. Turn into ungreased 2-quart souffle dish. Bake at 325\u00b0 for 45 minutes.
