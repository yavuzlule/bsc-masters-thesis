Melt the butter in a sauce pan, do not brown. Add Worcestershire sauce. Mix in the garlic salt and pepper. Put in the shrimp and mushrooms. Cook, stirring over medium heat for about 8 minutes. The shrimp should begin to turn pink. Stir to mix and add the parsley flakes. Remove from heat, cover and let sit for 4 to 5 minutes. Serve over rice.
