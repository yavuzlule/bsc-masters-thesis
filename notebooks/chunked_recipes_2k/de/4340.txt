Line a buttered 9 x 13-inch pan with bread. Top with chicken, mushrooms and water chestnuts. Dot with mayonnaise; top with cheese slices. Combine eggs, milk and salt; pour over all. Mix soups and spoon over. Cover with foil; refrigerate overnight. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 1 1/2 hours. Uncover; top with 1 cup bread crumbs. Brown 15 minutes longer. Can substitute turkey or ham.
