With a very sharp knife, cut beef across grain into thin strips, 1/8-inch thick. Combine soy sauce, garlic and ginger. Add beef; toss and set aside while preparing vegetables. Heat oil in large frying pan or wok. Add beef; toss over high heat until browned. Taste meat. If it is not tender, cover and simmer for 30 to 40 minutes over low heat. Turn heat up. Add vegetables; toss until vegetables are tender-crisp, about 10 minutes. Mix cornstarch with water; add to pan. Stir and cook until thickened. Add tomatoes; heat through. Makes 4 servings.
