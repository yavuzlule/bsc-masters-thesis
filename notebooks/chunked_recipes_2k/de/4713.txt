Can use a mixer to blend flour, salt and Crisco. Add egg, vinegar and water, mixing lightly with a fork. Pour on plastic wrap. Press dough to oblong shape and wrap and let stand a few minutes. Cut in 4 even shapes and roll out each one. Bake shells 10 to 15 minutes. Prick with fork before baking in a 425\u00b0 oven. Makes 2 double crusts or 4 single.
