Cream butter and sugar. Add nuts, eggs and vanilla. Use a separate bowl; sift flour, soda and salt. Mix contents of two bowls together (mixture may stick to hands). Sprinkle lightly with flour until stops sticking. Make 4 or 5 logs. Wrap in wax paper. Put in freezer overnight. Slice cookies very thin. Place on a cookie sheet. Bake 6 minutes at 350\u00b0. Yield:, 6 to 7 dozen cookies.
