Saute garlic and onion in olive oil until translucent. Add salt, pepper, basil, oregano, tarragon, rosemary, carrots and wine. Cover and cook 5 to 10 minutes, stirring occasionally. Add green pepper, stock, puree and beans. Cover and simmer 30 minutes. Add chopped tomatoes, cooked pasta and zucchini and simmer for another 15 minutes. Makes 6 quarts.
