Mix cornstarch and 2 tablespoons sugar with water and cook together until clear. Set aside. Add a dash of salt to the egg whites. Beat until foamy and standing in peaks. Add cornstarch mixture and continue to beat until creamy. Add 6 tablespoons sugar gradually. Beat until stiff. Pile on pie and bake at 325\u00b0 for approximately 30 minutes or until a delicate brown.
