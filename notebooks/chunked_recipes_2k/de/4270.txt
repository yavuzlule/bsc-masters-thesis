Place partially cooked asparagus in a greased 9-inch square baking dish. In a skillet over medium heat, brown the chicken in oil on both sides. Season with salt and pepper. Arrange chicken over asparagus. In a bowl, mix soup, mayonnaise and lemon juice; pour over chicken. Cover and bake at 375\u00b0 for 40 minutes or until the chicken is tender and juices run clear. Sprinkle with cheese. Makes 4 servings.
