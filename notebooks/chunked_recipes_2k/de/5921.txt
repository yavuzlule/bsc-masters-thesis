Combine yeast, water, salt and sugar. Add to flour and combine thoroughly. Knead 8 to 10 minutes. Cover and let rise 1 to 2 hours; covered with damp cloth. Punch dough down; divide in two equal parts. Grease two 15 x 9 x 1-inch pans with vegetable oil and lightly sprinkle cornmeal on pan. Pat out dough in pan or roll out and place in pan.
