Stew chicken breasts. Let cool. Remove bones and skin, cut into chunks. Add soups, milk and broth. Mix well. Melt margarine and mix with stuffing. Put a layer, about half of mixture, into bottom of a 9 x 12-inch pan. Add chicken mixture and top with remaining stuffing mixture. Bake at 350\u00b0 for about 30 minutes or until top is brown.
