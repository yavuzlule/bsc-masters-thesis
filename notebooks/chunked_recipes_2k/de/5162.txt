Drain juice from peaches. Makes 1 1/4 cups (add water if necessary). Put juice in saucepan and bring to a boil. Put juice in bowl and add jello; dissolve. Add vanilla ice cream and refrigerate 20 minutes. Cut peaches into small pieces. Fold into mixture (after refrigerated). Pour into baked pie shell. Refrigerate 2 to 3 hours. Top with Cool Whip.
