Saute green peppers, onion and pimento in oil until onion is soft. Stir in tomato paste, olives, cheese, bread crumbs, salt, chili powder and pepper. Remove from heat. Spread thin layer of mixture over each steak; roll up and fasten with wooden picks. Place, seam side down, in a shallow baking pan. Stir water into remaining tomato mixture. Heat to boiling, then pour over steaks. Bake in a 375\u00b0 oven for 45 minutes, or until meat is tender.
