Cut candy, nuts and fruits in small pieces. Toss in 1 cup flour. Set aside. Cream shortening and sugar until fluffy. Add eggs one at a time. Add rest of flour alternately with milk; mix well. Add soda, baking powder and salt. Mix all other fruits. Put into well greased tube pan. Bake at 275\u00b0 for about 2 1/2 hours. While still hot make glaze using 2 cups orange juice, 2 cups sugar and 1 tablespoon grated orange rind. Simmer at least 1 hour, then pour over cake.
