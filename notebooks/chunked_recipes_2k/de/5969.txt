Melt margarine in large sauce pan. Add flour, salt and basil. Stir until thick and comes to a boil. Add chicken. Cheese mixture consists of cottage cheese and egg. In 9x13 pan, layer chicken mixture, then noodles, then cheese mixture, spinach. Mozzarella cheese. Repeat layers. Last layer should be chicken. Cover with Parmesan cheese. Bake at 375\u00b0 for 45 minutes.
