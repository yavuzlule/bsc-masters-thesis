Combine the sugar, margarine and milk in heavy 2 1/2-quart saucepan. Bring to a full, rolling boil, stirring constantly. Continue boiling 5 minutes over medium heat, stirring constantly to prevent scorching. Remove from heat; stir in chocolate pieces until melted. Add Marshmallow Creme, nuts and vanilla; beat until well blended. Pour into greased 13 x 9-inch pan. Cool at room temperature. Cut into squares. Makes approximately 3 pounds.
