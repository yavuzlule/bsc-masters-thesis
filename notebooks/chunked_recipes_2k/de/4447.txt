In small mixing bowl, combine chopped red pepper, green onion, mayonnaise, lemon juice, seasoned salt and cayenne pepper. Adjust seasonings to personal preference. Stir in egg, salmon and 4 tablespoons bread crumbs. Form mixture into 6 to 8 balls. Roll salmon balls in remaining bread crumbs; flatten into cakes, about 1/2-inch thick. Fry salmon cakes in melted butter over medium heat for 3 to 4 minutes per side. Makes 4 servings.
