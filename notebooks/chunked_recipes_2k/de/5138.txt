Mix together the first four ingredients with fork or pastry blender until thoroughly mixed. In a small bowl, beat remaining ingredients. Combine the two mixtures, stirring with a fork until all is moist. With your hands, mold into balls. Chill at least 15 minutes before using. Dough can be kept in refrigerator for up to 3 days or wrapped tightly in Saran Wrap and frozen until ready to use. Makes four crusts.
