In a heavy saucepan, combine gelatin, cinnamon, nutmeg and salt. Stir in milk and eggs. Mix well and let stand for 1 minute. Cook over low heat, stirring constantly, until gelatin dissolves and mixture thickens slightly (about 10 minutes). Remove from heat. Stir in pumpkin and mix well. Pour into prepared crust. Chill 3 hours or until set. Garnish as desired. Refrigerate leftovers.
