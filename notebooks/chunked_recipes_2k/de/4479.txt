Roll out 1 package of crescent rolls in a lightly greased 9 X 13 baking dish. Pat out rolls into corners if necessary. Mix cream cheese, 1 cup sugar, and vanilla. Spread mixture over crescent rolls. Cover cream cheese mixture with a layer of crescent rolls. Melt 1/4 cup margarine. Add remaining sugar and cinnamon. Spread over top. Bake 350 degrees for 20-25 minutes.
