Dissolve Jell-O in hot water and stir in cold water. Chill until almost firm. Stir until fluffy. Blend in cream, marshmallows, pineapple, nuts and cherries. Break 1/2 an angel food cake into bite size pieces. Arrange in a 9 x 4-inch cake pan. Cover with part of Jell-O. Break up remainder of cake and repeat layers. Chill overnight and cut into squares.
