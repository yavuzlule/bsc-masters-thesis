Cream sugar and shortening. Add eggs and beat well. Sift flour, baking powder and soda together. Add flour mixture alternately with milk and vanilla. Drop 1 teaspoon dough on cookie sheet. Dip bottom of teaspoon in flour; use this to make a hole in top of cookie. Place 1 teaspoon filling in hole. Bake in hot oven about 400\u00b0. This cookie can be frosted, if desired. Quick and easy!. Yum.
