Cream together shortening and sugar. Add eggs, one at a time. Beat well after each. Stir in applesauce and pumpkin. Sift together dry ingredients and add alternately with apple juice. Stir in chopped nuts. Turn into 2 greased 9 x 5 x 3-inch loaf pans. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 1 hour or until done. May be wrapped in foil and frozen.
