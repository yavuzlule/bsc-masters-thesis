Cook sausage meat over medium-heat in skillet until browned, 10 minutes. Stir often; spoon off fat. Reserve 1 egg yolk. In a large bowl, combine remaining eggs, sausage meat, spinach. Mozzarella cheese. Ricotta cheese, salt, pepper and garlic. Prepare pie crust in a 9 or 10-inch pie pan. Spoon meat mixture into shell. Roll out top crust and put over filling in pie shell. Make steam vent in top shell. Brush top with egg yolk and 1 tablespoon water. Bake at 375\u00b0 for 1 1/4 hours, until golden. Let stand 10 minutes. Cut. Great with a salad.
