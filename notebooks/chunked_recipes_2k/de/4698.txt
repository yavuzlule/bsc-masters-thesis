Cream margarine, sugar and egg in mixing bowl and beat for 3 minutes; blend in vanilla. Stir in flour mixture to make a stiff dough; wrap in wax paper and chill for 3 hours. Roll dough on a lightly floured board. Cut into desired shapes. Can sprinkle colored sugars on tops. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 8 to 10 minutes.
