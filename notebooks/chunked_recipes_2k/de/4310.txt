Put sugar, corn syrup, 1/2 cup water, salt and peanuts in a 2-quart saucepan or casserole. Cook on High (maximum power) for 5 minutes; stir. Cook on High (maximum power) for 13 to 15 minutes or until the syrup separates and threads. This is the hard crack stage or 300\u00b0 on a candy thermometer. Check temperature with thermometer several times during the last few minutes. Dont leave thermometer in oven while cooking.
