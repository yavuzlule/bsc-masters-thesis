Melt margarine in a large saucepan over low heat. Add marshmallows; stir until completely melted. Remove from heat. Add Rice Krispies. , (May add mini chocolate chips. M&Ms, caramel, ice cream topping, etc. ). Press mixture into a pan coated with cooking spray. Layer about 1/2-inch thick. When cool, cut into your favorite shapes with cookie cutter; decorate if you like.
