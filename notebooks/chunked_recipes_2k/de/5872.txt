Drain pineapple, reserving juice. Add water to juice to make 1 1/2 cups. Stir boiling water into gelatin in large bowl 2 minutes or until dissolved. Stir in measured liquid. Pour 2 cups of the gelatin into medium bowl. Refrigerate about 1 1/2 hours or until thickened (spoon drawn through leaves a definite impression). Stir in pineapple.
