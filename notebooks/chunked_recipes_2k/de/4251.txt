In a mixing bowl, cream butter and sugar until fluffy. Add eggs, one at a time, beating well after each addition. Blend in potatoes and vanilla. Combine flour, cocoa and baking soda. Add alternately with milk, blending well after each addition. Stir in nuts. Pour into a greased 13 x 9 x 2-inch baking pan. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 40 to 45 minutes or until cake tests done. Cool on a wire rack.
