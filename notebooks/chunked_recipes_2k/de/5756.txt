Cook spinach; drain. Combine eggs, sour cream, milk, 4 tablespoons Parmesan cheese, onion, salt and pepper. Add cheese and mix. Add spinach and cooked spaghetti. Put into ungreased 13 x 9-inch baking dish. Bake, covered, at 350\u00b0 for 15 minutes, then continue baking, uncovered, for 10 to 15 minutes. Sprinkle with remaining Parmesan cheese before baking.
