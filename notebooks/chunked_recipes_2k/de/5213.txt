Melt marshmallows in milk in saucepan over medium heat. Cool, then add vanilla. Next crush graham crackers and add melted butter. Mix well. Pat half of mixture into a square pan. Put a layer of sliced bananas on top of crumbs. Add whipped cream to marshmallow mixture. Put a layer of this mixture over bananas, then a second layer of bananas and a second layer of cream mixture. Sprinkle remaining crumbs on top. Chill several hours or overnight.
