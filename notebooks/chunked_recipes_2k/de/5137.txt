Melt butter in heavy 3-quart saucepan over medium heat. Add brown sugar, salt and corn syrup; mix well. Add condensed milk gradually, stirring constantly. Cook until mixture reaches 245\u00b0 on candy thermometer, firm ball stage. Remove from heat and stir in vanilla. Pour into buttered 9 x 13-inch pan. Let stand until cool. Cut into squares. Yield: 36 servings.
