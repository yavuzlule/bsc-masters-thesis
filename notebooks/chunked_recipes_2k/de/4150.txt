Place sliced apples in a 9 x 13-inch baking dish. Combine flour, sugar, baking powder and salt. Cut in egg with pastry blender until crumbly. Sprinkle mixture over apples. Pour melted butter over top, then sprinkle lightly with cinnamon. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 45 minutes. Serve with ice cream or whipped cream.
