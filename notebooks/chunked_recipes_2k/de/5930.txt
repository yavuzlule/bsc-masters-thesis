Boil beans for 1 1/2 hours. Drain. In a roaster, add beans, brown sugar, dry mustard, catsup and pancake syrup. Mix together, then put in ham hock. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 4 to 5 hours until beans are soft. Add more water and sugar as necessary. Can take ham hock after baking and break up the meat and put in the beans.
