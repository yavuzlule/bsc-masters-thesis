Saute chicken in butter over low heat about 20 minutes or until tender. Remove chicken to a warm platter. Blend flour into pan drippings; cook over low heat, stirring constantly, until bubbly. Gradually stir in broth and half and half; cook until smooth and thickened, stirring constantly. Stir in mustard. Add chicken; cover and simmer about 10 minutes. Yield:, 2 servings.
