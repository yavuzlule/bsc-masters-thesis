Place spinach in strainer; thaw and press out water. Melt butter in pan over medium heat; add onion and mushrooms. Cook until onion is limp. In a bowl, beat eggs with fork; stir in bread crumbs, soup, 2 tablespoons cheese, pepper, basil, oregano, spinach and onion. Mix. Turn into well-oiled 9-inch pan. Sprinkle with remaining cheese. Bake uncovered for 35 minutes at 325\u00b0 or until set.
