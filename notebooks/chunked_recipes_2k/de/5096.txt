In large skillet, melt butter over medium heat; stir in turmeric. Add rice, onion and garlic. Cook about 3 minutes until onion is translucent, stirring often. Pour in broth mixture; bring to a boil. Reduce heat; cover and simmer 15 minutes. Add scallops, shrimp and peas. Simmer covered 5 minutes longer or until scallops are opaque, shrimp turn pink and peas are Taste and season. Makes 4 servings.
