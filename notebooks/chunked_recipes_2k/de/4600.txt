Brown ground beef in skillet, stirring until crumbly; drain. Add onion and pepper. Cook for several minutes, stirring frequently. Stir in salt, seasoning mix and tomato sauce. Spoon into 9 x 13-inch baking dish. Combine muffin mix and evaporated milk. Spread evenly over casserole. Top with cheese. Bake at 400\u00b0 for 20 to 25 minutes, or until golden brown. Yield: 6 to 8 servings.
