Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. Combine flour, sugar and cocoa; cut in margarine until crumbly. Add eggs; mix well. Reserve 1 1/2 cups crumb mixture. Press remainder evenly on bottom of greased 13 x 9-inch baking pan. Bake 10 minutes. In a saucepan, combine Eagle Brand and 1 cup chocolate chips. Over low heat, cook and stir until chips melt and mixture is smooth. Spread evenly over prepared crust. Add nuts and remaining 1 cup chocolate chips to reserved crumb mixture; sprinkle evenly over top. Bake 25 to 30 minutes or until center is set. Cool. Cut into bars. Store covered at room temperature.
