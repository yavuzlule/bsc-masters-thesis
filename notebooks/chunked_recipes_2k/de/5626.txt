Wash broccoli well; pick off leaves and peel outer skin. Cut florets and chop well. Cut and chop stem of broccoli and mix with florets. Wash cauliflower well; pick off leaves. Use only florets of cauliflower; chop florets and add to broccoli. Mix in onion and bacon. Add mayonnaise to broccoli and cauliflower. Refrigerate 1 hour. Serves 4 to 6 people.
