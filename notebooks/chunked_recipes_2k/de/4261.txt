Cook bacon in skillet on medium heat until crisp. Drain bacon; reserve 2 tablespoons drippings. Crumble bacon; reserve 2 tablespoons. Cook beans as directed on package; drain well. Add mushrooms and onion to reserved drippings in skillet. Cook and stir on medium-high heat 10 minutes; drain. Add bacon, hot cooked green beans and pepper. Mix lightly. Spoon into serving dish. Microwave process cheese spread as directed on label. Pour over green bean mixture. Sprinkle with reserved 2 tablespoons crumbled bacon.
