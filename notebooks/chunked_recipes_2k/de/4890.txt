In, double boiler, melt butter. Stir in flour, nutmeg, paprika, salt, sherry, cream and herbs. Add crabmeat and stir until, thickened. Add mushrooms. Divide into individual oven plates, (4). Sprinkle, grated, cheese over each. Place under broiler, until, cheese, melts and mixture bubbles. \tServe over rice or Chinese noodles.
