Separate broccoli florets and trim tough skins from stalks; cut up. Bring to a boil the water and salt. Add broccoli. Cook 5 to 8 minutes. Add spaghetti, cut in halves. Cook until both are al dente. Drain. Heat olive oil in a pan. Add garlic and brown. Pour mixture over cooked spaghetti and broccoli. Add pepper and cheese. Serve with salad and crusty bread.
