Melt margarine. Blend in flour and salt. Cook over low heat; stir until smooth and bubbly. Remove from heat and gradually stir in milk. Return to heat and bring to boil, stirring constantly. Cook 1 minute. Remove from heat. Stir in spinach and onion. Beat Egg Beaters; fold in spinach mixture. Put in greased casserole. Bake at 325\u00b0 for 50 minutes.
