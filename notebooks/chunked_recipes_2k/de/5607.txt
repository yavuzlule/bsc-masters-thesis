Beat butter and shortening until creamy. Gradually add sugar until well blended. Add eggs, one at a time. Stir in melted chocolate. Combine flour, baking powder, salt and cocoa. Add to butter mixture alternately with milk. Mix until blended. Add pecans and flavorings. Spoon batter into a greased and floured tube pan. Bake at 325\u00b0 for 1 hour and 35 minutes or until wooden pick comes out clean. May need to shield with aluminum foil last 10 minutes.
