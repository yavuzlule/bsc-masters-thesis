Mix oil, chocolate and sugar. Blend in eggs, one at a time, until well mixed. Add vanilla. Stir in salt, flour and baking powder. Chill several hours or overnight. Drop teaspoonful of dough into 10x sugar. Shape into ball. Place about 2-inches apart on greased baking sheet. Bake 10 to 12 minutes at 350\u00b0. Makes about 50.
