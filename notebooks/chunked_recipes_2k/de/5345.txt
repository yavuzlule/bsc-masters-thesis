Line a jelly roll pan with graham crackers. Boil margarine and sugar for 3 minutes. Pour over crackers. Sprinkle with nuts. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 10 minutes. After removing from oven, lay Hershey bars on top; let melt and then spread over crackers. Do not leave in pan too long, as they will stick to pan and be hard to remove.
