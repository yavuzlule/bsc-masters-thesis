Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. Crush crackers to fine crumbs. In 2 qt bowl, mix together crumbs, parsley flakes, oregano, garlic powder, and red pepper, stir in butter. Add scallops and cheese, mix well. Turn into individual baking dishes that have been sprayed with non-stick coating, or well buttered. Sprinkle with paprika. Bake 15 minutes. Serve with brown rice. Serve 4 - 6 people.
