Place olive oil and crushed garlic in a large deep frying pan. Heat on medium heat. Open anchovies and place anchovies oil in pan. Chop anchovies and add. When garlic turns slightly golden brown, add tomatoes (crush tomatoes first) and Ginl. Add raisins and pine nuts. Simmer for 25 minutes. Add olives and simmer 5 minutes more. Cook pasta. Pour sauce over and mix well.
