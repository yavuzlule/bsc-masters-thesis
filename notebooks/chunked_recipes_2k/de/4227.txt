Heat oven to 375\u00b0. Combine flour, 1/3 cup sugar, salt and nutmeg. Cut in butter until mixture is crumbly. Peel and core pears and cut into chunks. Drain cherries. Combine pears and cherries with remaining 2/3 cup sugar, cornstarch and almond extract. Put fruit mixture in a 2-quart baking dish. Sprinkle crumb topping over fruit. Bake until golden, about 1 hour.
