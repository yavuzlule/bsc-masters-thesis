Heat oven to 350\u00b0. Blend baking mix, sugar, eggs and milk in large mixer bowl on low speed for 1/2 minute, scraping bowl frequently. Beat 3 minutes on medium speed. Stir in remaining ingredients. Pour batter into greased and floured loaf pan (9 x 5 x 3-inch). Bake 50 to 60 minutes or until toothpick inserted in center comes out clean. Cool 10 minutes, remove from pan. Cool thoroughly before serving.
