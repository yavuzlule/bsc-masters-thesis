Put broccoli in 11 x 7-inch baking dish. Melt 1 stick of margarine in saucepan. Add flour and milk. Cook until thick. Add bouillon cubes. Stir until dissolved. Pour over broccoli and work through with a fork. Mix 6 tablespoons melted margarine, stuffing and water. Spread over broccoli. Bake at 350\u00b0 for 30 minutes.
