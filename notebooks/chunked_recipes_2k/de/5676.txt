Melt butter in medium saucepan. Add sugar, stirring well. Add peanut butter and chocolate flavor; stir until well blended. Stir in cereal; mix until cereal is well coated. Pack mixture into well-buttered 8-inch ring mold. Chill until firm. Unmold by running spatula around inside of mold. Serve with ice cream balls, if desired. Makes 6 to 8 servings.
