Sift together flour, cocoa, baking soda and salt. Set aside. Cream together butter, oil, sugar and vanilla until light and creamy with mixer. Beat in eggs one at a time. Add dry ingredients alternating with buttermilk. Stir in zucchini. Pour batter into 9 x 13-inch pan. Sprinkle with chocolate chips and walnuts. Bake at 325\u00b0 for 55 minutes.
