Place asparagus and water in 1-quart casserole; cover. Microwave at High 4 1/2 to 5 1/2 minutes, or until asparagus is hot, stirring after half the cooking time to break apart. Drain. Cover and set aside. In small bowl or 1 cup measure, combine lemon juice. Tabasco sauce and seasonings. Pour mixture over asparagus. Toss to coat. Sprinkle with sunflower seeds. Garnish with lemon slices if desired.
