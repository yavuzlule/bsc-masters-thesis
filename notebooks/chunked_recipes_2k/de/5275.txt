Mix all dry ingredients in a huge bowl. Add the liquids and beaten eggs and mix well. You can store in a sealed covered container in refrigerator for up to 6 weeks. Bake in greased muffin tins sprayed with cooking spray. Fill 2/3 full and bake at 400\u00b0 for 15 to 18 minutes. Dont overcook. These freeze well. These are very good.
