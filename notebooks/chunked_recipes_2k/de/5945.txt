Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. Mix first 3 ingredients together. Spread in 8 x 8-inch ungreased pan and bake for 20 minutes. Mix next 4 ingredients. Add flour and baking powder to mixture. Pour this over crust and bake for 25 minutes. Remove from oven and sprinkle with powdered sugar while warm. Cut into 1 or 2-inch bars when cold.
