Heat oven to 250\u00b0 and grease 2 cookie sheets. Mix cereal and peanuts. Heat brown sugar, margarine, corn syrup and salt in pan until bubbly around edges. Stir 2 minutes longer. Remove from heat and stir in soda. Pour over cereal and spread in pans. Bake 15 minutes. Cool 10 minutes. Loosen mixture. Let stand until firm, then break into bite size pieces.
