Cut carrots into long thin strips. Set aside. Combine brown sugar, margarine, water, salt and cinnamon in 1 1/2-quart casserole dish. Microwave on High (100%) until margarine is melted, 45 seconds to 1 1/2 minutes. Stir to blend. Stir in carrots until coated. Cover, microwave on High (100%) until carrots are tender, 5 to 8 minutes, stirring once. Serves 4 to 6.
