Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. Grease large cookie sheet (15 1/2 x 10 1/2 x 1-inch. Sift together flour, baking powder and salt; set aside. In large saucepan, melt butter and stir in brown sugar; cool 10 minutes. Add eggs, one at a time, beating well after each. Stir in flour mixture and chocolate pieces. Blend well and spread in pan. Bake 25 to 30 minutes. Cool and cut into squares. Makes about 3 dozen.
