Mix flour with baking soda. Combine butter, sugars and pudding mix in a large mixer bowl. Beat until smooth and creamy. Beat in eggs. Gradually add flour mixture, then stir in oats and raisins. (Batter will be stiff. ). Drop by rounded measuring teaspoonfuls onto ungreased baking sheets, about 2-inches apart. Bake at 375\u00b0 for 10 to 12 minutes. Makes about 5 dozen.
