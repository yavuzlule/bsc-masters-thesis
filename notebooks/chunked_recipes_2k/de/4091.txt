Drain oysters, reserving liquor and chop. Arrange alternate layers of celery, rice and oysters in a kettle; season with salt, pepper and Old Bay. Add water and oyster liquid; simmer 1/2 hour. Mix egg yolks and milk and add to chowder. Heat below boiling point for only a few minutes and serve piping hot. If allowed to cook too long after adding the eggs, it will curdle. Serves 6.
