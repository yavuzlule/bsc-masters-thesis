Brown bacon in skillet for 6 minutes. Put bacon on paper towels. Pour off fat. Use 3 tablespoons fat in skillet. Put chopped onion in fat. Cook until tender, about 5 minutes. Pour pork and beans in casserole dish. Stir in brown sugar, catsup. Worcestershire sauce and mustard until well mixed. Stir in onion. Bake in a 350\u00b0 oven for 1 hour. Stir beans and crumble cooked bacon over beans.
