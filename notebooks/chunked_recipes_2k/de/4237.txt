Cut potatoes in half and cook in boiling water until firm but tender (or microwave 8 minutes on High). Allow to cool. Coarsely grate potatoes and place into a greased casserole dish. Add onions and half the cheese. In a bowl, combine cream, milk and pepper. Pour over potatoes and cheese, then top with remaining cheese and the nutmeg. Chill for 2 hours or overnight. Bake, uncovered, in the oven at 350\u00b0 for 40 minutes. Serves 6.
