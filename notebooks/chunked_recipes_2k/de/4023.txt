Preheat oven to 350\u00b0. Place salmon in a shallow baking dish. Pour in the olive oil and lemon juice. Smear the pressed garlic onto the salmon. Spoon the oil and lemon over the fish. Sprinkle the salt, pepper and garlic powder onto the salmon. Place tinfoil over the baking dish and bake the salmon for 10 minutes at 350\u00b0.
