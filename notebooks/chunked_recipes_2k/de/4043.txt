Heat oven to 375\u00b0. Measure flour; blend with nuts. Bring corn syrup, shortening and sugar to boil in saucepan over medium heat, stirring constantly. Remove from heat; gradually stir in flour and nuts. Drop batter by level teaspoonful, about 3 inches apart, on lightly greased baking sheet. Bake only 8 to 9 cookies at a time. Bake for 5 to 6 minutes; remove from oven and allow to stand 5 minutes before removing from baking sheet. Makes about 4 dozen cookies.
