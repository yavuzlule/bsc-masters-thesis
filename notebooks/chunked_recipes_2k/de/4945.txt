Heat oven to 350\u00b0 (moderate). Mix butter, sugar, vanilla and chocolate thoroughly. Measure flour by dipping method or by sifting. Blend in flour and salt. If dry, add 1 to 2 tablespoons cream. Wrap level tablespoonful of dough around well drained maraschino cherry. Place 1-inch apart on ungreased baking sheet. Bake 12 to 15 minutes. Cool.
