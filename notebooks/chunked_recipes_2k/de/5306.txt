Mix together shortening and sugars; add egg. Now sift together flour, soda and cinnamon. Add to first mixture. Now add oats, walnuts or pecans and vanilla. Chill for 1 hour. Put walnut-sized pieces on a greased cookie sheet. Butter the bottom of a small glass. Dip it in granulated sugar and flatten out the little pieces. Just keep doing this; you dont need to rebutter the glass bottom each time, just resugar each time. Bake at 350\u00b0 for ten minutes.
