Heat the oven to 350\u00b0. Butter an 8-inch square pan. Line it with aluminum foil and then butter the foil. Mix all the ingredients together, except the jam. Press 2 cups of the mixture into the bottom of the prepared pan. Spread the jam to within 1/4-inch of the edge. Sprinkle the remaining crumb mixture over the top and lightly press it into the jam. Bake 35 to 40 minutes and allow to cool on a wire rack before cutting. Makes 24 (2 x 1 1/2-inch) bars.
