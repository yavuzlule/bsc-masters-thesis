Beat eggs on high for 5 minutes, gradually beat in sugar. Stir in pumpkin and lemon juice. Stir together flour, baking powder, cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg and salt. Fold into pumpkin mixture. Spread in greased and floured 15x10x1-inch pan. Top with finely chopped nuts. Bake at 375\u00b0 for 15 minutes. Turn out on towel. Sprinkle with powdered sugar. Roll towel and cake together, let cool.
