Titles Anagrams used for titles afford scope for some types of wit. Examples: Homer Hickam's book Rocket Boys was adapted into the 1999 film October Sky. The tapes for the revival of the BBC show Doctor Who were labeled with the anagram Torchwood, which later went on to be used as the name for a spin-off show. In multi-episode shows, the program occasionally substitutes the anagram of an actor's name for the actual name to prevent revealing the true identity of the role (for instance, The Master) being played by the actor. The New Wave band Missing Persons' best-selling album was called Spring Session M. Hip-hop artist MF Doom recorded a 2004 album called Mm..Food. Brian Eno's album Before and After Science includes a song entitled "King's Lead Hat", an anagram of "Talking Heads", a band Eno has worked with.
