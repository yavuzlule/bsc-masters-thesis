NGC 253-dw2 (2016) is a dwarf spheroidal (dSph) galaxy candidate undergoing tidal disruption around the nearby galaxy NGC 253. The galaxy was discovered by an amateur astronomer with a small-aperture amateur telescope. KIC 8462852 (2016) is an F-type star showing unusual dimming events. HD 74389 (2016) contains a debris disk. It is the first debris disk discovered around a star with a companion white dwarf. AWI0005x3s (2016) is the oldest M-dwarf with a debris disk detected in a moving group at the time of the discovery. PSR J1913+1102 (2016) is a binary neutron star with the highest total mass at the time of the discovery. Donatiello I (2016) a nearby spheroidal dwarf galaxy discovered by the Italian amateur astronomer Giuseppe Donatiello. It is also the first galaxy to be named after an amateur astronomer.
